Bugs & Butterflies

Toddler 1 week Pro Theme: Insects & Gardens

Weekly focus

Explore gentle insect life through sensory play, process art, and outdoor noticing walks — without requiring perfect crafts.

Learning objectives

  • Notice living things outdoors with adult support
  • Use new vocabulary during circle and play
  • Practice fine-motor pinching and sorting

Vocabulary

butterfly wing crawl garden gentle

Master materials

  • Magnifying glasses · baskets · leaves
  • Watercolor · coffee filters · clothespins
  • Picture books from the book list

Family connection

  • Take a “noticing walk” and count bugs spotted
  • Sing one kit song at home
