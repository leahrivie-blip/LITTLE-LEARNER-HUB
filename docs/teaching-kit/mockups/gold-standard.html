5. Activity depth + Substitute This Activity

Example photo · setup photo · full teach fields · materials-aware swap
Activity · Outdoor Bug Hunt
Example photo — children using magnifying glasses outdoors
Setup photo — tray ready before children arrive

Setup instructions

  1. Choose a safe outdoor edge.
  2. Hand out magnifying glasses at the door.
  3. Mark a look-with-eyes-first boundary.
  4. Keep a leaf-sorting tray ready for return.

Cleanup tips

  • Collect glasses in a basket at the door.
  • Wipe lenses once before storing.
  • Quick hand wash before next activity.

Materials

Magnifying glasses · optional bug ID cards · clipboard · water

Learning objective

Children notice living things outdoors with care and practice descriptive words using a simple science tool.

Teacher prompts

Look “What do you notice under the leaf?”
Wonder “Where might a bug hide when it’s sunny?”
Care “How can we look without touching?”

Observation ideas

  • Uses magnifying glass independently?
  • Which describing words appear?
  • Shows gentle looking / waiting?
