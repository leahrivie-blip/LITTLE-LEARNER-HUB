4. Activity detail (full teaching depth)

Photo · setup · materials · objectives · prompts · cleanup · observations
Activity · Outdoor Bug Hunt
Example setup: toddlers with magnifying glasses at garden edge (replace with real kit photo)

Setup instructions

  1. Choose a safe outdoor edge (garden, planter, mulch path).
  2. Hand each child a magnifying glass before going out.
  3. Mark a “look with eyes first” boundary with cones or chalk.
  4. Keep a small tray ready for found-leaf sorting afterward.

Cleanup tips

  • Collect magnifying glasses in a basket at the door.
  • Wipe lenses once before storing.
  • Shake outdoor shoes; quick hand wash before next activity.

Materials

Magnifying glasses · optional bug ID cards · clipboard for notes · water bottle

Learning objectives

  • Notice living things outdoors with care
  • Use simple science tools (magnifying glass)
  • Practice descriptive words (tiny, shiny, crawling)

Teacher prompts

Look “What do you notice under the leaf?”
Wonder “Where might a bug hide when it’s sunny?”
Care “How can we look without touching?”

Observation prompts (tied to this activity)

  • Did the child use the magnifying glass independently?
  • Which describing words did they try?
  • Did they show gentle looking / waiting?

Binder contents

  1. Cover & week overview
  2. Monday Morning Setup
  3. Daily Classroom pages (Mon–Fri)
  4. Activity cards with photo cues
  5. Books, songs, vocabulary
  6. Parent connection messages

How to use

Keep Monday Setup on the fridge or prep shelf. Leave each Daily Classroom page open during the day. Slip activity cards into a sheet protector for outdoor use.

Sample — Tuesday Classroom page

Schedule: Arrival song → Sensory bin → Read-aloud → Bug hunt → Paint prints → Cleanup

Parent message: Today we explored bugs and butterflies! Ask your child to show a butterfly flutter…

Observe: magnifying-glass use · describing words · gentle looking

Printed output should feel like a professional curriculum binder a provider keeps in a sleeve all week — not a stack of generic lesson plans.
