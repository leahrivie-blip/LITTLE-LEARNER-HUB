Bugs & Butterflies

A gentle week of outdoor noticing, sensory sorting, and process art — ready to teach without scrambling for extras.

Toddler · 18–36 mo 1 week Pro Theme: Insects & Gardens

Weekly focus

Invite toddlers to notice living things outdoors with calm adult support — using simple vocabulary, gentle hands, and open-ended art.

Learning objectives

  • Notice insects and plants with adult support
  • Use new words during circle and play
  • Practice pinching, sorting, and pouring
  • Move bodies like butterflies and bugs

Leaf & Bug Sort

Sensory · Setup 5 min · 15 min play · Indoor or shaded outdoor table

Materials

  • Leaves · small cups · 4–6 toy bugs
  • Substitution: paper leaf shapes

Setup

  • Scatter leaves in a low tray
  • Place cups for color or size sorting

Steps

  • Invite children to find a bug
  • Ask: “Where should it go?”
  • Model gentle hands

Teacher language

  • “You found a tiny one.”
  • “Gentle hands on the bugs.”

Adaptations

  • Simplified: sort only by color
  • Extension: count bugs in each cup

Observe · Family

  • Watch for sorting strategies
  • Family: sort socks by color at home
