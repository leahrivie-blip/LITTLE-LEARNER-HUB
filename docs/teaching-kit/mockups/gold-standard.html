Teaching Kit vs Lesson Plan

Lesson plan (library)

  • Curriculum catalog item
  • “What is this unit?”
  • Browse, favorite, assign
  • Source of week’s content

Teaching Kit (operations)

  • Classroom binder for the week
  • “What do I do today?”
  • Prep · Today · Print packs
  • Stays open during care

Same underlying week of content — different job and different default screens.
