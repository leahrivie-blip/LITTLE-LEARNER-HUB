WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will investigate safe textures through caregiver-guided touching, pressing, grasping, patting, rubbing, tummy-time exploration, sealed sensory experiences, books, songs, and movement. The week emphasizes sensory awareness, early comparison, hand development, cause and effect, visual attention, body awareness, and responsive communication.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore soft, smooth, bumpy, crinkly, cool, and squishy textures
Strengthen reaching, grasping, transferring, pressing, and releasing
Support tummy-time tolerance and mobile exploration
Build early descriptive vocabulary through repeated caregiver language
Encourage cause-and-effect discovery
Develop sensory preferences and self-regulation
Strengthen shared attention and secure caregiver interaction
Practice comparing two contrasting textures

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to name safe everyday textures during routines, such as a soft towel, smooth spoon, or bumpy washcloth. Encourage adults to follow the infant's cues and stop when the child turns away or becomes uncomfortable.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe texture preferences, withdrawal or approach, grasp patterns, bilateral hand use, repeated cause-and-effect actions, tummy-time engagement, transfer between hands, vocal responses, and calming strategies.

ADAPTATIONS:
Introduce one texture at a time for younger or sensory-sensitive infants. Provide hand-under-hand support without forcing contact. Position materials under hands, feet, or beside the body depending on mobility. Offer extra time, familiar caregiver proximity, and a quiet retreat when needed.

DAILY_THEME:
Soft and Cozy

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Explore soft materials with hands and feet.
Practice grasping and rubbing.
Build calm shared attention.
Hear descriptive words during play.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Soft, Cozy, Touch, Rub, Gentle

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Fleece squares
Plush ball

Floor mat

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:

This Is the Way We Touch and Feel

CIRCLE_TIME:
Offer one soft square at a time. Rub it gently on the infant's hand or foot only after observing acceptance. Repeat "soft" and pause for eye contact, movement, or vocalization.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Provide blanket time in shade and compare the soft blanket with the gentle outdoor breeze.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Notice relaxed hands, grasping, rubbing, smiling, turning away, mouthing attempts, and duration of interest.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Place fabric beside the cheek or under feet for infants who avoid hand contact. Let mobile infants crawl across a secured soft mat.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use tightly woven, washable fabrics with no loose fibers, ribbons, buttons, or strings.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soft Texture Treasure Basket
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Explore several soft textures through safe open-ended play.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants choose from a shallow basket containing large soft materials while a caregiver labels sensations.
MATERIALS:
Fleece square
Soft scarf
Plush ball
Shallow basket
SETUP:
Place two or three large items in a shallow basket on a clean mat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Offer one item at a time, follow the infant's choices, and narrate actions.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Soft scarf. You are rubbing it. The ball feels cozy in your hands."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the basket.
2. Offer one soft item.
3. Allow touching, grasping, or pushing.
4. Compare a second soft texture.
5. Help return items to the basket when finished.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory exploration, grasping, choice making, and vocabulary exposure.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record preferred items, grasp duration, and calming or avoidance cues.
VOCABULARY:
Soft, Scarf, Ball, Touch, Basket
EXTENSIONS:
Add a mirror so infants can watch themselves hold the item.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use one item for younger infants; place the basket slightly away for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Inspect and wash all materials before use.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Caregiver presents and supports touch. 6-12 months: Infant selects, transfers, and releases.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soft Feet Path
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Build body awareness through foot-based sensory exploration.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants press or kick their feet against securely placed soft fabric surfaces.
MATERIALS:
Large fleece mat
Rolled towel for positioning
SETUP:
Lay the fleece flat and position the infant safely on back, side, or supported sitting.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support posture and describe foot movements.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Your feet are touching something soft. Kick, kick."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Place feet on the fabric.
2. Pause for spontaneous movement.
3. Gently flex and release legs if welcomed.
4. Let the infant kick or rub independently.
5. End when interest decreases.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Body awareness, leg movement, sensory regulation, and cause and effect.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe kicking strength, repeated movement, and sensory comfort.
VOCABULARY:
Feet, Soft, Kick, Touch
EXTENSIONS:
Add a second smooth mat for contrast later in the week.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use side-lying or supported sitting based on development.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep fabric flat and prevent bunching near the face.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Back-lying kicks. 6-12 months: Supported standing or crawling over the mat.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Smooth Mirror Touch
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Explore a smooth surface while building self-recognition and reaching.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants touch, pat, and slide hands across an unbreakable mirror.
MATERIALS:
Unbreakable mirror
Floor mat
SETUP:
Secure mirror flat or at a safe low angle.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay beside the infant and label reflection and movements.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Smooth mirror. I see you. Your hand is sliding."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the mirror at midline.
2. Wait for the infant to focus.
3. Model one gentle pat.
4. Encourage touching or sliding.
5. Name the reflection and end with a wave.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Visual attention, self-awareness, reaching, and tactile exploration.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe gaze duration, touching, smiling, and social response.
VOCABULARY:
Smooth, Mirror, Hand, Slide, You
EXTENSIONS:
Place a smooth laminated picture beside the mirror for comparison.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use supported sitting or tummy-time positioning.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Check mirror edges and secure placement before use.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Visual focus and hand contact. 6-12 months: Patting, waving, and moving toward reflection.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Satin Pull and Release
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen grasp, pull, and release skills.
DESCRIPTION:
A large satin scarf is partly placed through a wide basket opening for infants to pull.
MATERIALS:
Large satin scarf
Wide shallow basket
SETUP:
Thread part of the scarf through the basket while keeping the full length supervised.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Hold the basket steady and celebrate attempts.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Pull the smooth scarf. You pulled it out."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the scarf end.
2. Place it within reach.
3. Wait for grasping.
4. Let the infant pull while securing the basket.
5. Repeat once or twice, then remove the scarf.

Grasp strength, bilateral coordination, cause and effect, and persistence.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice grasp type, pulling force, and repeated attempts.
VOCABULARY:
Pull, Smooth, Scarf, Out
EXTENSIONS:
Mobile infants can place the scarf back into the basket with help.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer hand-under-hand support or a larger exposed section.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Scarves remain under direct adult control at all times.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Supported grasp and pull. 6-12 months: Independent pull and container play.

Pass one large bumpy ball from caregiver to infant. Repeat "bumpy" and demonstrate patting slowly.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bumpy Ball Roll
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Encourage reaching, tracking, and early turn-taking.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver slowly rolls a large textured ball toward or across the infant's space.
MATERIALS:
Large textured ball
Floor mat
SETUP:
Clear a flat play area.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Control the ball's speed and respond to each movement.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Bumpy ball. It rolls to you. Push it back."

1. Let the infant touch the stationary ball.
2. Roll it slowly a short distance.

Use a lightweight ball too large to mouth fully.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Touch and track. 6-12 months: Reach, crawl, push, and roll.

THURSDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Crinkly and Noisy

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Discover sound through touch.
Practice squeezing and releasing.
Build cause-and-effect awareness.
Attend to rhythm and repeated actions.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Crinkle, Sound, Squeeze, Shake, Listen

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Infant-safe crinkle squares
Crinkle book
Large sealed crinkle bottle
Floor mat

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Cognitive Development, Fine Motor, Language & Literacy, Music & Movement

DAILY_BOOK:
That's Not My Puppy | Fiona Watt

DAILY_SONG:
Shake and Stop

CIRCLE_TIME:
Crinkle one square gently, pause, and wait for the infant's response. Repeat the action and label the sound.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Listen for gentle outdoor sounds and compare them with the crinkle sound indoors.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe startle or calming response, repeated squeezing, sound localization, two-handed use, and anticipation.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Keep sound low and predictable. Let sound-sensitive infants watch before touching.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use commercially made or securely sewn infant-safe crinkle materials only.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Make It Crinkle
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Develop cause-and-effect understanding through hand movement.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants squeeze, pat, or kick a large infant-safe crinkle square.
MATERIALS:
Large crinkle square
Floor mat
SETUP:
Place the crinkle square within reach or under feet.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model one action, pause, and let the infant repeat.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"You squeezed it. Crinkle. You made that sound."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Make one quiet crinkle sound.
2. Offer the square.
3. Allow squeezing, patting, or kicking.
4. Pause after each sound.
5. Repeat only while the infant remains engaged.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect, grasping, listening, and motor planning.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe whether the infant repeats an action to recreate the sound.
VOCABULARY:
Crinkle, Sound, Squeeze, Again
EXTENSIONS:
Compare a crinkle square with a silent soft square.
ADAPTATIONS:
Place under feet or provide hand-under-hand support.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Inspect seams and remove immediately if damaged.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Adult-supported touch and kicks. 6-12 months: Independent squeeze, transfer, and comparison.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Crinkle Crawl Target
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Motivate reaching, rolling, or crawling toward a sound source.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver places a crinkle item just within an achievable movement range.
MATERIALS:
Large crinkle square
Floor mat
SETUP:
Create a clear safe floor space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Position the item based on developmental level and celebrate effort.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"The crinkle is over here. Reach. You moved closer."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show and crinkle the item.
2. Place it slightly to one side or ahead.
3. Wait for turning, rolling, reaching, or crawling.
4. Move it closer if frustration appears.
5. Allow success and exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Motor planning, persistence, localization, and goal-directed movement.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice movement strategy, effort, and response to encouragement.
VOCABULARY:
Reach, Roll, Crawl, Crinkle, Here
EXTENSIONS:
Move the target between two reachable positions.
ADAPTATIONS:
Keep directly beside younger infants; use a longer crawl distance for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep the floor clear and remain within arm's reach.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Head turn, reach, or roll. 6-12 months: Crawl, scoot, cruise, or retrieve.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Squishy Sensory Bag Press
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

Explore pressure, movement, and resistance.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants press a securely sealed gel bag and watch the material move.

Double-sealed gel sensory bag
Painter's tape

SAFETY_NOTES:

AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Hands or feet press while lying down. 6-12 months: Sitting, kneeling, or crawling interaction.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Choose Your Texture
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Encourage choice making and comparison.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants choose between two contrasting large texture items while caregivers describe the selection.
MATERIALS:
Soft square
Smooth square
Bumpy mat
Crinkle square
SETUP:
Present only two clean items at a time.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Hold both within reach and honor the infant's gaze, reach, or body movement as a choice.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"You chose bumpy. This one feels soft. Which one will you touch?"
DIRECTIONS:
1. Present two contrasting textures.
2. Wait for a gaze or reach.
3. Offer the selected item.
4. Describe the texture and action.
5. Switch choices once if interest continues.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Choice making, comparison, communication, and sensory awareness.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe consistent preferences, reaching accuracy, and engagement.
VOCABULARY:
Choose, Soft, Smooth, Bumpy, Touch
EXTENSIONS:
Create a weekly texture photo note for families.
ADAPTATIONS:
Interpret gaze or body orientation as a valid choice.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only oversized, intact, washable materials.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Visual or body-orientation choice. 6-12 months: Reaching, selecting, transferring, and returning.
