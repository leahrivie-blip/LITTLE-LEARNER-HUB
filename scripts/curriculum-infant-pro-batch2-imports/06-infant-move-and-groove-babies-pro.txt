WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore rhythm, body movement, balance, reaching, rolling, tummy time, crawling, supported standing, and caregiver-infant connection through music and movement. Activities are designed to follow each infant's developmental level and cues while encouraging joyful movement without forcing positions or milestones.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build body awareness through repeated movement words
Encourage kicking, reaching, rolling, crawling, pulling up, and supported cruising
Develop balance and postural control
Explore fast, slow, stop, go, up, and down
Strengthen rhythm and listening skills
Support imitation of simple gestures and sounds
Promote secure relationships through responsive movement games
Encourage choice making and self-regulation during active play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to repeat one favorite movement song at home and pause so the infant can anticipate the next motion. Remind adults that movement should always follow the infant's cues and that babies should never be pulled into sitting, standing, or walking before they are ready.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe head and trunk control, symmetrical movement, reaching, rolling attempts, tummy-time tolerance, crawling patterns, supported standing, rhythm response, gesture imitation, anticipation, and calming after active play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer movement in positions already comfortable for the infant. Use slower songs, smaller motions, or one-on-one interaction for easily overstimulated infants. Support the body at the trunk rather than pulling arms. Provide extra floor space and stable furniture for mobile infants.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Kick, Reach, and Wiggle

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Encourage spontaneous arm and leg movement.
Practice reaching toward a safe target.
Build awareness of body parts.
Respond to rhythm and pauses.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Kick, Reach, Wiggle, Arms, Legs

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Large scarf
Soft rattle
Floor mat
Unbreakable mirror
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Physical Development, Music & Movement, Language & Literacy, Social Emotional

DAILY_BOOK:
From Head to Toe | Eric Carle

DAILY_SONG:
Head, Shoulders, Knees, and Toes

CIRCLE_TIME:
Sing slowly while touching or naming body parts with permission. Pause after each line and respond to kicks, wiggles, smiles, or vocalizations.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Provide shaded blanket movement and let infants watch scarves move in the breeze.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe symmetrical kicking, reaching accuracy, hand opening, visual tracking, and response to rhythm.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Place targets closer for younger infants. Position toys slightly to the side for rolling practice or farther ahead for mobile infants.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use floor-based movement whenever possible. Never pull an infant by the hands or arms.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scarf Reach and Kick
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Encourage reaching, kicking, tracking, and body awareness.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver moves a large scarf near the infant's hands or feet and pauses for voluntary movement.
MATERIALS:
Large scarf
Floor mat
SETUP:
Position the infant safely on the back, side, tummy, or supported sitting.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Move slowly and place the scarf within an achievable range.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Reach for the scarf. Kick with your feet. You made it move."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the scarf at midline.
2. Move it near the hands.
3. Pause for reaching.
4. Move it near the feet and pause for kicking.
5. Celebrate movement and end before fatigue.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Visual-motor coordination, body awareness, cause and effect, and gross-motor control.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record preferred limbs, movement strength, tracking, and repeated actions.
VOCABULARY:
Reach, Kick, Scarf, Move
EXTENSIONS:
Use the scarf beside the body to invite a safe rolling attempt.
ADAPTATIONS:
Keep the scarf stationary for younger infants; encourage crawling toward it for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Scarves remain in adult hands and never cover the face.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Tracking, reaching, and kicking. 6-12 months: Rolling, crawling, and pulling to stand toward the scarf.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Wiggle Time
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Build self-awareness and imitation through movement.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch themselves and a caregiver move arms, legs, head, and facial expressions in a mirror.
MATERIALS:
Unbreakable mirror
Floor mat
SETUP:
Secure the mirror at floor level.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Mirror the infant's movements and add one simple gesture at a time.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"I see you wiggle. Arms up. Legs kick. You are moving."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Position the infant safely near the mirror.
2. Copy one spontaneous movement.
3. Add a slow wave or clap.
4. Pause for imitation or attention.
5. Finish with a smile and wave.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-recognition, imitation, social connection, and motor planning.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe mirror gaze, smiling, repeated gestures, and social engagement.
VOCABULARY:
Mirror, Wiggle, Wave, Move, You
EXTENSIONS:
Add a familiar song with one repeated movement.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use tummy time, side-lying, or supported sitting based on development.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Inspect and secure the mirror before use.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Watch and respond. 6-12 months: Imitate, wave, clap, and move closer.

DAILY_THEME:
Roll and Turn

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Encourage head turning and weight shifting.
Support safe rolling attempts.
Track a moving object across midline.
Build spatial vocabulary.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Roll, Turn, Side, Over, Here

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Large lightweight ball
Soft rattle

Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:

DAILY_BOOK:

DAILY_SONG:
Roll, Roll, Roll the Ball

CIRCLE_TIME:
Roll a large ball slowly across the group mat and repeat "roll." Pause so infants can track or reach.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Offer supervised side-lying and tummy-time movement on a shaded blanket.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe head turning, trunk rotation, weight shifting, rolling attempts, and frustration cues.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Use a rolled towel behind the back for supported side-lying. Do not complete a roll for the infant unless part of safe repositioning.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain a clear floor area and stay within arm's reach.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Ball Across the Body
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Encourage tracking across midline and safe weight shifting.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver slowly moves or rolls a large ball from one side of the infant's body to the other.
MATERIALS:
Large lightweight ball
Floor mat
SETUP:
Place the infant on a clear mat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Adjust the ball's distance and support only as needed.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"The ball rolls to the side. Turn and look. You reached across."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Present the ball at midline.
2. Move it slowly toward one side.
3. Pause for head turning or reaching.
4. Repeat toward the other side.
5. Let the infant touch and push the ball.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Midline crossing, trunk rotation, tracking, and cause and effect.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe head control, reaching across, side preference, and rolling attempts.
VOCABULARY:
Ball, Roll, Side, Turn
EXTENSIONS:
Place the ball slightly ahead during tummy time or crawling.
ADAPTATIONS:
Keep movement close for younger infants; increase distance for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use a soft ball too large to swallow and keep the area obstacle-free.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Track and turn. 6-12 months: Roll, crawl, push, and retrieve.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Side-to-Side Song
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Build trunk awareness and anticipation through gentle movement.
DESCRIPTION:
Caregivers sing while helping infants shift attention and body weight side to side in a developmentally appropriate position.
MATERIALS:
Floor mat
Small cushion
SETUP:
Position infant in side-lying, supported sitting, or seated on caregiver lap.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support the trunk and use very small movements.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"To this side. Back to the middle. Now the other side."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Begin at the infant's center.
2. Sing one slow line.
3. Shift attention or body slightly to one side.
4. Return to center and pause.
5. Repeat to the other side.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Balance reactions, trunk awareness, rhythm, and anticipation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice head control, balance adjustments, enjoyment, and stress cues.
VOCABULARY:
Side, Middle, Turn, Slow
EXTENSIONS:
Mobile infants can follow a caregiver side to side between two floor markers.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use only visual side-to-side movement if physical shifting is not comfortable.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Support head and trunk; never swing or bounce forcefully.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Visual and tiny supported shifts. 6-12 months: Seated balance and crawling direction changes.

WEDNESDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Tummy Time, Crawl, and Go

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Strengthen head, shoulder, arm, and trunk muscles.
Encourage forward movement toward a motivating object.
Explore stop-and-go language.
Build persistence through achievable movement challenges.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Tummy, Crawl, Go, Stop, Forward

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Tummy-time pillow
Large crinkle toy
Mirror
Floor mat
Movement cards

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Physical Development, Cognitive Development, Language & Literacy, Social Emotional

DAILY_BOOK:
Barnyard Dance! | Sandra Boynton

DAILY_SONG:
The Wheels on the Bus

CIRCLE_TIME:
Use a simple "go" and "stop" scarf routine. Move the scarf during "go" and hold it still during "stop."

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Provide a safe shaded floor area for tummy time, crawling, or cruising near a stable surface.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe head lifting, forearm support, pivoting, scooting, crawling pattern, persistence, and need for breaks.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Use short tummy-time periods throughout the day. Place a rolled towel beneath the chest if helpful and accepted.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Infants must be awake and directly supervised during tummy time. Keep floor space clear.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Go to the Crinkle
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Motivate head lifting, reaching, rolling, scooting, or crawling.
DESCRIPTION:
A large crinkle toy is placed within an achievable distance as the caregiver uses "go" and "stop" language.
MATERIALS:
Large infant-safe crinkle toy
Floor mat
SETUP:
Clear a large movement space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Match the distance to the infant's ability and reduce it before frustration rises.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Ready, go. Move toward the crinkle. Stop and rest. You moved forward."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show and sound the toy.
2. Place it within achievable range.
3. Say "go" and wait.
4. Celebrate any movement, including reaching or head lifting.
5. Bring the toy closer for success and rest.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Motor planning, strength, persistence, and receptive language.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record movement strategy, distance traveled, and response to encouragement.
VOCABULARY:
Go, Stop, Crawl, Reach, Forward
EXTENSIONS:
Move the toy between two safe points for mobile infants.
ADAPTATIONS:
Place beside the hands for younger infants; use a longer pathway for crawlers.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Stay within arm's reach and remove obstacles.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Head lift, reach, pivot, or roll. 6-12 months: Scoot, crawl, cruise, or retrieve.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Tummy-Time Talk
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Increase tummy-time engagement through faces and social interaction.
DESCRIPTION:
An unbreakable mirror and caregiver face provide motivation during tummy time.
MATERIALS:
Unbreakable mirror
Tummy-time pillow
Floor mat
SETUP:
Secure the mirror at floor level and position the infant comfortably.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay face-to-face, sing softly, and offer breaks.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Lift your head. I see you. You are strong. Rest when you need to."
DIRECTIONS:

2. Place the mirror ahead.

5. End or reposition at the first stress cue.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Head control, shoulder strength, social attention, and body confidence.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe head-lift duration, arm position, reaching, and emotional regulation.
VOCABULARY:
Tummy, Head, Up, Rest, Strong
EXTENSIONS:
Place a familiar photo beside the mirror.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use caregiver chest tummy time or a rolled towel support.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Only while awake and continuously supervised.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported tummy time. 6-12 months: Pivoting, reaching, kneeling, or crawling near mirror.

Up, Down, and Balance

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Experience safe changes in level.
Practice supported sitting or standing when developmentally ready.
Build balance reactions.
Respond to up-and-down language.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Up, Down, Sit, Stand, Balance

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Stable low support surface
Cushions

The Grand Old Duke of York

CIRCLE_TIME:
Move a scarf up and down while singing. Infants may watch, reach, sit with support, or imitate arm movements.

Music & Movement

A caregiver moves a scarf between low and high positions with repeated language and pauses.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Supported Balance Play
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen trunk control and balance in a developmentally appropriate position.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants practice supported sitting, kneeling, or standing while reaching for a large soft ball.
MATERIALS:
Large soft ball
Stable support surface
Floor mat
SETUP:
Clear and pad the area around the infant.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support at the trunk or hips and let the infant control the effort.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"You are balancing. Reach for the ball. Sit down and rest."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a position the infant already uses.
2. Support trunk or hips.
3. Present the ball within reach.
4. Allow one reach or weight shift.
5. Return to a secure rest position.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Postural control, balance, reaching, and body confidence.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice trunk stability, weight shift, hand support, and fatigue.

Balance, Sit, Stand, Reach, Rest
EXTENSIONS:
Move the ball slightly to each side for controlled weight shifting.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use reclined sitting, side sitting, kneeling, or visual participation.
SAFETY_NOTES:
An adult remains hands-on and within immediate reach.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Supported recline or sitting only when ready. 6-12 months: Sitting, kneeling, pulling up, and supported cruising.

FRIDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Dance, Clap, and Choose

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Review favorite weekly movements.
Imitate simple gestures.
Choose between two songs or movement props.
Transition from active movement to calm regulation.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Dance, Clap, Wave, Choose, Rest

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Scarves
Soft rattles
Movement picture cards
Mirror
Floor mat
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Music & Movement, Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Cognitive Development

DAILY_BOOK:
Clap Your Hands | Lorinda Bryan Cauley

DAILY_SONG:
If You're Happy and You Know It

CIRCLE_TIME:
Review clap, wave, kick, reach, up, down, go, and stop. Present two songs or props and honor the infant's gaze, reach, or body orientation as a choice.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Have a short shaded movement celebration followed by quiet stroller or blanket observation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe gesture imitation, preferred songs, choice making, rhythm response, social engagement, and ability to calm after active play.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Use very short songs and slower movements. Provide a quiet lap or floor-rest option at all times.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep volume low, avoid bouncing or spinning, and stop active movement at fatigue or stress cues.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Baby's Choice Dance
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Encourage choice making, imitation, and joyful movement.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants choose between two movement props or songs and participate at their own developmental level.
MATERIALS:
Large scarf
Soft rattle
Two movement picture cards
SETUP:
Place two choices clearly within view.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Interpret gaze, reach, or body orientation as communication and follow the infant's choice.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"You chose the scarf. Let's wave slowly. Now stop and rest."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Present two safe choices.
2. Wait for a gaze, reach, or movement.
3. Name the chosen prop or song.
4. Lead one short movement routine.
5. Finish with a clear stop and calm cuddle or floor rest.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Communication, autonomy, rhythm, imitation, and regulation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record choice signals, favorite movements, and response to stopping.
VOCABULARY:
Choose, Dance, Wave, Shake, Stop
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the preferred choice and add one new gesture.
ADAPTATIONS:
Hold choices near the visual field or offer one at a time.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Props remain supervised and music stays at a gentle volume.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Gaze choice and caregiver-led movement. 6-12 months: Reach choice, wave, shake, clap, and dance while sitting or standing with support.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Move Then Rest
CATEGORY:
Social Emotional
OBJECTIVE:
Practice transitioning from active movement to calm regulation.
DESCRIPTION:
A brief movement song is followed by slow breathing, quiet humming, and stillness with a trusted caregiver.
MATERIALS:
Floor mat
Soft scarf
SETUP:
Create a calm area with reduced noise and light.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Watch for arousal level and model a predictable active-to-calm routine.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"We moved our bodies. Now we stop. Slow and quiet. Your body can rest."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Sing one short active verse.
2. Say "stop" and make the scarf still.
3. Lower voice and movement.
4. Hum or breathe slowly while holding or sitting nearby.
5. End when the infant appears calm or ready for the next routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-regulation, transition awareness, secure attachment, and receptive language.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe how the infant responds to the stop cue and which calming supports work best.
VOCABULARY:
Move, Stop, Slow, Quiet, Rest
EXTENSIONS:
Use the same routine before naps or other transitions.
ADAPTATIONS:
Calm on the floor, in arms, or with a familiar comfort item according to individual needs.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Follow safe sleep rules and never use weighted items.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Adult-led co-regulation. 6-12 months: Add a simple stop gesture or sitting pause.
