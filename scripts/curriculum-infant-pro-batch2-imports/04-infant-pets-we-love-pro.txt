Pets and Caring Relationships

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore familiar pets through soft animal toys, photographs, sounds, movement, songs, books, and responsive caregiver interactions. Experiences focus on secure relationships, early communication, visual tracking, reaching, grasping, tummy-time movement, cause and effect, and gentle caregiving routines.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build recognition of familiar pet animals
Support early receptive and expressive language
Encourage reaching, grasping, tracking, rolling, crawling, and supported sitting
Explore soft, smooth, fuzzy, and textured materials
Develop cause-and-effect awareness through sounds and movement
Strengthen secure caregiver-infant interaction
Introduce gentle touch and caring routines
Encourage imitation of simple sounds, gestures, and facial expressions

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to share a photo of a household pet or a favorite animal. Encourage caregivers to name the pet, imitate one simple sound, and practice gentle touch during calm moments at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, responses to animal sounds, reaching and grasping, tummy-time tolerance, imitation attempts, shared attention, calming with familiar songs, and reactions to different textures.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer activities individually or in very small groups. Position materials within each infant's comfortable visual and physical range. Use high-contrast pet images for younger infants, larger lightweight objects for easier grasping, and extra floor space for mobile infants. Follow feeding, sleep, and sensory cues and stop whenever an infant shows stress.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Dogs: Friendly Faces and Gentle Touch

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Track a large dog puppet with the eyes.
Reach toward or grasp a soft pet toy.
Respond to repeated words and dog sounds.
Experience gentle social interaction with a caregiver.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Dog, Woof, Soft, Gentle, Hello

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Large dog puppet
Soft plush dog
Laminated dog photographs
Floor mat
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy, Social Emotional, Physical Development, Cognitive Development

DAILY_BOOK:
Where Is Baby's Puppy? | Karen Katz

DAILY_SONG:
How Much Is That Doggie in the Window

CIRCLE_TIME:
Seat or hold infants securely. Introduce the dog puppet slowly, say hello, and repeat "dog" and "woof." Pause for eye contact, smiles, movement, or vocal responses. Sing one short verse while gently moving the puppet from side to side.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take a slow stroller or blanket walk and notice safe neighborhood sounds. Name any distant dog sounds without approaching unfamiliar animals.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Notice tracking, smiling, quiet alertness, vocalizing, reaching, grasping, and responses to repeated sounds.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
For younger infants, hold the puppet 8-12 inches from the face. For mobile infants, place the plush dog slightly out of reach to encourage crawling or cruising with close supervision.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large washable materials with no loose eyes, fur, strings, or small parts. Never bring unfamiliar live animals into infant space.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Follow the Friendly Puppy
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen visual tracking and shared attention.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver slowly moves a large dog puppet through the infant's visual field while naming its movements and pausing for responses.
MATERIALS:
Large dog puppet
Floor mat
SETUP:
Position the infant comfortably on the back, tummy, or supported sitting.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Move slowly, follow the infant's gaze, and respond warmly to every cue.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Here is the dog. The dog says woof. The puppy is moving slowly. You found the puppy."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the puppet at the infant's midline.
2. Wait for the infant to focus.
3. Move it slowly left, right, up, and down.
4. Pause whenever the infant looks, reaches, or vocalizes.
5. End with a gentle hello and goodbye routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Visual tracking, attention, language exposure, and relationship building.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record how far and how smoothly the infant tracks and whether they anticipate repeated movement.
VOCABULARY:
Dog, Woof, Move, Hello, Bye-bye
EXTENSIONS:
Add a mirror so the infant can see the puppet beside their reflection.
ADAPTATIONS:
Reduce movement range for younger infants; encourage crawling toward the puppet for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep puppet movements slow and away from the infant's face.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short tracking periods with close positioning. 6-12 months: Add reaching, crawling, and peekaboo.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soft Puppy Pat
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Explore texture while practicing gentle touch.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants touch, pat, squeeze, and hold a large washable plush dog with caregiver support.
MATERIALS:
Large washable plush dog
SETUP:
Place the toy on a clean mat within easy reach.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model gentle touch and describe the texture.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Soft puppy. Pat gently. Your hand is touching the soft fur."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Offer the plush dog at the infant's midline.
2. Allow looking before touching.
3. Guide an open hand to pat if welcomed.
4. Let the infant hold, squeeze, or push the toy.
5. Name the infant's actions and end when interest decreases.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory processing, grasping, hand opening, and social-emotional learning.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice texture preferences, grasp strength, two-handed exploration, and withdrawal cues.
VOCABULARY:
Soft, Pat, Gentle, Dog
EXTENSIONS:
Compare the plush dog with one smooth laminated dog image.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer hand-under-hand support or place the toy under the chest during tummy time.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Inspect seams and wash materials between uses.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Brief supported touch. 6-12 months: Encourage passing, hugging, or placing the toy in a basket.

DAILY_THEME:
Cats: Quiet Sounds and Cozy Textures

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Listen and respond to soft cat sounds.
Explore fuzzy and smooth textures.
Practice grasping and releasing.
Build calm shared attention.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Cat, Meow, Fuzzy, Smooth, Cozy

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Cat puppet
Texture squares
Plush cat
Basket
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Sensory Exploration, Language & Literacy, Fine Motor, Social Emotional

DAILY_BOOK:
Touch and Feel: Pets | DK

DAILY_SONG:
Soft Kitty, Gentle Kitty

CIRCLE_TIME:
Show a large cat puppet, whisper "meow," and invite infants to watch or touch. Sing softly and pause between lines for movement or vocal responses.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Provide blanket play in a shaded area with soft scarves to watch and grasp in the breeze.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe calming responses, listening, grasping, releasing, texture preferences, and attempts to imitate sounds.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Limit textures to one at a time for infants who become overstimulated. Give mobile infants a basket for putting in and taking out the plush cat.

SAFETY_NOTES:
All texture pieces must be securely sewn, larger than a choking-test cylinder, and used with direct supervision.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Cat Texture Basket
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Compare soft, fuzzy, and smooth surfaces.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore a small basket of large pet-themed texture items while a caregiver labels each sensation.
MATERIALS:
Large texture squares
Plush cat
Smooth laminated cat picture
Shallow basket
SETUP:
Place two or three items in a shallow basket on a clean floor mat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Present one texture at a time and observe the infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Fuzzy cat. Smooth picture. You are rubbing the soft square."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the basket.
2. Offer one item and wait.
3. Allow mouthing only if the item is designed and sanitized for infant mouthing.
4. Compare a second texture.
5. Help the infant place an item back in the basket.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory discrimination, fine-motor control, and vocabulary exposure.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record preferred textures and methods of exploration.
VOCABULARY:
Cat, Fuzzy, Smooth, Soft, Basket
EXTENSIONS:
Add a simple hide-and-find game with the plush cat under a scarf.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use one texture for younger infants; provide a wider basket for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Check all materials before each use and sanitize after mouthing.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Caregiver presents one item. 6-12 months: Infant selects, transfers, and releases items.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Where Is the Kitty?
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Develop object permanence and anticipation.
DESCRIPTION:
A plush cat is partly hidden and revealed under a lightweight scarf.
MATERIALS:
Plush cat
Lightweight scarf
SETUP:
Seat or position the infant securely on the floor.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Repeat the hide-and-reveal routine and follow the infant's pace.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Where is the kitty? There it is. You found the cat. Meow."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the cat.
2. Cover part of it with the scarf.
3. Pause and watch the infant.
4. Reveal the cat or help the infant pull the scarf.
5. Repeat two or three times.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Object permanence, attention, reaching, and early problem-solving.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice anticipation, searching, scarf pulling, and emotional response.
VOCABULARY:
Where, Cat, Here, Meow
EXTENSIONS:
Hide the cat partly inside a wide basket for mobile infants.
ADAPTATIONS:
Keep the face visible for younger infants; increase hiding difficulty gradually.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Never leave scarves with infants unattended.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Partial cover only. 6-12 months: Encourage independent reveal and searching.

WEDNESDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Fish: Watching, Reaching, and Water-Like Movement

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Track slow swimming motions.
Reach for moving scarves or sealed sensory bags.
Explore cause and effect.
Hear words connected to water and fish.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Fish, Swim, Water, Blue, Splash

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Fish puppet
Blue scarves
Sealed water sensory bag
Fish photographs
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Cognitive Development, Physical Development, Sensory Exploration, Language & Literacy

DAILY_BOOK:
Dear Zoo | Rod Campbell

DAILY_SONG:
The Fish in the Water

CIRCLE_TIME:
Move a fish puppet slowly as if swimming. Repeat "fish" and "swim," then wave a blue scarf gently while singing.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Offer supervised water-free blue-scarf movement on a blanket or stroller observation of light reflecting outdoors.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe tracking, reaching, kicking, hand pressure on the sensory bag, and responses to slow versus faster movement.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Place the sensory bag under hands during tummy time or tape it securely to the floor for mobile infants.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Sensory bags must be double sealed, inspected before use, taped flat, and discarded immediately if damaged.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Swimming Fish Tracking
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Build tracking, midline crossing, and attention.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver moves a large fish puppet in smooth swimming patterns.
MATERIALS:
Large fish puppet
Blue scarf
SETUP:
Position infant safely with a clear view.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Adjust speed and distance to the infant's response.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"The fish swims. It goes up, down, and across. Your eyes found the fish."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Present the fish at midline.
2. Move it horizontally.
3. Add a gentle up-and-down path.
4. Pause for reaching or vocalizing.
5. Let the fish rest beside the infant at the end.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Visual tracking, coordination, and early spatial concepts.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note tracking range, head turning, reaching, and attention duration.
VOCABULARY:
Fish, Swim, Up, Down, Across
EXTENSIONS:
Add a second contrasting fish image for visual choice.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use shorter paths for younger infants; encourage crawling after the fish for mobile infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep scarves and puppets under adult control.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Visual tracking. 6-12 months: Reaching, crawling, and choosing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Ocean Sensory Bag
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Explore pressure, movement, and cause and effect.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants press a securely sealed sensory bag containing blue gel and large floating shapes.
MATERIALS:
Double-sealed sensory bag
Blue gel or colored water
Large sealed-in foam fish shapes
Strong tape
SETUP:
Tape the bag flat to the floor or tray and inspect all seals.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model pressing and narrate how the contents move.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Press. The fish moves. You made the blue water slide."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the bag and press once.
2. Place the infant's hands near the surface.
3. Allow patting, pushing, and watching.
4. Move the bag to tummy-time position if appropriate.
5. Remove the bag after the activity.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect, hand strength, sensory exploration, and visual attention.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe repeated actions, pressure, bilateral hand use, and visual focus.
VOCABULARY:
Press, Move, Fish, Blue, Water
EXTENSIONS:
Place a high-contrast fish card beneath a clear bag.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use hand-under-hand assistance or position vertically on a secured tray for supported sitters.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Direct supervision is required. Do not allow biting or lifting the bag.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Hands or feet press while lying down. 6-12 months: Sitting, kneeling, or crawling interaction.

THURSDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Birds: Sounds, Scarves, and Up-and-Down Movement

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Attend to soft bird sounds.
Practice reaching overhead and across the body.
Imitate simple movements or vocalizations.
Experience rhythm and airflow.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Bird, Tweet, Fly, Up, Down

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Bird puppet
Large scarves
Soft bell rattle
Bird pictures
Board book

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy, Gross Motor, Music & Movement, Cognitive Development

DAILY_BOOK:
Touch and Feel: Pets | DK

DAILY_SONG:
Two Little Blackbirds

CIRCLE_TIME:
Introduce the bird puppet and make one quiet "tweet." Move scarves up and down while singing slowly and pausing for infants to respond.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Look upward from a shaded blanket or stroller and notice birds, tree movement, and sky.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe head lifting, overhead reaching, sound localization, arm movement, and imitation attempts.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Keep sounds low and movements slow. Use side-to-side scarf movement for infants not yet reaching overhead.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Scarves remain in adult hands. Avoid loud recordings and direct sun exposure.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Birds Fly Up and Down
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Develop visual-motor coordination and movement anticipation.
DESCRIPTION:
Caregivers move bird puppets and scarves through simple repeated flight patterns.
MATERIALS:
Bird puppet
Large scarf
SETUP:
Clear the movement area and position infants comfortably.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Sing, pause, and mirror infant movements.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Bird flies up. Bird comes down. Your arms are moving too."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the bird at eye level.
2. Move it slowly upward.
3. Bring it down and pause.
4. Repeat with a scarf.
5. Invite reaching or arm waving without forcing movement.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Tracking, reaching, rhythm, and movement concepts.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice anticipation, arm extension, head movement, and excitement.
VOCABULARY:
Bird, Fly, Up, Down
EXTENSIONS:
For mobile infants, place the bird on a low safe surface to encourage pulling to stand.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use smaller movement ranges for younger infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Do not allow scarves to cover the infant's face.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Watch and reach. 6-12 months: Crawl, pull up, and imitate flight motions.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Find the Tweet
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Support sound localization and attention.
DESCRIPTION:
A caregiver makes a soft bird sound from different nearby positions.
MATERIALS:
Bird puppet
Soft bell rattle
SETUP:
Reduce background noise and position the infant securely.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Make one gentle sound, pause, and reward attention with warm interaction.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Tweet, tweet. Where is the bird? You turned toward the sound."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the bird and make the sound.
2. Move slightly to one side.
3. Make one soft sound.
4. Wait for eye or head movement.
5. Repeat on the other side and finish at midline.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Auditory attention, localization, and social engagement.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Record turning, stilling, eye widening, smiling, or vocal response.
VOCABULARY:
Bird, Tweet, Listen, Where
EXTENSIONS:
Pair the sound with a matching bird photograph.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual cues along with sound for infants with hearing differences.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep sound level gentle and avoid startling infants.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Near-midline sound. 6-12 months: Wider sound locations and crawling search.

FRIDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Caring for Pets: Gentle Hands and Helping Routines

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Experience simple pretend-care routines.
Practice placing, carrying, or patting large objects.
Build empathy through responsive adult modeling.
Review pet words and sounds.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Care, Gentle, Feed, Brush, Pet

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Large plush pets
Large toy bowl
Soft baby brush
Basket
Pet photographs

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Dramatic Play, Fine Motor, Language & Literacy

DAILY_BOOK:
Where Is Baby's Puppy? | Karen Katz

DAILY_SONG:
B-I-N-G-O

CIRCLE_TIME:
Review dog, cat, fish, and bird pictures. Model one gentle pat and sing a short familiar pet song.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take plush pets on a supervised stroller or blanket "pet walk" and name outdoor sights.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe gentle-touch attempts, imitation, placing objects in containers, shared attention, and preferred pet images.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Adults complete pretend-care actions beside younger infants. Mobile infants may carry a lightweight plush pet or place it into a basket.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large infant-safe pretend-care materials. Avoid real pet food, brushes with removable pieces, or small accessories.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Gentle Pet Care
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Introduce caring routines and gentle touch.
DESCRIPTION:
Caregivers model patting, brushing, comforting, and placing a plush pet in a basket bed.
MATERIALS:
Large plush pet
Soft baby brush
Basket
Small blanket
SETUP:
Arrange materials on a clean mat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model each action slowly and invite participation without expectation.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"We care for the pet. Gentle hands. Brush softly. The pet is resting."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the plush pet.
2. Model one gentle pat.
3. Offer the soft brush with support.
4. Help place the pet in the basket.
5. Cover part of the pet with a blanket and say goodnight.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Empathy foundations, imitation, fine-motor practice, and language.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice imitation, grasping, patting force, and emotional engagement.
VOCABULARY:
Care, Gentle, Brush, Rest, Pet
EXTENSIONS:
Add a second plush pet and allow mobile infants to choose which one to care for.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use hand-under-hand guidance or simply narrate adult modeling.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep blanket and brush under direct adult supervision.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Watch and touch. 6-12 months: Brush, place, carry, and choose.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Pet Basket Clean-Up
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Practice grasping, transferring, and releasing large objects.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants place large plush pets or laminated pet cards into a wide basket with caregiver encouragement.
MATERIALS:
Large plush pets
Large laminated pet cards
Wide shallow basket
SETUP:
Place materials around the basket within safe reach.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model one transfer and celebrate attempts.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Pick up the cat. In the basket. You let go. All done."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the empty basket.
2. Place one pet inside.
3. Offer another pet to the infant.
4. Support moving it toward the basket.
5. Repeat, then remove items together if interest continues.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Grasp-release coordination, object permanence, following simple routines, and cleanup participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe one- or two-handed grasp, release, persistence, and imitation.
VOCABULARY:
In, Out, Basket, Pet, All done
EXTENSIONS:
Sort two pet types into two large baskets for older mobile infants.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use a very wide container or place items directly in the infant's hand.
SAFETY_NOTES:
All objects must be oversized, smooth-edged, and washable.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Adult-supported hold and release. 6-12 months: Independent transfer and container play.
