TITLE:
Easter Eggs, Chicks & Spring Science

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Easter & Spring

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore Easter and spring through life cycle investigations, egg experiments, nature studies, literacy activities, engineering challenges, art projects, and outdoor exploration. Children will develop scientific thinking while learning about growth, change, and new beginnings.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore life cycles and spring changes
• Practice counting, measuring, and sorting
• Develop observation and scientific inquiry skills
• Strengthen literacy and vocabulary development
• Encourage creativity, problem-solving, and teamwork

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic eggs, real eggs, toy chicks, magnifying glasses, measuring tools, flower seeds, soil, paint, books, graph paper, sensory materials

VOCABULARY:
Egg, Chick, Hatch, Nest, Spring, Life Cycle, Grow, Seed, Flower, Observe

BOOKS:
The Easter Egg
From Egg to Chick
The Tiny Seed
We're Going on an Egg Hunt
Chicken Little

SONGS:
Five Little Easter Eggs
Spring Is Here
The Bunny Hop
Old MacDonald Had a Farm
The Chick Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to look for signs of spring, observe birds and flowers, and discuss how plants and animals grow.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe scientific thinking, counting skills, vocabulary development, social interactions, and participation during investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive tools, sensory alternatives, larger materials, and additional teacher assistance as needed.
