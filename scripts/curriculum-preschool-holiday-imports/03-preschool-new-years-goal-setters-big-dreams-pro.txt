TITLE:
New Year's Goal Setters & Big Dreams

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
New Year's

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will celebrate the New Year through goal setting, reflection, STEM challenges, literacy activities, counting games, art projects, and social-emotional learning. Children will explore the concepts of growth, perseverance, and new beginnings while building kindergarten readiness skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Reflect on past experiences and accomplishments
• Practice goal setting and growth mindset skills
• Strengthen early literacy and writing skills
• Develop counting, graphing, and measurement abilities
• Encourage problem-solving, teamwork, and self-confidence

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Chart paper, stickers, building blocks, balloons, streamers, mirrors, journals, markers, counting manipulatives, books, craft supplies

VOCABULARY:
Goal, Growth, Celebrate, Challenge, Dream, Achieve, Success, Practice, Learn, New Year

BOOKS:
The Night Before New Year's
Squirrel's New Year's Resolution
What Do You Do With an Idea?
The Magical Yet
The Most Magnificent Thing

SONGS:
Celebrate Together
The Countdown Song
If You're Happy and You Know It
This Little Light of Mine
I Can Do Hard Things

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss goals, dreams, and things children would like to learn during the coming year. Invite families to share a family goal.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe communication skills, self-confidence, problem-solving, counting abilities, writing development, and participation during group discussions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive writing tools, sensory alternatives, larger manipulatives, and additional teacher assistance as needed.
