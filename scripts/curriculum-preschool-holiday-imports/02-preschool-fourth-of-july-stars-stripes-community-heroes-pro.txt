TITLE:
Fourth of July Stars, Stripes & Community Heroes

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Fourth of July

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore the Fourth of July through community helpers, patriotic symbols, STEM investigations, literacy activities, art projects, and outdoor games. Children will learn about community, teamwork, celebrations, and national symbols while building kindergarten readiness skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Learn about patriotic symbols and celebrations
• Explore community helpers and citizenship
• Practice counting, graphing, and measurement
• Develop observation and scientific inquiry skills
• Strengthen literacy and communication skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Flags, star cutouts, red/white/blue manipulatives, measuring tools, books, paint, magnets, chart paper, sensory materials, community helper props

VOCABULARY:
Flag, Community, Citizen, Hero, Celebrate, Parade, America, Symbol, Freedom, Respect

BOOKS:
F Is for Flag
Red White and Boom!
America the Beautiful
My Fourth of July
What If Everybody Did That?

SONGS:
You're a Grand Old Flag
This Land Is Your Land
Yankee Doodle
America Song
Red White and Blue

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss community helpers, local celebrations, and ways families work together to help their communities.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe teamwork, communication skills, counting abilities, scientific thinking, social awareness, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive tools, larger manipulatives, sensory alternatives, and additional teacher support as needed.
