TITLE:

AGE_GROUP:

THEME:
Family Connections — Kindness and Gentle Care

published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants experience kindness, gentle care, and loving relationships through everyday interactions. Children build trust while exploring nurturing play, music, books, sensory experiences, and comforting routines.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build trusting relationships with caregivers.
Experience kindness through nurturing interactions.
Strengthen early communication skills.
Explore sensory materials using all their senses.
Develop fine and gross motor skills.
Participate in songs, stories, and movement.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to tell their child one thing they love about them each day this week. Encourage extra cuddles, songs, and bedtime stories.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe social smiles, eye contact, comfort with trusted adults, reaching and grasping, participation in music, vocalizations, curiosity during sensory play, and gross motor progress.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger infants: extra one-on-one cuddles, shorter activities, gentle sensory experiences, and supported tummy time. Older infants: encourage crawling and cruising, independent exploration, more pretend play with dolls, and increased movement opportunities.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Baby Doll Care with Blankets
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Practice gentle caregiving routines.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants care for baby dolls using blankets and bottles with caregiver modeling.
MATERIALS:
Baby dolls
Small blankets
Toy bottles
SETUP:
Prepare a clean floor mat and place materials within easy reach before infants arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate gently, and follow each infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. gentle. You are exploring. I see you trying. We are together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Offer a doll.
2. Model a gentle hug.
3. Cover with a blanket.
4. Offer a bottle prop.
5. Narrate kind words.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Nurturing play and social-emotional learning.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch for gentle touch and attention to care routines.
VOCABULARY:
Gentle, Care, Baby, Hug
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the experience later in the day during a calm one-on-one moment.
ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten the experience for younger infants and offer more movement space for older infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large, washable, infant-safe materials with no small parts. Supervise closely.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported experiences with close positioning. 6-12 months: Encourage reaching, crawling, cruising, and more independent exploration.
