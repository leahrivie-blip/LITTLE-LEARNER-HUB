TITLE:
My Home & My Family

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0-12 Months

THEME:
Family Connections — Home Routines and Belonging

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants explore the people, routines, and comforting experiences that make home feel safe and familiar. Through songs, stories, sensory play, and loving interactions, children strengthen their sense of belonging and security.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Strengthen secure attachments with trusted adults.
Recognize familiar household objects and routines.
Develop communication through songs, stories, and conversations.
Explore sensory materials using multiple senses.
Strengthen fine and gross motor development.
Build early social-emotional skills.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Family photos
Baby dolls
Soft blankets
Pillows
Stuffed animals
Mirrors
Toy kitchen items
Plastic bowls and spoons
Scarves
Sensory bottles
Soft blocks
Musical instruments
Washable infant-safe paint
Cardstock
Bubble machine
Board books
Nesting cups

VOCABULARY:
Home
Family
Love
Baby
Together

BOOKS:
Everywhere Babies | Susan Meyers
Baby Faces | Margaret Miller
Peek-a Who? | Nina Laden
Time for Bed | Mem Fox
Goodnight Moon | Margaret Wise Brown

SONGS:
Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star
Rock-a-Bye Baby
Skidamarink
The More We Get Together
Row, Row, Row Your Boat

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to share one favorite bedtime routine or family tradition. Display photos of children's families and revisit them throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe comfort with familiar adults, interest in family photos, social smiles, vocalizations, fine motor grasping, crawling or rolling progress, participation during music, and curiosity during sensory play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger infants: extra one-on-one interactions, shorter activities, more tummy time support, and gentle sensory exploration. Older infants: encourage crawling and cruising, independent toy exploration, simple pretend play, and increased movement opportunities.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Cozy Blanket Basket
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Explore comforting home-like materials.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore a cozy blanket and stuffed animal basket with caregiver support.
MATERIALS:
Soft blankets
Stuffed animals
Basket
SETUP:
Prepare a clean floor mat and place materials within easy reach before infants arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate gently, and follow each infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. home. You are exploring. I see you trying. We are together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite the infant to the cozy basket.
2. Offer a blanket to touch.
3. Add a stuffed animal.
4. Narrate comfort words.
5. End with a gentle cuddle or rest.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Security, sensory comfort, and relationship building.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch for calming responses and preference for soft materials.
VOCABULARY:
Home, Soft, Blanket, Cozy
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the experience later in the day during a calm one-on-one moment.
ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten the experience for younger infants and offer more movement space for older infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large, washable, infant-safe materials with no small parts. Supervise closely.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported experiences with close positioning. 6-12 months: Encourage reaching, crawling, cruising, and more independent exploration.
