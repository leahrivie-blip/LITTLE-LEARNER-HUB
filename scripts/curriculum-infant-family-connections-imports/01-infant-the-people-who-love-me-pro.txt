TITLE:
The People Who Love Me

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0-12 Months

THEME:
Family Connections — Familiar Faces and Loving Care

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants strengthen relationships with trusted caregivers while exploring familiar faces, voices, songs, and everyday interactions through play-based experiences. Experiences focus on secure attachment, early communication, sensory exploration, and gentle motor practice.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build secure relationships with trusted adults.
Recognize familiar faces and voices.
Develop early communication skills.
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills.
Explore new textures and sensory experiences.
Participate in music and movement.
Enjoy books and shared reading experiences.

Laminated classroom family photos
Soft scarves
Stuffed animals

Washable infant-safe paint
Cardstock
Baby wipes

Sensory balls
Ribbon rings
Soft blocks
Textured fabric squares
Musical instruments
Blanket
Water sensory bags
Large paper
Chunky crayons (older infants)

Family
Love
Smile

Together

BOOKS:
I Love You Through and Through | Bernadette Rossetti-Shustak
Mama Loves You So | Terry Pierce
Global Babies | Global Fund for Children

Where Is Baby's Belly Button? | Karen Katz

SONGS:
Skidamarink
You Are My Sunshine
The More We Get Together
If You're Happy and You Know It
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Please send 3–5 family photos to create a classroom family wall. Talk about your family's favorite bedtime song or tradition with your child this week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe and document eye contact, recognition of familiar faces, smiling during interactions, vocalizations, reaching and grasping, participation during songs, tummy time progress, and social interactions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger infants: shorter activities, more one-on-one interaction, extra tummy time support, and gentle sensory exploration. Older infants: encourage crawling, introduce simple cause-and-effect toys, support more independent exploration, and increase movement opportunities.

DAILY_THEME:

DAILY_SONG:
Skidamarink

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome each child by name. Sing Skidamarink while showing family photos and pausing for smiles, eye contact, or vocal responses.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take a stroller walk while talking about the trees, sky, and birds in a calm, narrated voice.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Notice eye contact, photo interest, reaching, and responses to the welcome song.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Hold photos closer for younger infants; place mirrors on the floor for mobile infants.

SAFETY_NOTES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror and Scarf Exploration
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Explore reflections and soft materials with caregiver support.
DESCRIPTION:
Place baby-safe mirrors on the floor with soft scarves for looking, reaching, and gentle play.
MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors
Soft scarves
Floor mat
SETUP:
Prepare a clean floor mat and place materials within easy reach before infants arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate gently, and follow each infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. family. You are exploring. I see you trying. We are together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Place a mirror within the infant's visual range.
2. Drape a soft scarf nearby.
3. Narrate what the infant sees and touches.
4. Pause for smiles or vocalizations.
5. End with a gentle goodbye wave.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Social-emotional connection, visual attention, reaching, and sensory exploration.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch for recognition of self/caregiver reflection and scarf grasping.
VOCABULARY:
Smile, Look, Soft, Family, Hello
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the experience later in the day during a calm one-on-one moment.
ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten the experience for younger infants and offer more movement space for older infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large, washable, infant-safe materials with no small parts. Supervise closely.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported experiences with close positioning. 6-12 months: Encourage reaching, crawling, cruising, and more independent exploration.

Celebrate relationships through favorite songs from the week.
DESCRIPTION:
Sing all favorite songs from the week while infants move, clap, or rest in caregiver arms.
MATERIALS:
Song list
Scarves
Instruments (optional)
SETUP:
Prepare a clean floor mat and place materials within easy reach before infants arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate gently, and follow each infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. celebrate. You are exploring. I see you trying. We are together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Begin with a welcome verse.
2. Sing 2–3 favorite songs.
3. Offer scarves for movement.
4. Pause for infant cues.
5. Close with a calm lullaby.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Belonging, music enjoyment, and social connection.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice which songs elicit the strongest responses.
VOCABULARY:
Sing, Happy, Together, Love
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the experience later in the day during a calm one-on-one moment.
ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten the experience for younger infants and offer more movement space for older infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large, washable, infant-safe materials with no small parts. Supervise closely.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported experiences with close positioning. 6-12 months: Encourage reaching, crawling, cruising, and more independent exploration.
