TITLE:
We Belong Together

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0-12 Months

THEME:
Family Connections — Classroom Community and Belonging

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants celebrate the special connections they have with their families, teachers, and classmates. Through shared experiences, music, stories, and play, children strengthen relationships while developing a sense of belonging in their classroom community.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build positive relationships with familiar adults and peers.
Participate in shared classroom experiences.
Develop early communication skills.
Strengthen fine and gross motor development.
Explore sensory materials.
Enjoy music, books, and movement together.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Share a class photo or Our Classroom Family keepsake with families. Encourage families to talk with their child about the friends and teachers they spend time with each day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe social interactions, eye contact, smiling and laughter, participation during songs, fine motor skills, gross motor development, curiosity during sensory play, and communication attempts.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger infants: more one-on-one support, shorter activities, gentle sensory experiences, and extra tummy time opportunities. Older infants: encourage independent movement, more peer interactions, introduce simple turn-taking, and increase exploration time.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Play with a Friend
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Share mirror play with a teacher or nearby friend.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore mirrors with a friend or teacher, noticing faces and smiles together.
MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors
Floor mat
SETUP:
Prepare a clean floor mat and place materials within easy reach before infants arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate gently, and follow each infant's cues.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. friend. You are exploring. I see you trying. We are together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit together at a mirror.
2. Wave hello.
3. Point to faces.
4. Pause for smiles.
5. Sing a short together song.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Belonging, social awareness, and communication.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch for peer glances, smiles, and joint attention.
VOCABULARY:
Friend, Look, Smile, Together
EXTENSIONS:
Repeat the experience later in the day during a calm one-on-one moment.
ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten the experience for younger infants and offer more movement space for older infants.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only large, washable, infant-safe materials with no small parts. Supervise closely.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
0-6 months: Short supported experiences with close positioning. 6-12 months: Encourage reaching, crawling, cruising, and more independent exploration.
