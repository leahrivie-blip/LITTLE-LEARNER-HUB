WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore different types of weather through hands-on activities, movement games, sensory experiences, songs, books, and outdoor observations. Children will discover sunshine, rain, clouds, wind, and changing weather patterns while building vocabulary, observation skills, curiosity, and creativity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore different weather types
- Build weather-related vocabulary
- Develop observation skills
- Encourage scientific thinking and curiosity
- Strengthen gross and fine motor skills
- Express ideas through art and play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about the weather each day. Look outside together and discuss what children see, hear, and feel.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe weather vocabulary use, curiosity, participation, observation skills, communication, and engagement during science activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real photos and visual supports. Allow children to observe before participating and provide sensory alternatives when needed.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rain Cloud Spray Bottles

Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore rain with water mist and clouds.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers mist blue paper clouds and watch water droplets make rain tracks.

MATERIALS:
Spray bottles with water
Blue paper
Cotton clouds
Smocks
Trays

SETUP:
Set paper on trays with lightly filled spray bottles.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep sprays aimed at paper only.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place cotton clouds on paper.
2. Show gentle spraying.
3. Watch droplets run like rain.
4. Talk about drip and splash.
5. Hang wet pictures to dry.

- Uses rain words

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Splash Puddle Dance

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Move like raindrops and jump puddles.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers tip-toe as rain, then jump on foam puddles when thunder drums.

MATERIALS:
Foam puddle shapes
Drum
Rain stick or shaker

SETUP:
Scatter foam puddles in an open space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model safe jumping feet.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Make soft rain sounds and tip-toe.
2. Drum thunder and freeze.
3. Jump on puddles carefully.
4. Sway like wind between jumps.
5. End with sunshine stretches.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor
- Listening
- Imaginative movement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Tip-toes to rain sound
- Jumps on puddle
- Freezes for thunder

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

Notice wind moving outdoor pinwheels.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers carry pinwheels outdoors and watch them spin in the breeze or from their breath.

MATERIALS:
Pinwheels
Outdoor space

SETUP:
Check outdoor conditions and hand out pinwheels.

TEACHER_ROLE:

DIRECTIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
