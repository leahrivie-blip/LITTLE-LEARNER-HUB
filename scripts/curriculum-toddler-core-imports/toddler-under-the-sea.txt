WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will dive into an exciting underwater adventure as they explore ocean animals, sea plants, shells, water movement, and marine habitats. Through sensory play, art, music, movement, dramatic play, and science exploration, children will develop curiosity, vocabulary, problem-solving skills, and a love for ocean life.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common ocean animals
- Build ocean-related vocabulary
- Explore water and marine environments
- Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
- Encourage creativity and imaginative play
- Develop observation and problem-solving skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Ocean animal toys
Blue scarves

Ocean picture cards

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to visit aquariums, read ocean books, or talk about ocean animals during play and story time. Ask children which sea creatures are their favorites.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe vocabulary development, curiosity, imaginative play, sensory exploration, communication skills, and participation during science activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger ocean animals for easier handling. Use visual supports and real photographs. Allow children to explore materials at their own pace.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Ocean Blue Discovery Bin

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore ocean textures and sea creatures.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dig through blue water beads or water with sea animals, shells (large), and scoops.

MATERIALS:
Shallow bin
Blue water or water beads
Sea animal toys
Scoops
Smocks

SETUP:
Fill bins shallowly and set towels underneath.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate ocean vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the ocean bin.
2. Scoop and pour water.
3. Find hiding sea creatures.
4. Name fish, crab, or whale.
5. Dry hands and put creatures to rest.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory exploration
- Fine motor pouring
- Ocean vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Scoops water
- Finds a creature
- Names or points to animal

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Shallow water; water beads only if not mouthing; wipe spills; supervise.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Splash Ocean Story Stretch

Literacy

Connect an ocean book to movement.

DESCRIPTION:

MATERIALS:
Ocean board book
Open space

- Imaginative movement
- Vocabulary

- Copies a swim move
- Freezes

Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.
