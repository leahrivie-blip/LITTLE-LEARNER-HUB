WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore the world around them using their five senses: sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste-safe exploration. Through hands-on sensory activities, movement games, songs, books, and discovery experiences, children will learn how their bodies help them understand and interact with their environment.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore the five senses through play
- Build vocabulary related to sensory experiences
- Develop observation and comparison skills
- Encourage curiosity and exploration
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor development
- Promote communication about sensory discoveries

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about what children see, hear, touch, smell, and taste throughout the day. Ask simple questions that help children describe their experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory exploration, vocabulary use, curiosity, problem-solving, fine motor development, and participation in group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide sensory materials that match children's comfort levels. Allow children to observe before participating and offer alternatives for children sensitive to certain textures or sounds.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Texture Exploration Station

Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore different textures using hands and fingers.

DESCRIPTION:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scent Garden Jars

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore gentle smells with curiosity.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers sniff sealed scent jars (citrus peel, herbs, vanilla) and choose favorites.

MATERIALS:
Clear jars with lids
Cotton balls
Safe scent drops or peels
Picture choice cards

SETUP:
Prepare jars ahead and seat children at a low table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Never force smelling; keep scents mild.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show how to smell gently.
2. Pass one jar at a time.
3. Invite pointing to like or not sure.
4. Compare two smells.
5. Wash hands and put jars away.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory awareness
- Choice-making
- Descriptive language

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Sniffs a jar
- Shows a preference
- Uses a smell word or gesture

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only mild, non-toxic scents; lids stay closed; watch for allergies.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
I Hear a Pickle Sound Walk

CATEGORY:
Literacy

OBJECTIVE:
Connect hearing vocabulary to story and environment sounds.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers listen to a short senses book, then hunt for classroom sounds.

MATERIALS:
Senses picture book
Sound clue cards

SETUP:
Create a quiet story spot, then a short indoor route.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep the walk calm and model listening.

2. Practice cupping ears to listen.
3. Walk slowly and pause for sounds.
4. Name each sound together.
5. Return to circle and share one sound.

- Auditory attention
- Vocabulary
- Curiosity

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sound Detective Freeze

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Listen carefully and move to sound cues.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers move when they hear a shaker and freeze when they hear a bell.

MATERIALS:
Shakers
Bell
Music space

SETUP:
Clear space and practice each sound once.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep cues distinct and celebrate attempts.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Demonstrate shaker means move.
2. Demonstrate bell means freeze.
3. Start the game with clear cues.
4. Switch roles so a child shakes.
5. End with quiet listening breaths.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening discrimination
- Body control
- Turn-taking

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Moves on shaker
- Freezes on bell
- Watches peers

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.
