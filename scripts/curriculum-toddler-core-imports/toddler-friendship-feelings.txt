WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore friendship, kindness, emotions, and positive social interactions through stories, songs, movement activities, dramatic play, art, and hands-on experiences. Throughout the week, children will learn how to recognize feelings, build relationships, practice kindness, and develop important social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Recognize basic emotions
- Develop friendship and social skills
- Build emotional vocabulary
- Practice kindness and cooperation
- Strengthen communication skills
- Encourage empathy and positive interactions

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about feelings at home and model positive ways to express emotions. Practice sharing, helping, and using feeling words during daily routines.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe emotional awareness, social interactions, communication skills, sharing, cooperation, empathy, and participation in group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual emotion supports and model feeling words frequently. Provide extra support for children learning to identify and express emotions.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feeling Faces Mirror Play

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Recognize and explore emotions through facial expressions.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers use mirrors to explore happy, sad, surprised, and excited expressions.

MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors
Emotion cards

SETUP:
Place mirrors and emotion cards in a small group area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model expressions and introduce feeling vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show an emotion card.
2. Name the feeling.
3. Demonstrate the expression.
4. Encourage children to copy.
5. Discuss when they feel that way.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Emotional awareness
- Vocabulary development
- Self-expression

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies emotions
- Imitates expressions
- Uses feeling words

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers run a cafe where friends take orders and serve stuffed animals together.

MATERIALS:
Play dishes
Menus with pictures
Stuffed animals
Aprons

SETUP:
Set a small cafe table with two chairs.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Coach turn-taking language gently.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Open the friendship cafe.
2. Take a picture order together.
3. Serve food to animal friends.
4. Practice please and thank you.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Friendship Bead Pass

CATEGORY:
Fine Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Work together passing large beads onto a shared rope.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers take turns adding large beads to a classroom friendship rope.

MATERIALS:
Large beads
Thick rope
Trays

SETUP:
Seat children so each can reach the rope.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Support grasp and celebrate teamwork.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the friendship rope.
2. Add one bead and pass the rope.
3. Name a friend while adding.
4. Continue until beads fill a section.
5. Hang the rope where all can see.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Turn-taking
- Fine motor
- Friendship vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Adds a bead
- Passes to a friend
- Says a peer name

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Beads too large to choke on; supervise constantly; secure rope ends.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Thermometer Check

CATEGORY:
Circle Time

OBJECTIVE:
Identify feelings and calm-down tools.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers point to a feelings thermometer and practice squeeze-hug calming.

MATERIALS:
Feelings thermometer chart
Calm-down bottles
Soft pillows

SETUP:
Place chart at eye level in circle.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Validate all feelings before calming tools.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show low, medium, and high feelings.
2. Invite pointing to today's feeling.
3. Practice three belly breaths.
4. Squeeze a pillow hug.
5. Shake a calm bottle and watch.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Emotional awareness
- Self-regulation
- Communication

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Points to feeling
- Tries breaths
- Uses a calm tool

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.
