WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore colors through hands-on play, movement, art, songs, books, sensory experiences, and everyday discoveries. Throughout the week, children will identify, sort, match, create, and talk about colors while developing language, problem-solving, fine motor skills, and creativity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify and explore a variety of colors
- Build color vocabulary through play and conversation
- Practice sorting and matching skills
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor development
- Encourage creativity through open-ended art experiences
- Promote social interaction and cooperative play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colors during daily routines. Ask children to find colors at home, in the grocery store, outdoors, and during playtime.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color recognition, matching skills, language use, participation in activities, fine motor development, and social interactions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for easier grasping. Offer hand-over-hand support when needed. Focus on exploration and exposure rather than color mastery.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Color Mixing Magic

CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Explore what happens when colors mix.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers finger-mix two washable paints and notice new colors.

MATERIALS:
Washable paint (primary colors)
Paper
Smocks

SETUP:
Set out two colors per tray with paper ready.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate color changes and invite short color words.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show two paint colors and name them.
2. Invite children to dab and swirl the colors together.
3. Ask what new color they notice.
4. Compare papers and celebrate discoveries.
5. Hang dry artwork at child height.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Color vocabulary
- Cause and effect
- Fine motor control

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Tries mixing colors
- Names or points to a color
- Stays engaged with the paint

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Nature Color Walk

CATEGORY:
Outdoor Play

OBJECTIVE:
Find colors outdoors.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers walk outdoors and point to colors in nature and on the playground.

MATERIALS:
Color cards
Collection bags (optional)
Clipboards

SETUP:
Prepare a short outdoor route and color cards.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay close, narrate finds, and keep the walk short.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Review 2–3 color cards before going out.
2. Walk slowly and pause often.
3. Invite children to point to matching colors.
4. Collect a few large safe nature items if appropriate.
5. Talk about favorites back inside.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Outdoor exploration
- Color recognition
- Language

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Points to colors outdoors
- Walks safely with the group
- Shows interest in nature finds

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise outdoors at all times; avoid small seeds/berries; check for hazards.
