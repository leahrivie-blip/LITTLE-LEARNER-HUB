WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become paleontologists as they dig, explore, discover, create, and investigate the fascinating world of dinosaurs. Through sensory experiences, dramatic play, movement activities, science investigations, art projects, and hands-on discovery, children will build curiosity, problem-solving skills, vocabulary, and confidence while exploring one of childhood's most beloved themes.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Introduce basic dinosaur concepts
- Encourage scientific exploration and discovery
- Develop vocabulary and communication skills
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor abilities
- Promote problem-solving and critical thinking
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to read dinosaur books, look for dinosaur toys at home, and discuss concepts such as big, small, footprints, and discovery during play.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving, curiosity, vocabulary use, dramatic play, fine motor skills, and participation in science investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger dinosaurs for easier handling. Offer sensory alternatives and visual supports. Allow children to participate at their own developmental level.

Sensory Play

Dig to discover hidden dinosaurs.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dig in a sand table with brushes to uncover toy dinosaurs and fossils.

MATERIALS:
Sand table or bin
Toy dinosaurs
Paintbrushes
Goggles (optional play)

SETUP:
Bury dinosaurs shallowly and set brushes nearby.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate discoveries with excitement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Put on paleontologist jobs.
2. Dig gently with hands or brushes.
3. Uncover a dinosaur.
4. Name big or little dinos.
5. Rebury for another dig if interested.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Curiosity
- Fine motor
- Dinosaur vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Digs purposefully
- Finds a dinosaur
- Uses brush

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Taste-safe sand if needed; no throwing sand; supervise eyes.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Big and Little Dino Sort

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Compare dinosaur sizes and sorts.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers sort toy dinosaurs into big and little trays and line them up for a parade.

MATERIALS:
Toy dinosaurs (varied sizes)
Big/little trays
Sorting mats

SETUP:
Set trays labeled with big and little pictures.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep sorting playful and open.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show a big and little dinosaur.
2. Invite sorting into trays.
3. Count how many in each.
4. Line up a mini parade.
5. Roar softly for the big ones.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Comparison
- Classification
- Language

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Sorts by size
- Chooses trays
- Roars or points

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.
