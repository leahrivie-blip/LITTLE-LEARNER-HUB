WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become little gardeners as they explore seeds, plants, flowers, vegetables, insects, and the life cycle of a garden. Through hands-on planting experiences, sensory exploration, art, movement, and observation activities, children will develop curiosity, responsibility, and a deeper understanding of the natural world.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore how plants grow
- Identify parts of a plant
- Build gardening vocabulary
- Encourage observation and scientific thinking
- Strengthen fine motor skills through planting activities
- Foster appreciation for nature and living things

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Seeds
Potting soil
Small pots
Watering cans

SONGS:

The Garden Song
Mr. Sun

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to plant seeds at home, visit a garden, or observe plants growing outdoors. Talk about what plants need to stay healthy.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe curiosity, vocabulary use, fine motor skills, scientific thinking, participation, and care for living things.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger gardening tools and containers when needed. Allow children to participate through observation if they are hesitant to touch soil or plants.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Seed Exploration Station

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce seeds and plant growth.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers examine and compare different seeds using magnifying glasses and observation skills.

MATERIALS:
Various seeds
Magnifying glasses
Observation trays

SETUP:
Place seeds on trays at child level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage observation and discussion.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce different seeds.
2. Examine seeds closely.
3. Compare sizes and shapes.
4. Discuss what seeds become.
5. Record observations verbally.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Observation skills
- Scientific thinking
- Vocabulary development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Examines seeds closely
- Uses descriptive language
- Shows curiosity

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Plant a Seed

CATEGORY:
Fine Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Explore the planting process.

DESCRIPTION:
Children plant seeds in small containers and care for them throughout the week.

MATERIALS:

SAFETY_NOTES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Seed Sorting Discovery

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Explore and sort large garden seeds.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers sort large seeds (beans, pumpkin) into cups and feel differences.

MATERIALS:
Large dried beans
Pumpkin seeds
Sorting cups
Trays
Magnifiers

SETUP:
Offer seeds too large for choking risk and sorting cups.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Count aloud and keep seeds contained.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Feel two kinds of seeds.
2. Look with a magnifier.
3. Sort into cups.
4. Talk about hard and smooth.
5. Plant a few in a demo cup with help.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Scientific exploration
- Sorting
- Fine motor

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Sorts seeds
- Uses magnifier
- Describes a seed

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Seeds must be too large to choke on; supervise constantly; no mouthing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Tiny Seed Story Garden

CATEGORY:
Literacy

OBJECTIVE:

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers hear a short seed story and act out planting motions on the carpet.

MATERIALS:
Garden picture book
Seed packets (sealed)
Carpet space

Story circle with room to act.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep packets sealed.

DIRECTIONS:

2. Read a few pages.
3. Curl up like seeds.
4. Sprout arms up like plants.
5. Pass sealed seed packets to look at.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening
- Comprehension
- Movement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Attends to story
- Acts out sprouting
- Looks at packet

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.
