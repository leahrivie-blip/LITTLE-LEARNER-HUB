WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore music, rhythm, movement, and self-expression through songs, dancing, instruments, action games, and creative movement experiences. Throughout the week, children will strengthen coordination, balance, listening skills, social interaction, and confidence while having fun moving their bodies to music.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop rhythm and listening skills
- Strengthen gross motor coordination
- Encourage self-expression through movement
- Build confidence during group participation
- Support language development through songs
- Promote social interaction and cooperation

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to dance, sing, and move together at home. Playing music during routines helps children build confidence, language, and coordination skills.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, balance, coordination, listening skills, rhythm awareness, social interaction, and confidence during movement activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow children to participate at their own comfort level. Provide seated movement options when needed and simplify directions for younger toddlers.

Music & Movement

Introduce rhythm and movement through music.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rhythm Stick Echo Band

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Copy simple rhythms with sticks.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers tap rhythm sticks to echo short teacher patterns.

MATERIALS:
Rhythm sticks
Open floor

SETUP:
Seat children with space between elbows.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep patterns short and praise participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show gentle tapping.
2. Tap a two-beat pattern for children to echo.
3. Invite a child to lead a pattern.
4. Try fast and slow taps.
5. Rest sticks quietly on the floor.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening
- Rhythm
- Imitation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Taps sticks
- Attempts to echo
- Waits for a turn

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use soft sticks; model safe tapping away from faces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Concert Stage Pretend

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Perform for friends in a pretend concert.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers take turns on a tape stage with scarves and shakers while peers clap.

MATERIALS:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Shaker Bottle Fillers

CATEGORY:
Fine Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Build hand strength filling shaker bottles.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers drop large pom-poms into wide bottles and seal them to make shakers.

MATERIALS:
Wide plastic bottles
Large pom-poms
Lids (teacher seals)
Trays

SETUP:
Prep bottles and pom-poms at a table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Seal all lids yourself; never leave unsealed.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show dropping one pom-pom in.
2. Invite filling bottles.
3. Count aloud as they drop.
4. Teacher seals lids tightly.
5. Shake and dance briefly.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Fine motor
- Cause and effect
- Counting exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Drops pom-poms in
- Persists filling
- Shakes sealed bottle

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Pom-poms must be too large to choke on; teacher seals lids; supervise shaking.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Paint to the Beat

CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Make marks that match music tempo.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers paint dots and swirls while music switches between slow and fast.

MATERIALS:
Washable paint
Paper
Music
Smocks

SETUP:
Set painting trays and cue two music tracks.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate tempo changes without correcting art.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Start slow music and model slow dots.
2. Invite painting to the slow beat.
3. Switch to fast music for quick marks.
4. Return to slow for a calm finish.
5. Hang paintings to dry.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Creative expression
- Listening
- Fine motor

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Makes marks to music
- Notices tempo change
- Engages through both tempos

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Washable paint only; smocks on; keep paint out of mouths.
