WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will enter the magical world of fairy tales through storytelling, dramatic play, sensory experiences, building activities, art, and movement games. Children will explore castles, dragons, princes, princesses, magical forests, and beloved fairy tale characters while developing imagination, language skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Build early literacy and storytelling skills
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
- Strengthen communication and vocabulary
- Develop creativity through art and exploration
- Promote problem-solving and critical thinking
- Foster social interaction and cooperation

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Fairy tale books
Dress-up clothes
Crowns
Toy dragons
Building blocks

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to read fairy tales together and retell stories using toys, puppets, and dramatic play. Ask children about their favorite fairy tale characters.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe storytelling skills, dramatic play, creativity, communication, problem-solving, social interactions, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual story supports and simplified retellings. Allow children to participate in dramatic play at their comfort level and provide alternative props as needed.
