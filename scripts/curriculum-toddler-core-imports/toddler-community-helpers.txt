WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will learn about the people who help us every day in our homes, schools, and communities. Through dramatic play, books, songs, movement activities, and hands-on exploration, children will discover how helpers such as firefighters, doctors, teachers, farmers, mail carriers, and police officers make our communities safe and strong.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify community helpers and their roles
- Build vocabulary related to community jobs
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
- Strengthen communication skills
- Develop social awareness and appreciation for helpers
- Promote cooperation and problem-solving

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about helpers they see in the community. Discuss jobs family members do and how they help others each day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe dramatic play, vocabulary use, social interactions, communication skills, problem-solving, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and real-life photos. Offer simple job props and encourage participation at each child's developmental level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Community Helper Discovery Basket

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce community helper tools and roles.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore a basket filled with community helper props and discuss who uses them.

MATERIALS:
Toy stethoscope
Toy hammer
Mailbag
Toy firefighter hat

SETUP:
Place items inside a discovery basket.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Introduce each tool and discuss how helpers use them.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Gather children around the basket.
2. Remove one item at a time.
3. Name the helper who uses it.
4. Allow children to explore.
5. Discuss how helpers assist others.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Vocabulary development
- Community awareness
- Exploration skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies helper tools
- Uses new vocabulary
- Participates in discussion

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Firefighter Suit-Up Station

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Pretend to be firefighters who help others.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers put on firefighter hats, drive a block fire truck, and rescue stuffed animals.

MATERIALS:
Firefighter hats
Hose prop (soft)
Stuffed animals
Block truck

SETUP:
Set a clear fire station corner.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Emphasize helping, not scary emergencies.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce firefighter helpers.
2. Invite hat try-on.
3. Drive to the rescue spot.
4. Gently rescue animals.
5. Return to the station and hang hats.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Pretend play
- Empathy
- Helper vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Wears a hat
- Uses helping actions
- Shows gentle rescue

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Police Officer Stop and Go

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Practice stop and go with helper cues.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers walk or drive scooters when the green helper sign shows and freeze on red.

MATERIALS:
Red/green signs
Ride-on toys or walking space

SETUP:
Clear a path and practice signals once.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep speeds slow and paths clear.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show green means go.
2. Show red means stop.
3. Start moving on green.
4. Freeze on red.
5. Cheer for safe helpers.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-control
- Following directions
- Gross motor

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Goes on green
- Stops on red
- Watches the sign

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise ride-ons; helmets if required; clear collisions.
