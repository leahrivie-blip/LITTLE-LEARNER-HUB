WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore who they are through activities focused on their names, families, bodies, feelings, preferences, and unique characteristics. Through books, songs, art, mirrors, movement, and discussions, children will build self-awareness, confidence, communication skills, and positive relationships with others.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop self-awareness and confidence
- Learn and recognize their names
- Explore body parts and facial features
- Build emotional awareness and vocabulary
- Strengthen communication skills
- Celebrate similarities and differences among peers

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to share photos and talk about family members. Encourage children to practice saying their names and identifying body parts at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe self-recognition, emotional awareness, communication skills, social interactions, confidence, and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, family photos, and individualized assistance as needed. Encourage participation at each child's comfort level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Me

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Develop self-awareness through observation.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore their reflections and identify facial features using mirrors.

MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors at toddler eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and encourage observation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to look into mirrors.
2. Point out eyes, nose, mouth, and ears.
3. Encourage children to identify features.
4. Make silly faces together.
5. Discuss what they see.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Language development
- Observation skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies facial features
- Engages with reflection
- Uses vocabulary

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Name Song Mirror Circle

CATEGORY:
Circle Time

OBJECTIVE:
Hear and respond to own name with joy.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers sit with mirrors and sing a name song as each friend is celebrated.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors
Name cards with photos
Soft seating

SETUP:
Arrange mirrors in a circle with name cards ready.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Use clear photos and keep turns brief.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Greet the group and show today's name cards.
2. Sing a hello song using each name.
3. Invite children to look in the mirror when named.
4. Clap for each friend.
5. End with a group name cheer.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Name recognition
- Belonging
- Self-awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks when named
- Looks in mirror
- Smiles or claps

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Me Portrait Dough Faces

CATEGORY:
Fine Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Explore facial features with playdough.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers press eyes, noses, and smiles onto dough faces using large safe pieces.

MATERIALS:
Playdough
Large safe facial feature pieces
Mirrors
Trays

SETUP:
Set dough balls and feature pieces at a low table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Focus on exploration, not realistic portraits.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Look in mirrors and name face parts.
2. Offer dough and model making a face.
3. Invite adding eyes, nose, and smile.
4. Compare faces with a friend.
5. Squish and start again if desired.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body part vocabulary
- Fine motor
- Creativity

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Names a face part
- Presses pieces into dough
- Looks in mirror

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep pieces large; wash hands after dough.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Body Parts Action Path

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Move while pointing to body parts.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers hop, stretch, and wiggle along a path as body parts are called.

MATERIALS:
Tape path
Body part cards
Music optional

SETUP:
Mark a short path and practice one motion.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model movements and allow observation first.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show a body part card.
2. Point to that body part together.
3. Move along the path with a matching action.
4. Repeat with new cards.
5. Freeze and give bodies a hug.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Gross motor
- Following directions

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Points to a body part
- Attempts the movement
- Follows the path

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Me Shadow Chase

CATEGORY:
Outdoor Play

OBJECTIVE:
Notice body shapes outdoors with shadows.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers find their shadows, wave, and jump to make shadows move.

MATERIALS:
Sunny outdoor space
Chalk optional

SETUP:
Choose a sunny safe paved area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate discoveries and keep play short.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Point out shadows on the ground.
2. Invite waving and watching the shadow.
3. Jump and freeze to change the shadow.
4. Trace around shoes with chalk if desired.
5. Say goodbye to shadows before going in.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Outdoor exploration
- Cause and effect

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Finds own shadow
- Moves to change it
- Shows interest

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Check pavement heat/safety; supervise outdoors; sunglasses optional for bright sun.
