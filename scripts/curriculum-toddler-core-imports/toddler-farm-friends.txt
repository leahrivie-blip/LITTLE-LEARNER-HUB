WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore life on the farm through hands-on activities, animal sounds, movement games, books, songs, sensory play, and dramatic play experiences. Children will learn about farm animals, their sounds, where they live, and how they help people while building language, social, cognitive, and motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common farm animals
- Explore animal sounds and movements
- Develop language through farm-themed vocabulary
- Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
- Build social skills through cooperative activities

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about animals during story time, visits to parks, farms, or petting zoos. Practice making animal sounds together and identifying animals in books.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal recognition, participation in songs, language development, imaginative play, social interactions, and gross motor skills.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger animal figures for easier handling. Offer visual supports and picture cards. Allow children to participate at their own comfort level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Barnyard Animal Freeze Dance

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Move like farm animals and freeze on cue.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dance as cows, pigs, and chickens, then freeze when the music stops.

MATERIALS:
Music player
Farm animal picture cards
Open floor space

SETUP:
Clear a movement area and keep animal cards nearby.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model animal movements and keep cues playful.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show an animal card and practice its movement.
2. Start music and invite animal dancing.
3. Pause music and call freeze.
4. Switch to a new animal card.
5. End with a soft animal stretch.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Gross motor imitation
- Animal vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Imitates an animal move
- Freezes with support
- Names or sounds an animal

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support. Allow parallel play and observation first.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, clear walkways, and check outdoor surfaces before play.
