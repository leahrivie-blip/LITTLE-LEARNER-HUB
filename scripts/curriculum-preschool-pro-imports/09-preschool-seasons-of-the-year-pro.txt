WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will explore the four seasons through hands-on investigations, sensory experiences, art, dramatic play, movement activities, and nature exploration. Throughout the week, children will discover how weather, plants, animals, clothing, and activities change throughout the year while building observation skills and scientific thinking.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify the four seasons
Observe seasonal changes in nature
Compare weather patterns
Develop vocabulary related to seasons and weather
Strengthen sorting and classification skills
Explore seasonal clothing and activities
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster curiosity about the natural world

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss seasonal changes they notice outside. Take walks together and observe weather, plants, animals, and clothing choices that relate to the current season.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's understanding of seasonal changes, ability to sort and classify items, use of seasonal vocabulary, participation in discussions, and curiosity during investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and seasonal picture cards. Offer adaptive tools when needed. Allow children to participate through movement, discussion, art, or hands-on exploration.
