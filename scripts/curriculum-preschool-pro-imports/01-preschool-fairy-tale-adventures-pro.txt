WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will enter a magical world of fairy tales as they explore castles, dragons, princes, princesses, giants, and enchanted forests through hands-on play. Throughout the week, children will retell stories, build castles, create fairy tale art, solve problems, engage in dramatic play, and strengthen early literacy and math skills while bringing beloved fairy tales to life.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop storytelling and story retelling skills
Strengthen vocabulary and communication
Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
Explore building and engineering concepts
Practice counting, sorting, and measuring
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build social skills through cooperative play
Foster creativity and problem-solving

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite your child to retell a favorite fairy tale at home. Ask questions about the characters, setting, and events in the story. Encourage your child to create a new ending to a familiar fairy tale and share it with family members.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to retell stories, engage in dramatic play, cooperate with peers, solve building challenges, use new vocabulary, and demonstrate creativity during open-ended activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual story cards for children needing additional support. Offer adaptive art tools as needed. Simplify directions into smaller steps and provide one-on-one guidance when appropriate.
