WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will blast off into outer space as they explore planets, stars, astronauts, rockets, and the solar system through hands-on investigations, dramatic play, movement activities, art experiences, and STEM challenges. Throughout the week, children will develop curiosity about space while strengthening critical thinking, creativity, and early academic skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn about planets, stars, and astronauts
Develop observation and investigation skills
Build vocabulary related to space exploration
Strengthen counting and measurement skills
Encourage imaginative play and storytelling
Explore simple STEM concepts
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster curiosity about science and discovery

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to observe the moon and stars with their families. Discuss what they notice in the night sky and share favorite space discoveries from the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's curiosity during investigations, use of new vocabulary, participation in dramatic play, counting skills, problem-solving during STEM challenges, and cooperative interactions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and picture cards. Offer larger materials when needed. Allow children to participate through drawing, acting, building, or verbal responses.
