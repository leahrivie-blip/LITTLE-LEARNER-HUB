WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will strengthen the foundational skills needed for kindergarten success through engaging, play-based learning experiences. Throughout the week, children will practice independence, communication, early literacy, math concepts, problem-solving, classroom routines, social skills, and confidence-building activities designed to support a successful transition into kindergarten.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Strengthen letter and name recognition
Develop early math and counting skills
Practice following multi-step directions
Build independence and self-help skills
Strengthen social and emotional readiness
Encourage problem-solving and critical thinking
Develop fine and gross motor coordination
Build confidence in classroom participation

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to practice independence skills at home such as cleaning up toys, putting on shoes, washing hands, and following simple routines. Talk positively about kindergarten and new learning opportunities.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's independence, ability to follow directions, letter recognition, counting skills, social interactions, problem-solving abilities, and confidence during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual schedules and picture supports. Offer adaptive writing tools when needed. Allow children multiple ways to demonstrate learning through movement, discussion, art, and hands-on exploration.
