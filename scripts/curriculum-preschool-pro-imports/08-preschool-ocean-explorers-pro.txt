TITLE:

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy, Social Emotional, Physical Development, Creative Arts, Science, Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will dive into an exciting underwater adventure as they explore ocean animals, habitats, waves, coral reefs, and ocean conservation. Through hands-on investigations, sensory experiences, dramatic play, art projects, and STEM challenges, children will discover the wonders of the ocean while developing curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common ocean animals and habitats
Develop scientific observation skills
Explore floating and sinking concepts
Strengthen vocabulary related to ocean life
Practice sorting, counting, and comparing
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Encourage imaginative play and storytelling
Foster appreciation for ocean conservation

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about ocean animals and water habitats. Visit a library to explore ocean-themed books and discuss ways to help keep oceans clean.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's curiosity during investigations, use of ocean vocabulary, ability to classify animals, participation in cooperative play, and understanding of ocean habitats.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and picture cards. Offer adaptive tools when needed. Allow children multiple ways to participate through art, discussion, movement, and sensory exploration.
