WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will explore the exciting world of transportation through hands-on investigations, dramatic play, building challenges, art experiences, movement activities, and early math concepts. Throughout the week, children will discover how people and goods travel by land, air, and water while developing problem-solving, creativity, and communication skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify different forms of transportation
Compare transportation by land, air, and water
Develop vocabulary related to vehicles and travel
Strengthen counting, sorting, and measuring skills
Encourage imaginative dramatic play
Explore movement and motion concepts
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster cooperation and teamwork

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about the different types of transportation they use each day. Point out vehicles during errands and discuss how they help people travel.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's transportation vocabulary, problem-solving skills during building challenges, counting abilities, social interactions, dramatic play participation, and creativity.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for children needing additional support. Offer visual transportation cards and allow multiple ways for children to participate and demonstrate understanding.
