WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become young scientists, engineers, mathematicians, and inventors as they explore hands-on STEM activities. Throughout the week, children will investigate, build, test, predict, measure, sort, and solve problems while developing critical thinking skills through engaging experiments and challenges.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop scientific observation skills
Practice predicting and testing outcomes
Strengthen counting and measuring skills
Encourage problem-solving and critical thinking
Build engineering and design skills
Develop communication and teamwork
Explore cause and effect relationships
Foster curiosity and creativity

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage your child to ask questions and investigate the world around them. Invite them to make predictions during everyday activities and discuss what happened afterward.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving skills, persistence, cooperation, measuring abilities, scientific thinking, communication, and curiosity during investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and step-by-step picture directions. Offer larger materials when needed and allow extra time for exploration and problem-solving.
