LEARNING_DOMAINS:

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will travel back to prehistoric times as they investigate dinosaurs through hands-on exploration, dramatic play, sensory experiences, STEM challenges, art, movement, and early literacy activities. Throughout the week, children will become paleontologists, discover fossils, compare dinosaur sizes, explore volcanoes, and strengthen kindergarten readiness skills through engaging dinosaur-themed learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop curiosity about prehistoric animals
Strengthen observation and investigation skills
Build vocabulary related to dinosaurs and fossils
Practice counting, sorting, and measuring
Encourage imaginative dramatic play
Strengthen fine and gross motor development
Promote problem-solving and critical thinking
Foster cooperation through group activities

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite your child to share their favorite dinosaur at home. Visit the library to explore dinosaur books together. Encourage children to compare the size of dinosaurs to familiar objects around the house.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's use of dinosaur vocabulary, participation in dramatic play, counting abilities, problem-solving during STEM activities, curiosity during investigations, and social interactions with peers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for children needing additional fine motor support. Offer visual aids and picture cards. Simplify instructions when needed and allow children multiple ways to participate.
