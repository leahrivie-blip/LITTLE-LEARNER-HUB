WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will learn how healthy choices help their bodies grow strong. Throughout the week, children will explore nutrition, exercise, hygiene, sleep, dental health, and self-care through hands-on activities, movement games, dramatic play, experiments, and creative experiences. Children will build lifelong healthy habits while strengthening social, physical, and cognitive skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify healthy foods and healthy habits
Learn the importance of exercise and movement
Understand proper handwashing techniques
Explore dental health and hygiene routines
Develop self-care skills and independence
Practice healthy decision-making
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build vocabulary related to health and wellness

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to involve children in healthy daily routines. Talk about healthy foods, handwashing, exercise, and bedtime habits at home. Invite children to share healthy choices they make with their families.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's understanding of healthy choices, participation in self-care routines, ability to identify healthy foods, gross motor development during exercise activities, and communication during discussions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports for routines. Offer adaptive tools when needed. Break activities into smaller steps and allow children multiple ways to participate.
