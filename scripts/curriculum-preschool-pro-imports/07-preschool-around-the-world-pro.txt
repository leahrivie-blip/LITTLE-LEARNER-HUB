WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will travel around the world through imaginative play, cultural exploration, music, art, movement, and hands-on learning experiences. Throughout the week, children will discover different countries, traditions, foods, landmarks, languages, and celebrations while developing an appreciation for diversity and global communities.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore different cultures and traditions
Develop awareness of similarities and differences
Learn about world landmarks and geography
Build vocabulary related to travel and culture
Strengthen social-emotional skills through cultural appreciation
Practice counting, sorting, and comparing
Develop creativity through multicultural art experiences
Foster curiosity about the world

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to share traditions, foods, languages, or celebrations that are important to their family. Discuss places family members have visited or would like to visit.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's curiosity about different cultures, participation in discussions, use of new vocabulary, social interactions, and understanding of similarities and differences among people.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, photographs, and simplified maps. Allow children to participate through art, movement, discussion, or dramatic play based on their strengths and interests.
