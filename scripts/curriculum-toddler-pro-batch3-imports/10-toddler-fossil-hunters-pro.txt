TITLE: Fossil Hunters
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Fossil Hunters
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers become fossil hunters as they dig, brush, match, press, sort, build, and move through playful investigations about bones, rocks, dinosaurs, and clues from long ago.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Use simple fossil vocabulary; compare rough/smooth and big/small; practice digging and brushing; count to five; match prints and shapes; build persistence and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Kinetic sand or safe digging medium, large toy bones, dinosaur figures, brushes, scoops, rocks, playdough, shells, blocks, magnifiers, paper, paint, books.
VOCABULARY: Fossil, bone, rock, dig, brush, print, dinosaur, old, rough, smooth.
BOOKS: Digging Up Dinosaurs; Bones, Bones, Dinosaur Bones; Dinosaur Bones; How Do Dinosaurs Say Good Night?
SONGS: We Are the Dinosaurs; Dinosaur Stomp; Five Little Dinosaurs.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to look for interesting rocks or leaf prints and talk about clues found in nature.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe tool use, matching, counting, persistence, comparison language, sensory preferences, and peer interaction.
ADAPTATIONS: Use sealed digging bags, larger tools, picture cards, seated activities, and pre-exposed objects.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME: What Is a Fossil?
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Explore fossils as clues from long ago.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Fossil, old, clue, rock.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Fossil photos, rocks, shells, magnifiers.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language; Sensory.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Compare fossil pictures with ordinary rocks and discuss how fossils are clues.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Search for rocks with different colors and textures.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note curiosity and descriptive language.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Offer large photo cards and touch-free observation.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use rocks too large to swallow and inspect for sharp edges.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Fossil Look-and-Touch Table
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Compare rocks, shells, and fossil replicas.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children examine safe objects with magnifiers.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Fossil replicas, smooth rocks, shells, magnifiers, trays.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Arrange objects in individual trays.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose an object. 2. Look closely. 3. Touch gently. 4. Compare with another. 5. Describe rough, smooth, big, or small.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model careful observation and comparison words.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Scientific observation; vocabulary; sensory discrimination.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note attention and comparison attempts.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use photos or sealed display bags.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise all objects.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Big Fossil, Small Fossil
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Sort by size.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children sort large fossil pictures into big and small groups.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Large laminated fossil cards, two mats.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Label mats big and small.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a card. 2. Compare size. 3. Place on a mat. 4. Repeat. 5. Count each group.

ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note sorting accuracy.
