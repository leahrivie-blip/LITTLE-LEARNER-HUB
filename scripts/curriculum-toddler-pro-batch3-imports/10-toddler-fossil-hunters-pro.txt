TITLE:
Fossil Hunters
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Fossil Hunters
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers become fossil hunters as they dig, brush, match, press, sort, build, and move through playful investigations about bones, rocks, dinosaurs, and clues from long ago.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Use simple fossil vocabulary; compare rough/smooth and big/small; practice digging and brushing; count to five; match prints and shapes; build persistence and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Kinetic sand or safe digging medium, large toy bones, dinosaur figures, brushes, scoops, rocks, playdough, shells, blocks, magnifiers, paper, paint, books.
VOCABULARY:
Fossil, bone, rock, dig, brush, print, dinosaur, old, rough, smooth.
BOOKS:
Digging Up Dinosaurs; Bones, Bones, Dinosaur Bones; Dinosaur Bones; How Do Dinosaurs Say Good Night?
SONGS:
We Are the Dinosaurs; Dinosaur Stomp; Five Little Dinosaurs.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to look for interesting rocks or leaf prints and talk about clues found in nature.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe tool use, matching, counting, persistence, comparison language, sensory preferences, and peer interaction.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use sealed digging bags, larger tools, picture cards, seated activities, and pre-exposed objects.
