TITLE: Space Explorers STEM
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Space Explorers STEM
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Engineering; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers explore rockets, the moon, stars, planets, and astronauts through building, sensory play, movement, counting, art, and dramatic play.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Name basic space objects; count to five; compare big and small; build simple structures; strengthen balance, grasp, communication, and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Cardboard tubes, blocks, foil, stars, balls, black paper, paint, scarves, helmets, boxes, glow stars, books.
VOCABULARY: Space, rocket, moon, star, planet, astronaut, launch, orbit, big, small.
BOOKS: Roaring Rockets; Goodnight Moon; Mousetronaut; There's No Place Like Space.
SONGS: Twinkle Twinkle Little Star; Zoom Zoom Zoom We're Going to the Moon; Five Little Astronauts.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to look for the moon or stars together and share what they noticed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe vocabulary, counting, spatial reasoning, movement imitation, creativity, and persistence.
ADAPTATIONS: Use visual cards, larger pieces, seated movement, pre-built models, and simplified counting.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME: Rockets and Launching
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Identify rockets and explore up/down movement.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Rocket, launch, up, down.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Tubes, blocks, scarves, boxes.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Engineering; Language; Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Read Roaring Rockets and practice a countdown.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Run a rocket-launch path with countdowns.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note sequencing and movement imitation.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use seated arm launches.
SAFETY_NOTES: Keep movement area clear.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Engineering
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Build a Rocket
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Create a standing structure.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children use blocks and tubes to build rockets.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Blocks, cardboard tubes, foil shapes.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Arrange materials on a rug.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a base. 2. Stack pieces. 3. Add a top. 4. Test if it stands. 5. Pretend to launch.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Encourage problem-solving and rebuilding.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Stability; planning; persistence.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note response to collapse.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use wide foam blocks.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep structures low.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Gross Motor
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Rocket Countdown Jumps
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Follow a simple movement sequence.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children crouch, count down, and jump or reach up.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Floor spots.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Space spots apart.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Stand on spot. 2. Count 5-1. 3. Crouch. 4. Jump or reach. 5. Land and freeze.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Lead slowly and model safe landing.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Counting; balance; listening.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note anticipation and control.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Reach from seated position.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Maintain spacing.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Foil Rocket Collage
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Assemble simple shapes.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children glue foil and paper shapes into rockets.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Foil shapes, paper, glue, crayons.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Pre-cut large shapes.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose body. 2. Add top. 3. Add windows. 4. Draw flames. 5. Name the rocket.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Support shape words and creativity.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor; spatial arrangement; art.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note placement and choice.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Use stickers.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Use non-toxic glue.
