TITLE:
Space Explorers STEM
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Space Explorers STEM
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Engineering; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore rockets, the moon, stars, planets, and astronauts through building, sensory play, movement, counting, art, and dramatic play.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name basic space objects; count to five; compare big and small; build simple structures; strengthen balance, grasp, communication, and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Cardboard tubes, blocks, foil, stars, balls, black paper, paint, scarves, helmets, boxes, glow stars, books.
VOCABULARY:
Space, rocket, moon, star, planet, astronaut, launch, orbit, big, small.
BOOKS:
Roaring Rockets; Goodnight Moon; Mousetronaut; There's No Place Like Space.
SONGS:
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star; Zoom Zoom Zoom We're Going to the Moon; Five Little Astronauts.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to look for the moon or stars together and share what they noticed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe vocabulary, counting, spatial reasoning, movement imitation, creativity, and persistence.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual cards, larger pieces, seated movement, pre-built models, and simplified counting.
