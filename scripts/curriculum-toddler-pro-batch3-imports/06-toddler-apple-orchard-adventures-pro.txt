TITLE: Apple Orchard Adventures
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Apple Orchard Adventures
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers explore apples through color sorting, tasting, painting, counting, dramatic play, and simple science investigations.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Name apple colors; compare size and texture; count apples to five; strengthen grasping, pouring, balancing, and pretend-play skills.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Red, green, and yellow apples, baskets, toy apples, paint, paper, blocks, tongs, cups, cinnamon, playdough, picture cards, books.
VOCABULARY: Apple, orchard, tree, seed, red, green, yellow, crunchy, sweet, pick.
BOOKS: Ten Apples Up on Top; The Apple Pie Tree; Apples; Tap the Magic Tree.
SONGS: Way Up High in the Apple Tree; Five Red Apples; Apple Tree Song.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to taste one apple variety at home and share whether it was sweet, tart, soft, or crunchy.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe color matching, counting, sensory reactions, vocabulary, turn-taking, and fine-motor coordination.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer soft cooked apples, picture choices, larger tools, seated options, and non-food sensory alternatives.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME: Apple Colors
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Identify red, green, and yellow; sort by color.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Red, green, yellow, same, different.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Apples, baskets, color mats, paint.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Math; Language; Fine Motor; Art.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Compare three apples and sing Five Red Apples.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Find red, green, and yellow objects outside.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note color matching and comparison words.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Begin with two colors.
SAFETY_NOTES: Cut food into developmentally safe pieces.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Apple Color Sort
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Match apples to color groups.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children place toy apples onto red, green, and yellow mats.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Toy apples, color mats, baskets.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Arrange three color mats and mixed apples.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose an apple. 2. Name or point to its color. 3. Place it on the matching mat. 4. Compare groups. 5. Count each group.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model matching and simple comparison language.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Classification; color recognition; counting.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Watch accuracy and persistence.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use only two colors or hand-over-hand support.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Use large toy apples.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Apple Stamp Painting
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Explore printmaking.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children stamp apple halves into paint and onto paper.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Apple halves, washable paint, paper.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Adult cuts apples and places them cut-side down in trays.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Dip apple. 2. Press onto paper. 3. Lift and observe print. 4. Repeat with another color.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Describe shapes and colors.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor; cause and effect; creativity.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note pressure control and choices.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Add handles to apple stamps.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Adult handles cutting.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Gross Motor
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Apple Basket Carry
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Practice balance and transporting objects.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children carry soft apples through a simple path.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Baskets, soft toy apples, floor markers.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Create a short path.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Put apples in basket. 2. Walk path. 3. Deliver apples. 4. Return basket. 5. Take turns.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Support pacing and balance.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Coordination; responsibility; turn-taking.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note balance and control.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Use lighter baskets.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep path clear.
