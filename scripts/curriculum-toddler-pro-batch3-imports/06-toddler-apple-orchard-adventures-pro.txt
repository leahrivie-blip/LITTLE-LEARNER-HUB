TITLE:
Apple Orchard Adventures
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Apple Orchard Adventures
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore apples through color sorting, tasting, painting, counting, dramatic play, and simple science investigations.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name apple colors; compare size and texture; count apples to five; strengthen grasping, pouring, balancing, and pretend-play skills.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Red, green, and yellow apples, baskets, toy apples, paint, paper, blocks, tongs, cups, cinnamon, playdough, picture cards, books.
VOCABULARY:
Apple, orchard, tree, seed, red, green, yellow, crunchy, sweet, pick.
BOOKS:
Ten Apples Up on Top; The Apple Pie Tree; Apples; Tap the Magic Tree.
SONGS:
Way Up High in the Apple Tree; Five Red Apples; Apple Tree Song.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to taste one apple variety at home and share whether it was sweet, tart, soft, or crunchy.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color matching, counting, sensory reactions, vocabulary, turn-taking, and fine-motor coordination.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer soft cooked apples, picture choices, larger tools, seated options, and non-food sensory alternatives.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME:
Apple Colors
DAILY_OBJECTIVES:

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Red, green, yellow, same, different.
DAILY_MATERIALS:
Apples, baskets, color mats, paint.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Note color matching and comparison words.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Begin with two colors.
SAFETY_NOTES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Color Sort
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Match apples to color groups.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place toy apples onto red, green, and yellow mats.
MATERIALS:
Toy apples, color mats, baskets.
SETUP:
Arrange three color mats and mixed apples.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose an apple.
2. Name or point to its color.
3. Place it on the matching mat.
4. Compare groups.
5. Count each group.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model matching and simple comparison language.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Classification; color recognition; counting.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch accuracy and persistence.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use only two colors or hand-over-hand support.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use large toy apples.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Stamp Painting
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Explore printmaking.
DESCRIPTION:
Children stamp apple halves into paint and onto paper.
MATERIALS:
Apple halves, washable paint, paper.
SETUP:
Adult cuts apples and places them cut-side down in trays.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Dip apple.
2. Press onto paper.
3. Lift and observe print.
4. Repeat with another color.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Describe shapes and colors.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Fine motor; cause and effect; creativity.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note pressure control and choices.
ADAPTATIONS:
Add handles to apple stamps.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Adult handles cutting.
