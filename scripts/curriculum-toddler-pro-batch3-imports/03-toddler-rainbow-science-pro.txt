TITLE:
Rainbow Science
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2–3 Years)
THEME:
Rainbow Science
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional Development

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers investigate colors through mixing, sorting, light, water, movement, and open-ended art. Activities emphasize prediction, cause and effect, descriptive language, matching, counting, and joyful sensory exploration.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Name or match common colors.
- Explore simple color mixing.
- Practice sorting, counting, and comparing.
- Strengthen fine and gross motor coordination.
- Use prediction and observation language.
- Participate in collaborative art and movement.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Washable red, yellow, and blue paint; clear cups; water; droppers; color cards; scarves; hoops; pom-poms; large beads; playdough; contact paper; flashlights; prisms or CDs; white paper; blocks; books about colors.

VOCABULARY:
Color; rainbow; red; orange; yellow; green; blue; purple; mix; light; dark; same; different; bright.

BOOKS:
Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See? by Bill Martin Jr.; Mouse Paint by Ellen Stoll Walsh; Mix It Up! by Hervé Tullet; A Rainbow of My Own by Don Freeman.

SONGS:
I Can Sing a Rainbow; Colors Everywhere; Red and Yellow Make Orange; Rainbow, Rainbow.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to choose a color of the day at home and find three safe objects that match it.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color matching, prediction, cause-and-effect understanding, counting, expressive language, grip, bilateral coordination, and willingness to experiment.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use high-contrast cards, fewer choices, larger tools, sealed sensory bags, seated movement, and gesture or picture responses.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Primary Color Sort
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Sort objects by color.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place large red, yellow, and blue objects into matching bowls.
MATERIALS:
Colored bowls; large matching objects.
SETUP:
Place three bowls in a row and model one match.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Pick an object.
2. Name or notice its color.
3. Find the matching bowl.
4. Place it inside.
5. Compare the groups.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model color words and accept approximations.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Classification; visual discrimination; vocabulary.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Matching accuracy and flexibility.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use only two bowls.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Inspect all objects for size and damage.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Color Hoop Hop
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Respond to color cues while moving safely.
DESCRIPTION:
Children travel to large colored hoops using different movements.
MATERIALS:
Red, yellow, and blue hoops or mats.
SETUP:
Space hoops widely apart.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Name a color.
2. Point to the matching hoop.
3. Walk, tiptoe, or march there.
4. Freeze inside.
5. Repeat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Demonstrate and maintain safe spacing.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Listening; coordination; color recognition.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Cue response and balance.
ADAPTATIONS:
Touch colors from a seated position.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Avoid jumping if the floor is slippery.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Primary Color Marks
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Explore color and mark-making.
DESCRIPTION:
Children make open-ended marks with red, yellow, and blue tools.
MATERIALS:
Paper; chunky crayons; dot markers.
SETUP:
Tape paper to the table.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a color.
2. Make dots or lines.
3. Switch colors.
4. Notice overlaps.
5. Name a favorite area.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate actions without directing the final product.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Fine motor control; creativity; choice-making.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Grip and color preference.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use adaptive grips.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use non-toxic supplies.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Dropper Color Lab
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen hands while mixing colored water.
DESCRIPTION:
Children squeeze droppers into clear cups.
MATERIALS:
Droppers; colored water; clear cups; trays.
SETUP:
Use shallow amounts of colored water.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Squeeze the dropper.
2. Release it in colored water.
3. Move drops to another cup.
4. Add a second color.
5. Observe the change.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Assist grip and narrate each step.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Hand strength; transfer skills; cause and effect.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Squeeze-release control and attention.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use squeeze bottles.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Water is not for drinking; wipe spills.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Two-Color Roll Painting
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Explore blending through rolling.
DESCRIPTION:
Children roll balls through two paint colors inside a covered tray.
MATERIALS:
Shallow lidded tray; paper; large balls; paint.
SETUP:
Secure paper and add small paint amounts.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Add a ball.
2. Tilt the tray.
3. Watch the tracks cross.
4. Continue until colors blend.
5. Open with adult help and observe.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support turn-taking and descriptive language.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Bilateral coordination; color mixing; cooperation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Movement control and shared attention.
ADAPTATIONS:
Roll by hand on a large paper sheet.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep the lid secure during rolling.
