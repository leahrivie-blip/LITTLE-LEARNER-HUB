TITLE:
Pond Life Explorers
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Pond Life Explorers
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Language & Literacy; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers investigate frogs, ducks, fish, turtles, lily pads, and pond water through sensory play, movement, sorting, art, and simple building challenges.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common pond animals; compare wet/dry and sink/float; count to five; imitate animal movements; practice sorting and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Pond animals, blue fabric, water table, lily-pad shapes, blocks, sponges, paint, scoops, cups, nets, books.
VOCABULARY:
Pond, frog, duck, fish, turtle, lily pad, splash, swim, float, wet.
BOOKS:
In the Small, Small Pond; Little Green Frogs; Five Little Ducks; Over and Under the Pond.
SONGS:
Five Little Ducks; Five Green and Speckled Frogs; Row Row Row Your Boat.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to look for birds, puddles, ponds, or water animals and share what they noticed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal naming, counting, prediction, movement imitation, sensory tolerance, and peer interaction.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use dry bins, picture cards, larger animals, seated movements, and shortened turns.

ACTIVITY_NAME:

Dramatic Play

DESCRIPTION:
Children arrange blue fabric, blocks, and animals to make a pond.

Blue fabric, blocks, toy animals, green shapes.
SETUP:
Place loose parts on a rug.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Spread blue fabric.
2. Add blocks as rocks.
3. Add green lily pads.
4. Place animals.
5. Tell where each animal goes.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model habitat language and encourage choices.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Symbolic play; spatial awareness; vocabulary.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

ADAPTATIONS:
