TITLE: Pond Life Explorers
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Pond Life Explorers
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language & Literacy; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers investigate frogs, ducks, fish, turtles, lily pads, and pond water through sensory play, movement, sorting, art, and simple building challenges.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Identify common pond animals; compare wet/dry and sink/float; count to five; imitate animal movements; practice sorting and cooperative play.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Pond animals, blue fabric, water table, lily-pad shapes, blocks, sponges, paint, scoops, cups, nets, books.
VOCABULARY: Pond, frog, duck, fish, turtle, lily pad, splash, swim, float, wet.
BOOKS: In the Small, Small Pond; Little Green Frogs; Five Little Ducks; Over and Under the Pond.
SONGS: Five Little Ducks; Five Green and Speckled Frogs; Row Row Row Your Boat.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to look for birds, puddles, ponds, or water animals and share what they noticed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe animal naming, counting, prediction, movement imitation, sensory tolerance, and peer interaction.
ADAPTATIONS: Use dry bins, picture cards, larger animals, seated movements, and shortened turns.

ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Dramatic Play
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Build a Pretend Pond
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Create a simple habitat.

ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Place loose parts on a rug.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Spread blue fabric. 2. Add blocks as rocks. 3. Add green lily pads. 4. Place animals. 5. Tell where each animal goes.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model habitat language and encourage choices.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Symbolic play; spatial awareness; vocabulary.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note planning and animal placement.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Pre-build part of the pond.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep fabric flat to prevent slipping.

ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children use nets or tongs to move pond animals into bowls.
