TITLE: Nature Explorers
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2–3 Years)
THEME: Nature Explorers
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language & Literacy; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional Development

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers investigate leaves, sticks, rocks, flowers, weather, and outdoor sounds through safe, play-based exploration. Children practice observing, comparing, collecting, sorting, creating, moving, and caring for nature.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Notice and name common nature items.
- Compare color, size, texture, and sound.
- Practice sorting and counting small groups.
- Strengthen observation and descriptive language.
- Build fine and gross motor skills.
- Show gentle, respectful behavior outdoors.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Large leaves; smooth rocks; sticks; pinecones; flower photos; baskets; magnifiers; sensory trays; playdough; crayons; washable paint; glue; contact paper; blocks; scarves; hoops; spray bottles; books about nature.

VOCABULARY:
Nature; leaf; stick; rock; flower; tree; smooth; rough; soft; hard; big; small; collect; observe; weather.

BOOKS:
We're Going on a Leaf Hunt by Steve Metzger; A Tree Is Nice by Janice May Udry; The Listening Walk by Paul Showers; Leaf Man by Lois Ehlert.

SONGS:
Going on a Nature Walk; Leaves Are Falling; The Green Grass Grows All Around; I Hear Thunder.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to take a short nature walk and collect one safe fallen item or send a photo of something interesting they notice.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe descriptive vocabulary, visual attention, sorting choices, counting, balance, grip, curiosity, turn-taking, and respect for living things.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide picture choices, large materials, seated exploration, gloves or tools for sensory-sensitive children, reduced quantities, and adult-supported movement.

MONDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Leaves Everywhere
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Notice leaf colors, sizes, and textures; practice sorting.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Leaf; green; brown; smooth; rough.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Large leaves; baskets; magnifiers; paper; crayons; contact paper.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Fine Motor; Art.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Compare several leaves and sing Leaves Are Falling.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Collect only fallen leaves and carry them in baskets.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Color words, texture reactions, sorting, and collection skills.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use artificial leaves or photos.
SAFETY_NOTES: Inspect all materials and avoid unknown plants.
ACTIVITY_1:
CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_NAME: Leaf Look Lab
OBJECTIVE: Observe and compare leaf details.
DESCRIPTION: Children examine leaves with magnifiers.
MATERIALS: Large leaves; shatter-resistant magnifiers; trays.
SETUP: Place one leaf on each tray.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a leaf. 2. Look closely. 3. Touch the surface. 4. Compare two leaves. 5. Share a word or gesture.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model words such as smooth, rough, big, and small.
LEARNING_GOALS: Observation; comparison; vocabulary.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Visual focus and descriptive communication.
ADAPTATIONS: Use enlarged photos.
SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise and prevent mouthing.
ACTIVITY_2:
CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_NAME: Sort the Leaves
OBJECTIVE: Sort by color or size.
DESCRIPTION: Children place leaves into two simple groups.
MATERIALS: Leaves; two baskets; color or size cards.
SETUP: Model one sorting rule.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Pick a leaf. 2. Notice its feature. 3. Choose a basket. 4. Continue sorting. 5. Compare groups.
TEACHER_ROLE: Narrate choices and keep the rule simple.
LEARNING_GOALS: Classification; reasoning; comparison.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Rule following and flexibility.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer matching pairs.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use clean, dry leaves.
ACTIVITY_3:
CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_NAME: Leaf Rubbings
OBJECTIVE: Explore texture and pressure.
DESCRIPTION: Children rub chunky crayons over paper placed on leaves.
MATERIALS: Leaves; paper; peeled chunky crayons.
SETUP: Tape leaves under paper.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Feel the hidden leaf. 2. Hold a crayon sideways. 3. Rub across the paper. 4. Watch the pattern appear. 5. Choose another color.
TEACHER_ROLE: Assist grip and describe cause and effect.
LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor control; texture awareness; creativity.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Pressure and hand use.
ADAPTATIONS: Use paint rollers.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use non-toxic crayons.

TUESDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Rocks and Stones
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Compare weight, size, texture, and sound.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Rock; heavy; light; hard; smooth.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Large smooth rocks; balance baskets; paint; blocks.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Fine Motor; Engineering.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Pass two large rocks and compare heavy and light.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Follow a rock path made from flat stepping markers.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Comparison language and lifting control.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use foam rocks.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use only large, smooth, lightweight rocks.
ACTIVITY_1:
CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_NAME: Rock Compare
OBJECTIVE: Compare visible and touchable features.
DESCRIPTION: Children inspect rocks and choose words to describe them.
MATERIALS: Large smooth rocks; trays; magnifiers.
SETUP: Place two contrasting rocks per tray.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Touch both rocks. 2. Lift one at a time. 3. Look closely. 4. Choose a describing word. 5. Trade with a friend.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model safe handling and comparison words.
LEARNING_GOALS: Sensory science; language; turn-taking.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Grip, comparison, and vocabulary.
ADAPTATIONS: Use picture symbols.
SAFETY_NOTES: No throwing.
ACTIVITY_2:
CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_NAME: Big Rock, Small Rock
OBJECTIVE: Sort by size.
DESCRIPTION: Children place large and small rocks on matching mats.
MATERIALS: Large safe rocks; big/small mats.
SETUP: Model one example.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Pick a rock. 2. Compare it with the examples. 3. Place it on a mat. 4. Continue. 5. Count each group.
TEACHER_ROLE: Use simple comparison language.
LEARNING_GOALS: Size discrimination; sorting; counting.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Visual matching and one-to-one counting.
ADAPTATIONS: Use only four rocks.
SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise handling.
ACTIVITY_3:
CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_NAME: Painted Nature Stones
OBJECTIVE: Make intentional marks on a natural surface.
DESCRIPTION: Children paint large smooth stones.
MATERIALS: Large stones; washable paint; brushes; smocks.
SETUP: Wash and dry stones.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a stone. 2. Select paint. 3. Add dots or lines. 4. Turn the stone with help. 5. Place it to dry.
TEACHER_ROLE: Encourage experimentation.
LEARNING_GOALS: Creativity; wrist control; choice-making.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Brush grip and color choices.
ADAPTATIONS: Use sponge daubers.
SAFETY_NOTES: Prevent mouthing and wash hands.

WEDNESDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Sticks and Building
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Explore length, balance, and simple construction.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Stick; long; short; build; balance.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Clean craft sticks; blocks; playdough; cardboard bases.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Engineering; Math; Fine Motor; Language.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Compare long and short sticks and make simple shapes.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Carry lightweight foam sticks through a safe obstacle path.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Stacking, persistence, and length comparison.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use foam or jumbo craft sticks.
SAFETY_NOTES: Avoid sharp natural sticks.
ACTIVITY_1:
CATEGORY: Engineering
ACTIVITY_NAME: Stick and Block Bridges
OBJECTIVE: Build a simple bridge.
DESCRIPTION: Children place sticks across blocks and test toy animals on top.
MATERIALS: Jumbo craft sticks; blocks; toy animals.
SETUP: Arrange materials on the floor.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Place two blocks apart. 2. Lay a stick across. 3. Test an animal. 4. Adjust if it falls. 5. Build another bridge.
TEACHER_ROLE: Prompt testing and revision.
LEARNING_GOALS: Cause and effect; spatial reasoning; persistence.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Planning and response to failure.
ADAPTATIONS: Provide a completed model.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use blunt, oversized materials.
ACTIVITY_2:
CATEGORY: Fine Motor
ACTIVITY_NAME: Playdough Stick Forest
OBJECTIVE: Push and position materials vertically.
DESCRIPTION: Children press sticks into playdough to create a forest.
MATERIALS: Playdough; jumbo craft sticks; leaf shapes.
SETUP: Place dough on trays.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Flatten the dough. 2. Choose a stick. 3. Push it upright. 4. Add leaves. 5. Count the trees.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model pressing and count naturally.
LEARNING_GOALS: Hand strength; balance; symbolic play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Force control and persistence.
ADAPTATIONS: Use softer dough.
SAFETY_NOTES: Non-toxic materials only.
ACTIVITY_3:
CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_NAME: Long and Short Match
OBJECTIVE: Match sticks by length.
DESCRIPTION: Children compare and place sticks on long and short mats.
MATERIALS: Jumbo sticks in two lengths; mats.
SETUP: Demonstrate one match.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Pick a stick. 2. Compare it with another. 3. Choose long or short. 4. Place it on the mat. 5. Repeat.
TEACHER_ROLE: Reinforce comparison words.
LEARNING_GOALS: Measurement concepts; sorting.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Visual discrimination.
ADAPTATIONS: Use only two contrasting sticks.
SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise use.

THURSDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Nature Sounds
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Listen, identify, imitate, and create sounds.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Listen; quiet; loud; bird; wind.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Sound recordings; shakers; leaves; paper tubes; scarves.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Music; Science; Language; Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Take a quiet listening moment and name sounds heard.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Complete a slow listening walk.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Auditory attention and sound imitation.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use headphones at low volume or visual sound cards.
SAFETY_NOTES: Keep volume gentle.
ACTIVITY_1:
CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_NAME: Listening Walk
OBJECTIVE: Attend to environmental sounds.
DESCRIPTION: Children walk slowly and pause to listen.
MATERIALS: Picture sound cards; basket.
SETUP: Choose a safe route.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Walk together. 2. Stop at a marker. 3. Listen quietly. 4. Name or imitate a sound. 5. Continue.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model quiet attention and validate all observations.
LEARNING_GOALS: Auditory discrimination; self-regulation; vocabulary.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Focus and recall.
ADAPTATIONS: Listen near an open window.
SAFETY_NOTES: Maintain close supervision outdoors.
ACTIVITY_2:
CATEGORY: Music
ACTIVITY_NAME: Leaf Shaker Sounds
OBJECTIVE: Explore loud and quiet sound.
DESCRIPTION: Children shake sealed containers holding dry leaves.
MATERIALS: Secure clear containers; dry leaves; tape.
SETUP: Seal and inspect containers.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Hold a shaker. 2. Shake gently. 3. Shake more strongly. 4. Stop and listen. 5. Copy a simple rhythm.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model safe shaking and contrast words.
LEARNING_GOALS: Rhythm; cause and effect; listening.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Imitation and impulse control.
ADAPTATIONS: Use teacher-held instruments.
SAFETY_NOTES: Double-seal containers.
ACTIVITY_3:
CATEGORY: Gross Motor
ACTIVITY_NAME: Move Like the Wind
OBJECTIVE: Respond physically to music and speed cues.
DESCRIPTION: Children sway scarves like gentle or strong wind.
MATERIALS: Scarves; soft music.
SETUP: Clear the area.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Hold a scarf. 2. Sway slowly. 3. Move faster when cued. 4. Freeze when the wind stops. 5. Finish with slow breaths.
TEACHER_ROLE: Model and regulate pace.
LEARNING_GOALS: Coordination; listening; self-regulation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Cue response and movement control.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer seated movements.
SAFETY_NOTES: Do not wrap scarves around necks.

FRIDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Nature Museum
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Review, sort, display, and communicate discoveries.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Collection; display; favorite; same; different.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Week's nature items; trays; labels; paper; crayons; blocks.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Language; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Review photos and let each child choose a favorite item.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Revisit a favorite nature activity from the week.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Recall, preference, classification, and peer sharing.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use picture choices and adult scribing.
SAFETY_NOTES: Reinspect all collected items.
ACTIVITY_1:
CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_NAME: Classroom Nature Museum
OBJECTIVE: Organize and display nature objects.
DESCRIPTION: Children arrange safe items on trays.
MATERIALS: Leaves; rocks; craft sticks; trays; picture labels.
SETUP: Prepare simple category trays.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose an item. 2. Decide where it belongs. 3. Place it on a tray. 4. Add another. 5. Tour the museum together.
TEACHER_ROLE: Support classification and language.
LEARNING_GOALS: Organization; recall; communication.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Sorting logic and confidence.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer two choices.
SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise all objects.
ACTIVITY_2:
CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_NAME: Nature Collage
OBJECTIVE: Combine textures into an open-ended artwork.
DESCRIPTION: Children glue large safe nature pieces or paper replicas onto cardstock.
MATERIALS: Cardstock; leaves; paper nature shapes; glue sticks.
SETUP: Arrange materials by type.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a piece. 2. Add glue. 3. Press it onto paper. 4. Layer another texture. 5. Describe the collage.
TEACHER_ROLE: Encourage independent choices and narrate textures.
LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor control; creativity; sensory awareness.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Selection and placement.
ADAPTATIONS: Use contact paper.
SAFETY_NOTES: Avoid fragile or sharp natural materials.
ACTIVITY_3:
CATEGORY: Language
ACTIVITY_NAME: My Nature Favorite
OBJECTIVE: Express a preference and recall an experience.
DESCRIPTION: Children select a nature picture and dictate a word or phrase.
MATERIALS: Nature photos; paper; crayons.
SETUP: Display photo choices.
DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a favorite. 2. Point or name it. 3. Draw a mark or picture. 4. Tell one detail. 5. Teacher records the child's words.
TEACHER_ROLE: Accept gestures, sounds, or words as communication.
LEARNING_GOALS: Recall; expressive language; confidence.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Choice-making and communication mode.
ADAPTATIONS: Use yes/no questions.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use age-appropriate art tools.
