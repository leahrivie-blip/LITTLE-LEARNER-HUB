TITLE:
Growing Gardens STEM
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Growing Gardens STEM
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore seeds, soil, roots, stems, flowers, and garden care through sensory play, planting, measuring, sorting, building, and movement.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify basic plant parts; explore what plants need; compare size and texture; count seeds; strengthen scooping, pouring, pinching, and cooperative problem-solving.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Soil, large seeds, pots, scoops, watering cans, toy vegetables, flowers, blocks, craft sticks, paint, paper, magnifiers, books.
VOCABULARY:
Garden, seed, soil, root, stem, leaf, flower, grow, water, sun.
BOOKS:
Planting a Rainbow; The Tiny Seed; Up in the Garden and Down in the Dirt; Lola Plants a Garden.
SONGS:
The Farmer Plants the Seeds; Mary Mary Quite Contrary; I'm a Little Seed.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to water a plant together and share a photo or note about what changed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe curiosity, sequencing, counting, sensory tolerance, tool use, and care for living things.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer sealed soil bags, larger seeds, picture steps, seated planting, and pre-filled scoops.

ACTIVITY_NAME:

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:

DESCRIPTION:

MATERIALS:
Soil, large seeds, scoops, cups, tray.
SETUP:
Fill shallow tray and place tools nearby.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Touch soil.

3. Find a seed.
4. Compare sizes.
5. Pour back.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model descriptive words and safe exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness; comparison; fine motor.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note grasp and language.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use dry beans in sealed bags.
SAFETY_NOTES:
