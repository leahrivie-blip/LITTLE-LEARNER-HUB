TITLE: Growing Gardens STEM
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Growing Gardens STEM
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Language & Literacy; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers explore seeds, soil, roots, stems, flowers, and garden care through sensory play, planting, measuring, sorting, building, and movement.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Identify basic plant parts; explore what plants need; compare size and texture; count seeds; strengthen scooping, pouring, pinching, and cooperative problem-solving.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Soil, large seeds, pots, scoops, watering cans, toy vegetables, flowers, blocks, craft sticks, paint, paper, magnifiers, books.
VOCABULARY: Garden, seed, soil, root, stem, leaf, flower, grow, water, sun.
BOOKS: Planting a Rainbow; The Tiny Seed; Up in the Garden and Down in the Dirt; Lola Plants a Garden.
SONGS: The Farmer Plants the Seeds; Mary Mary Quite Contrary; I'm a Little Seed.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to water a plant together and share a photo or note about what changed.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe curiosity, sequencing, counting, sensory tolerance, tool use, and care for living things.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer sealed soil bags, larger seeds, picture steps, seated planting, and pre-filled scoops.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME: Seeds and Soil
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Explore seed and soil textures; compare big and small.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Seed, soil, big, small.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Soil, large seeds, trays, scoops, magnifiers.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Fine Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Examine seeds and predict what they may become.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Search for seeds, pods, and leaves.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note comparison language and sensory response.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use sealed sensory bags.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use only non-toxic, non-choking materials with close supervision.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Sensory Play
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Seed and Soil Lab
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Compare textures and sizes.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children scoop soil and examine large seeds.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Soil, large seeds, scoops, cups, tray.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Fill shallow tray and place tools nearby.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Touch soil. 2. Scoop into cup. 3. Find a seed. 4. Compare sizes. 5. Pour back.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model descriptive words and safe exploration.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Sensory awareness; comparison; fine motor.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note grasp and language.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use dry beans in sealed bags.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Prevent mouthing.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Math

ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children place large seed counters into numbered pots.

ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Label cups 1-5.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose cup. 2. Say number. 3. Add seeds. 4. Touch-count. 5. Check together.

ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Number sense; matching.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note counting range.
