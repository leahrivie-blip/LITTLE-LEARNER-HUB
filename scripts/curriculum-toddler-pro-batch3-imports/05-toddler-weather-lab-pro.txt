TITLE:
Weather Lab
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Weather Lab
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Language & Literacy; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore sun, clouds, wind, rain, and changing weather through sensory play, movement, simple experiments, art, and observation.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name basic weather types; observe changes outdoors; compare wet/dry and warm/cool; build vocabulary; strengthen coordination; practice prediction and turn-taking.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Weather cards, scarves, spray bottles, cotton balls, blue paper, foil, cups, water, ice, fans, ribbons, paint, blocks, umbrellas, books.
VOCABULARY:
Sun, cloud, rain, wind, storm, warm, cool, wet, dry, sky.
BOOKS:
The Wind Blew; Little Cloud; Rain; Maisy's Wonderful Weather Book.
SONGS:
You Are My Sunshine; Rain Rain Go Away; It's Raining It's Pouring; Wind the Bobbin Up.
FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to talk about the weather each morning and send a photo of their child dressed for the day's conditions.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice weather vocabulary, prediction attempts, sensory preferences, counting, cooperation, and gross-motor control.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer visual cards, larger tools, seated options, dry sensory alternatives, and shorter turns.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME:
Sunny Skies
DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Identify the sun; explore light and warmth; practice yellow color recognition.
DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Sun, bright, warm, yellow.
DAILY_MATERIALS:
Yellow paper, flashlights, blocks, scarves, paint.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science; Language; Art; Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME:
Observe sunlight near a window and sing You Are My Sunshine.
OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Make body shadows and trace around large shadows with chalk.
DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Watch for noticing light, naming yellow, and following movement prompts.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Use indoor flashlights if outdoor light is limited.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Never look directly at the sun; use washable non-toxic materials.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Light and Shadow Search
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Notice how light creates shadows.
DESCRIPTION:
Children use flashlights to make shadows on walls and objects.
MATERIALS:
Flashlights, blocks, wall space.
SETUP:
Dim one area and arrange blocks nearby.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Shine light on a block.
2. Point out the shadow.
3. Move the light closer and farther.
4. Let children try.
5. Name big and small shadows.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model safe flashlight use and describe changes.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect; visual tracking; vocabulary.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice persistence and comparison language.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger objects or hand-over-hand support.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Do not shine lights into eyes.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sun Sponge Painting
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Create a sun using stamping motions.
DESCRIPTION:
Children stamp yellow paint and add rays.
MATERIALS:
Yellow paint, round sponges, paper, brushes.
SETUP:
Place paper and paint trays at the table.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Stamp a yellow circle.
2. Add lines around it.
3. Talk about warm sunshine.
4. Allow free painting.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage process art and color words.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Fine motor; creativity; color recognition.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe grip and mark making.
ADAPTATIONS:
Tape paper down or use thicker handles.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise paint use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sunshine Stretch
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen balance and body awareness.
DESCRIPTION:
Children reach like sun rays and curl like the setting sun.
MATERIALS:
Yellow scarves, music.
SETUP:
Clear open floor space.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold scarves.
2. Reach high.
3. Stretch wide.
4. Spin slowly.
5. Curl down.

Model movements and pace transitions.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

ADAPTATIONS:
Complete movements seated.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain spacing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Droplet Race
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Observe water moving downward.
DESCRIPTION:
Children squeeze water drops onto an angled tray.
MATERIALS:
Droppers, water, tray, towel.
SETUP:
Prop tray slightly and place towels underneath.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Fill droppers.
2. Squeeze drops at top.
3. Watch them travel.
4. Compare fast and slow.
5. Repeat.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Ask what children notice.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect; observation; fine motor.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note squeezing control and prediction.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use squeeze bottles.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep floor dry.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Count the Raindrops
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Practice one-to-one counting to five.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place blue gems beneath numbered clouds.
MATERIALS:
Cloud cards, blue counters.
SETUP:
Prepare cards numbered 1-5.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a card.
2. Say the number.
3. Add matching drops.
4. Count together.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model touching each counter once.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Number sense; matching.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note counting sequence.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use 1-3 only.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use counters too large to swallow.

Umbrella Walk
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

Practice pretend play and spatial awareness.

Children carry small umbrellas through a rainy-day path.
MATERIALS:
Child umbrellas, blue floor spots.

Create a wide path.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Open umbrellas with help.
2. Walk around puddles.
3. Step over spots.
4. Close and park umbrellas.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support safe handling and pretend language.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Coordination; imagination; routines.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note balance and turn-taking.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use ribbon rain sticks instead.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain distance between umbrellas.

STEM/Discovery

Children test feathers, paper, and blocks in front of a fan.

Guarded fan, feather, tissue, paper, block.

Place objects on a low table.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Predict.
2. Turn fan on low.
3. Test each object.
4. Sort moved/not moved.
5. Discuss light and heavy.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide predictions and comparisons.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Scientific inquiry; classification.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note cause-and-effect understanding.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use hand fans.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep fingers away from fan.
