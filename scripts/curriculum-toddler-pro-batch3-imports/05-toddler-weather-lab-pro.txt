TITLE: Weather Lab
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Weather Lab
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language & Literacy; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor; Creative Arts; Social-Emotional
WEEKLY_OVERVIEW: Toddlers explore sun, clouds, wind, rain, and changing weather through sensory play, movement, simple experiments, art, and observation.
LEARNING_OBJECTIVES: Name basic weather types; observe changes outdoors; compare wet/dry and warm/cool; build vocabulary; strengthen coordination; practice prediction and turn-taking.
WEEKLY_MATERIALS: Weather cards, scarves, spray bottles, cotton balls, blue paper, foil, cups, water, ice, fans, ribbons, paint, blocks, umbrellas, books.
VOCABULARY: Sun, cloud, rain, wind, storm, warm, cool, wet, dry, sky.
BOOKS: The Wind Blew; Little Cloud; Rain; Maisy's Wonderful Weather Book.
SONGS: You Are My Sunshine; Rain Rain Go Away; It's Raining It's Pouring; Wind the Bobbin Up.
FAMILY_CONNECTION: Invite families to talk about the weather each morning and send a photo of their child dressed for the day's conditions.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Notice weather vocabulary, prediction attempts, sensory preferences, counting, cooperation, and gross-motor control.
ADAPTATIONS: Offer visual cards, larger tools, seated options, dry sensory alternatives, and shorter turns.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME: Sunny Skies
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Identify the sun; explore light and warmth; practice yellow color recognition.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Sun, bright, warm, yellow.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Yellow paper, flashlights, blocks, scarves, paint.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language; Art; Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Observe sunlight near a window and sing You Are My Sunshine.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Make body shadows and trace around large shadows with chalk.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Watch for noticing light, naming yellow, and following movement prompts.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use indoor flashlights if outdoor light is limited.
SAFETY_NOTES: Never look directly at the sun; use washable non-toxic materials.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Light and Shadow Search
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Notice how light creates shadows.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children use flashlights to make shadows on walls and objects.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Flashlights, blocks, wall space.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Dim one area and arrange blocks nearby.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Shine light on a block. 2. Point out the shadow. 3. Move the light closer and farther. 4. Let children try. 5. Name big and small shadows.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model safe flashlight use and describe changes.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Cause and effect; visual tracking; vocabulary.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Notice persistence and comparison language.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use larger objects or hand-over-hand support.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Do not shine lights into eyes.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Sun Sponge Painting
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Create a sun using stamping motions.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children stamp yellow paint and add rays.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Yellow paint, round sponges, paper, brushes.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Place paper and paint trays at the table.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Stamp a yellow circle. 2. Add lines around it. 3. Talk about warm sunshine. 4. Allow free painting.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Encourage process art and color words.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor; creativity; color recognition.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Observe grip and mark making.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Tape paper down or use thicker handles.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise paint use.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Gross Motor
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Sunshine Stretch
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Strengthen balance and body awareness.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children reach like sun rays and curl like the setting sun.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Yellow scarves, music.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Clear open floor space.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Hold scarves. 2. Reach high. 3. Stretch wide. 4. Spin slowly. 5. Curl down.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Model movements and pace transitions.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Balance; coordination; listening.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note imitation and control.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Complete movements seated.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Maintain spacing.

TUESDAY
DAILY_THEME: Clouds Above
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Identify clouds; compare soft and fluffy textures; explore shape.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Cloud, white, soft, float.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Cotton balls, glue, blue paper, scarves, cloud cards.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Fine Motor; Language; Art.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Read Little Cloud and compare cloud pictures.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Lie on blankets and look for cloud shapes.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Notice descriptive language and visual attention.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Offer felt pieces instead of cotton.
SAFETY_NOTES: Monitor small loose materials.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Sensory Play
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Cloud Touch Tray
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Compare soft textures.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children touch cotton, felt, and soft fabric clouds.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Cotton balls, felt, fleece, tray.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Arrange materials in a shallow tray.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Touch each texture. 2. Squeeze gently. 3. Name soft and fluffy. 4. Sort similar pieces.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Narrate sensory words and respect preferences.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Sensory discrimination; vocabulary.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note tolerance and sorting.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Provide tongs or sealed sensory bags.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Prevent mouthing.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Cotton Cloud Collage
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Strengthen pinching and placing skills.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children glue cloud pieces onto blue paper.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Blue paper, glue, cotton or felt.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Pre-draw large cloud outlines.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Add glue. 2. Pinch pieces. 3. Press onto cloud. 4. Describe the finished cloud.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Support glue use and language.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Fine motor; creativity; shape awareness.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Watch hand strength and attention.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Use self-stick shapes.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Use non-toxic glue.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Music & Movement
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Floating Clouds
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Move slowly and respond to tempo.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children float scarves like clouds.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: White scarves, calm music.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Clear movement space.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Lift scarves. 2. Float slowly. 3. Move high and low. 4. Freeze when music stops.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Model controlled movement.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Listening; gross motor; self-regulation.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note start-stop control.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Move scarves while seated.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Avoid wrapping scarves around necks.

WEDNESDAY
DAILY_THEME: Rainy Day
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Explore water droplets; compare wet and dry; count raindrops.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Rain, drop, wet, puddle.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Spray bottles, water table, droppers, blue gems, umbrellas.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Math; Fine Motor; Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Make rain sounds with fingers and sing Rain Rain Go Away.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Stomp in pretend chalk puddles or real puddles with appropriate gear.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Watch counting, cause-and-effect thinking, and sensory reactions.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Use sealed water bags for children avoiding wet play.
SAFETY_NOTES: Wipe spills promptly.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Droplet Race
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Observe water moving downward.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children squeeze water drops onto an angled tray.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Droppers, water, tray, towel.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Prop tray slightly and place towels underneath.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Fill droppers. 2. Squeeze drops at top. 3. Watch them travel. 4. Compare fast and slow. 5. Repeat.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Ask what children notice.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Cause and effect; observation; fine motor.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note squeezing control and prediction.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use squeeze bottles.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep floor dry.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Math
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Count the Raindrops
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Practice one-to-one counting to five.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children place blue gems beneath numbered clouds.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Cloud cards, blue counters.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Prepare cards numbered 1-5.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose a card. 2. Say the number. 3. Add matching drops. 4. Count together.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Model touching each counter once.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Number sense; matching.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note counting sequence.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Use 1-3 only.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Use counters too large to swallow.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Dramatic Play
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Umbrella Walk
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Practice pretend play and spatial awareness.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children carry small umbrellas through a rainy-day path.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Child umbrellas, blue floor spots.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Create a wide path.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Open umbrellas with help. 2. Walk around puddles. 3. Step over spots. 4. Close and park umbrellas.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Support safe handling and pretend language.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Coordination; imagination; routines.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note balance and turn-taking.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Use ribbon rain sticks instead.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Maintain distance between umbrellas.

THURSDAY
DAILY_THEME: Windy Weather
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Observe moving air; test light and heavy objects; build breath control.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Wind, blow, move, light, heavy.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Fans, feathers, ribbons, paper, blocks, pinwheels.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science; Language; Gross Motor; Math.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Feel air from a fan and discuss what wind can move.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Run with ribbons and watch wind direction.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Notice predictions and comparison language.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Let children observe rather than blow.
SAFETY_NOTES: Use guarded fans and sanitize shared blowing tools.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: What Can Wind Move?
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Compare how objects respond to air.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children test feathers, paper, and blocks in front of a fan.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Guarded fan, feather, tissue, paper, block.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Place objects on a low table.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Predict. 2. Turn fan on low. 3. Test each object. 4. Sort moved/not moved. 5. Discuss light and heavy.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Guide predictions and comparisons.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Scientific inquiry; classification.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note cause-and-effect understanding.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use hand fans.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep fingers away from fan.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Fine Motor
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Pinwheel Practice
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Coordinate breath and visual tracking.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children blow or wave pinwheels.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Large child-safe pinwheels.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Give each child space.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Hold pinwheel. 2. Blow gently. 3. Try harder. 4. Wave it. 5. Watch it spin.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Demonstrate gentle breathing.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Oral-motor control; cause and effect.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note breath control and tracking.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Teacher holds pinwheel or child waves it.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Use individual pinwheels.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Gross Motor
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: Windy Ribbon Run
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Strengthen running and directional movement.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children move ribbons fast, slow, high, and low.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Ribbon rings.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Clear open space.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Walk with ribbons. 2. Run slowly. 3. Move high and low. 4. Freeze. 5. Make circles.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Call movement cues.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Coordination; listening; spatial awareness.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note control and imitation.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Use seated arm motions.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Keep ribbon handles short.

FRIDAY
DAILY_THEME: Weather Watchers
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Review weather types; make a simple forecast; choose appropriate clothing.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Forecast, weather, sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Weather chart, clothing props, cards, blocks, art supplies.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Language; Science; Social-Emotional; Dramatic Play.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Review the week and let children choose today's weather card.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Take a weather walk and collect observations.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Notice recall, choices, and vocabulary use.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Offer two visual choices.
SAFETY_NOTES: Match outdoor time to actual conditions.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Language & Literacy
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Toddler Weather Report
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Use words or gestures to describe weather.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children take turns choosing a weather symbol and giving a simple report.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Weather symbols, toy microphone.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Place symbols near a class chart.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Look outside. 2. Choose a symbol. 3. Say or point to weather. 4. Add symbol to chart. 5. Celebrate each report.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Model short phrases and accept gestures.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Communication; observation; confidence.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note expressive language.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Use eye gaze or pointing.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Sanitize microphone prop.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Dramatic Play
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Dress for the Weather
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Match clothing to weather.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children dress a doll or themselves with weather props.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Hat, coat, boots, sunglasses, umbrella, dolls.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Arrange clothing in baskets.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Pick a weather card. 2. Choose clothing. 3. Dress doll. 4. Explain choice. 5. Switch weather.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Prompt practical reasoning.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Matching; self-help; vocabulary.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note clothing knowledge.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Use picture matching.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Remove cords and small fasteners.
ACTIVITY_3_CATEGORY: Creative Arts
ACTIVITY_3_NAME: My Weather Wheel
ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Review weather symbols through art.
ACTIVITY_3_DESCRIPTION: Children decorate a simple weather wheel.
ACTIVITY_3_MATERIALS: Paper plates, weather pictures, glue, crayons, fastener assembled by adult.
ACTIVITY_3_SETUP: Pre-cut pictures and prepare wheels.
ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Color plate. 2. Add weather pictures. 3. Adult attaches arrow. 4. Turn arrow to favorite weather.
ACTIVITY_3_TEACHER_ROLE: Discuss each symbol.
ACTIVITY_3_LEARNING_GOALS: Recall; fine motor; choice making.
ACTIVITY_3_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Note matching and preferences.
ACTIVITY_3_ADAPTATIONS: Use stickers.
ACTIVITY_3_SAFETY_NOTES: Adult handles fasteners.
