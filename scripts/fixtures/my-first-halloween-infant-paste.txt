Title:
My First Halloween

Age Group:
Infant 0–12 Months

Theme:
Gentle Halloween Sensory Discoveries

Weekly Overview:
A gentle infant Halloween week focused on supervised sensory looking, tracking, textures, and caregiver-led movement.

Weekly Objectives:
Notice orange and black contrasts during calm play
Track slow caregiver-led movement and peekaboo faces
Explore safe textures with adult support

Weekly Materials:
Orange and black scarves
Soft white fabric ghosts
Washable orange and black paint
Pumpkin-shaped sponges
Large zip bags for mess-free exploration

Weekly Safety:
No loose fabric over faces, no small parts, no food tasting, active supervision at all times.

Weekly Teacher Preparation:
Pre-cut ghost peekaboo panels and prepare paint trays before infants arrive.

Weekly Books:
Book title:
Peek-a-Who?
Author:
Nina Laden
Why this book:
Simple peekaboo faces support visual tracking.
Book questions:
Who do you see?
Can you look at the picture?

Weekly Songs and Movement:
Song title:
Slowly, Slowly, Very Slowly
Teacher directions:
Move fingers slowly down baby's arm during the song.
Lyrics:
Slowly, slowly, very slowly creeps the garden snail.

Weekly Printables:
Idea title:
Gentle Halloween family photo strip
Purpose:
Send home a simple strip for families to add one fall photo.
Instructions:
Print letter-size and trim with adult scissors only.

Cover Image:
Realistic photo of a caregiver and infant doing a calm Halloween sensory activity. No masks on infants.

Family Connection:
Share orange and black laundry items at home and name colors during diaper changes.

Teacher Notes:
Keep each experience short and follow infant cues.

Monday:
Activity name:
Pumpkin Footprint Keepsake
Weekday:
Monday
Category / developmental domain:
Creative Expression and Sensory
Recommended age:
Infant 0–12 Months
Estimated duration:
8 minutes
Activity objective:
Support bonding while caregivers create a supervised footprint keepsake.
Activity objective:
Repeat label must stay in the same activity, not start a new one.
What children will do:
Infants watch and touch the caregiver's hands during the adult-led footprint craft.
Materials:
- Washable orange paint
- Heavy cardstock
- Wipes
Teacher preparation:
Tape cardstock to the table before infants arrive.
Setup:
Seat infant safely in caregiver lap beside the prepared cardstock.
Step-by-step directions:
1. Caregiver paints one foot with a thin layer of washable paint.
2. Press foot gently onto cardstock with full support.
3. Wipe foot immediately and display the keepsake out of reach.
Teacher questions:
Do you see the orange paint?
Safety and supervision:
Adult completes the footprint; infant never holds small paint caps alone.
Cleanup:
Wash skin and sanitize the table.
Vocabulary:
Orange
Pumpkin
Soft

Tuesday:
Activity name:
Orange and Black Texture Exploration
Weekday:
Tuesday
Category:
Sensory
Recommended age:
Infant 0–12 Months
Estimated duration:
6 minutes
Activity objective:
Explore safe fabric textures with caregiver narration.
What children will do:
Infants pat, squeeze, and look at orange and black fabric squares.
Materials:
- Orange fleece square
- Black velour square
Step-by-step directions:
1. Offer one square at a time.
2. Name the color and texture slowly.
Safety and supervision:
Adult keeps fabrics away from the face and mouth.

Wednesday:
Activity name:
Ghost Peekaboo and Face-to-Face Movement
Weekday:
Wednesday
Category:
Social-Emotional
Recommended age:
Infant 0–12 Months
Estimated duration:
5 minutes
Activity objective:
Build social engagement through peekaboo faces and slow movement.
What children will do:
Infants watch caregiver hide and reveal a soft ghost panel.
Materials:
- Soft white ghost panel on a stick
Step-by-step directions:
1. Hide the ghost below eye level.
2. Reveal slowly and pause for a smile.
Safety and supervision:
Adult holds the panel at all times.

Thursday:
Activity name:
Pumpkin Water Painting
Weekday:
Thursday
Category:
Sensory
Recommended age:
Infant 0–12 Months
Estimated duration:
7 minutes
Activity objective:
Explore water and color on a sealed surface with full support.
What children will do:
Infants slap and watch orange water move inside a sealed bag.
Materials:
- Sealed zip bag with orange water and pumpkin sponge
Step-by-step directions:
1. Place the bag on a towel in front of baby.
2. Tap the bag together and watch movement.
Safety and supervision:
Inspect the seal before use; discard if leaking.

Friday:
Activity name:
Halloween Color Scarf Movement
Weekday:
Friday
Category:
Movement
Recommended age:
Infant 0–12 Months
Estimated duration:
4 minutes
Activity objective:
Encourage visual tracking with slow orange and black scarf movement.
What children will do:
Infants track a scarf moving side to side while supported.
Materials:
- Lightweight orange scarf
- Lightweight black scarf
Step-by-step directions:
1. Move one scarf slowly across baby's line of sight.
2. Pause and allow eyes to find the scarf again.
Safety and supervision:
Adult holds scarves; never cover infant's face.
