TITLE:
Construction Engineers

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Construction & Engineering

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become construction engineers as they design buildings, test structures, operate construction sites, explore tools, and solve engineering challenges. Through hands-on building experiences and STEM investigations, children will develop creativity, problem-solving, and teamwork skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore engineering and construction concepts
• Practice planning and problem-solving
• Develop measurement and building skills
• Strengthen teamwork and communication
• Build fine and gross motor abilities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Blocks, measuring tapes, toy tools, hard hats, cardboard boxes, blueprints, rulers, clipboards, construction vehicles

VOCABULARY:
Engineer, Builder, Blueprint, Structure, Measure, Construct, Tool, Foundation, Design, Build

BOOKS:
Goodnight Goodnight Construction Site
Mighty Mighty Construction Site
Roadwork
Construction Zone
Diggersaurs

SONGS:
The Construction Song
Johnny Works With One Hammer
Driving in My Truck
Build It Up
The Builder Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to identify construction projects in the community and discuss how buildings are made.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving, teamwork, measuring skills, communication, and creativity during building activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger building materials, visual supports, adaptive tools, peer partnerships, and additional teacher support.
