WEEKLY_OVERVIEW
Welcome to My Classroom helps toddlers build confidence and feel secure as they begin a new school year. Throughout the week, children will become familiar with their classroom, meet teachers and classmates, practice simple routines, and explore learning centers through hands-on play. Activities encourage social interaction, independence, communication, and a sense of belonging while helping children develop positive feelings about coming to school.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES
- Become familiar with the classroom environment.
- Build trusting relationships with teachers.
- Practice simple classroom routines.
- Learn classmates' names.
- Explore classroom learning centers.
- Increase confidence and independence.
- Express feelings about school.
- Practice listening and following simple directions.

FAMILY_CONNECTION
Invite families to talk with their child about school each evening. Encourage children to share one favorite activity from their day and, if possible, bring a small family photo to display in the classroom. Share positive updates during the first week to help families feel connected and reassure children as they adjust to their new classroom routine.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES
- Observe how children separate from caregivers.
- Observe participation in classroom routines.
- Observe interactions with teachers and peers.
- Observe communication skills during play.
- Observe exploration of learning centers.
- Observe ability to follow one-step directions.
- Observe confidence during transitions.
- Observe curiosity and engagement throughout the day.

ADAPTATIONS
- Provide a visual daily schedule.
- Offer comfort items or family photos when appropriate.
- Allow extra transition time for children who need it.
- Use simple one-step directions with visual cues.
- Pair hesitant children with a supportive peer or teacher.
- Offer sensory breaks as needed.
- Adapt materials for varying developmental levels and physical abilities.
- Celebrate each child's progress and participation at their own pace.

CIRCLE_TIME
Gather children in a circle and welcome everyone by name. Sing a Hello Song while pointing to each child. Introduce the teacher, talk about the classroom, discuss gentle hands and listening ears, and take a short classroom tour together.

OUTDOOR_PLAY
Take a classroom walk outside to explore the playground. Practice walking together, climbing safely, kicking balls, blowing bubbles, and identifying outdoor play equipment.

ACTIVITY 1

CATEGORY
Social-Emotional

ACTIVITY_NAME
Hello, Friends!

OBJECTIVE
Help children learn classmates' names and feel welcomed.

DESCRIPTION
Children sit in a circle while greeting one another with smiles, waves, and simple introductions.

MATERIALS
- Name tags
- Welcome song

SETUP
Have children sit in a circle with their name tags on.

DIRECTIONS
1. Welcome each child by name.
2. Encourage everyone to wave hello.
3. Sing the Hello Song together.
4. Let each child say or hear their name.
5. Celebrate everyone's participation with claps.

TEACHER_ROLE
Model greetings, encourage participation, and reassure children who may be nervous.

LEARNING_GOALS
- Build classroom community.
- Recognize names.
- Develop social confidence.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES
Observe eye contact, participation, and comfort level with peers.

ADAPTATIONS
Allow children to wave instead of speaking if they are shy.

SAFETY_NOTES
Provide enough personal space for all children during circle time.

FRIDAY

DAILY_THEME
All My Feelings Matter

DAILY_OBJECTIVES
- Review the emotions learned throughout the week.
- Identify happy, sad, mad, scared, and calm feelings.
- Demonstrate healthy ways to express emotions.
- Celebrate emotional growth and friendships.
- Build confidence using feeling words.

DAILY_VOCABULARY
- Happy
- Sad
- Mad
- Scared
- Calm
- Proud
- Feelings
- Friend
- Kind
- Brave

DAILY_MATERIALS
- Emotion picture cards
- Feelings chart
- Handheld mirrors
- Puppets
- Stuffed animals
- Construction paper
- Crayons
- Stickers
- Emotion sorting cards
- Music player
- Scarves
- Bean bags
- Calm-down bottle
- "Feelings Superstar" certificates (optional)

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS
- Social-Emotional Development
- Language & Literacy
- Physical Development
- Cognitive Development
- Creative Arts
- Approaches to Learning

CIRCLE_TIME

Review every feeling learned this week using the Feelings Chart. Hold up emotion cards one at a time and invite children to identify each feeling and make the matching facial expression. Talk about how everyone has many different feelings and every feeling is okay. Discuss healthy ways to express emotions, such as talking to a teacher, taking deep breaths, asking for help, or finding a quiet space to calm down. End circle time by reading *The Color Monster* and celebrating everything the class has learned.

OUTDOOR_PLAY

Play a Feelings Adventure game. Teachers call out different emotions and children move in fun ways to match each feeling—happy jumps, calm slow walks, excited skipping, brave marches, and quiet deep breaths under the shade. Finish with bubbles and free play while encouraging kind interactions.
