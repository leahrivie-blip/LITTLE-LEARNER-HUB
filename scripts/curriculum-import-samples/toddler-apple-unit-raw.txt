WEEKLY_OVERVIEW
This week, toddlers will explore the wonderful world of apples through hands-on investigations, sensory experiences, movement, music, dramatic play, art, and early science activities. Children will discover that apples come in different colors, sizes, and tastes while learning where apples grow and how they become healthy foods we can enjoy. Throughout the week, they will strengthen language skills, early math concepts, fine and gross motor development, and social-emotional skills by working together during engaging apple-themed experiences.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES
- Identify apples as a healthy fruit.
- Recognize red, green, and yellow apples.
- Explore apples using the five senses.
- Compare apples by size, color, and texture.
- Practice counting, sorting, and matching apples.
- Strengthen fine motor skills through art and sensory play.
- Build vocabulary related to apples and orchards.
- Encourage curiosity through simple science investigations.
- Participate in cooperative play and group discussions.
- Develop confidence while exploring new foods and experiences.

CIRCLE_TIME

Show children a basket filled with red, green, and yellow apples. Allow each child to hold an apple and describe what they notice. Talk about the different colors, shapes, textures, and smells. Read "Apple Farmer Annie" and discuss where apples come from. Sing "Way Up High in the Apple Tree" while pretending to pick apples from an imaginary tree.

OUTDOOR_PLAY

Go on an "Apple Hunt" around the playground. Hide plastic apples for children to find and place into baskets. Practice counting the apples collected together and compare which baskets have more or fewer apples.

ACTIVITY 1

CATEGORY
Science

ACTIVITY_NAME
Apple Investigation

OBJECTIVE
Introduce children to observing apples using their five senses.

DESCRIPTION
Children investigate real apples by looking, touching, smelling, and listening while learning descriptive vocabulary.

MATERIALS
- Red, green, and yellow apples
- Magnifying glasses

SETUP
Place apples in the center of the table with magnifying glasses nearby.

DIRECTIONS
1. Give each child an apple.
2. Look closely using magnifying glasses.
3. Feel the smooth skin.
4. Smell the apple.
5. Tap gently and listen to the sound.
6. Talk about what everyone noticed.

TEACHER_ROLE
Encourage observation by asking open-ended questions and introducing new vocabulary.

LEARNING_GOALS
- Observation skills
- Science exploration
- Vocabulary development
- Curiosity

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES
Observe children's willingness to explore and describe the apples.

ADAPTATIONS
Provide larger apples or additional teacher support as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES
Closely supervise children while handling real apples.
