TITLE:
V3 Full Workflow Sample

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3–4 Years

THEME:
Discovery Week

PLAN:
Free

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science, Language & Literacy, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
A complete sample week used to verify the v3 import workflow end to end.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore materials with curiosity
- Use descriptive language
- Practice cooperation

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Bins, scoops, paper, crayons, blocks, books, songs chart

VOCABULARY:
explore, compare, share, observe, create

BOOKS:
Book One | Author One | Morning read aloud
Book Two | Author Two | Small group
Book Three | Author Three | Closing circle

SONGS:
Song One | Welcome song
Song Two | Transition song
Song Three | Goodbye song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to send one object related to the theme.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note language, cooperation, and persistence across the week.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger grips and visual supports as needed.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Monday Activity 1
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
