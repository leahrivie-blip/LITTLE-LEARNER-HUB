WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants are naturally fascinated by faces, expressions, movement, and reflections. Throughout this week, babies will explore mirrors through sensory play, movement, songs, facial expressions, and social interactions. These experiences support self-awareness, visual tracking, communication development, emotional connections, and early cognitive skills while encouraging joyful exploration and discovery.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Promote self-awareness and identity development
- Encourage visual tracking and attention skills
- Support language development through face-to-face interactions
- Strengthen social-emotional connections
- Encourage reaching, grasping, and movement toward reflections
- Develop curiosity and exploration skills

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to spend time looking in mirrors with their infants. Talk about facial features, emotions, and expressions. Use mirrors during daily routines such as diaper changes and getting dressed to encourage communication and self-awareness.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, social smiles, facial expression imitation, visual tracking, vocalizations, reaching attempts, curiosity, and engagement with reflections.

ADAPTATIONS:
Position mirrors at different angles and heights. Provide extra support for younger infants. Reduce stimulation if infants become overwhelmed and allow participation at each child's developmental level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Face Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual exploration and self-awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore their reflections while teachers identify facial features and encourage observation.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Floor mat

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely in the play area at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model language, point to facial features, and encourage interaction with reflections.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant near the mirror.
2. Point to the reflection.
3. Name facial features.
4. Smile and make expressions.
5. Encourage observation and interaction.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Visual attention
- Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Watches facial movements
- Shows curiosity


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Smile Back Mirror Play

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Promote social interaction and emotional awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe smiles, expressions, and reactions while interacting with teachers in front of mirrors.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror

SETUP:
Sit with infants in front of the mirror.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model smiles, expressions, and positive interactions.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit beside infant.
2. Smile into the mirror.
3. Make simple expressions.
4. Pause for infant responses.
5. Celebrate interactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Emotional awareness
- Social engagement
- Communication skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Smiles back
- Watches expressions
- Vocalizes during interaction


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Music Time

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Combine movement, music, and visual exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe themselves while participating in songs and movement activities.

MATERIALS:
Mirror
Music player

SETUP:
Position mirrors safely during music time.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead songs and encourage movement while drawing attention to reflections.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Begin a familiar song.
2. Encourage gentle movement.
3. Point to reflections.
4. Sing and move together.
5. Repeat favorite songs.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Music appreciation
- Visual exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches reflection while moving
- Responds to music
- Engages during songs

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Peek-A-Boo Mirror Fun

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop object permanence and visual attention.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in peek-a-boo games using mirrors and scarves.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Soft scarves

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely in the play area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead peek-a-boo games and encourage anticipation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Cover part of the reflection with a scarf.
2. Ask "Where did baby go?"
3. Reveal the reflection.
4. Celebrate the surprise.
5. Repeat several times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Object permanence
- Visual tracking
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches closely
- Anticipates reveal
- Smiles or laughs


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Reach & Explore

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage reaching and movement toward reflections.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants reach, crawl, scoot, or move toward mirrors during exploration time.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Floor mat

SETUP:
Place mirrors safely within view and reach.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage movement and celebrate exploration.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position mirror nearby.
2. Encourage infant movement.
3. Talk about the reflection.
4. Allow exploration.
5. Celebrate effort and discovery.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Reaching skills
- Curiosity and exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Moves toward mirror
- Reaches intentionally
- Maintains engagement
