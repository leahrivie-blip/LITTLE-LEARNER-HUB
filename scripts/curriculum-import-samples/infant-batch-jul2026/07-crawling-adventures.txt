WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will strengthen crawling, creeping, reaching, and exploration skills through engaging movement-based experiences. Throughout the week, children will navigate safe obstacle courses, chase toys, explore tunnels, and interact with caregivers while building confidence, coordination, balance, and problem-solving abilities.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Strengthen crawling and creeping skills
- Develop coordination and balance
- Encourage exploration and curiosity
- Promote problem-solving during movement activities
- Support language development through movement vocabulary
- Build confidence and independence

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to create safe floor spaces for movement and exploration. Celebrate crawling milestones and provide opportunities for infants to practice moving toward favorite toys and family members.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe crawling patterns, balance, coordination, problem-solving skills, persistence, confidence, and interactions with peers and caregivers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional support for infants who are not yet crawling by encouraging reaching, rolling, and scooting. Modify obstacle courses based on developmental abilities.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Toy Chase Adventure

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage crawling toward a desired object.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl toward colorful toys placed just beyond their reach.

MATERIALS:
Colorful toys
Floor mat

SETUP:
Create a safe open crawling space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage movement and celebrate effort and progress.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant on the floor.
2. Position a favorite toy nearby.
3. Encourage infant to move toward it.
4. Gradually increase distance.
5. Celebrate successful attempts.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Crawling skills
- Coordination
- Motivation to move

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Crawls toward toy
- Reaches successfully
- Shows persistence


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Crawl Discovery

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Promote movement through visual motivation.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl toward a baby-safe mirror to explore their reflection.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Floor mat

SETUP:
Place mirror securely at one end of the crawling area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage infants to move toward the mirror and describe what they see.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant several feet away.
2. Point out the mirror.
3. Encourage crawling forward.
4. Celebrate arrival at the mirror.
5. Allow exploration time.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Crawling endurance
- Visual attention
- Self-awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Crawls independently
- Shows interest in reflection
- Maintains focus


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Tunnel Explorer

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Build confidence navigating through spaces.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl through a soft tunnel while exploring a new environment.

MATERIALS:
Soft play tunnel

SETUP:
Place tunnel securely on a padded surface.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Provide encouragement from both ends of the tunnel.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the tunnel.
2. Demonstrate crawling through.
3. Encourage infant entry.
4. Cheer progress through tunnel.
5. Celebrate completion.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Crawling confidence
- Spatial awareness
- Problem-solving

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Enters tunnel willingly
- Crawls through independently
- Explores surroundings
