WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore music, rhythm, movement, and sound through active play experiences designed for older infants. Throughout the week, children will dance, crawl, shake instruments, move scarves, listen to songs, and participate in musical games that support coordination, language development, sensory exploration, and social engagement.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop listening and sound awareness
- Encourage purposeful movement and coordination
- Strengthen gross motor skills through music-based activities
- Support language development through songs and repetition
- Promote social interaction during group experiences
- Encourage creativity and self-expression

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to sing songs throughout the day and provide opportunities for babies to move, clap, bounce, and dance to music. Repetition helps strengthen language and memory development.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation during songs, body movement, rhythm awareness, communication attempts, coordination, and interactions with peers and teachers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow infants to participate at their developmental level. Provide seated options for infants who are not yet standing. Reduce noise levels if infants become overstimulated.

MONDAY:



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Shake and Dance Party

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage movement and rhythm exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants shake musical instruments while moving to upbeat children's music.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe shakers
Music player

SETUP:
Create an open floor space for movement and exploration.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model shaking, dancing, and moving to the beat while encouraging participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Give each infant a shaker.
2. Start a familiar song.
3. Demonstrate shaking to the rhythm.
4. Encourage movement and dancing.
5. Celebrate participation and exploration.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Rhythm awareness
- Gross motor development
- Auditory processing

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Shakes instrument independently
- Moves body to music
- Responds to rhythm changes



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scarf Dance Adventure

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop coordination and visual tracking.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants wave colorful scarves while listening and moving to music.

MATERIALS:
Colorful scarves
Music player

SETUP:
Provide each infant with a scarf in a safe movement area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model scarf movements and encourage imitation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Distribute scarves.
2. Start music.
3. Wave scarves high and low.
4. Encourage infants to imitate movements.
5. Explore fast and slow movements.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Hand-eye coordination
- Visual tracking
- Movement exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Waves scarf purposefully
- Watches scarf movement
- Engages with activity



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bell Ring Parade

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage crawling, cruising, and sound exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants move around the classroom while ringing bells and listening to sounds.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe bells

SETUP:
Provide open space for movement.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead a simple musical parade and encourage movement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Give each infant a bell.
2. Demonstrate ringing.
3. Encourage crawling or cruising.
4. Follow the teacher around the room.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Sound awareness
- Social participation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Moves while holding bell
- Responds to music
- Participates with peers

TUESDAY:



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Wheels on the Bus Movement Game

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage imitation and body awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in simple movements while singing The Wheels on the Bus.

MATERIALS:
Music player
Picture cards

SETUP:
Seat infants in a circle or open floor area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model actions and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sing The Wheels on the Bus.
2. Demonstrate hand motions.
3. Encourage imitation.
4. Repeat favorite verses.
5. Celebrate all participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Language development
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Attempts movements
- Watches teacher
- Participates during songs



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bubble Music Chase

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Promote movement and visual tracking.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl, cruise, and reach for bubbles while music plays.

MATERIALS:
Bubble machine
Music player

SETUP:
Create a safe open floor space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Blow bubbles and encourage movement toward them.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Start music.
2. Release bubbles.
3. Encourage infants to move toward bubbles.
4. Allow reaching and popping attempts.
5. Repeat throughout activity.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Crawling and cruising
- Visual tracking
- Coordination

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Moves toward bubbles
- Reaches intentionally
- Maintains engagement
