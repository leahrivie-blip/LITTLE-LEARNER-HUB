WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Communication begins long before babies say their first words. Throughout this week, infants will engage in meaningful back-and-forth interactions through facial expressions, coos, babbles, songs, mirrors, gestures, and responsive caregiving. These experiences help build the foundation for language development, social connection, listening skills, and emotional security.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage early communication skills
- Support cooing, babbling, and vocal play
- Strengthen caregiver-child interactions
- Develop listening and turn-taking skills
- Promote facial recognition and social engagement
- Build early language foundations

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk with babies throughout the day. Pause after speaking to allow babies opportunities to respond with facial expressions, sounds, or movements. These simple conversations help build strong language foundations.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, cooing, babbling, vocalizations, facial expressions, turn-taking behaviors, social smiles, and responses to voices.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow infants additional processing time before responding. Reduce distractions during communication activities and position infants where they can clearly see faces and expressions.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Face-to-Face Chat Time

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage social interaction and early communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in one-on-one face-to-face conversations with caregivers who respond to every sound, smile, and expression.

MATERIALS:
Floor mat

SETUP:
Position infant comfortably facing the teacher.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Maintain eye contact, respond to vocalizations, and model conversational turn-taking.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit face-to-face with infant.
2. Smile and greet the infant.
3. Talk using a calm voice.
4. Pause and wait for a response.
5. Respond to all sounds and expressions.
6. Continue the conversation naturally.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Social engagement
- Communication development
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Makes eye contact
- Coos or babbles
- Responds to facial expressions


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Conversations

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Promote self-awareness and communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore their reflections while teachers engage them in simple conversations and facial imitation games.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror

SETUP:
Place mirror securely during floor play.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Talk about facial expressions and respond to infant reactions.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant near mirror.
2. Point to reflections.
3. Smile and talk to the infant.
4. Encourage observation.
5. Respond to all communication attempts.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Language exposure
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Smiles or vocalizes
- Watches facial expressions


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Hello Song Circle

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce communication through songs and repetition.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in a personalized greeting song experience where each child hears their name and receives individual attention.

MATERIALS:
Music player

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably in a circle.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Sing greeting songs and engage individually with each infant.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Begin the Hello Song.
2. Sing each infant's name.
3. Make eye contact.
4. Pause for responses.
5. Celebrate all participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Name recognition
- Listening skills
- Social connection

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Responds to voice
- Watches teacher
- Shows excitement during songs

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Puppet Talk Time

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage attention to voices and communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants interact with friendly puppets that engage them in simple conversations and greetings.

MATERIALS:
Soft hand puppet

SETUP:
Seat infants where they can easily view the puppet.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Use expressive voices and respond to infant reactions.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the puppet.
2. Have the puppet greet infants.
3. Pause after speaking.
4. Respond to infant sounds.
5. Continue simple interactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening development
- Social communication
- Attention skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches puppet
- Responds to voice
- Vocalizes during interaction


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Family Photo Conversations

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Build connections between familiar faces and communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore family photos while teachers talk about important people in their lives.

MATERIALS:
Family photos

SETUP:
Arrange photos at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Name family members and encourage observation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show family photos.
2. Name the people pictured.
3. Describe facial features.
4. Encourage looking and pointing.
5. Respond to infant reactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Social awareness
- Language development
- Family connections

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at photos
- Shows recognition
- Vocalizes during activity
