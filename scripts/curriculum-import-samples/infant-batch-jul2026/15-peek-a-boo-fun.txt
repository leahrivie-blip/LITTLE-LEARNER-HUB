WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Peek-a-boo is more than a game—it helps infants develop object permanence, social connections, communication skills, memory, anticipation, and emotional security. Throughout the week, infants will participate in playful peek-a-boo experiences using scarves, mirrors, puppets, tunnels, boxes, and movement activities designed to strengthen early learning through joyful interactions.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop object permanence skills
- Encourage social interaction and communication
- Strengthen visual tracking abilities
- Promote anticipation and memory development
- Support emotional security through predictable play
- Encourage movement, reaching, and exploration

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to play peek-a-boo throughout daily routines. Hiding and revealing faces, toys, and objects helps babies develop memory, anticipation, and communication skills while strengthening family bonds.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe anticipation, eye contact, social smiles, laughter, visual tracking, object permanence understanding, communication attempts, and emotional responses.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger visual cues for younger infants. Allow infants to participate from seated, floor, or supported positions. Adjust activity pacing to meet individual developmental needs.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Classic Scarf Peek-A-Boo

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage anticipation and social engagement.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in a traditional peek-a-boo game using colorful scarves and expressive interactions.

MATERIALS:
Soft scarves

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably in a quiet play area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model excitement, anticipation, and positive social interaction.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold scarf in front of your face.
2. Ask "Where did I go?"
3. Reveal your face while saying "Peek-a-boo!"
4. Smile and engage with the infant.
5. Repeat several times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Social engagement
- Visual tracking
- Anticipation skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches scarf closely
- Anticipates reveal
- Smiles or laughs


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Peek-A-Boo Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Promote self-awareness and visual exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore reflections while participating in peek-a-boo games using mirrors.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Soft scarf

SETUP:
Position mirror securely at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide interactions and encourage observation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit with infant near mirror.
2. Cover part of the reflection.
3. Reveal the reflection.
4. Talk about what baby sees.
5. Repeat and encourage interaction.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Visual attention
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches reflection
- Responds to reveal
- Vocalizes during activity


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Puppet Peek-A-Boo Friends

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop communication and object permanence skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch friendly puppets appear and disappear during playful interactions.

MATERIALS:
Hand puppet

SETUP:
Seat infants where they can clearly see the puppet.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Use expressive voices and movements to maintain engagement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the puppet.
2. Hide the puppet behind your back.
3. Reveal the puppet while greeting infants.
4. Repeat with playful interactions.
5. Encourage observation and responses.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Object permanence
- Communication skills
- Social engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches puppet movement
- Anticipates reappearance
- Responds with smiles or sounds

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Find the Toy Peek-A-Boo

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen object permanence and problem-solving skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch a favorite toy disappear under a cloth and then discover where it went.

MATERIALS:
Soft toy
Small blanket

SETUP:
Place materials in front of infants during floor play.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model hiding and finding while encouraging curiosity.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the toy.
2. Cover it with a blanket.
3. Ask "Where did it go?"
4. Reveal the toy.
5. Repeat several times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Object permanence
- Memory development
- Problem-solving skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches hiding process
- Searches for toy
- Shows excitement when found


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Tunnel Peek-A-Boo Adventure

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage movement and exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl or move through a tunnel while teachers play peek-a-boo from the opposite side.

MATERIALS:
Soft play tunnel

SETUP:
Place tunnel securely on a padded surface.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage movement and provide positive reinforcement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant near tunnel entrance.
2. Peek through the tunnel.
3. Call the infant's name.
4. Encourage movement through the tunnel.
5. Celebrate success.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Exploration skills
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Moves toward teacher
- Explores tunnel
- Shows confidence and curiosity
