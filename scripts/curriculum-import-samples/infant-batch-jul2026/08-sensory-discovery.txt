WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore the world through their senses using hands-on experiences that encourage touching, shaking, squeezing, listening, crawling, and investigating. Throughout the week, children will engage with a variety of textures, sounds, colors, and movement activities that support brain development, curiosity, problem-solving, and sensory processing.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage sensory exploration through play
- Strengthen fine motor and grasping skills
- Develop problem-solving abilities
- Promote crawling and movement during exploration
- Support language development through sensory vocabulary
- Build curiosity and confidence through discovery

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to provide opportunities for safe sensory exploration at home using household textures, sounds, and everyday objects. Talk about what babies are touching, hearing, and experiencing.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, curiosity, fine motor skills, problem-solving, communication attempts, crawling, and reactions to different textures and sounds.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for easier grasping. Reduce sensory stimulation if needed. Allow infants to explore at their own pace and comfort level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Texture Treasure Basket

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage exploration of different textures using hands and fingers.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants investigate a basket filled with safe textured materials while practicing grasping, touching, and comparing sensations.

MATERIALS:
Texture squares
Soft fabric pieces
Large textured toys
Discovery basket

SETUP:
Arrange various textured items inside a low basket on the floor.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model touching and describing textures while encouraging exploration.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place basket near infants.
2. Introduce one item at a time.
3. Encourage touching and holding.
4. Describe textures using simple vocabulary.
5. Allow independent exploration.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory awareness
- Fine motor development
- Curiosity and exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Explores textures willingly
- Transfers items between hands
- Shows texture preferences


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Shake and Listen Bottles

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop auditory awareness and cause-and-effect understanding.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants shake sensory bottles to hear sounds and observe movement inside.

MATERIALS:
Sensory bottles filled with safe materials

SETUP:
Place bottles on the floor within easy reach.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model shaking and encourage infants to experiment with sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Present a sensory bottle.
2. Demonstrate shaking.
3. Encourage infant participation.
4. Listen to different sounds together.
5. Compare bottles with different contents.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Auditory development
- Cause-and-effect understanding
- Hand-eye coordination

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Shakes bottle independently
- Watches moving contents
- Responds to sounds


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Crawl and Discover Path

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage crawling while exploring sensory materials.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants crawl across a pathway of safe textures and sensory items.

MATERIALS:
Floor mats
Texture squares
Soft sensory toys

SETUP:
Create a sensory pathway across the floor.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage movement and describe discoveries along the path.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Arrange materials in a simple path.
2. Place infant at the beginning.
3. Encourage crawling and exploration.
4. Allow time to investigate materials.
5. Celebrate discoveries.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Crawling skills
- Sensory exploration
- Problem-solving

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Crawls independently
- Stops to explore materials
- Demonstrates curiosity
