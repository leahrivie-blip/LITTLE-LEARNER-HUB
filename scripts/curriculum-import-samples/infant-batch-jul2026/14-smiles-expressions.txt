WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants communicate long before they can speak through smiles, facial expressions, eye contact, body movements, and sounds. Throughout this week, babies will explore emotions, facial expressions, social interactions, mirrors, songs, and playful games that help develop communication skills, emotional awareness, social connections, and confidence. These experiences support healthy attachment relationships and early social-emotional development.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage social smiling and positive interactions
- Develop awareness of facial expressions and emotions
- Support communication through nonverbal interactions
- Strengthen eye contact and social engagement
- Build emotional connections with caregivers
- Promote confidence through responsive interactions

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to spend time face-to-face with their infants each day. Talk about emotions, smile often, and respond to babies' facial expressions and sounds. These simple interactions build strong emotional connections and communication skills.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe social smiles, eye contact, facial expression imitation, vocalizations, emotional responses, social engagement, and interactions with peers and caregivers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional one-on-one support when needed. Reduce distractions during social activities and allow infants to participate at their own comfort level and developmental stage.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Smile Back Game

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage social smiling and eye contact.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in a face-to-face interaction game where teachers smile, pause, and respond to babies' reactions.

MATERIALS:
Floor mat

SETUP:
Position infants comfortably facing the teacher.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model warm facial expressions, maintain eye contact, and respond to infant reactions.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit face-to-face with the infant.
2. Smile warmly.
3. Pause and wait for a response.
4. Respond to any smile, sound, or movement.
5. Continue the interaction naturally.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Social engagement
- Eye contact development
- Communication skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Returns smiles
- Maintains eye contact
- Vocalizes during interaction


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Expressions

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Develop awareness of facial expressions.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe facial expressions in a mirror while teachers model different emotions.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror

SETUP:
Place mirror securely at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model happy, surprised, excited, and silly expressions while talking about feelings.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant near the mirror.
2. Make a happy face.
3. Describe the expression.
4. Repeat with different expressions.
5. Encourage observation and interaction.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Emotional recognition
- Visual attention

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches expressions
- Smiles at reflection
- Shows curiosity


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Happy Face Song Time

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Support emotional awareness through music.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in songs that include facial expressions and simple emotions.

MATERIALS:
Music player

SETUP:
Create a comfortable circle time area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Sing songs and model expressions that match the lyrics.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Begin a familiar song.
2. Model happy expressions.
3. Encourage observation.
4. Repeat favorite songs.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Emotional awareness
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches teacher
- Responds to songs
- Smiles during activity

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Puppet Feelings Fun

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce basic emotions through play.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch puppets demonstrate different feelings and expressions.

MATERIALS:
Hand puppets

SETUP:
Seat infants where they can easily see the puppets.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Use expressive voices and facial expressions while interacting with the puppets.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the puppet.
2. Show a happy expression.
3. Talk about the feeling.
4. Repeat with other simple expressions.
5. Encourage observation and participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Emotional awareness
- Social development
- Attention skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches puppet
- Responds to expressions
- Vocalizes during interaction


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Family Smiles Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen connections with familiar faces.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore photos of family members displaying happy expressions.

MATERIALS:
Family photos

SETUP:
Arrange photos at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Name family members and talk about their smiles and expressions.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show family photos.
2. Point out smiles.
3. Name family members.
4. Encourage observation.
5. Respond to infant reactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Family connections
- Social recognition
- Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at photos
- Shows recognition
- Engages with familiar faces
