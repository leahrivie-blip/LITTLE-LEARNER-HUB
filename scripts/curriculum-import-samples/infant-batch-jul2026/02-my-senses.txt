WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore the world through their senses of sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste-safe exploration. Through play-based experiences, babies will engage with different textures, sounds, colors, movements, and caregiver interactions that support brain development, sensory processing, and early learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage sensory exploration through play
- Strengthen visual tracking skills
- Develop listening and sound awareness
- Support reaching and grasping abilities
- Build positive caregiver-child interactions
- Promote curiosity and exploration

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about what babies are seeing, hearing, and touching throughout daily routines. Describe textures, sounds, and objects during play and caregiving moments.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, reactions to sounds, exploration of textures, engagement with sensory materials, and communication attempts through facial expressions, sounds, and movements.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional support positioning as needed. Reduce sensory input for infants who become overstimulated. Offer larger materials for easier grasping and exploration.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Texture Touch Exploration

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce infants to a variety of safe textures.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore soft, fuzzy, smooth, and bumpy materials through supervised sensory play.

MATERIALS:
Soft fabric squares
Textured fabric pieces

SETUP:
Arrange texture materials on a clean floor mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Describe each texture and encourage gentle exploration.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant comfortably on the mat.
2. Introduce one texture at a time.
3. Allow touching and exploration.
4. Name the texture being explored.
5. Observe infant reactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory exploration
- Touch awareness
- Early language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Reaches toward textures
- Shows preferences
- Explores with hands


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sound and Listen Rattles

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop awareness of sounds.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants listen to and watch rattles as they move and make sounds.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe rattles

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably or place on floor mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Shake rattles gently while describing sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show rattle to infant.
2. Shake slowly.
3. Pause and repeat.
4. Move rattle to different sides.
5. Encourage listening and tracking.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Auditory awareness
- Visual tracking
- Sensory engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Turns toward sounds
- Watches movement
- Smiles or vocalizes

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Baby Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual exploration and self-awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe reflections and facial expressions in baby-safe mirrors.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely during floor play.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Talk about what babies see in the mirror.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position mirror safely.
2. Encourage infant to look.
3. Point to facial features.
4. Smile and interact.
5. Allow free observation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual attention
- Self-awareness
- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks toward reflection
- Smiles at mirror
- Reaches toward mirror


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bright Color Tracking

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen visual tracking abilities.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants visually follow colorful objects as they move.

MATERIALS:
Color scarves

SETUP:
Prepare open floor space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Move scarves slowly while naming colors.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold scarf within view.
2. Move slowly side to side.
3. Pause often.
4. Repeat with multiple colors.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Focus skills
- Color exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Tracks movement
- Maintains attention
- Reaches toward objects
