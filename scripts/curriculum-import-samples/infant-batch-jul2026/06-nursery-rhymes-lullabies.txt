WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will experience the comfort, rhythm, and language of classic nursery rhymes and lullabies. Through songs, fingerplays, gentle movement, visual props, and caregiver interactions, babies will develop listening skills, language exposure, social connections, and early literacy foundations while enjoying familiar melodies and predictable patterns.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Build listening and sound awareness
- Increase exposure to language and vocabulary
- Encourage social interactions with caregivers
- Support visual tracking and attention skills
- Promote comfort and emotional security through music
- Introduce early literacy through repetition and rhyme

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to sing favorite nursery rhymes during bedtime, diaper changes, and playtime. Repetition helps infants recognize familiar sounds, voices, and language patterns.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe listening behaviors, responses to familiar songs, visual attention, engagement during fingerplays, vocalizations, and social interactions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional support positioning as needed. Reduce stimulation for infants who become overwhelmed and allow participation at each child's developmental level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Twinkle Star Scarf Dance

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce infants to rhythm, music, and visual tracking.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch soft scarves move gently while listening to Twinkle Twinkle Little Star.

MATERIALS:
Soft scarves
Music player

SETUP:
Create a calm floor play area with infants comfortably positioned.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Sing the nursery rhyme while moving scarves slowly above and around infants.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infants comfortably.
2. Begin singing Twinkle Twinkle Little Star.
3. Move scarves gently through the air.
4. Pause occasionally for visual focus.
5. Repeat song and movements.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Visual tracking
- Language exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches scarf movement
- Responds to music
- Smiles or vocalizes


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Nursery Rhyme Mirror Time

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Promote social engagement and self-awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe themselves in mirrors while teachers sing familiar nursery rhymes.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely during floor play.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Sing nursery rhymes while encouraging infants to look into the mirror.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position mirror safely.
2. Encourage infant to look.
3. Sing familiar rhymes.
4. Point to facial features.
5. Allow time for exploration.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Social interaction
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Smiles during songs
- Engages with teacher


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Baa Baa Black Sheep Puppet Play

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Support listening and visual attention.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch a simple puppet presentation while listening to Baa Baa Black Sheep.

MATERIALS:
Sheep puppet
Soft floor mat

SETUP:
Seat infants where they can easily see the puppet.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Animate the puppet while singing the nursery rhyme.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the puppet.
2. Sing Baa Baa Black Sheep.
3. Move puppet slowly.
4. Pause for infant reactions.
5. Repeat favorite parts.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual attention
- Language development
- Social engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches puppet
- Tracks movement
- Responds with sounds or smiles
