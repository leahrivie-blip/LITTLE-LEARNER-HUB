WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore music, rhythm, movement, and sound through developmentally appropriate play experiences. Throughout the week, babies will listen to songs, experience gentle movements, explore musical instruments, and participate in caregiver-led activities that support sensory development, language exposure, and social connections.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop listening and sound awareness
- Encourage movement and body awareness
- Support language development through songs and repetition
- Promote sensory exploration through music
- Strengthen caregiver-child interactions
- Encourage participation in musical experiences

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to sing familiar songs during daily routines. Babies learn through repetition, rhythm, and hearing caregiver voices throughout the day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe reactions to music, body movement, listening skills, engagement during songs, visual tracking, and social interactions during musical activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust volume levels for individual needs. Provide additional support during movement activities and allow infants to participate at their own developmental level.

MONDAY:



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rattle Rhythm Fun

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce infants to rhythm and sound awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants listen to and watch rattles while exploring cause and effect through sound.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe rattles

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably or place on floor mats.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Shake rattles gently, model rhythm, and describe sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the rattle to the infant.
2. Shake slowly near the infant.
3. Pause and repeat.
4. Move the rattle side to side.
5. Encourage visual tracking and listening.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Auditory awareness
- Visual tracking
- Sensory exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Turns toward sounds
- Watches movement
- Smiles or vocalizes



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scarf Dance Exploration

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual tracking and movement experiences.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe colorful scarves moving to music while experiencing rhythm and motion.

MATERIALS:
Colorful scarves
Music player

SETUP:
Prepare a safe floor play area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Move scarves slowly with the music while naming colors and encouraging engagement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Play gentle music.
2. Move scarves through the air.
3. Pause for visual focus.
4. Change movement speed occasionally.
5. Encourage infants to watch and reach.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Attention skills
- Music appreciation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows scarf movement
- Maintains visual focus
- Attempts to reach

TUESDAY:



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Gentle Lap Bounce Songs

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Promote body awareness and social connection.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants experience gentle bouncing movements while listening to familiar songs.

MATERIALS:
Teacher lap
Music

SETUP:
Hold infant securely while seated.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Provide gentle movement and sing familiar songs.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold infant securely.
2. Sing a simple song.
3. Gently bounce to the rhythm.
4. Pause and repeat.
5. Observe infant responses.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Social bonding
- Rhythm recognition

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Smiles during movement
- Engages with teacher
- Responds to music



ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bell Listening Discovery

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop listening and sound discrimination skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants listen to soft bell sounds from different directions.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe bells

SETUP:
Position infant comfortably on a mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Ring bells gently while describing sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Ring bell softly.
2. Pause.
3. Ring from another direction.
4. Encourage listening.
5. Repeat several times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Sound awareness
- Attention development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Turns toward sound
- Watches bell
- Shows curiosity
