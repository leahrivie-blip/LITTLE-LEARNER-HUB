WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore bright colors through sensory play, movement, music, and caregiver interactions. Throughout the week, babies will observe, reach for, and engage with colorful materials while building visual tracking, early communication, and sensory awareness skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop visual tracking skills
- Encourage reaching and grasping
- Support sensory exploration
- Build early language through color words
- Promote positive caregiver interactions

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colors during everyday routines. Talk about colorful clothing, toys, foods, and household objects to build language connections.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, reaching attempts, engagement during songs, reactions to colorful materials, and early communication behaviors.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger graspable materials when needed. Adjust activity positioning to support each infant's developmental level and comfort.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rainbow Scarf Visual Tracking

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop visual tracking skills using colorful moving objects.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe brightly colored scarves moving through their visual field while teachers describe the colors being seen.

MATERIALS:
Rainbow scarves
Soft floor mat

SETUP:
Place infants comfortably on a floor mat in a quiet area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Move scarves slowly and name colors while encouraging visual attention.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant comfortably.
2. Hold scarf within visual range.
3. Slowly move scarf left and right.
4. Pause frequently.
5. Repeat with different colors.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Attention skills
- Language exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows scarf with eyes
- Maintains attention
- Shows excitement through smiles or sounds


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Colorful Tummy Time

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage head lifting and reaching.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in tummy time while colorful toys are placed nearby to encourage movement.

MATERIALS:
Bright colored toys
Tummy time mat

SETUP:
Place toys around the tummy time area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage infants and reposition toys to maintain interest.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant on tummy.
2. Position toys within sight.
3. Encourage looking and reaching.
4. Celebrate movement attempts.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Neck strength
- Reaching skills
- Visual engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Head lifting
- Reaching attempts
- Interest in toys
