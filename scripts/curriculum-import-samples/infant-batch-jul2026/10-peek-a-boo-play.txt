WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will participate in playful peek-a-boo experiences that encourage social interaction, visual tracking, language development, and early object permanence skills. Through songs, scarves, mirrors, movement activities, and playful interactions with caregivers, babies will build relationships, confidence, and curiosity while having fun.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop social connections with trusted caregivers
- Strengthen visual tracking skills
- Encourage early object permanence development
- Support language development through repetition
- Promote smiling, laughing, and social engagement
- Encourage reaching and movement during play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to play peek-a-boo during daily routines, diaper changes, and playtime. Repeating simple games helps infants build trust, anticipation, and early learning skills.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, social smiles, laughter, anticipation, visual tracking, reaching attempts, and responses to familiar people and games.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional visual cues for younger infants. Allow infants to participate at their own pace and adjust activity length based on attention span and comfort.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scarf Peek-A-Boo Fun

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual tracking and social engagement.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch as teachers hide and reveal their faces using colorful scarves while playing peek-a-boo.

MATERIALS:
Soft scarves

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably on the floor or in supportive seating.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model peek-a-boo, use expressive facial expressions, and encourage interaction.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold scarf in front of your face.
2. Say "Peek-a-boo!"
3. Remove scarf and smile.
4. Repeat several times.
5. Encourage infants to watch and respond.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Social interaction
- Attention skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches scarf movement
- Smiles during game
- Anticipates reveal


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Peek-A-Boo

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Promote self-awareness and visual exploration.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore reflections while participating in peek-a-boo games with teachers.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely in the play area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model peek-a-boo and encourage infants to notice reflections.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position mirror safely.
2. Sit with infant near mirror.
3. Cover and reveal your face.
4. Talk about reflections.
5. Allow infants to explore.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Social development
- Visual attention

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Watches teacher
- Smiles or vocalizes


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Puppet Hello and Goodbye

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage social communication and engagement.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants interact with a puppet that appears and disappears during a simple peek-a-boo game.

MATERIALS:
Finger puppet

SETUP:
Seat infants where they can easily see the puppet.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Use playful voices and movements to encourage engagement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the puppet.
2. Hide the puppet behind your back.
3. Bring it back and say hello.
4. Repeat several times.
5. Encourage infants to watch and respond.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Communication skills
- Social interaction
- Object permanence

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches puppet
- Responds to appearance
- Shows excitement

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Blanket Peek-A-Boo

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop anticipation and object permanence skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in a simple peek-a-boo game using soft blankets.

MATERIALS:
Soft baby blankets

SETUP:
Create a comfortable floor play area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide the game and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Cover your face with blanket.
2. Ask "Where did I go?"
3. Reveal your face.
4. Smile and interact.
5. Repeat throughout activity.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Object permanence
- Social connection
- Attention skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches carefully
- Anticipates reveal
- Smiles or laughs


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Peek-A-Boo Ball Roll

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage movement and visual tracking.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch and chase a ball that appears and disappears from behind objects.

MATERIALS:
Soft ball

SETUP:
Create a safe floor play space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Roll the ball and encourage observation and movement.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the ball.
2. Hide it briefly.
3. Roll it into view.
4. Encourage reaching or crawling.
5. Repeat several times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Movement skills
- Problem-solving

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows ball movement
- Reaches or moves toward ball
- Shows curiosity
