WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Tiny Artist Studio introduces babies to their first creative experiences through adorable caregiver-assisted handprint and footprint keepsakes, mess-free color exploration, safe sensory art, movement, texture, and visual discovery. Babies are never expected to complete a craft. Instead, caregivers build meaningful art experiences around natural infant behaviors such as looking, kicking, reaching, opening and closing hands, patting, pressing, and responding to new sensations. The week combines cute family-worthy keepsakes with genuine developmental experiences involving body awareness, sensory processing, visual attention, cause and effect, communication, and caregiver bonding.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore color through safe visual and sensory experiences.
Build awareness of hands, fingers, feet, and toes.
Practice natural reaching, kicking, patting, and pressing movements.
Experience simple cause and effect.
Strengthen visual attention and tracking.
Experience contrasting textures.
Respond to caregiver narration and facial expressions.
Build caregiver-infant connection through one-to-one interaction.
Show preferences through gaze, movement, expression, or vocalization.
Participate in early creative experiences without pressure for a finished product.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Send home at least one meaningful handprint or footprint keepsake from the week. When appropriate, explain the development behind the experience, such as sensory exploration, body awareness, visual attention, or cause and effect. If program policy permits, pair an artwork with a photograph of the baby participating.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice responses to new textures.
Watch for hand opening and closing.
Observe kicking and toe movement.
Notice which colors hold visual attention.
Observe reaching toward materials.
Watch for repeated patting or pressing.
Notice responses to cool or wet sensations.
Listen for coos and vocalizations.
Watch for repeated actions after an interesting result.
Observe signs of enjoyment, uncertainty, overstimulation, or disengagement.

ADAPTATIONS:
Follow baby's cues. Keep sessions short. Never force hands or feet open. Stop for distress, mouthing of paint, or overstimulation.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
My First Handprint

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce gentle tactile exploration while helping baby notice their hand and fingers.

DESCRIPTION:
Baby feels a very small amount of washable paint on the palm while a caregiver gently creates one natural handprint.

MATERIALS:
Washable non-toxic paint
Heavy cardstock
Warm washcloth
Protective surface

SETUP:
Hold or position baby securely with continuous adult support.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Here is your hand.
Look at your little fingers.
Do you feel that?
Press.
You made a mark.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Talk about baby's hand.
2. Allow baby to see the caregiver's face and hear calm narration.
3. Apply a thin amount of paint to the palm.
4. Pause and observe baby's reaction.
5. If baby remains comfortable, gently bring the hand to cardstock.
6. Allow the natural hand position to create the print.
7. Lift the hand without forcing fingers open.
8. Clean immediately.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Observe tactile response
- natural hand opening
- visual attention
- and caregiver interaction.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe tactile response, natural hand opening, visual attention, and caregiver interaction.

ADAPTATIONS:
Follow baby's cues and keep the experience brief.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Never force fingers open. Stop for distress, mouthing, or skin irritation.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Squishy Hand Painting

Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:

DESCRIPTION:
Baby presses, pats, or rests their hands against a securely sealed paint sensory bag.

SETUP:

TEACHER_ROLE:
Look at the color move.
Press.
Pat, pat.
You made it squish.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position baby safely.
2. Allow baby's hand to naturally contact the bag.
3. Pause.
4. Describe the color moving.
5. Model one gentle press if needed.
6. Allow spontaneous patting or pressing.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Cause and effect
- hand movement
- visual tracking
- repeated action.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Cause and effect, hand movement, visual tracking, repeated action.

ADAPTATIONS:
Follow baby's cues and keep the experience brief.

SAFETY_NOTES:
A loose plastic bag must never be accessible. Inspect continuously.

Adult sealing tape

SETUP:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Baby Chooses a Color

Sensory Play

Paper
