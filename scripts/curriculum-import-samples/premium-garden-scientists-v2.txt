@WEEKLY_START@

Science, Math, Language & Literacy

Preschoolers investigate soil, seeds, leaves, and garden helpers through hands-on science play.

Children will observe and describe plant or soil changes using science words.
Children will count, compare, and sort garden materials.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Soil trays, seeds, magnifying glasses, measuring cups

WEEKLY_VOCABULARY:
seed, soil, sprout, observe, measure

@WEEKLY_SONGS_END@

@ACTIVITY_START@
ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soil Scientists Tray
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
MATERIALS:
Soil trays, scoops, magnifying glasses, smocks
SETUP:
Place one tray per pair on low tables with smocks nearby.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to scoop and feel the soil.
2. Ask what they notice: wet, dry, bumpy.
3. Record one group observation on the chart.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate texture words and model gentle scooping.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"I notice the soil feels damp when I squeeze it."
LEARNING_GOAL:
Observe and describe soil properties
VOCABULARY:
soil, texture, scoop
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise mouthing; use pasteurized soil only.
@ACTIVITY_END@

@ACTIVITY_START@
ACTIVITY_NAME:
Seed Sort Lab
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
MATERIALS:
Mixed seeds, sorting trays, tweezers optional
SETUP:
Place mixed seeds in shallow bowls at each tray.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show two seed types and name them.
2. Sort seeds into trays.
3. Count how many are in each tray.
LEARNING_GOAL:
Sort by attribute and strengthen fine-motor control
@ACTIVITY_END@

@MONDAY_TRANSITIONS_START@
Sing a two-line cleanup song while carrying trays to the bin.
@MONDAY_TRANSITIONS_END@

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Carry lightweight watering cans along a marked path.

@MONDAY_OBSERVATIONS_START@
Document sorting strategy: by color, size, or tool use.
@MONDAY_OBSERVATIONS_END@

SAFETY_NOTES:
Monitor water spills on the floor near outdoor play.
@MONDAY_END@
