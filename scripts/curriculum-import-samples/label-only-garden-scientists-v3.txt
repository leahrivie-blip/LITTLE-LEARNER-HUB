TITLE:
Garden Scientists

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Garden Scientists

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science, Language & Literacy, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week children become garden scientists as they explore seeds, soil, and growing plants through sensory play and hands-on discovery.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Children will observe and describe plant parts.
Children will compare wet and dry soil using descriptive language.
Children will practice caring for living things.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Seed trays, potting soil, watering cans, magnifying glasses, plant labels, smocks

VOCABULARY:
seed, soil, root, sprout, water, grow, garden

BOOKS:
The Tiny Seed | Eric Carle | Read during circle time
Planting a Rainbow | Lois Ehlert | Pair with color sorting

SONGS:
The Farmer Plants the Seeds | Traditional | Movement song
Growing Up | Original | Transition song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to share photos of plants at home or in the neighborhood.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note how children describe texture, color, and changes in seeds over the week.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger tools, reduce visual clutter, and provide one-on-one support for fine motor tasks.

MONDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soil Exploration Bin
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Children explore soil texture using hands and tools while building descriptive vocabulary.
DESCRIPTION:
Children use scoops and magnifying glasses to explore soil at a sensory table.
MATERIALS:
Bin of potting soil, scoops, small pots, smocks
SETUP:
Place the soil bin on a low table with smocks nearby.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Narrate textures and invite children to compare wet and dry soil.
DIRECTIONS:
Invite children to scoop and feel the soil.
Offer magnifying glasses to look closely at particles.
Encourage children to describe what they notice.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Use descriptive words for texture
Compare wet and dry materials
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note vocabulary children use to describe soil.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Seed Sorting Trays
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Children sort seeds by size and color while strengthening pinching skills.
DESCRIPTION:
Children use tweezers to sort mixed seeds into trays by color or size.
MATERIALS:
Mixed seeds, sorting trays, tweezers
SETUP:
Place seeds in small bowls at a table with empty sorting trays.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model sorting one seed at a time and name colors and sizes.
DIRECTIONS:
Invite children to pick up seeds with tweezers.
Sort seeds into trays by size or color.
Count seeds together at the end.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Strengthen pincer grasp
Sort objects by one attribute
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Watch for hand preference and sorting strategies.

TUESDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Planting Station
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Children plant seeds and predict what will happen as they grow.
DESCRIPTION:
Children fill pots, plant seeds, water gently, and label their plants.
MATERIALS:
Small pots, soil, seeds, watering cans, plant labels
SETUP:
Set out planting materials at individual workspaces.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide children through each planting step and label their pots.
DIRECTIONS:
Fill a pot with soil.
Push a seed into the soil.
Water gently and place a label on the pot.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Follow multi-step directions
Make simple predictions about growth
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note how children sequence planting steps.

WEDNESDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Garden Dramatic Play
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Children role-play caring for a garden using props and cooperative language.
DESCRIPTION:
Children choose garden roles and practice caring for pretend plants and vegetables.
MATERIALS:
Garden hats, pretend tools, baskets, play vegetables
SETUP:
Arrange a garden shop area with labeled baskets.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Join play briefly to model caring language and turn-taking.
DIRECTIONS:
Invite children to choose a garden role.
Practice watering, harvesting, and selling vegetables.
Switch roles when children are ready.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Use cooperative play language
Practice caring for living things
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Document social interactions during pretend play.

THURSDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Leaf Rubbing Art
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Children notice leaf shapes and textures through rubbing art.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place leaves under paper and rub with crayons to reveal shapes and veins.
MATERIALS:
Leaves, paper, crayons
SETUP:
Offer a small tray of leaves and paper at the art table.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model gentle rubbing and talk about leaf shapes.
DIRECTIONS:
Choose a leaf and place it under the paper.
Rub the side of a crayon over the paper.
Compare the rubbing with a friend's leaf.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Explore texture and shape
Use fine-motor control with crayons
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice grip and interest in comparing leaf details.

FRIDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Garden Sharing Circle
CATEGORY:
Circle Time
OBJECTIVE:
Children share one garden discovery and practice listening turns.
DESCRIPTION:
Children sit in a short circle to share what they planted, noticed, or pretended in the garden.
MATERIALS:
Talking piece (soft leaf or ball), garden photos
SETUP:
Prepare a small circle space with one talking piece.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Facilitate short turns and echo children's garden words.
DIRECTIONS:
Pass the talking piece around the circle.
Share one garden word or discovery.
Listen while friends take a turn.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Take turns speaking and listening
Use garden vocabulary
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Document language and turn-taking during the circle.
