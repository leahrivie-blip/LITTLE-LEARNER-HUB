WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore healthy habits through movement, nutrition, hygiene, self-care, and wellness activities. Through songs, stories, sensory experiences, dramatic play, and hands-on learning, children will begin to understand how caring for their bodies helps them grow strong, healthy, and happy.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Learn basic healthy habits
- Explore nutritious foods
- Practice self-care routines
- Develop body awareness
- Strengthen gross and fine motor skills
- Build vocabulary related to health and wellness

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss healthy foods, handwashing, sleep routines, and active play at home. Share simple healthy habits children can practice each day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe self-help skills, participation in routines, vocabulary use, body awareness, social interactions, and healthy habit understanding.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, model routines, and offer additional assistance when needed. Encourage participation at each child's developmental level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Healthy Food Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce healthy food choices.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore toy fruits and vegetables while learning about nutritious foods.

MATERIALS:
Toy fruits
Toy vegetables
Discovery basket

SETUP:
Arrange foods in a basket on a table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Name foods and discuss healthy eating.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the foods.
2. Name each item.
3. Discuss colors and shapes.
4. Encourage exploration.
5. Sort foods together.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Food recognition
- Vocabulary development
- Healthy habit awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies foods
- Uses vocabulary
- Participates in discussion
