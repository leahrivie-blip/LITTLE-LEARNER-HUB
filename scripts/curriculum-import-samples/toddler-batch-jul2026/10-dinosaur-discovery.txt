WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become paleontologists as they dig, explore, discover, create, and investigate the fascinating world of dinosaurs. Through sensory experiences, dramatic play, movement activities, science investigations, art projects, and hands-on discovery, children will build curiosity, problem-solving skills, vocabulary, and confidence while exploring one of childhood's most beloved themes.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Introduce basic dinosaur concepts
- Encourage scientific exploration and discovery
- Develop vocabulary and communication skills
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor abilities
- Promote problem-solving and critical thinking
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to read dinosaur books, look for dinosaur toys at home, and discuss concepts such as big, small, footprints, and discovery during play.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving, curiosity, vocabulary use, dramatic play, fine motor skills, and participation in science investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger dinosaurs for easier handling. Offer sensory alternatives and visual supports. Allow children to participate at their own developmental level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Dinosaur Discovery Dig

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage exploration and scientific discovery.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dig through sensory sand to uncover hidden dinosaurs and fossils.

MATERIALS:
Sensory sand
Toy dinosaurs
Paint brushes
Toy fossils

SETUP:
Hide dinosaurs and fossils in the sensory bin.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model digging techniques and encourage investigation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the discovery bin.
2. Demonstrate careful digging.
3. Encourage children to search.
4. Discuss discoveries.
5. Compare findings.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Scientific exploration
- Fine motor development
- Problem-solving

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Investigates independently
- Uses tools purposefully
- Shows curiosity


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Dinosaur Footprint Hunt

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Develop movement and observation skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Children follow dinosaur footprints throughout the classroom while pretending to be explorers.

MATERIALS:
Footprint cards

SETUP:
Place footprints around the room.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide exploration and encourage observation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce dinosaur footprints.
2. Follow the trail together.
3. Search for hidden dinosaurs.
4. Discuss discoveries.
5. Celebrate successful explorers.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Observation skills
- Following directions

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows trail
- Identifies clues
- Participates actively


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Dinosaur Stomp Dance

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage movement and creative expression.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers stomp, roar, and move like dinosaurs while listening to music.

MATERIALS:
Music player

SETUP:
Create open movement space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model dinosaur movements and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Play dinosaur-themed music.
2. Demonstrate stomping.
3. Encourage roaring and movement.
4. Explore fast and slow motions.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Body awareness
- Creative movement
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Participates enthusiastically
- Follows movement cues
- Demonstrates coordination
