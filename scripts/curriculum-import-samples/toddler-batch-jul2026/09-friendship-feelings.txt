WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore friendship, kindness, emotions, and positive social interactions through stories, songs, movement activities, dramatic play, art, and hands-on experiences. Throughout the week, children will learn how to recognize feelings, build relationships, practice kindness, and develop important social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Recognize basic emotions
- Develop friendship and social skills
- Build emotional vocabulary
- Practice kindness and cooperation
- Strengthen communication skills
- Encourage empathy and positive interactions

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about feelings at home and model positive ways to express emotions. Practice sharing, helping, and using feeling words during daily routines.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe emotional awareness, social interactions, communication skills, sharing, cooperation, empathy, and participation in group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual emotion supports and model feeling words frequently. Provide extra support for children learning to identify and express emotions.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feeling Faces Mirror Play

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Recognize and explore emotions through facial expressions.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers use mirrors to explore happy, sad, surprised, and excited expressions.

MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors
Emotion cards

SETUP:
Place mirrors and emotion cards in a small group area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model expressions and introduce feeling vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show an emotion card.
2. Name the feeling.
3. Demonstrate the expression.
4. Encourage children to copy.
5. Discuss when they feel that way.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Emotional awareness
- Vocabulary development
- Self-expression

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies emotions
- Imitates expressions
- Uses feeling words
