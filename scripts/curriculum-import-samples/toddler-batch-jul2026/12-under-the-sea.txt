WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will dive into an exciting underwater adventure as they explore ocean animals, sea plants, shells, water movement, and marine habitats. Through sensory play, art, music, movement, dramatic play, and science exploration, children will develop curiosity, vocabulary, problem-solving skills, and a love for ocean life.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common ocean animals
- Build ocean-related vocabulary
- Explore water and marine environments
- Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
- Encourage creativity and imaginative play
- Develop observation and problem-solving skills

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to visit aquariums, read ocean books, or talk about ocean animals during play and story time. Ask children which sea creatures are their favorites.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe vocabulary development, curiosity, imaginative play, sensory exploration, communication skills, and participation during science activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger ocean animals for easier handling. Use visual supports and real photographs. Allow children to explore materials at their own pace.
