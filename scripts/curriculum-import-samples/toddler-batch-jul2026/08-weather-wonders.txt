WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore different types of weather through hands-on activities, movement games, sensory experiences, songs, books, and outdoor observations. Children will discover sunshine, rain, clouds, wind, and changing weather patterns while building vocabulary, observation skills, curiosity, and creativity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore different weather types
- Build weather-related vocabulary
- Develop observation skills
- Encourage scientific thinking and curiosity
- Strengthen gross and fine motor skills
- Express ideas through art and play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about the weather each day. Look outside together and discuss what children see, hear, and feel.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe weather vocabulary use, curiosity, participation, observation skills, communication, and engagement during science activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real photos and visual supports. Allow children to observe before participating and provide sensory alternatives when needed.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Weather Watchers

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce daily weather observations.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers observe the weather outside and discuss what they notice.

MATERIALS:
Weather cards
Window access

SETUP:
Gather children near a window or outdoor observation area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide observations and introduce weather vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Look outside together.
2. Discuss what children see.
3. Match observations to weather cards.
4. Introduce weather vocabulary.
5. Record the day's weather.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Observation skills
- Vocabulary development
- Scientific thinking

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Notices weather conditions
- Uses weather words
- Participates in discussion


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sunshine Movement Game

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Explore movement through weather-themed play.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers move like sunshine, rain, wind, and clouds.

MATERIALS:
Weather picture cards

SETUP:
Create open movement space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model weather movements and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show a weather card.
2. Demonstrate movement.
3. Encourage imitation.
4. Switch weather types.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Listening skills
- Weather awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows directions
- Participates actively
- Demonstrates coordination

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Windy Day Pinwheels

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Explore the effects of wind.

DESCRIPTION:
Children investigate how wind makes pinwheels spin.

MATERIALS:
Pinwheels

SETUP:

TEACHER_ROLE:

- Scientific thinking
