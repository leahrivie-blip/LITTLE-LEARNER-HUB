WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will learn about the people who help us every day in our homes, schools, and communities. Through dramatic play, books, songs, movement activities, and hands-on exploration, children will discover how helpers such as firefighters, doctors, teachers, farmers, mail carriers, and police officers make our communities safe and strong.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify community helpers and their roles
- Build vocabulary related to community jobs
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
- Strengthen communication skills
- Develop social awareness and appreciation for helpers
- Promote cooperation and problem-solving

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about helpers they see in the community. Discuss jobs family members do and how they help others each day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe dramatic play, vocabulary use, social interactions, communication skills, problem-solving, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and real-life photos. Offer simple job props and encourage participation at each child's developmental level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Community Helper Discovery Basket

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce community helper tools and roles.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore a basket filled with community helper props and discuss who uses them.

MATERIALS:
Toy stethoscope
Toy hammer
Mailbag
Toy firefighter hat

SETUP:
Place items inside a discovery basket.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Introduce each tool and discuss how helpers use them.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Gather children around the basket.
2. Remove one item at a time.
3. Name the helper who uses it.
4. Allow children to explore.
5. Discuss how helpers assist others.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Vocabulary development
- Community awareness
- Exploration skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies helper tools
- Uses new vocabulary
- Participates in discussion


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Doctor's Office Dramatic Play

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage imaginative play and communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Children pretend to be doctors caring for stuffed animals and friends.

MATERIALS:
Toy stethoscope
Bandages
Stuffed animals

SETUP:
Create a simple doctor's office center.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model caring interactions and encourage conversation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the doctor's office.
2. Demonstrate checking a stuffed animal.
3. Encourage role-play.
4. Ask questions about helping others.
5. Rotate roles throughout play.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Dramatic play skills
- Communication development
- Social awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Engages in pretend play
- Uses helper vocabulary
- Interacts with peers


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Firefighter Rescue Relay

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Develop coordination through movement play.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers participate in a rescue-themed obstacle course while pretending to be firefighters.

MATERIALS:
Cones
Stuffed animals
Buckets

SETUP:
Create a simple obstacle course.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage safe movement and teamwork.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Explain the rescue mission.
2. Navigate the obstacle course.
3. Pick up a stuffed animal.
4. Return it to safety.
5. Celebrate successful rescues.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Problem-solving
- Cooperative play

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Completes obstacles
- Follows directions
- Participates enthusiastically

5. Celebrate successful deliveries.

- Social interaction

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Build a Community

CATEGORY:
Fine Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage creativity and problem-solving.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers use blocks to build places where community helpers work.

MATERIALS:
Blocks
Helper picture cards

SETUP:
Create a building center.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage discussion and creative thinking.

2. Discuss workplaces.
3. Build structures with blocks.

- Creativity
- Community awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

- Uses imagination
- Talks about community helpers
