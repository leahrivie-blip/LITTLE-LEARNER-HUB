WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore who they are through activities focused on their names, families, bodies, feelings, preferences, and unique characteristics. Through books, songs, art, mirrors, movement, and discussions, children will build self-awareness, confidence, communication skills, and positive relationships with others.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop self-awareness and confidence
- Learn and recognize their names
- Explore body parts and facial features
- Build emotional awareness and vocabulary
- Strengthen communication skills
- Celebrate similarities and differences among peers

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to share photos and talk about family members. Encourage children to practice saying their names and identifying body parts at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe self-recognition, emotional awareness, communication skills, social interactions, confidence, and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, family photos, and individualized assistance as needed. Encourage participation at each child's comfort level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Me

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Develop self-awareness through observation.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore their reflections and identify facial features using mirrors.

MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors at toddler eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and encourage observation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to look into mirrors.
2. Point out eyes, nose, mouth, and ears.
3. Encourage children to identify features.
4. Make silly faces together.
5. Discuss what they see.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Language development
- Observation skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies facial features
- Engages with reflection
- Uses vocabulary


ACTIVITY_NAME:
My Name Discovery

CATEGORY:
Literacy

OBJECTIVE:
Recognize and become familiar with individual names.

DESCRIPTION:
Children explore name cards and practice identifying their own names.

MATERIALS:
Name cards
Photos

SETUP:
Arrange name cards on a table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Help children recognize their names and connect names with photos.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show each child's name card.
2. Say the name aloud.
3. Match photo to name.
4. Encourage children to find their card.
5. Celebrate successes.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Name recognition
- Language development
- Identity awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Recognizes own name
- Matches photo correctly
- Participates willingly


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Family Photo Sharing

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Strengthen family connections and communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore family photos and talk about important people in their lives.

MATERIALS:
Family photos

SETUP:
Display photos at child level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage discussion and model language.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show family photos.
2. Name family members.
3. Encourage children to point and identify.
4. Talk about families.
5. Celebrate each child's family.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Family awareness
- Communication skills
- Social development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies family members
- Communicates about family
- Engages in discussion
