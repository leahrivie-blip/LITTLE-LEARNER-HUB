WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore life on the farm through hands-on activities, animal sounds, movement games, books, songs, sensory play, and dramatic play experiences. Children will learn about farm animals, their sounds, where they live, and how they help people while building language, social, cognitive, and motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common farm animals
- Explore animal sounds and movements
- Develop language through farm-themed vocabulary
- Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
- Encourage imaginative and dramatic play
- Build social skills through cooperative activities

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about animals during story time, visits to parks, farms, or petting zoos. Practice making animal sounds together and identifying animals in books.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal recognition, participation in songs, language development, imaginative play, social interactions, and gross motor skills.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger animal figures for easier handling. Offer visual supports and picture cards. Allow children to participate at their own comfort level.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Farm Animal Discovery Basket

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce toddlers to common farm animals.

DESCRIPTION:
Children explore a basket filled with farm animal toys while learning animal names and sounds.

MATERIALS:
Farm animal toys
Basket

SETUP:
Place farm animals inside a basket in the discovery area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model animal names, sounds, and simple facts.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Gather children around the basket.
2. Introduce one animal at a time.
3. Name the animal.
4. Demonstrate its sound.
5. Encourage children to repeat.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Animal recognition
- Vocabulary development
- Social participation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies animals
- Attempts animal sounds
- Participates in discussion


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Old MacDonald Sing Along

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop listening and language skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Children sing and move along to Old MacDonald while practicing animal sounds.

MATERIALS:
Music player
Animal picture cards

SETUP:
Create an open group area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead the song and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce farm animal cards.
2. Begin singing.
3. Pause for children to make sounds.
4. Add movement and gestures.
5. Repeat with different animals.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Language development
- Listening skills
- Rhythm and participation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Joins in singing
- Makes animal sounds
- Follows directions


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Farm Animal Walk

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Explore animal movements through active play.

DESCRIPTION:
Children move like different farm animals around the classroom.

MATERIALS:
Animal picture cards

SETUP:
Create open movement space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Demonstrate movements and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show an animal card.
2. Demonstrate movement.
3. Invite children to imitate.
4. Switch animals frequently.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Animal awareness
- Following directions

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Imitates movements
- Participates actively
- Demonstrates coordination
