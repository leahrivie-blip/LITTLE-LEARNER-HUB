WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore music, rhythm, movement, and self-expression through songs, dancing, instruments, action games, and creative movement experiences. Throughout the week, children will strengthen coordination, balance, listening skills, social interaction, and confidence while having fun moving their bodies to music.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop rhythm and listening skills
- Strengthen gross motor coordination
- Encourage self-expression through movement
- Build confidence during group participation
- Support language development through songs
- Promote social interaction and cooperation

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to dance, sing, and move together at home. Playing music during routines helps children build confidence, language, and coordination skills.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, balance, coordination, listening skills, rhythm awareness, social interaction, and confidence during movement activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow children to participate at their own comfort level. Provide seated movement options when needed and simplify directions for younger toddlers.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Shake, Rattle, and Dance

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce rhythm and movement through music.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore musical instruments while dancing and moving to upbeat songs.

MATERIALS:
Shakers
Music player

SETUP:
Create an open movement space.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model instrument use and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Give each child a shaker.
2. Start music.
3. Demonstrate shaking to the beat.
4. Encourage dancing and movement.
5. Celebrate participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Rhythm awareness
- Gross motor development
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Uses instrument appropriately
- Moves to music
- Participates in group activity

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Freeze Dance Fun

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Practice listening and self-control.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dance when music plays and freeze when it stops.

MATERIALS:
Music player

SETUP:
Create a safe open area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Control music and encourage listening.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Start music.
2. Encourage dancing.
3. Stop music unexpectedly.
4. Freeze together.
5. Repeat throughout activity.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Body control
- Following directions

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Stops when music stops
- Follows directions
- Demonstrates body awareness

TUESDAY:

Rhythm Stick Beats

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Explore rhythm and sound patterns.

DESCRIPTION:
Children tap rhythm sticks together while listening to music.

MATERIALS:
Rhythm sticks

TEACHER_ROLE:

2. Demonstrate tapping.
3. Encourage imitation.
4. Explore loud and soft beats.
5. Practice together.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Rhythm development
- Fine motor control
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Taps rhythm sticks
- Imitates patterns
- Participates actively
