WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore colors through hands-on play, movement, art, songs, books, sensory experiences, and everyday discoveries. Throughout the week, children will identify, sort, match, create, and talk about colors while developing language, problem-solving, fine motor skills, and creativity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify and explore a variety of colors
- Build color vocabulary through play and conversation
- Practice sorting and matching skills
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor development
- Encourage creativity through open-ended art experiences
- Promote social interaction and cooperative play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colors during daily routines. Ask children to find colors at home, in the grocery store, outdoors, and during playtime.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color recognition, matching skills, language use, participation in activities, fine motor development, and social interactions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for easier grasping. Offer hand-over-hand support when needed. Focus on exploration and exposure rather than color mastery.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Color Hunt Adventure

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce and explore colors found in the classroom.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers search for colorful objects around the room and place them into matching color baskets.

MATERIALS:
Color baskets
Classroom objects

SETUP:
Place colored baskets around the room.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide children as they search, identify, and sort colors.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce color baskets.
2. Name each color.
3. Invite children to find matching objects.
4. Place objects into baskets.
5. Discuss discoveries together.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Color recognition
- Sorting skills
- Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies colors
- Matches objects
- Participates in group activity

TUESDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Color Sorting Cups

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Practice matching and sorting colors.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers sort pom-poms into matching colored cups.

MATERIALS:
Colored cups
Pom-poms

SETUP:
Arrange cups on a table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model sorting and encourage independent attempts.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the cups.
2. Demonstrate sorting.
3. Invite children to participate.
4. Discuss colors.
5. Celebrate successes.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Matching skills
- Problem-solving
- Color recognition

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Sorts correctly
- Attempts matching
- Stays engaged


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Red, Blue, Yellow Ball Roll

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Develop color recognition during movement play.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers roll and chase colored balls while identifying colors.

MATERIALS:
Colored balls

SETUP:
Create a safe rolling area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Name colors and encourage participation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show colored balls.
2. Roll balls across the floor.
3. Encourage chasing and retrieving.
4. Name colors together.
5. Repeat with different colors.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Gross motor development
- Color vocabulary
- Coordination

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies colors
- Chases balls
- Participates actively
