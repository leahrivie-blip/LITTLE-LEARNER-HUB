WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore the world around them using their five senses: sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste-safe exploration. Through hands-on sensory activities, movement games, songs, books, and discovery experiences, children will learn how their bodies help them understand and interact with their environment.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Explore the five senses through play
- Build vocabulary related to sensory experiences
- Develop observation and comparison skills
- Encourage curiosity and exploration
- Strengthen fine motor and gross motor development
- Promote communication about sensory discoveries

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about what children see, hear, touch, smell, and taste throughout the day. Ask simple questions that help children describe their experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory exploration, vocabulary use, curiosity, problem-solving, fine motor development, and participation in group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide sensory materials that match children's comfort levels. Allow children to observe before participating and offer alternatives for children sensitive to certain textures or sounds.

MONDAY:


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Texture Exploration Station

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore different textures using hands and fingers.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers investigate soft, rough, smooth, and bumpy materials while describing what they feel.

MATERIALS:
Texture squares
Cotton balls
Pinecones
Fabric pieces

SETUP:
Arrange materials on a discovery tray or table.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model touching materials and introduce descriptive vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce each texture.
2. Encourage touching and exploration.
3. Describe how materials feel.
4. Compare different textures.
5. Discuss favorites.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory awareness
- Vocabulary development
- Observation skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Explores materials willingly
- Uses descriptive language
- Compares textures


ACTIVITY_NAME:
What Do You Hear?

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop listening and sound discrimination skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Children listen to different sounds and identify whether they are loud, quiet, fast, or slow.

MATERIALS:
Musical instruments
Sound makers

SETUP:
Prepare instruments in a group area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Create sounds and encourage children to listen carefully.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce instruments.
2. Play one sound at a time.
3. Ask children what they hear.
4. Compare loud and quiet sounds.
5. Allow children to make sounds.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Listening skills
- Auditory awareness
- Sound discrimination

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Responds to sounds
- Identifies differences
- Participates actively


ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Me

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Explore the sense of sight through observation.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers observe themselves in mirrors and identify facial features.

MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors

SETUP:
Place mirrors at toddler eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage observation and introduce vocabulary.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to look in mirrors.
2. Point out eyes, nose, mouth, and ears.
3. Encourage children to identify features.
4. Make silly faces together.
5. Discuss what they see.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual awareness
- Self-recognition
- Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Identifies facial features
- Engages with reflection
- Uses new vocabulary
