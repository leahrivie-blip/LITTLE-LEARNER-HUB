TITLE:
Toddler Building Buddies

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Building Buddies

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Physical Development, Social Emotional, Math, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers build with blocks, soft materials, and recycled boxes while practicing sharing space, stacking, knocking down, and rebuilding with friends.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Toddlers will stack at least two to three items with intention.
Toddlers will practice waiting or trading materials with support.
Toddlers will describe builds using simple size and place words.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Unit blocks, soft blocks, cardboard boxes, masking tape, toy people/animals, baskets, music for clean-up, paper and crayons for build sketches

VOCABULARY:
stack, tall, short, balance, share, rebuild, next to, under, tower

BOOKS:
Changes Changes | Pat Hutchins | Wordless building story
Block City | Robert Louis Stevenson / Daniel Kirk | Celebrate building ideas

SONGS:
London Bridge | Connect to building and rebuilding
Clean Up Song | Use during block reset

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to send a clean recycled box or tube for the Friday build museum, labeled with the child's name if they like.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note stacking persistence, peer negotiation language, and how children recover after a tower falls.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide wider bases for children needing more success. Offer one-on-one coaching for turn-taking. Allow solo build zones for children who need less crowding.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Building Buddies: Soft Block Stack Challenge and related play (introduce the theme through movement, sensory play, and simple language)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support building buddies learning through soft block stack challenge.
Support building buddies learning through carry and deliver blocks.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
stack, tall, short, balance, share, rebuild

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Soft blocks, clear floor space
Soft blocks, tape path

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Physical Development, Social Emotional, Math, Creative Arts

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to building buddies.
Sing "London Bridge" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Soft Block Stack Challenge) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to building buddies: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Soft Block Stack Challenge
Engagement during Carry and Deliver Blocks
Participation and interest in Building Buddies play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide wider bases for children needing more success. Offer one-on-one coaching for turn-taking. Allow solo build zones for children who need less crowding.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soft Block Stack Challenge
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Support building buddies learning through soft block stack challenge.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers stack soft blocks and notice height. Children explore building buddies through soft block stack challenge.
MATERIALS:
Soft blocks, clear floor space
SETUP:
Toddlers stack soft blocks and notice height.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model simple steps, stay close for support, and use short theme vocabulary during soft block stack challenge.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Model stacking two blocks.
2. Invite children to stack their own.
3. Count blocks together when a tower stands.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Practice controlled placement
Explore tall and short
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Soft Block Stack Challenge
Use of building buddies vocabulary or interest
Peer interaction and independence
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
