TITLE:
Toddler Building Buddies

AGE GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Building Buddies

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING DOMAINS:
Physical Development, Social Emotional, Math, Creative Arts

WEEKLY OVERVIEW:
Toddlers build with blocks, soft materials, and recycled boxes while practicing sharing space, stacking, knocking down, and rebuilding with friends.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
Toddlers will stack at least two to three items with intention.
Toddlers will practice waiting or trading materials with support.
Toddlers will describe builds using simple size and place words.

WEEKLY MATERIALS:
Unit blocks, soft blocks, cardboard boxes, masking tape, toy people/animals, baskets, music for clean-up, paper and crayons for build sketches

VOCABULARY:
stack, tall, short, balance, share, rebuild, next to, under, tower

BOOKS:
Changes Changes | Pat Hutchins | Wordless building story
Block City | Robert Louis Stevenson / Daniel Kirk | Celebrate building ideas

SONGS:
London Bridge | Connect to building and rebuilding
Clean Up Song | Use during block reset

FAMILY CONNECTION:
Invite families to send a clean recycled box or tube for the Friday build museum, labeled with the child's name if they like.

OBSERVATION OPPORTUNITIES:
Note stacking persistence, peer negotiation language, and how children recover after a tower falls.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide wider bases for children needing more success. Offer one-on-one coaching for turn-taking. Allow solo build zones for children who need less crowding.

MONDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Soft Block Stack Challenge
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
MATERIALS:
Soft blocks, clear floor space
SETUP:
Toddlers stack soft blocks and notice height.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Model stacking two blocks.
2. Invite children to stack their own.
3. Count blocks together when a tower stands.
LEARNING GOAL:
Practice controlled placement
Explore tall and short

ACTIVITY NAME:
Carry and Deliver Blocks
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
MATERIALS:
Soft blocks, tape path
SETUP:
Toddlers carry blocks along a short path to a build zone.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the path to the build zone.
2. Carry one block at a time.
3. Place it gently in the basket or stack.
LEARNING GOAL:
Coordinate carrying and walking
Follow a simple pathway

TUESDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Block Town Friends
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
MATERIALS:
Blocks, toy figures
SETUP:
Toddlers add toy people and animals to block roads and homes.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Build a simple road or house together.
2. Invite figures to visit the build.
3. Narrate greetings and short stories.
LEARNING GOAL:
Combine building with pretend stories
Practice social language

ACTIVITY NAME:
Tape Stick Construction
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
MATERIALS:
Cardboard boxes, masking tape strips
SETUP:
Toddlers press masking tape pieces onto boxes to connect parts.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Offer short pre-torn tape pieces.
2. Model sticking two box edges.
3. Let children press and smooth tape.
LEARNING GOAL:
Strengthen finger pressure
Explore joining materials

WEDNESDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Knock Down and Rebuild
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
MATERIALS:
Soft blocks
SETUP:
Children intentionally knock down towers and rebuild.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Build a tower together.
2. Invite a gentle knock-down.
3. Rebuild with a new idea.
LEARNING GOAL:
Accept change and try again
Explore cause and effect

ACTIVITY NAME:
Hammer Dance Beat
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
MATERIALS:
Soft mallets or hands, rhythm music
SETUP:
Toddlers tap soft mallets or hands to a building beat.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Tap a slow beat together.
2. Pretend to hammer in time.
3. Freeze when the music stops.
LEARNING GOAL:
Match movement to rhythm
Practice stop-and-start control

THURSDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
People on Top Placement
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
MATERIALS:
Blocks, small figures
SETUP:
Toddlers carefully place figures on block surfaces.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Stack a sturdy base.
2. Model gentle figure placement.
3. Talk about on top and next to.
LEARNING GOAL:
Use precise hand control
Practice position words

FRIDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Build Museum Sketch
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
MATERIALS:
Paper, crayons, finished builds
SETUP:
Toddlers make simple marks about a favorite build before photos or display.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Look at a favorite build together.
2. Invite marks or shapes on paper.
3. Dictate a short label if the child wants.
LEARNING GOAL:
Represent an idea on paper
Reflect on weekly building work

ACTIVITY NAME:
Buddy Block Parade
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
MATERIALS:
Soft blocks, music
SETUP:
Children parade a small build or block to music, then reset the area.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose one block or small build to carry.
2. Parade carefully around the rug.
3. Return materials and sing clean-up.
LEARNING GOAL:
Move carefully with materials
Practice group clean-up routines
