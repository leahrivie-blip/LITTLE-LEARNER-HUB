===TITLE===
Preschool Community Helpers Week

===AGE_GROUP===
Preschool

===THEME===
Community Helpers

===PLAN===
Free

===STATUS===
draft

===LEARNING_DOMAINS===
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Creative Arts, Physical Development

===WEEKLY_OVERVIEW===
Preschoolers explore community helper roles through dramatic play, movement obstacle courses, thank-you writing, and cooperative problem-solving about how helpers keep communities caring and safe.

===OBJECTIVES===
Children will name several community helpers and one way each helps.
Children will use helper tools in pretend scenarios with peers.
Children will practice gratitude language through notes or drawings.

===FAMILY_CONNECTION===
Invite families to share the title of a helper in their family or neighborhood (nurse, driver, cook, firefighter, etc.) for the classroom helper map.

===WEEKLY_MATERIALS===
Helper hats/props, clipboards, paper, crayons, thank-you cards, obstacle course markers, toy phones, first-aid pretend kit, building tools set, music

===VOCABULARY===
helper, community, thank you, safe, map, job, cooperate, problem, solution

===OBSERVATIONS===
Note role vocabulary, cooperative play quality, and how children explain why helpers matter.

===ADAPTATIONS===
Offer picture cards for helper names. Provide dictated thank-you options for emerging writers. Create quieter helper stations for children who need less noise.

===BOOKS===
Whose Hands Are These? | Miranda Paul | Helper jobs and hands
Community Helpers from A to Z | Bobbie Kalman | Browse for role ideas

===SONGS===
People in Your Neighborhood | Name helpers during chorus
Helpful Friends Song | Create verses for classroom helpers

===MONDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Dramatic Play
Title: Helper Office Open
Description: Children choose helper roles and set up a community office.
Materials: Hats, phones, clipboards, badges
Steps:
1. Introduce 3-4 helper roles with props.
2. Children choose a role and badge.
3. Practice greeting community friends.
Learning Goals:
- Explore helper roles through pretend play
- Use greeting and helping language

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Helper March Introductions
Description: Children march and freeze to introduce their helper role.
Materials: Music, helper badges
Steps:
1. March to the beat.
2. Freeze and say your helper name.
3. Tell one way you help.
Learning Goals:
- Build confidence speaking in a group
- Connect movement with identity play

===TUESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Gross Motor
Title: Rescue Path Obstacle
Description: Children move through a safe obstacle path delivering a pretend supply bag.
Materials: Cones/cushions, soft supply bag
Steps:
1. Demonstrate the path: climb, balance, deliver.
2. Carry the bag carefully to the end.
3. High-five the next helper.
Learning Goals:
- Coordinate whole-body movement
- Practice persevering through a sequence

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Clipboard Checklists
Description: Children mark helper checklists with crayons or stickers.
Materials: Clipboards, checklist paper, crayons, stickers
Steps:
1. Look at a simple 3-item checklist.
2. Mark each item after pretend completion.
3. Sign or scribble a name at the bottom.
Learning Goals:
- Practice early writing marks
- Follow a visual sequence

===WEDNESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Community Map Makers
Description: Children draw places helpers work and dictate labels.
Materials: Large paper, crayons, helper cards
Steps:
1. Brainstorm places: clinic, fire station, school.
2. Draw places on a shared map.
3. Add helper cards to each place.
Learning Goals:
- Represent community places
- Connect helpers to locations

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Tool Texture Table
Description: Children explore safe helper-tool textures (brushes, fabric gloves, soft hoses).
Materials: Texture tools, trays
Steps:
1. Feel each tool and describe it.
2. Match tools to helper picture cards.
3. Sort hard/soft or rough/smooth.
Learning Goals:
- Describe textures with detail
- Link tools to helper roles

===THURSDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Dramatic Play
Title: Thank-You Mail Center
Description: Children create thank-you notes for community helpers.
Materials: Cards, crayons, stickers, mailbox prop
Steps:
1. Choose a helper to thank.
2. Draw or dictate a thank-you message.
3. Mail it in the classroom mailbox.
Learning Goals:
- Practice gratitude language
- Communicate appreciation to others

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Badge Making Studio
Description: Children decorate helper badges with controlled coloring and stickers.
Materials: Badge cutouts, crayons, stickers
Steps:
1. Write or stamp a helper title.
2. Decorate the badge edges.
3. Wear or display the badge.
Learning Goals:
- Strengthen hand control
- Take pride in role identity

===FRIDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Gross Motor
Title: Cooperative Helper Relay
Description: Teams move supplies from station to station working together.
Materials: Soft supply items, station markers
Steps:
1. Explain that helpers cooperate.
2. Carry items together across stations.
3. Celebrate the team finish.
Learning Goals:
- Practice cooperation
- Move safely in a group game

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Helper Parade Closing
Description: Class parades helper roles and sings a thank-you verse.
Materials: Music, badges, props
Steps:
1. Line up with badges.
2. Parade and wave to classmates.
3. Sing a thank-you closing verse.
Learning Goals:
- Celebrate community learning
- Close the week with shared joy
