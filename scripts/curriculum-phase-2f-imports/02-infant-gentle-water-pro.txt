===TITLE===
Infant Gentle Water Play

===AGE_GROUP===
Infant

===THEME===
Gentle Water Discovery

===PLAN===
Pro

===STATUS===
draft

===LEARNING_DOMAINS===
Science, Physical Development, Social Emotional, Language & Literacy

===WEEKLY_OVERVIEW===
Infants explore safe water play through supervised splash trays, wet cloths, and calm pouring. Keep water shallow, stay within arm's reach, and dry babies promptly.

===OBJECTIVES===
Infants will notice wet and dry textures with caregiver support.
Infants will practice reaching, patting, and early pouring motions.
Infants will stay regulated during short sensory water moments.

===FAMILY_CONNECTION===
Invite families to describe bath-time preferences (warmth, songs, comfort cues) so classroom water play can feel familiar and safe.

===WEEKLY_MATERIALS===
Shallow sensory trays, warm water, washcloths, soft sponges, cups, towels, waterproof smocks, non-slip mats, soft ducks

===VOCABULARY===
wet, dry, splash, drip, warm, wipe, pour, soft

===OBSERVATIONS===
Watch for enjoyment vs. startle responses, reaching into water, and calming strategies that help each infant stay regulated.

===ADAPTATIONS===
Offer a damp cloth only for babies who dislike open water trays. Support seated infants with rolled towels. Keep sessions very short for sensitive babies.

===MONDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Warm Washcloth Explore
Description: Infants feel warm damp washcloths on hands and arms with caregiver support.
Materials: Warm washcloths, towels
Steps:
1. Offer a warm damp cloth for touching.
2. Gently wipe hands while naming wet and warm.
3. Switch to a dry towel and name dry.
Learning Goals:
- Notice wet and dry contrasts
- Stay calm during sensory caregiving

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Boat Rock Lullaby
Description: Caregiver rocks with infant while singing a soft boat song.
Materials: Calm seating space
Steps:
1. Hold infant securely.
2. Rock gently and sing slowly.
3. Pause if the baby needs stillness.
Learning Goals:
- Connect rhythm with calming movement
- Build trust through predictable care

===TUESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Shallow Splash Tray
Description: Supervised fingertip and hand exploration in a shallow warm water tray.
Materials: Shallow tray, warm water, towels, smocks
Steps:
1. Seat infant safely with caregiver support.
2. Invite hand dips and soft pats on water.
3. Dry promptly and narrate splash words.
Learning Goals:
- Explore water with hands
- Hear simple science words

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Soft Sponge Squeeze
Description: Infants squeeze soft wet sponges with help.
Materials: Soft sponges, tray, towels
Steps:
1. Show a gentle squeeze over the tray.
2. Support the infant's hands as needed.
3. Celebrate drips and squeezes.
Learning Goals:
- Strengthen early grasp
- Notice cause and effect with drips

===WEDNESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Gross Motor
Title: Kick and Splash Toes
Description: Supported infants kick toes in a shallow tray.
Materials: Shallow tray, warm water, towels, non-slip mat
Steps:
1. Support infant in a safe seated or reclined position.
2. Bring toes to the water surface.
3. Mirror kicks and smile with the baby.
Learning Goals:
- Practice leg movement
- Enjoy shared water play

===THURSDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Floating Duck Watch
Description: Infants watch and reach toward soft floating toys.
Materials: Soft ducks, shallow tray, towels
Steps:
1. Float one toy in shallow water.
2. Move it slowly for visual tracking.
3. Allow reaching or batting.
Learning Goals:
- Track moving objects
- Reach toward interesting toys

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Water Word Talk
Description: Caregiver narrates water actions with simple repeated words.
Materials: Washcloth or tray already in use
Steps:
1. Choose two words such as drip and splash.
2. Repeat them during play.
3. Pause for infant vocalizations.
Learning Goals:
- Hear repeated water vocabulary
- Practice early turn-taking sounds

===FRIDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Dramatic Play
Title: Bath Time Care Doll
Description: Caregiver models gentle doll washing with a damp cloth.
Materials: Soft doll, washcloth, towel
Steps:
1. Show wiping the doll gently.
2. Invite the infant to touch the cloth or doll.
3. Dry the doll and wrap in a towel.
Learning Goals:
- Observe caregiving routines
- Explore gentle pretend care

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Dry and Dance Fingers
Description: After drying, wiggle fingers to a short goodbye rhyme.
Materials: Dry towels, calm space
Steps:
1. Dry hands thoroughly.
2. Wiggle fingers to a short rhyme.
3. End with a calm cuddle or smile.
Learning Goals:
- Transition from wet play to dry calm
- Enjoy closing rhythm play
