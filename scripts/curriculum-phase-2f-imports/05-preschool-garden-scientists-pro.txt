TITLE:
Preschool Garden Scientists

AGE GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Garden Scientists

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING DOMAINS:
Science, Math, Language & Literacy, Physical Development

WEEKLY OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers investigate soil, seeds, leaves, and garden helpers through hands-on science play, measurement, and outdoor-style movement indoors or outside.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES:
Children will observe and describe plant or soil changes using science words.
Children will count, compare, and sort garden materials.
Children will cooperate during shared garden investigations.

WEEKLY MATERIALS:
Soil trays, seeds, magnifying glasses, measuring cups, spray bottles, paper, crayons, sorting trays, pretend gardening tools, plant picture cards

VOCABULARY:
seed, soil, sprout, root, observe, measure, compare, moisture, garden

BOOKS:
The Tiny Seed | Eric Carle | Life cycle discussion
Up in the Garden and Down in the Dirt | Kate Messner | Garden layers and helpers

SONGS:
Oats Peas Beans and Barley Grow | Planting circle song
The Farmer Plants the Seeds | Action song for planting sequence

FAMILY CONNECTION:
Ask families to send a leaf, seed packet photo, or garden story from home for the Friday scientist share circle.

OBSERVATION OPPORTUNITIES:
Listen for science vocabulary, note careful tool use, and document how children explain predictions vs. observations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide pre-moistened soil for children avoiding dry dust. Offer visual sequence cards for multi-step planting. Pair peers for shared tool turns.

MONDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Soil Scientists Tray
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
MATERIALS:
Soil trays, scoops, magnifying glasses, smocks
SETUP:
Children explore moist soil with hands and tools, describing texture.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to feel and scoop soil.
2. Ask what they notice: wet, dry, bumpy.
3. Record one group observation on a chart.
LEARNING GOAL:
Observe and describe soil properties
Use science talk with peers

ACTIVITY NAME:
Seed Sort Lab
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
MATERIALS:
Mixed seeds, sorting trays, tweezers optional
SETUP:
Children sort seeds by size or type onto trays.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Show two seed types and name them.
2. Sort seeds into trays.
3. Count how many are in each tray.
LEARNING GOAL:
Sort by attribute
Strengthen fine-motor control

TUESDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Garden Center Shop
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
MATERIALS:
Seed packets, pots, play money, baskets
SETUP:
Children pretend to sell seeds, pots, and tools in a garden shop.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Set roles: gardener and shopkeeper.
2. Request and gather garden supplies.
3. Use polite customer language.
LEARNING GOAL:
Practice cooperative pretend roles
Use garden vocabulary in context

ACTIVITY NAME:
Watering Can Walk
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
MATERIALS:
Lightweight watering cans, small water amount, path markers
SETUP:
Children carry lightweight watering cans along a path without spilling.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Fill cans with a small amount of water.
2. Walk the path carefully.
3. Water a pretend plant bed at the end.
LEARNING GOAL:
Coordinate careful carrying
Control body movement with a tool

WEDNESDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Sprout Prediction Journal
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
MATERIALS:
Paper, crayons, seed picture cards
SETUP:
Children draw predictions about what seeds need to grow.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Look at seed cards together.
2. Ask what seeds might need.
3. Draw or dictate a prediction.
LEARNING GOAL:
Make a simple scientific prediction
Represent ideas through drawing

ACTIVITY NAME:
Seed to Sprout Dance
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
MATERIALS:
Music speaker
SETUP:
Children curl like seeds then slowly rise like sprouts to music.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Curl small on the floor like a seed.
2. Add rain and sun motions.
3. Rise slowly into a sprout stretch.
LEARNING GOAL:
Represent a life cycle through movement
Follow multi-step movement cues

THURSDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Mist and Moisture Test
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
MATERIALS:
Spray bottles, soil cups, observation chart
SETUP:
Children mist soil and compare damp vs. dry samples.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Feel a dry soil cup.
2. Mist a second cup lightly.
3. Compare and describe the difference.
LEARNING GOAL:
Compare moisture levels
Practice careful tool use

ACTIVITY NAME:
Leaf Rubbing Collectors
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
MATERIALS:
Leaves, paper, crayons
SETUP:
Children make crayon rubbings of leaves and compare shapes.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Place a leaf under paper.
2. Rub with the side of a crayon.
3. Compare leaf shapes and sizes.
LEARNING GOAL:
Notice natural details
Strengthen hand control

FRIDAY:
ACTIVITY NAME:
Scientist Share Circle
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
MATERIALS:
Family contributions, drawings, talking piece
SETUP:
Children share a garden item or drawing and one observation.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit in a circle with a talking piece.
2. Each child shares one observation.
3. Group thanks each scientist.
LEARNING GOAL:
Communicate an observation to peers
Practice listening and turn-taking

ACTIVITY NAME:
Garden Relay Reset
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
MATERIALS:
Tools, labeled baskets
SETUP:
Teams carry tools back to labeled spots to close the week.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Assign a tool to return.
2. Walk carefully to the matching label.
3. Check the garden center is ready.
LEARNING GOAL:
Follow clean-up systems
Move safely with classroom tools
