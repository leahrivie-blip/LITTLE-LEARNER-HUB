TITLE:
Preschool Garden Scientists

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Garden Scientists

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science, Math, Language & Literacy, Physical Development

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers investigate soil, seeds, leaves, and garden helpers through hands-on science play, measurement, and outdoor-style movement indoors or outside.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Children will observe and describe plant or soil changes using science words.
Children will count, compare, and sort garden materials.
Children will cooperate during shared garden investigations.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Soil trays, seeds, magnifying glasses, measuring cups, spray bottles, paper, crayons, sorting trays, pretend gardening tools, plant picture cards

VOCABULARY:
seed, soil, sprout, root, observe, measure, compare, moisture, garden

BOOKS:
The Tiny Seed | Eric Carle | Life cycle discussion
Up in the Garden and Down in the Dirt | Kate Messner | Garden layers and helpers

SONGS:
Oats Peas Beans and Barley Grow | Planting circle song
The Farmer Plants the Seeds | Action song for planting sequence

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to send a leaf, seed packet photo, or garden story from home for the Friday scientist share circle.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Listen for science vocabulary, note careful tool use, and document how children explain predictions vs. observations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide pre-moistened soil for children avoiding dry dust. Offer visual sequence cards for multi-step planting. Pair peers for shared tool turns.

CIRCLE_TIME:

Read or revisit The Tiny Seed by Eric Carle and ask 1–2 open-ended theme questions before centers.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor exploration connected to garden scientists — movement games, collaborative challenges, or nature observation linked to today's indoor activities (Soil Scientists Tray / Seed Sort Lab).

Engagement during Soil Scientists Tray

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soil Scientists Tray
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support garden scientists learning through soil scientists tray.
DESCRIPTION:
Children explore moist soil with hands and tools, describing texture. Children explore garden scientists through soil scientists tray.
MATERIALS:
Soil trays, scoops, magnifying glasses, smocks
SETUP:
Children explore moist soil with hands and tools, describing texture.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Facilitate soil scientists tray, ask open-ended questions, and connect discoveries back to garden scientists.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to feel and scoop soil.
2. Ask what they notice: wet, dry, bumpy.
3. Record one group observation on a chart.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Observe and describe soil properties
Use science talk with peers
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Soil Scientists Tray
Use of garden scientists vocabulary or interest
Peer interaction and independence
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual steps, partner support, or a simplified materials set. Extend with an open-ended challenge for children ready for more.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise tools and materials, review allergy-safe consumables, and keep pathways clear during active or outdoor play.
