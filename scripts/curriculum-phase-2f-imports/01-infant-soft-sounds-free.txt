TITLE:
Infant Soft Sounds & Faces

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–6 Months

THEME:
Soft Sounds and Familiar Faces

PLAN:
Free

STATUS:
draft

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This gentle week helps infants notice soft sounds, familiar faces, and calm one-to-one play. Keep sessions short, follow each baby's cues, and stop before overstimulation.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Infants will calm to familiar voices and soft music.
Infants will look toward faces and simple picture cards.
Infants will practice reaching, grasping, and tummy-time strength.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Soft scarves, mirror, face picture cards, rattles, soft books, tummy-time mat, basket of fabric squares, calm playlist speaker at low volume

VOCABULARY:
hello, soft, face, look, gentle, hush, reach, smile

BOOKS:
Global Babies | The Global Fund for Children | Short face-looking moments
Look Look! | Peter Linenthal | High-contrast visual play

SONGS:
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star | Soft hum or quiet voice
If You're Happy and You Know It | Gentle clap version for older infants

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to share one soft song or lullaby they use at home. Play a short clip or hum it during arrival so babies hear familiar sounds.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note eye contact during face play, calming responses to soft sound, and reaching or rolling during tummy time.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer side-lying support for babies who tire on tummy time. Use high-contrast cards for visual interest. Keep lighting soft and reduce background noise.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Soft Sounds and Familiar Faces: Soft Hello Song and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support soft sounds and familiar faces learning through soft hello song.
Support soft sounds and familiar faces learning through face mirror moments.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
hello, soft, face, look, gentle, hush

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Calm space, soft scarf optional
Unbreakable mirror, soft mat

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to soft sounds and familiar faces.
End with a brief look at Global Babies by The Global Fund for Children or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate soft sounds and familiar faces sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Soft Hello Song
Engagement during Face Mirror Moments
Participation and interest in Soft Sounds and Familiar Faces play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Offer side-lying support for babies who tire on tummy time. Use high-contrast cards for visual interest. Keep lighting soft and reduce background noise.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Soft Hello Song
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Support soft sounds and familiar faces learning through soft hello song.
DESCRIPTION:
Caregiver greets each infant with a calm hello song and soft eye contact. Children explore soft sounds and familiar faces through soft hello song.
MATERIALS:
Calm space, soft scarf optional
SETUP:
Caregiver greets each infant with a calm hello song and soft eye contact.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay within arm's reach, follow the infant's cues, and narrate soft sounds and familiar faces softly during soft hello song.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit close and make gentle eye contact.
2. Sing a short hello using the baby's name.
3. Pause and watch for smiles, looks, or calm body cues.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Connect with a familiar caregiver voice
Practice calm social attention
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Soft Hello Song
Use of soft sounds and familiar faces vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–5 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
