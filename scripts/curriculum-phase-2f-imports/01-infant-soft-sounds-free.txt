===TITLE===
Infant Soft Sounds & Faces

===AGE_GROUP===
Infant

===THEME===
Soft Sounds and Familiar Faces

===PLAN===
Free

===STATUS===
draft

===LEARNING_DOMAINS===
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development

===WEEKLY_OVERVIEW===
This gentle week helps infants notice soft sounds, familiar faces, and calm one-to-one play. Keep sessions short, follow each baby's cues, and stop before overstimulation.

===OBJECTIVES===
Infants will calm to familiar voices and soft music.
Infants will look toward faces and simple picture cards.
Infants will practice reaching, grasping, and tummy-time strength.

===FAMILY_CONNECTION===
Ask families to share one soft song or lullaby they use at home. Play a short clip or hum it during arrival so babies hear familiar sounds.

===WEEKLY_MATERIALS===
Soft scarves, mirror, face picture cards, rattles, soft books, tummy-time mat, basket of fabric squares, calm playlist speaker at low volume

===VOCABULARY===
hello, soft, face, look, gentle, hush, reach, smile

===OBSERVATIONS===
Note eye contact during face play, calming responses to soft sound, and reaching or rolling during tummy time.

===ADAPTATIONS===
Offer side-lying support for babies who tire on tummy time. Use high-contrast cards for visual interest. Keep lighting soft and reduce background noise.

===BOOKS===
Global Babies | The Global Fund for Children | Short face-looking moments
Look Look! | Peter Linenthal | High-contrast visual play

===SONGS===
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star | Soft hum or quiet voice
If You're Happy and You Know It | Gentle clap version for older infants

===MONDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Soft Hello Song
Description: Caregiver greets each infant with a calm hello song and soft eye contact.
Materials: Calm space, soft scarf optional
Steps:
1. Sit close and make gentle eye contact.
2. Sing a short hello using the baby's name.
3. Pause and watch for smiles, looks, or calm body cues.
Learning Goals:
- Connect with a familiar caregiver voice
- Practice calm social attention

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Face Mirror Moments
Description: Infants look at their reflection and caregiver faces in a safe mirror.
Materials: Unbreakable mirror, soft mat
Steps:
1. Place the mirror at infant eye level on the mat.
2. Sit beside the baby and point to faces gently.
3. Name features slowly: eyes, nose, smile.
Learning Goals:
- Notice faces and reflections
- Hear simple face vocabulary

===TUESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Soft Scarf Peek
Description: Caregiver slowly lifts and lowers a soft scarf for gentle peekaboo.
Materials: Soft scarves
Steps:
1. Hold the scarf where the infant can see it.
2. Lift and lower slowly while saying peekaboo softly.
3. Stop if the baby turns away or fusses.
Learning Goals:
- Track slow visual movement
- Enjoy predictable social games

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Reach for Rattle
Description: Infants reach toward a soft rattle held within safe reach.
Materials: Soft rattles, mat
Steps:
1. Place baby in a supported position.
2. Hold a rattle just within reach.
3. Celebrate any reach, bat, or grasp.
Learning Goals:
- Practice reaching and grasping
- Connect sound with movement

===WEDNESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Gross Motor
Title: Supported Tummy Time Songs
Description: Short tummy-time sessions paired with soft singing.
Materials: Tummy-time mat, soft book
Steps:
1. Place infant on tummy with caregiver nearby.
2. Sing softly and show a high-contrast page.
3. End after a short successful stretch.
Learning Goals:
- Build neck and upper-body strength
- Stay engaged during brief tummy time

===THURSDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Soft Book Cuddle
Description: One-to-one board book sharing with pauses for looking.
Materials: Soft or board books
Steps:
1. Hold the infant comfortably.
2. Show one page at a time and name one picture.
3. Follow the baby's gaze and pause often.
Learning Goals:
- Attend to pictures and voice
- Hear warm book language

===FRIDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Dramatic Play
Title: Caregiving Pretend Basket
Description: Older infants explore soft caregiving props with caregiver modeling.
Materials: Soft doll, small blanket, basket
Steps:
1. Offer one soft doll and blanket.
2. Model gentle patting and covering.
3. Narrate kind caregiving words.
Learning Goals:
- Explore gentle caregiving actions
- Hear caring language

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Calm Goodbye Sway
Description: Soft swaying and a short goodbye song to close the week.
Materials: Calm playlist optional
Steps:
1. Hold or sit with each infant.
2. Sway gently while humming goodbye.
3. End with a quiet smile and soft voice.
Learning Goals:
- Experience calm transitions
- Recognize a familiar closing routine
