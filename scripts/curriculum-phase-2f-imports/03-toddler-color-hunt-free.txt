===TITLE===
Toddler Color Hunt Friends

===AGE_GROUP===
Toddler

===THEME===
Color Hunt Friends

===PLAN===
Free

===STATUS===
draft

===LEARNING_DOMAINS===
Math, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Social Emotional

===WEEKLY_OVERVIEW===
Toddlers hunt for colors around the room, sort soft objects, and practice naming favorite colors through short active games and calm art.

===OBJECTIVES===
Toddlers will find and name at least two familiar colors.
Toddlers will sort objects by color with support.
Toddlers will practice turn-taking during color games.

===FAMILY_CONNECTION===
Ask families to notice one color on the way to care and tell you about it at drop-off so you can celebrate that color during the day.

===WEEKLY_MATERIALS===
Colored scarves, sorting bowls, pom-poms, color cards, tape spots on floor, crayons, paper, baskets, music speaker

===VOCABULARY===
red, blue, yellow, green, find, same, sort, match, look

===OBSERVATIONS===
Note which colors children recognize independently, how they handle sorting, and peer sharing during hunts.

===ADAPTATIONS===
Offer two-choice color prompts for emerging talkers. Use larger objects for easier grasp. Pair a visual card with every spoken color name.

===BOOKS===
Brown Bear Brown Bear What Do You See? | Bill Martin Jr. / Eric Carle | Color naming chorus
Color Dance | Ann Jonas | Movement and color connection

===SONGS===
Colors Colors Can You Find | Call out colors around the room
If You're Wearing Red | Stand or wave when color is called

===MONDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Gross Motor
Title: Color Spot Hop
Description: Toddlers hop or step onto colored floor spots when the color is named.
Materials: Colored tape or spot markers, open space
Steps:
1. Show two color spots and name them.
2. Call a color and model stepping onto it.
3. Invite toddlers to find the matching spot.
Learning Goals:
- Match spoken color to a location
- Practice balance and body control

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Soft Color Scarf Toss
Description: Toddlers toss and catch soft colored scarves.
Materials: Colored scarves
Steps:
1. Offer one scarf at a time.
2. Model gentle toss and catch.
3. Name the scarf color together.
Learning Goals:
- Explore movement with soft materials
- Hear and repeat color names

===TUESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Pom-Pom Color Sort
Description: Toddlers place pom-poms into matching color bowls.
Materials: Pom-poms, sorting bowls, color labels
Steps:
1. Set out two or three color bowls.
2. Demonstrate one match.
3. Invite toddlers to sort at their own pace.
Learning Goals:
- Sort by a single attribute
- Strengthen pincer grasp

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Color Freeze Dance
Description: Children dance and freeze when a color card is shown.
Materials: Music speaker, color cards
Steps:
1. Play music and invite dancing.
2. Pause music and show a color card.
3. Children freeze and point to that color nearby.
Learning Goals:
- Practice stop-and-go control
- Connect color cards to the environment

===WEDNESDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Dramatic Play
Title: Color Market Basket
Description: Toddlers shop for pretend foods matching a color card.
Materials: Baskets, toy foods, color cards
Steps:
1. Give each child a color card.
2. Model finding one matching food.
3. Place finds in the basket and name them.
Learning Goals:
- Use color cues in pretend play
- Practice simple choice-making

===THURSDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Open-Ended Exploration
Title: Color Hunt Walk
Description: A short indoor hunt for objects matching today's focus colors.
Materials: Color cards, collection basket
Steps:
1. Show the focus color card.
2. Walk slowly and look together.
3. Celebrate each find with the color word.
Learning Goals:
- Search with a purpose
- Build color vocabulary in context

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Fine Motor
Title: Crayon Color Marks
Description: Toddlers make marks with crayons matching the day's colors.
Materials: Paper, crayons
Steps:
1. Offer two crayon colors.
2. Name each color as children choose.
3. Talk about the marks they make.
Learning Goals:
- Strengthen hand control
- Connect color names to marks

===FRIDAY===
---ACTIVITY---
Category: Music & Movement
Title: Parade of Colors
Description: Toddlers parade with scarves while singing a color song.
Materials: Scarves, music
Steps:
1. Choose a scarf and name its color.
2. March in a short parade path.
3. Wave scarves on the chorus.
Learning Goals:
- Coordinate movement with music
- Celebrate weekly color learning

---ACTIVITY---
Category: Sensory Play
Title: Color Bag Feel
Description: Toddlers feel soft colored items inside a discovery bag.
Materials: Cloth bag, soft colored objects
Steps:
1. Invite a child to reach in and feel one item.
2. Pull it out and name the color.
3. Place it on a matching color mat.
Learning Goals:
- Use touch and sight together
- Practice matching after discovery
