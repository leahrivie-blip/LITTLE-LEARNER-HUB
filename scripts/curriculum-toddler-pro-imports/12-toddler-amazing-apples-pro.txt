WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week, toddlers will explore the wonderful world of apples through hands-on investigations, sensory experiences, movement, music, dramatic play, art, and early science activities. Children will discover that apples come in different colors, sizes, and tastes while learning where apples grow and how they become healthy foods we can enjoy. Throughout the week, they will strengthen language skills, early math concepts, fine and gross motor development, and social-emotional skills by working together during engaging apple-themed experiences.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify apples as a healthy fruit.
Recognize red, green, and yellow apples.
Explore apples using the five senses.
Compare apples by size, color, and texture.
Practice counting, sorting, and matching apples.
Strengthen fine motor skills through art and sensory play.
Build vocabulary related to apples and orchards.
Encourage curiosity through simple science investigations.
Participate in cooperative play and group discussions.
Develop confidence while exploring new foods and experiences.

Explore the inside of an apple.

DESCRIPTION:
Children observe an apple cut in half to discover seeds, the core, stem, and flesh while comparing different apples.

MATERIALS:
Apples
Apple halves
Magnifying glasses
Observation tray

SETUP:
Cut apples before the activity and place them on trays.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Look inside the apple.
2. Find the seeds.
3. Count the seeds together.
4. Feel the inside of the apple.
5. Compare different apples.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage children to ask questions and make observations.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Scientific observation
Counting
Vocabulary
Curiosity

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe curiosity, counting skills, and descriptive language.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger visuals if children have difficulty seeing the seeds.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Closely supervise handling of apple pieces and remind children not to place seeds in their mouths.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
My Apple Tree
CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Create an apple tree while reviewing the week's learning.

DESCRIPTION:
Children build an apple tree using handprints, fingerprints, or paper pieces to represent leaves and apples.

MATERIALS:
Brown construction paper
Green paint
Red pom-poms or fingerprints
Glue
Paper

SETUP:
Prepare tree trunks before children arrive.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Glue the tree trunk.
2. Add green leaves.
3. Add red apples.
4. Count the apples on your tree.
5. Share your artwork with the class.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage children to explain how apples grow on trees.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Creativity
Fine motor development
Counting
Science review

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe creativity, counting, and vocabulary.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide pre-cut tree pieces or sponge painting if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable, non-toxic materials.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Harvest Celebration
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate the week's learning through music and movement.

DESCRIPTION:
Children pretend to be apple trees growing from tiny seeds before becoming tall trees filled with apples ready to harvest.

MATERIALS:
Music player
Plastic apples
Baskets
Scarves

SETUP:
Scatter apples around the room and provide baskets.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Curl into a tiny seed.
2. Slowly grow into an apple tree.
3. Stretch your branches high.
4. Pick pretend apples.
5. Fill the baskets while dancing to music.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead movement while reviewing everything learned about apples.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Listening skills
Imagination
Science review

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe coordination, participation, and understanding of the apple life cycle.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated movements or teacher assistance when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain open space for movement and remind children to walk while carrying apples.
