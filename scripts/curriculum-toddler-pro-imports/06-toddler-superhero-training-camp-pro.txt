TITLE:

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Creative Arts, Science, Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become everyday superheroes as they explore kindness, helping others, courage, problem-solving, teamwork, and healthy habits through imaginative play. Throughout the week children will complete superhero missions, obstacle courses, rescue challenges, and creative activities while building confidence and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Practice kindness and helping behaviors.
Develop confidence and self-expression.
Strengthen gross and fine motor skills.
Encourage teamwork and cooperation.
Build problem-solving abilities.
Expand vocabulary related to emotions and community.
Engage in imaginative dramatic play.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Superhero Training Camp: Design Your Superhero Identity and related play (introduce the theme through movement, sensory play, and simple language)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Practice dramatic play skills during Design Your Superhero Identity.
Practice gross motor skills during Superhero Training Obstacle Course.
Practice fine motor skills during Hero Mask Decorating.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Superhero, Brave, Kindness, Rescue, Mission, Teamwork

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories
obstacle course materials
Cones or mats
Open floor space
Soft obstacles
masks
Play dough or crayons
Small manipulatives
Paper
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Creative Arts

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to superhero training camp.
Sing "Superhero Action Song" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Design Your Superhero Identity) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to superhero training camp: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
Participation and interest in Superhero Training Camp play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide adaptive movement options, visual supports, alternative costume choices, and individualized participation opportunities.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Design Your Superhero Identity
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Practice dramatic play skills during Design Your Superhero Identity.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Design Your Superhero Identity during our Superhero Training Camp week through dramatic play.
MATERIALS:
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Design Your Superhero Identity.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Join play briefly as a co-player, then step back so toddlers lead the story.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Set up a pretend-play area for Design Your Superhero Identity with a few clear props.
2. Model a short role (helper, keeper, captain) and invite children in.
3. Use simple language to support caregiving, teamwork, and problem-solving.
4. Follow the children's storyline and keep roles flexible.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Design Your Superhero Identity through toddler-friendly dramatic play.
Use theme vocabulary related to Superhero Training Camp.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
