WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become everyday superheroes as they explore kindness, helping others, courage, problem-solving, teamwork, and healthy habits through imaginative play. Throughout the week children will complete superhero missions, obstacle courses, rescue challenges, and creative activities while building confidence and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Practice kindness and helping behaviors.
Develop confidence and self-expression.
Strengthen gross and fine motor skills.
Encourage teamwork and cooperation.
Build problem-solving abilities.
Expand vocabulary related to emotions and community.
Engage in imaginative dramatic play.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about ways children help others at home. Invite children to share acts of kindness they complete throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe confidence, social interactions, problem-solving skills, cooperative play, emotional regulation, and participation in physical activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide adaptive movement options, visual supports, alternative costume choices, and individualized participation opportunities.

MONDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Design Your Superhero Identity

CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

OBJECTIVE:
Practice dramatic play skills during Design Your Superhero Identity.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Design Your Superhero Identity during our Superhero Training Camp week through dramatic play.

MATERIALS:
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories

SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Design Your Superhero Identity.

DIRECTIONS:
Set up a pretend-play area for Design Your Superhero Identity with a few clear props.
Model a short role (helper, keeper, captain) and invite children in.
Use simple language to support caregiving, teamwork, and problem-solving.
Follow the children's storyline and keep roles flexible.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Join play briefly as a co-player, then step back so toddlers lead the story.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Design Your Superhero Identity through toddler-friendly dramatic play.
Use theme vocabulary related to Superhero Training Camp.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.

SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Superhero Discovery Station.
