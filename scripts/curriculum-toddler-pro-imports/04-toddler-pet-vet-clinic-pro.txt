WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become caring veterinarians as they learn about pets, animal care, kindness, and responsibility. Children will engage in dramatic play, hands-on investigations, movement activities, and creative experiences while exploring what pets need to stay healthy and happy.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn about common household pets.
Practice caring and nurturing behaviors.
Develop empathy and social-emotional skills.
Build vocabulary related to animals and pet care.
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills.
Encourage imaginative play and problem-solving.
Explore basic animal needs and routines.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to share photos of pets or discuss animals children have interacted with. Encourage children to talk about how animals are cared for at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe nurturing behaviors, communication skills, empathy, dramatic play development, vocabulary growth, and fine motor skills.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer sensory-friendly materials, larger dramatic play tools, visual supports, and flexible participation opportunities.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Meet the Pets

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Meet the Pets.

DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Meet the Pets during our Pet Vet Clinic week through open-ended exploration.

MATERIALS:
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards

SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Meet the Pets.

DIRECTIONS:
Invite toddlers to explore Meet the Pets with hands-on materials related to Pet Vet Clinic.
Stay nearby and narrate what children notice using simple theme words.
Offer choices and follow each child's interest for a few minutes at a time.
Encourage pointing, naming, and short conversations about discoveries.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Observe, narrate discoveries, and introduce theme vocabulary without directing every action.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Meet the Pets through toddler-friendly open-ended exploration.
Use theme vocabulary related to Pet Vet Clinic.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
