LEARNING_DOMAINS:

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become little nature explorers as they investigate bugs, butterflies, caterpillars, and other tiny creatures. Through sensory play, movement, art, dramatic play, and hands-on discovery, children will develop curiosity about the natural world while strengthening observation, language, and motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore common insects and bugs.
Observe similarities and differences between insects.
Learn about butterfly life cycles.
Strengthen observation and discovery skills.
Develop fine and gross motor coordination.
Build nature-related vocabulary.
Encourage curiosity and respect for living things.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to take nature walks and look for bugs, butterflies, and insects outdoors. Invite children to share their discoveries with classmates.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe curiosity, language development, fine motor skills, nature exploration, scientific thinking, and participation in group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger bug models, visual supports, sensory alternatives, and opportunities for children to participate at their own comfort level.

DAILY_THEME:

insect books

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to bugs & butterflies.
Sing "The Ants Go Marching" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Bug Discovery Table) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to bugs & butterflies: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
Participation and interest in Bugs & Butterflies play

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bug Discovery Table
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Bug Discovery Table.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Bug Discovery Table during our Bugs & Butterflies week through open-ended exploration.
MATERIALS:
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Bug Discovery Table.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Observe, narrate discoveries, and introduce theme vocabulary without directing every action.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite toddlers to explore Bug Discovery Table with hands-on materials related to Bugs & Butterflies.
2. Stay nearby and narrate what children notice using simple theme words.
3. Offer choices and follow each child's interest for a few minutes at a time.
4. Encourage pointing, naming, and short conversations about discoveries.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Bug Discovery Table through toddler-friendly open-ended exploration.
Use theme vocabulary related to Bugs & Butterflies.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
