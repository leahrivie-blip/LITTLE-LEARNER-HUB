WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week, toddlers will discover how apples become delicious foods we enjoy every day. Children will explore washing, cutting, mixing, cooking, tasting, and preparing apples through safe, hands-on experiences. They will learn that apples can be used to make applesauce, apple juice, apple pie, muffins, and other healthy snacks while strengthening early math, science, language, fine motor, and social-emotional skills through engaging kitchen-themed activities.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Discover different foods made from apples.
Practice safe kitchen routines.
Explore healthy eating habits.
Compare raw and cooked apples.
Develop early measuring and counting skills.
Build vocabulary related to cooking and kitchens.
Strengthen fine motor skills through food preparation activities.
Encourage teamwork while preparing pretend recipes.
Explore changes in texture, smell, and taste.
Build confidence trying healthy foods.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to prepare a simple apple recipe together at home, such as applesauce, apple slices with cinnamon, or baked apples. Encourage children to help wash apples, stir ingredients, or measure simple items with adult supervision. Ask families to share their favorite apple recipe with the class.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Demonstrates healthy eating awareness.
Uses kitchen vocabulary during play.
Participates in pretend cooking activities.
Explores textures, smells, and tastes with curiosity.
Counts and measures during kitchen experiences.
Demonstrates fine motor control while stirring and pouring.
Participates cooperatively during group cooking activities.
Shows confidence trying new foods.

ADAPTATIONS:
Check for food allergies before all tasting activities.
Offer adaptive kitchen tools with larger handles.
Use picture recipe cards for visual learners.
Provide one-on-one assistance during measuring activities.
Modify food textures for children with sensory sensitivities.
Encourage participation at each child's developmental level.
Offer alternative foods when dietary restrictions are present.
Allow extra time for exploration and hands-on learning.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Recipe Review
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Review the steps for preparing apple recipes.

DESCRIPTION:
Children use picture cards to sequence how apples become applesauce, apple juice, and apple pie.

MATERIALS:
Recipe picture cards
Apple foods
Sequencing cards
Pocket chart

SETUP:
Mix the sequencing cards before beginning.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Find the apple.
2. Find the recipe steps.
3. Put the pictures in order.
4. Tell the story together.
5. Review each recipe as a class.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage children to explain each cooking step using complete sentences whenever possible.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Sequencing
Vocabulary development
Science review
Communication

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's understanding of simple recipes and sequencing.

ADAPTATIONS:
Reduce the number of sequencing cards if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use large laminated cards and supervise closely.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
My Favorite Apple Recipe Book
CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Create a class cookbook page featuring each child's favorite apple food.

DESCRIPTION:
Children draw their favorite apple recipe and dictate a sentence about why they like it. Teacher combines the pages into a classroom cookbook.

MATERIALS:
Drawing paper
Crayons
Markers
Apple stickers
Glue
Class binder

SETUP:
Prepare one page for each child with space for a drawing and teacher dictation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Think about your favorite apple food.
2. Draw your favorite recipe.
3. Decorate with apple stickers.
4. Tell the teacher about your picture.
5. Add your page to the class cookbook.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Write each child's words exactly as spoken and encourage conversation about healthy foods.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Fine motor development
Creativity
Language development
Self-expression

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe drawing skills, vocabulary, and recall of weekly learning.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger paper, adaptive crayons, or picture choices if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use only non-toxic art materials.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Kitchen Measuring Relay
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Practice measuring and counting while working as a team.

DESCRIPTION:
Children take turns measuring pretend ingredients and adding them to a large mixing bowl to "cook" together.

MATERIALS:
Measuring cups
Measuring spoons
Rice or pom-poms
Large bowl
Number cards

SETUP:
Arrange measuring stations around the room.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a measuring cup.
2. Scoop the ingredients.
3. Pour into the bowl.
4. Count your scoops aloud.
5. Cheer for your teammates when finished.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage counting, measuring vocabulary, and teamwork.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Counting
Measuring
One-to-one correspondence
Cooperative learning

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe measuring skills, counting accuracy, and teamwork.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger measuring tools or provide teacher assistance when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep materials contained and immediately clean spills.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Little Apple Chef Parade
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate the week's learning through music, movement, and imaginative play.

DESCRIPTION:
Children wear chef hats and aprons while marching around the classroom carrying pretend apple recipes, mixing bowls, and wooden spoons. They stop at different "kitchens" to pretend to cook before finishing with a celebration dance.

MATERIALS:
Chef hats
Aprons
Wooden spoons
Mixing bowls
Music player
Scarves

SETUP:
Create a parade route with several pretend kitchen stations.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Put on your chef hat.
2. Carry your cooking supplies.
3. March to each kitchen station.
4. Pretend to cook your favorite apple recipe.
5. Finish with a celebration dance and group cheer.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead the parade while reviewing cooking vocabulary and celebrating each child's accomplishments.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Confidence
Listening skills
Social-emotional development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, coordination, confidence, and enthusiasm.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated participation or simplified movements when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain clear walking paths and encourage safe movement throughout the celebration.
