WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Classroom Helpers introduces toddlers to simple classroom responsibilities while building confidence, independence, and teamwork. Throughout the week, children will learn that everyone has an important role in keeping the classroom safe, clean, and welcoming. Through hands-on activities, dramatic play, songs, and cooperative experiences, children will practice helping others, caring for classroom materials, following routines, and working together as a classroom family.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn simple classroom helper jobs.
Practice responsibility and independence.
Develop cooperation and teamwork skills.
Follow classroom routines with increasing confidence.
Care for classroom materials.
Build self-confidence through helping others.
Strengthen listening and communication skills.
Develop pride in contributing to the classroom community.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to give their child one simple helper job at home each day, such as putting toys away, throwing away trash, feeding a pet with supervision, or helping set the table. Ask families to celebrate their child's efforts and talk about how helping makes everyone feel proud.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe independence during classroom routines.
Observe willingness to help others.
Observe responsibility with classroom materials.
Observe communication during helper activities.
Observe cooperation with peers.
Observe confidence while completing simple tasks.
Observe listening and ability to follow directions.
Observe problem-solving during clean-up and organization.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use picture cards to demonstrate helper jobs.
Break tasks into simple one-step directions.
Pair children with a teacher or peer helper.
Offer adaptive cleaning tools when needed.
Provide extra encouragement for hesitant children.
Allow additional time to complete tasks.
Modify activities based on developmental abilities.
Celebrate every effort and accomplishment.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Helping Hands Parade
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate teamwork through music and movement.

DESCRIPTION:
Children wear their helper badges while marching around the classroom pretending to complete classroom jobs.

MATERIALS:
Helper badges
Rhythm instruments
Music player

SETUP:
Create an open path around the classroom.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Give each child a helper badge.
2. Play upbeat music.
3. March around the classroom.
4. Pretend to sweep, organize, water plants, and put toys away.
5. Finish with a classroom cheer.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model enthusiasm and encourage every child to participate.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Confidence
Cooperation
Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, coordination, and enthusiasm.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated participation or simplified movements if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Provide plenty of space for movement and supervise all activities.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Helper Celebration Ceremony
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Recognize each child's contributions to the classroom community.

DESCRIPTION:
Each child receives a Classroom Helper certificate while classmates clap and celebrate one another's efforts throughout the week.

MATERIALS:
Classroom Helper certificates
Stickers

SETUP:
Arrange children in a circle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Call each child by name.
2. Present their Classroom Helper certificate.
3. Share one positive helper job they completed.
4. Encourage classmates to clap and cheer.
5. End with a group celebration photo if permission has been provided.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Celebrate every child's growth and encourage continued responsibility.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-confidence
Sense of belonging
Positive classroom relationships
Emotional development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe confidence, peer support, and emotional responses.

ADAPTATIONS:
Present certificates individually for children who prefer less attention.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Ensure children remain seated while waiting for their turn.
