WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week, toddlers will explore the exciting world of apple orchards and discover how apples grow from blossoms into fruit before being picked by farmers. Children will learn about apple trees, orchards, harvesting, and the important people who care for fruit trees. Through dramatic play, science investigations, art, music, movement, sensory experiences, and early math activities, children will build a deeper understanding of where apples come from while strengthening communication, fine motor, gross motor, and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Understand that apples grow on trees in orchards.
Identify the basic parts of an apple tree.
Learn about farmers and orchard workers.
Explore the apple growing and harvesting process.
Practice counting, sorting, and comparing apples.
Build vocabulary related to orchards and harvesting.
Strengthen fine motor skills through hands-on activities.
Encourage imaginative play by pretending to work in an orchard.
Develop observation skills through nature exploration.
Build confidence working cooperatively with classmates.

Introduce children to an apple orchard by showing real photographs and discussing what they see. Explain that an orchard is a place where many apple trees grow together. Show an apple tree poster and point to the roots, trunk, branches, leaves, blossoms, and apples. Read *Apple Farmer Annie* and discuss how farmers care for apple trees. Take an "Orchard Walk" around the playground. Pretend playground equipment are apple trees. Encourage children to point out things that remind them of trees, leaves, branches, or apples while collecting pretend apples in baskets.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Orchard Review
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Review the apple growing process and orchard vocabulary.

DESCRIPTION:
Children use picture cards to retell the story of how apples grow, are harvested, and arrive at grocery stores.

MATERIALS:
Apple life cycle cards
Orchard picture cards
Apple tree poster

SETUP:
Lay all picture cards on the floor in random order.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Find the apple tree.
2. Find the blossoms.
3. Find the apples growing.
4. Find the harvest.
5. Find the grocery store.
6. Tell the story together.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage children to explain each step using complete sentences whenever possible.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Sequencing
Vocabulary development
Science review
Communication

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's understanding of the apple growing process.

ADAPTATIONS:
Reduce the number of sequencing cards if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use large laminated cards and supervise closely.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Orchard Relay Count
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Practice counting while working together.

DESCRIPTION:
Children work in teams to collect apples from the orchard and count how many apples each team gathered.

MATERIALS:
Plastic apples
Baskets
Number cards
Cones

SETUP:
Scatter apples throughout the activity area.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Walk to the orchard.
2. Pick one apple.
3. Carry it back to your basket.
4. Count the apples together.
5. Repeat until every apple has been collected.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model counting strategies and encourage teamwork instead of competition.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Counting
Cooperation
Gross motor development
One-to-one correspondence

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting accuracy, cooperation, and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Reduce walking distance or number of apples if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Encourage walking feet and safe carrying of baskets.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Orchard Parade
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate everything learned through music, movement, and imaginative play.

DESCRIPTION:
Children wear farmer hats, carry baskets of pretend apples, march through the classroom, sing apple songs, and celebrate becoming Little Apple Farmers.

MATERIALS:
Farmer hats
Plastic apples
Baskets
Music player
Scarves

SETUP:
Create a parade route around the classroom.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Put on your farmer hat.
2. Carry your apple basket.
3. March around the classroom.
4. Sing your favorite apple song.
5. Celebrate with a group cheer and high-fives.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead the parade while encouraging children to recall vocabulary and celebrate their accomplishments.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Confidence
Listening skills
Social-emotional development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, confidence, coordination, and enthusiasm.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated participation or smaller movements as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain clear walking paths and encourage safe movement throughout the celebration.
