WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
My Feelings at School helps toddlers recognize, name, and express their emotions in healthy ways while adjusting to the school environment. Throughout the week, children will explore feelings such as happy, sad, excited, frustrated, scared, and calm through stories, dramatic play, art, music, and hands-on activities. They will practice identifying emotions in themselves and others, learn simple calming strategies, and build empathy, kindness, and confidence as they develop positive relationships with teachers and classmates.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify and name common emotions.
Express feelings using words, facial expressions, and actions.
Develop empathy for classmates.
Learn simple calming strategies.
Build confidence and emotional security at school.
Strengthen communication skills.
Practice problem-solving during social situations.
Build positive friendships and classroom relationships.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about emotions at home each day. Ask children to describe how they felt during school and what helped them feel happy, calm, or safe. Practice naming emotions together using books, pictures, or everyday experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to identify emotions.
Observe how children communicate their feelings.
Observe interactions with peers during emotional situations.
Observe self-regulation and calming strategies.
Observe empathy and kindness toward classmates.
Observe participation during group discussions.
Observe confidence in expressing emotions.
Observe problem-solving during conflicts.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual emotion cards throughout the day.
Offer additional modeling for emotion vocabulary.
Provide sensory tools for children needing calming support.
Pair children with supportive peers.
Allow children to express feelings through art or play instead of words when appropriate.
Offer extra processing time.
Provide quiet calming spaces as needed.
Celebrate every child's emotional growth and efforts.

Read *The Color Monster* (red emotion pages) or discuss simple situations like not getting a turn with a toy. Reinforce positive coping skills with praise.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Closely supervise all activities and provide consistent emotional support. Reinforce that all feelings are okay while reminding children that safe hands, kind words, and calm bodies help everyone learn and play together.

End circle time by reading *The Color Monster* and celebrating everything the class has learned.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:

Play a Feelings Adventure game. Teachers call out different emotions and children move in fun ways to match each feeling—happy jumps, calm slow walks, excited skipping, brave marches, and quiet deep breaths under the shade. Finish with bubbles and free play while encouraging kind interactions.

Celebrate each child's emotional growth and participation.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Closely supervise all activities throughout the day. Reinforce kind words, gentle hands, walking feet, and respectful interactions. Continue reminding children that every feeling is okay and that teachers and friends are always available to help them feel safe, calm, and supported.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
My Feelings Wheel
CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Create a visual reminder of different emotions.

DESCRIPTION:
Children decorate a feelings wheel showing happy, sad, mad, scared, and calm faces.

MATERIALS:
Feelings wheel template
Crayons
Markers
Stickers
Paper fastener (teacher assembly)

SETUP:
Prepare wheel templates before the activity.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Color each emotion.
2. Talk about each feeling.
3. Assemble the wheel with teacher assistance.
4. Spin the wheel and name the feeling.
5. Take the wheel home to share with family.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Support children while encouraging conversations about emotions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Fine motor development
Emotional awareness
Creativity
Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe understanding of different emotions and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide pre-colored examples or adaptive crayons as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Teacher should assemble wheels using paper fasteners.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Freeze Dance
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Express emotions through movement.

DESCRIPTION:
Children dance while music plays. When the music stops, the teacher holds up an emotion card and children freeze showing that feeling with their face and body.

MATERIALS:
Music player
Emotion cards
Scarves

SETUP:
Clear a large open movement area.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Dance while music plays.
2. Freeze when the music stops.
3. Show the emotion on the card.
4. Talk briefly about the feeling.
5. Repeat using different emotions.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model facial expressions and encourage participation.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Listening skills
Emotional expression
Self-confidence

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe emotional recognition and body control.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated participation or simplified movements.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain safe spacing between children during movement.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Superstar Celebration
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate each child's emotional growth throughout the week.

DESCRIPTION:
Children receive recognition for learning about feelings and practicing kindness, bravery, and healthy emotional expression.

MATERIALS:
Feelings Superstar certificates
Stickers
Puppets

SETUP:
Arrange children in a circle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Review the week's emotions.
2. Call each child by name.
3. Share one positive thing the child practiced this week.
4. Present a certificate or sticker.
5. End with a class cheer and group photo if permission has been provided.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Celebrate every child's growth while building confidence and belonging.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-confidence
Positive relationships
Emotional awareness
Sense of belonging

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe confidence, peer encouragement, and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Present recognition individually for children who prefer less attention.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Ensure children remain seated while waiting for their turn.
