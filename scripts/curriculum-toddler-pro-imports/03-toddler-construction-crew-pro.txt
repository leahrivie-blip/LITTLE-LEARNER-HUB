TITLE:

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts, Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will become builders, engineers, and construction workers as they explore trucks, tools, building materials, and creative construction projects. Throughout the week children will build structures, move materials, investigate how things are made, and develop teamwork skills through hands-on play experiences.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore construction vehicles and tools.
Practice building and problem-solving skills.
Strengthen fine and gross motor development.
Develop teamwork and cooperative play.
Build vocabulary related to construction.
Investigate cause and effect through building activities.
Encourage creativity and persistence.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to point out construction projects, trucks, or buildings in their community and discuss what workers do to help build them.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving skills, persistence, teamwork, vocabulary growth, fine motor development, and creativity during building activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger building materials, adaptive tools, visual supports, and opportunities for individual or group participation.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Construction Crew: Construction Site Discovery and related play (introduce the theme through movement, sensory play, and simple language)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Construction Site Discovery.
Practice dramatic play skills during Hard Hat Pretend Play.
Practice music & movement skills during Dump Truck Movement Game.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Construction, Builder, Hammer, Truck, Crane, Bulldozer

DAILY_MATERIALS:
construction paper
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards
hard hats
play dough
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories
toy trucks
Scarves or music
Open movement space
large building bricks
Play dough or crayons
Small manipulatives
Paper

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to construction crew.
Sing "The Wheels on the Truck" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Construction Site Discovery) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to construction crew: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
Participation and interest in Construction Crew play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger building materials, adaptive tools, visual supports, and opportunities for individual or group participation.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Construction Site Discovery
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Construction Site Discovery.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Construction Site Discovery during our Construction Crew week through open-ended exploration.
MATERIALS:
construction paper
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Construction Site Discovery.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Observe, narrate discoveries, and introduce theme vocabulary without directing every action.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite toddlers to explore Construction Site Discovery with hands-on materials related to Construction Crew.
2. Stay nearby and narrate what children notice using simple theme words.
3. Offer choices and follow each child's interest for a few minutes at a time.
4. Encourage pointing, naming, and short conversations about discoveries.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Construction Site Discovery through toddler-friendly open-ended exploration.
Use theme vocabulary related to Construction Crew.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.

Construction Crew: Tool Exploration Table and related play (practice fine motor and social-emotional skills connected to the theme)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Tool Exploration Table.
Practice sensory play skills during Nuts and Bolts Sensory Investigation.
Practice gross motor skills during Crane Lift Challenge.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Construction, Builder, Hammer, Truck, Crane, Bulldozer

DAILY_MATERIALS:
toy tools
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards
sensory bin filler
Sensory bin
Scoops
Loose parts
Cones or mats
Open floor space
Soft obstacles
measuring tapes
large building bricks
Play dough or crayons
Small manipulatives
Paper
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to construction crew.
Sing "The Wheels on the Truck" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Tool Exploration Table) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to construction crew: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
Participation and interest in Construction Crew play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger building materials, adaptive tools, visual supports, and opportunities for individual or group participation.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Tool Exploration Table
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Tool Exploration Table.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Tool Exploration Table during our Construction Crew week through open-ended exploration.
MATERIALS:
toy tools
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Picture cards
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Tool Exploration Table.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Observe, narrate discoveries, and introduce theme vocabulary without directing every action.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite toddlers to explore Tool Exploration Table with hands-on materials related to Construction Crew.
2. Stay nearby and narrate what children notice using simple theme words.
3. Offer choices and follow each child's interest for a few minutes at a time.
4. Encourage pointing, naming, and short conversations about discoveries.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Tool Exploration Table through toddler-friendly open-ended exploration.
Use theme vocabulary related to Construction Crew.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
