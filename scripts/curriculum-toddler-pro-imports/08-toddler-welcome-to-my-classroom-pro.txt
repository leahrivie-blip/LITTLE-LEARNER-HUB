WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Welcome to My Classroom helps toddlers build confidence and feel secure as they begin a new school year. Throughout the week, children will become familiar with their classroom, meet teachers and classmates, practice simple routines, and explore learning centers through hands-on play. Activities encourage social interaction, independence, communication, and a sense of belonging while helping children develop positive feelings about coming to school.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Become familiar with the classroom environment.
Build trusting relationships with teachers.
Practice simple classroom routines.
Learn classmates' names.
Explore classroom learning centers.
Increase confidence and independence.
Express feelings about school.
Practice listening and following simple directions.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to talk with their child about school each evening. Encourage children to share one favorite activity from their day and, if possible, bring a small family photo to display in the classroom. Share positive updates during the first week to help families feel connected and reassure children as they adjust to their new classroom routine.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe how children separate from caregivers.
Observe participation in classroom routines.
Observe interactions with teachers and peers.
Observe communication skills during play.
Observe exploration of learning centers.
Observe ability to follow one-step directions.
Observe confidence during transitions.
Observe curiosity and engagement throughout the day.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide a visual daily schedule.
Offer comfort items or family photos when appropriate.
Allow extra transition time for children who need it.
Use simple one-step directions with visual cues.
Pair hesitant children with a supportive peer or teacher.
Offer sensory breaks as needed.
Adapt materials for varying developmental levels and physical abilities.
Celebrate each child's progress and participation at their own pace.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
We Did It!
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Build self-esteem by celebrating each child's first week of school.

DESCRIPTION:
Each child receives a certificate while classmates clap and cheer for one another.

MATERIALS:
First Week certificates
Stickers

SETUP:
Arrange children in a circle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Call each child by name.
2. Present their certificate.
3. Encourage classmates to clap.
4. Give each child a sticker.
5. End with a group photo if families have provided permission.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Celebrate every child's accomplishments and create a positive ending to the week.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Confidence
Sense of belonging
Positive classroom relationships

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe self-confidence, peer interactions, and emotional responses.

ADAPTATIONS:
Present certificates individually for children who prefer less attention.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Ensure all children remain seated while waiting for their turn.
