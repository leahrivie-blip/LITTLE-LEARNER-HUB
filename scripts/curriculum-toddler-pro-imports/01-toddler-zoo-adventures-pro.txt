LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts, Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will spend the week exploring exciting zoo animals through movement, sensory experiences, dramatic play, art, music, and hands-on investigations. Children will learn about animal characteristics, habitats, sounds, and movements while developing vocabulary, coordination, social skills, and curiosity about the world around them.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common zoo animals.
Practice animal names and sounds.
Explore animal habitats.
Strengthen gross and fine motor skills.
Develop pretend play and social interaction.
Build observation and comparison skills.
Encourage curiosity and language development.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss animals they have seen in books, parks, farms, or zoos. Invite children to share their favorite animal with classmates.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe vocabulary growth, animal recognition, dramatic play participation, gross motor skills, social interactions, and curiosity during exploration activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual animal cards, larger tools for fine motor activities, sensory alternatives, and individualized movement options.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Animal Discovery Table.
Practice music & movement skills during Roar Like A Lion.
Practice fine motor skills during Feed The Zoo Animals.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Zoo, Habitat, Keeper, Lion, Tiger, Zebra

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Stuffed zoo animals
animal figurines
animal masks
animal books
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
Scarves or music
Open movement space
Stuffed zoo animals
animal figurines
animal masks
animal books
Play dough or crayons
Small manipulatives
play dough
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to zoo adventures.
Sing "Going To The Zoo" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Animal Discovery Table) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to zoo adventures: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
Participation and interest in Zoo Adventures play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual animal cards, larger tools for fine motor activities, sensory alternatives, and individualized movement options.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Close supervision during movement and sensory play; avoid tiny pieces; check outdoor equipment before use.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Animal Discovery Table
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Practice open-ended exploration skills during Animal Discovery Table.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Animal Discovery Table during our Zoo Adventures week through open-ended exploration.
MATERIALS:
Stuffed zoo animals
animal figurines
animal masks
animal books
Theme toys/figurines
Baskets
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Animal Discovery Table.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Observe, narrate discoveries, and introduce theme vocabulary without directing every action.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite toddlers to explore Animal Discovery Table with hands-on materials related to Zoo Adventures.
2. Stay nearby and narrate what children notice using simple theme words.
3. Offer choices and follow each child's interest for a few minutes at a time.
4. Encourage pointing, naming, and short conversations about discoveries.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Animal Discovery Table through toddler-friendly open-ended exploration.
Use theme vocabulary related to Zoo Adventures.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
