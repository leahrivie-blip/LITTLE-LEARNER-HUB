WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week, toddlers will celebrate Johnny Appleseed while learning how apple trees grow from tiny seeds into large, fruit-bearing trees. Through storytelling, planting, dramatic play, science investigations, sensory exploration, art, music, and movement, children will discover how one person can help nature by planting trees. They will explore apple seeds, life cycles, kindness, caring for plants, and the importance of sharing with others while strengthening early literacy, math, science, fine motor, gross motor, and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn about Johnny Appleseed in a toddler-friendly way.
Discover that apple trees grow from seeds.
Explore the life cycle of an apple tree.
Practice planting and caring for seeds.
Build vocabulary related to gardening and nature.
Strengthen fine motor skills through planting and art.
Develop early math skills through counting and sorting seeds.
Encourage kindness and helping others.
Build observation skills while caring for plants.
Celebrate everything learned during the Apple unit.

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science
Language & Literacy
Mathematics
Physical Development
Social-Emotional Development
Creative Arts

CIRCLE_TIME:

Gather the children and review everything learned this week. Ask questions such as:
Who was Johnny Appleseed?
What grows inside an apple?
What does a seed need to grow?
What is an orchard?
How can we help take care of plants and trees?

Read *Johnny Appleseed* one final time and encourage children to retell parts of the story. Finish by singing "Way Up High in the Apple Tree" together while acting out the motions.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:

Create a "Johnny Appleseed Adventure Course." Children complete stations by planting pretend seeds, watering flowers, harvesting apples, carrying baskets, and delivering apples to the orchard stand. Celebrate everyone's hard work with cheering and high-fives.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Identifies Johnny Appleseed and his role in planting apple trees.
Understands the apple tree life cycle.
Uses gardening and orchard vocabulary independently.
Demonstrates counting and sorting skills.
Participates in cooperative dramatic play.
Shows kindness toward classmates and nature.
Celebrates learning with confidence and enthusiasm.

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Continue using visual vocabulary and sequencing cards.
Offer adaptive art and gardening tools.
Provide one-on-one support during counting activities.
Allow movement or sensory breaks as needed.
Encourage participation at each child's developmental level.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Wash hands after handling classroom materials and before snack time. Supervise all movement, dramatic play, and counting activities. Reinforce safe walking, gentle hands, sharing, kindness, and caring for plants and the environment as children celebrate becoming little gardeners just like Johnny Appleseed.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Tree Life Cycle Review
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Review the stages of an apple tree's growth.

DESCRIPTION:
Children work together to place oversized life cycle cards in the correct order while explaining what happens during each stage.

MATERIALS:
Large life cycle cards
Pocket chart
Apple tree posters

SETUP:
Mix the life cycle cards before children arrive.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Find the seed.
2. Find the sprout.
3. Find the young tree.
4. Find the blossoming tree.
5. Finish with the apple tree full of apples.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage children to explain each stage using complete sentences when possible.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Sequencing
Scientific thinking
Vocabulary development
Memory recall

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's understanding of the complete life cycle.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide picture prompts or fewer sequencing cards for additional support.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use laminated cards with rounded corners.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Johnny Appleseed Hat
CATEGORY:
Art

OBJECTIVE:
Create a wearable Johnny Appleseed hat.

DESCRIPTION:
Children decorate a paper pot hat to wear while pretending to be Johnny Appleseed planting apple trees.

MATERIALS:
Paper hat templates
Crayons
Apple stickers
Glue
Markers

SETUP:
Prepare hat templates before the activity.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Color your hat.
2. Decorate with apple stickers.
3. Write or trace your name.
4. Put on your hat.
5. Pretend to plant apple trees around the classroom.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Talk about Johnny Appleseed's kindness while helping children create their hats.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Creativity
Fine motor development
Self-expression
Imaginative play

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe creativity, hand control, and participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger templates or adaptive coloring tools if needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use child-safe art supplies and supervise materials.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Apple Orchard Counting Challenge
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Practice counting, sorting, and comparing quantities.

DESCRIPTION:
Children collect apples from the pretend orchard, count them, sort them by color or size, and compare which baskets have more or fewer apples.

MATERIALS:
Plastic apples
Small baskets
Number cards
Sorting mats

SETUP:
Scatter plastic apples throughout the classroom or playground.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Collect apples in your basket.
2. Count your apples.
3. Sort them by color or size.
4. Match your basket to a number card.
5. Compare baskets with a friend.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage mathematical language such as more, less, equal, biggest, and smallest.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Counting
Sorting
Comparing
Problem solving

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting accuracy, sorting skills, and mathematical vocabulary.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use smaller quantities or provide counting assistance as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Monitor children while using small manipulatives.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Johnny Appleseed Parade
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Celebrate the week's learning through music, movement, and imaginative play.

DESCRIPTION:
Children wear their Johnny Appleseed hats and parade around the classroom carrying baskets, watering cans, and pretend apples while singing apple songs and pretending to plant trees throughout the community.

MATERIALS:
Johnny Appleseed hats
Plastic apples
Small baskets
Toy watering cans
Music player

SETUP:
Create a parade route around the classroom with several planting stations.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Put on your Johnny Appleseed hat.
2. Carry your basket of apples.
3. Stop to plant pretend seeds.
4. Water each new tree.
5. Finish with a classroom celebration dance and cheer.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead the parade while celebrating each child's learning and encouraging kindness, teamwork, and caring for nature.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Gross motor development
Listening skills
Confidence
Social-emotional development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, coordination, cooperation, and enthusiasm.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow seated participation or simplified movements when appropriate.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Provide adequate personal space and encourage safe movement throughout the parade.
