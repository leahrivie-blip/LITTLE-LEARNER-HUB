WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Making New Friends helps toddlers develop positive social skills as they continue adjusting to the classroom. Throughout the week, children will practice greeting classmates, sharing materials, taking turns, working together during play, and using kind words. Activities encourage cooperation, communication, empathy, and building friendships while helping children feel connected and confident in their classroom community.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build positive relationships with classmates.
Practice sharing and taking turns.
Use kind words and friendly actions.
Participate in cooperative play.
Develop communication skills.
Strengthen listening skills.
Build confidence in group settings.
Learn that everyone belongs in the classroom.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Hello, Friend!
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Help children learn classmates' names and practice friendly greetings.

DESCRIPTION:
Children sit in a circle and take turns rolling a soft ball to a classmate while saying "Hello" and using each other's names.

MATERIALS:
Soft ball
Name tags

SETUP:
Seat children in a large circle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the greeting activity.
2. Roll the ball to a child while saying their name.
3. Encourage the child to greet another friend.
4. Continue until everyone has participated.
5. Celebrate everyone's friendly greetings.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model greetings and encourage children to use names and eye contact.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Social confidence
Name recognition
Communication skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe participation, eye contact, and willingness to greet others.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow children to wave or smile if they are not comfortable speaking.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Use a soft ball and remind children to roll instead of throw.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Friendship Celebration
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Recognize each child's positive contributions to the classroom community.

DESCRIPTION:
Each child receives a Friendship Superstar certificate while classmates clap and celebrate one another's kindness throughout the week.

MATERIALS:
Friendship certificates
Stickers

SETUP:
Arrange children in a circle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Call each child by name.
2. Present their Friendship Superstar certificate.
3. Share one positive thing each child did during the week.
4. Encourage classmates to clap and cheer.
5. End with a group photo if families have provided permission.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Celebrate every child's growth and reinforce positive social behaviors.

LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-confidence
Sense of belonging
Positive relationships
Emotional development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe confidence, peer support, and emotional responses.

ADAPTATIONS:
Present certificates individually for children who prefer less attention.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Ensure children remain seated while waiting for their turn.
