WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will set sail on an exciting pirate adventure filled with treasure hunts, map exploration, ocean discoveries, teamwork challenges, and imaginative play. Throughout the week children will build pirate ships, search for treasure, explore movement activities, and develop problem-solving skills while engaging in hands-on learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Encourage imaginative and dramatic play.
Develop problem-solving and exploration skills.
Strengthen gross and fine motor development.
Practice teamwork and cooperation.
Expand vocabulary through themed play.
Explore maps, directions, and simple navigation concepts.
Build confidence through adventure-based learning.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Meet the Pirate Crew
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Practice dramatic play skills during Meet the Pirate Crew.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Meet the Pirate Crew during our Pirate Adventure week through dramatic play.
MATERIALS:
Pirate hats
pirate-themed books.
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Meet the Pirate Crew.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Join play briefly as a co-player, then step back so toddlers lead the story.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Set up a pretend-play area for Meet the Pirate Crew with a few clear props.
2. Model a short role (helper, keeper, captain) and invite children in.
3. Use simple language to support caregiving, teamwork, and problem-solving.
4. Follow the children's storyline and keep roles flexible.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Meet the Pirate Crew through toddler-friendly dramatic play.
Use theme vocabulary related to Pirate Adventure.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
