LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts, Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore the fun of camping through pretend campfires, nature discoveries, animal adventures, outdoor-inspired sensory experiences, and imaginative play. Children will learn about nature, nighttime, friendship, exploration, and outdoor safety while building language, motor, and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore camping and outdoor experiences.
Learn about nature and forest animals.
Develop imagination through dramatic play.
Strengthen gross and fine motor skills.
Practice observation and exploration skills.
Build vocabulary related to camping.
Encourage teamwork and cooperative play.

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to camping under the stars.
Sing "The Bear Went Over the Mountain" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Set Up the Campsite) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Set Up the Campsite
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Practice dramatic play skills during Set Up the Campsite.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers explore Set Up the Campsite during our Camping Under the Stars week through dramatic play.
MATERIALS:
Pretend-play props
Costumes or badges
Theme accessories
SETUP:
Prepare a toddler-ready space and materials for Set Up the Campsite.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Join play briefly as a co-player, then step back so toddlers lead the story.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Set up a pretend-play area for Set Up the Campsite with a few clear props.
2. Model a short role (helper, keeper, captain) and invite children in.
3. Use simple language to support caregiving, teamwork, and problem-solving.
4. Follow the children's storyline and keep roles flexible.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engage in Set Up the Campsite through toddler-friendly dramatic play.
Use theme vocabulary related to Camping Under the Stars.
Practice social interaction, curiosity, and motor skills during play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe engagement, theme vocabulary, motor skills, and social interaction.
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
