TITLE:
Farm STEM
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Farm STEM
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science, Engineering, Math, Language & Literacy, Fine Motor, Gross Motor, Social-Emotional, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore farm life through hands-on STEM play. Children investigate animals, barns, seeds, soil, tractors, ramps, counting, sorting, and simple cause-and-effect experiments. Experiences are play-based, sensory-rich, and designed to build curiosity, early problem-solving, language, cooperation, and motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common farm animals and tools.
- Explore how seeds and plants grow.
- Count, sort, compare, and match objects.
- Build simple structures and test ideas.
- Use descriptive farm and STEM vocabulary.
- Strengthen fine and gross motor skills.
- Practice turn-taking and cooperative play.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Toy farm animals, toy tractors, blocks, cardboard boxes, craft sticks, plastic eggs, oats, shredded paper, soil, seeds, cups, scoops, funnels, water, paint, paper, playdough, cotton balls, glue, child-safe scissors, baskets, ramps, masking tape, magnifying glasses, picture cards.

VOCABULARY:
Farm, barn, tractor, farmer, cow, pig, chicken, horse, seed, soil, grow, build, roll, ramp, heavy, light, tall, short, count, sort, match, harvest.

BOOKS:
Big Red Barn; Barnyard Dance!; Click, Clack, Moo; The Little Red Hen; Mrs. Wishy-Washy.

SONGS:
Old MacDonald Had a Farm; The Farmer in the Dell; Five Little Ducks; BINGO; Mary Had a Little Lamb.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to notice foods that come from farms, water a plant together, or visit a farmers market. Ask families to send a photo of a garden, farm animal, tractor, or favorite fruit or vegetable.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal identification, counting, sorting, cause-and-effect thinking, building strategies, new vocabulary, tool use, sensory engagement, turn-taking, and persistence.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger materials for easier grasping, picture cues for language support, dry alternatives for sensory-sensitive children, fewer items for simpler counting, and added building challenges for children ready for extension.

MONDAY
DAILY_THEME:
Meet the Farm
DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Identify farm animals, imitate sounds and movements, explore textures, and use farm vocabulary.
DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Cow, pig, chicken, horse, barn, farmer.
DAILY_MATERIALS:
Farm animals, sensory filler, barn, blocks, playdough, picture cards.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Science, Language, Fine Motor, Dramatic Play, Gross Motor.
CIRCLE_TIME:
Read Big Red Barn. Introduce animals one at a time and invite children to copy each sound and movement.
OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Farm Animal Movement Trail: gallop like horses, waddle like ducks, crawl like pigs, and flap like chickens.
DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Animal naming, sound imitation, movement control, engagement, and turn-taking.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Let children point instead of speak; provide seated animal movements; use large animal figures.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise sensory materials and keep small pieces out of reach of children who mouth objects.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Farm Sensory Search
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Find and identify farm animals while scooping and pouring.
DESCRIPTION:
Children search through oats and shredded paper for hidden animals and tractors.
MATERIALS:
Bin, oats, shredded paper, farm animals, tractors, scoops, cups.
SETUP:
Hide animals in the filled bin and place tools beside it.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to explore.
2. Find one hidden item.
3. Name or imitate it.
4. Sort animals and vehicles.
5. Scoop and pour freely.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model words, ask simple questions, and narrate actions.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Vocabulary, fine motor control, sorting, sensory exploration.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Grasp, tool use, matching, language, persistence.
ADAPTATIONS:
Replace loose filler with fabric strips or large paper pieces.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Maintain close supervision; materials are not for eating.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Build a Barn
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Stack and connect blocks to make an animal shelter.
DESCRIPTION:
Children use blocks and boxes to create barns and pens.
MATERIALS:
Blocks, boxes, craft sticks, toy animals.
SETUP:
Arrange materials on a floor mat.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose an animal.
2. Build walls.
3. Add a doorway.
4. Place the animal inside.
5. Test whether the barn stays standing.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage planning and rebuilding without making the structure for the child.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Spatial awareness, problem-solving, persistence, pretend play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Stacking, balance, planning, response to collapse.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use large foam blocks; offer a picture model.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use lightweight blocks and keep towers below shoulder height.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Muddy Pig Painting
CATEGORY:
Art
OBJECTIVE:
Explore color and texture through process art.
DESCRIPTION:
Children add brown paint “mud” to pig-shaped paper using fingers or sponges.
MATERIALS:
Pig paper shapes, washable brown paint, sponges, smocks.
SETUP:
Tape paper to table and place small paint amounts in trays.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Look at the clean pig.
2. Touch or dab paint.
3. Add muddy marks.
4. Describe wet, smooth, or sticky.
5. Display when dry.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support sensory language and child-led choices.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Creativity, sensory awareness, hand control, descriptive language.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Tool choice, tolerance of texture, mark making.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer a sealed paint bag or long-handled brush.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable non-toxic paint and prevent paint from entering mouths.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Seed Sort
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Sort large seeds by size, color, or type.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place seeds into matching bowls using fingers or tongs.
MATERIALS:
Large beans or seed replicas, bowls, toddler tongs.
SETUP:
Mix two or three safe large item types in a tray.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Pick up one item.
2. Look at its features.
3. Find its matching bowl.
4. Place it inside.
5. Count each group together.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model same/different and count slowly.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Classification, counting, visual discrimination, pincer strength.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Sorting rule, one-to-one counting, grip.
ADAPTATIONS:
Sort only two groups or use large plastic seed shapes.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Avoid small choking hazards and supervise continuously.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Water Dropper Garden

Fine Motor

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Tractor Ramp Test
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Test how ramp height changes motion.
DESCRIPTION:
Children roll tractors down low and higher ramps.
MATERIALS:
Boards, blocks, tractors, tape.
SETUP:
Build two low stable ramps.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a tractor.
2. Place it at the top.
3. Let go.
4. Watch where it stops.
5. Try another ramp.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Ask which went faster or farther and encourage repeated testing.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect, prediction, comparison, experimentation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Release control, tracking, comparison language.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use one ramp and hand-over-hand release.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Secure ramps and prevent standing on them.
