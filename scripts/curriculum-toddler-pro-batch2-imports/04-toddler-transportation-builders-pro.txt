TITLE:
Transportation Builders
AGE_GROUP:
Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME:
Transportation Builders
PLAN:
Pro
STATUS:
published
LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Engineering, Science, Math, Language & Literacy, Fine Motor, Gross Motor, Social-Emotional, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers investigate cars, trucks, buses, trains, boats, and planes through building, rolling, ramp testing, road design, sorting, and dramatic play. Children explore how vehicles move, what wheels do, how roads and bridges help transportation, and how people work together to travel safely.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Identify common vehicles and where they travel.
- Explore wheels, ramps, speed, and cause and effect.
- Build roads, bridges, tracks, and garages.
- Count, sort, compare, and match vehicles.
- Use transportation vocabulary during play.
- Strengthen hand control, coordination, and large-muscle movement.
- Practice cooperation and problem-solving.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Toy cars, trucks, buses, trains, boats, planes, blocks, cardboard, tubes, masking tape, ramps, road signs, cones, large boxes, paint, paper, sponges, playdough, large lids, baskets, water table, foil, craft sticks, picture cards, sorting mats.

VOCABULARY:
Car, truck, bus, train, boat, plane, wheel, road, track, bridge, tunnel, garage, ramp, fast, slow, stop, go, float, fly, build.

BOOKS:
Freight Train; Little Blue Truck; The Wheels on the Bus; Goodnight, Goodnight Construction Site; Who Sank the Boat?

SONGS:
The Wheels on the Bus; Down by the Station; Row, Row, Row Your Boat; I’m a Little Airplane; Driving in My Car.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to notice vehicles during daily travel and talk about where they go. Invite children to bring or draw a picture of a favorite vehicle.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe vehicle naming, sorting, prediction, building strategies, cause-and-effect thinking, spatial language, turn-taking, persistence, and safe movement.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use large vehicles and foam blocks, visual stop/go cues, low ramps, simplified sorting choices, seated movement options, and prebuilt road sections for children needing support.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Roll and Compare
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Compare how different vehicles roll.
DESCRIPTION:
Children send vehicles down a low ramp and observe speed and distance.
MATERIALS:
Ramp, blocks, toy cars and trucks, tape finish lines.
SETUP:
Secure a low ramp and mark stopping distances.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a vehicle.
2. Place it at the top.
3. Let go.
4. Watch where it stops.
5. Try another and compare.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Ask which went faster or farther and encourage retesting.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Cause and effect, prediction, comparison, tracking.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Release control, attention, comparison words.
ADAPTATIONS:
Use one wide ramp and chunky vehicles.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Prevent standing or climbing on ramps.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Build a Road
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Design a path for vehicles.
DESCRIPTION:
Children use tape and blocks to create roads, turns, and parking areas.
MATERIALS:
Masking tape, blocks, vehicles, signs.
SETUP:
Mark a starting point on the floor.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose where the road goes.
2. Add tape lines.
3. Place signs or blocks.
4. Drive a vehicle through.
5. Fix areas that are blocked.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support planning and spatial language.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Engineering, problem-solving, collaboration, pretend play.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Planning, route changes, peer interaction.
ADAPTATIONS:
Provide a short pre-made road to extend.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Press tape flat to prevent tripping.

Wheel Track Painting

Art

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Land, Water, or Air Sort
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Classify vehicles by where they travel.
DESCRIPTION:
Children place vehicle pictures or toys on land, water, or air mats.
MATERIALS:
Three picture mats, large vehicle toys or cards.
SETUP:
Place mats side by side.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Choose a vehicle.
2. Name or point to it.
3. Decide where it travels.
4. Place it on the mat.
5. Review each group.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Ask simple either/or questions and model categories.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Classification, vocabulary, reasoning.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Category accuracy, explanation attempts, recall.
ADAPTATIONS:
Begin with only land and air.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use large cards and sturdy toys.
