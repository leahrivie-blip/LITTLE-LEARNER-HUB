TITLE: Little Bakers
AGE_GROUP: Toddler (2-3 Years)
THEME: Little Bakers
PLAN: PRO
STATUS: Published
LEARNING_DOMAINS: Math, Science, Language & Literacy, Fine Motor, Social-Emotional, Creative Arts, Dramatic Play, Self-Help

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore baking through safe, play-based experiences. Children scoop, pour, mix, count, compare, decorate, pretend to run a bakery, and observe simple changes in ingredients. Activities emphasize sensory exploration, early math and science, practical life skills, language, cooperation, and fine motor development.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Follow simple one- and two-step directions.
- Scoop, pour, stir, press, and transfer materials.
- Count and compare pretend ingredients.
- Use baking vocabulary in play.
- Explore changes caused by mixing and adding water.
- Practice turn-taking and cooperative dramatic play.
- Build independence with cleanup and self-help routines.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Mixing bowls, wooden spoons, measuring cups, scoops, trays, playdough, flour substitute or taste-safe dough, water, oats, large pom-poms, cupcake liners, muffin tins, cookie cutters, rolling pins, aprons, chef hats, toy food, bakery signs, baskets, tongs, paper plates, washable paint, glue, paper shapes, picture recipe cards.

VOCABULARY:
Bake, baker, bakery, mix, stir, scoop, pour, measure, roll, press, dough, flour, oven, recipe, cupcake, bread, cookie, full, empty, more, less.

BOOKS:
The Little Red Hen; If You Give a Mouse a Cookie; Whiskers, Tails & Wings: Animal Folktales; The Doorbell Rang; Pete the Cat and the Perfect Pizza Party.

SONGS:
Pat-a-Cake; Five Little Cupcakes; Muffin Man; This Is the Way We Stir the Batter; Hot Cross Buns.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to let children help stir, pour, wash produce, or place safe ingredients into a bowl. Send home a simple no-bake snack idea and encourage families to talk about recipe steps.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe grip strength, pouring control, counting, sequence understanding, sensory tolerance, pretend play, vocabulary, persistence, sharing, and cleanup participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger tools and lightweight bowls, offer dry sensory alternatives, provide picture steps, reduce ingredient choices, allow seated participation, and use hand-over-hand support only when welcomed.

WEDNESDAY:
DAILY_THEME: Dough Science
DAILY_OBJECTIVES: Explore texture, pressure, shape, and how materials change when mixed.
DAILY_VOCABULARY: Dough, mix, soft, sticky, roll, press.
DAILY_MATERIALS: Taste-safe dough or playdough, rollers, cutters, bowls, water droppers.
DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS: Science, Fine Motor, Creative Arts, Language.
CIRCLE_TIME_IDEAS: Compare dry ingredients with mixed dough and discuss changes.
OUTDOOR_PLAY: Make pretend mud pies with soil or damp sand and large tools.
DAILY_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Texture tolerance, squeezing, rolling, change noticing.
DAILY_ADAPTATIONS: Offer sealed dough bags or tools only.
SAFETY_NOTES: Verify allergies and use taste-safe ingredients when appropriate.
ACTIVITY_1_CATEGORY: Science
ACTIVITY_1_NAME: Mix a Simple Dough
ACTIVITY_1_OBJECTIVE: Observe how dry and wet materials combine.
ACTIVITY_1_DESCRIPTION: Children help combine a safe dry mixture with small amounts of water.
ACTIVITY_1_MATERIALS: Allergy-safe dry mix, water, bowls, spoons.
ACTIVITY_1_SETUP: Premeasure ingredients and review allergy records.
ACTIVITY_1_DIRECTIONS: 1. Touch or observe dry mix. 2. Add a little water. 3. Stir. 4. Notice the change. 5. Press the finished dough.
ACTIVITY_1_TEACHER_ROLE: Add water slowly and describe changes.
ACTIVITY_1_LEARNING_GOALS: Cause and effect, sequencing, sensory science.
ACTIVITY_1_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Stirring, prediction, descriptive words.
ACTIVITY_1_ADAPTATIONS: Teacher mixes inside a clear sealed bag.
ACTIVITY_1_SAFETY_NOTES: Follow allergy policies; do not treat as food unless approved.
ACTIVITY_2_CATEGORY: Fine Motor
ACTIVITY_2_NAME: Roll and Cut Bakery Dough
ACTIVITY_2_OBJECTIVE: Strengthen hands through rolling and pressing.
ACTIVITY_2_DESCRIPTION: Children roll dough and press large cookie cutters.
ACTIVITY_2_MATERIALS: Dough, chunky rollers, large cutters, trays.
ACTIVITY_2_SETUP: Portion dough on individual trays.
ACTIVITY_2_DIRECTIONS: 1. Press dough flat. 2. Roll forward and back. 3. Choose a cutter. 4. Press firmly. 5. Lift and examine the shape.
ACTIVITY_2_TEACHER_ROLE: Model motions and shape vocabulary.
ACTIVITY_2_LEARNING_GOALS: Hand strength, coordination, shape awareness.
ACTIVITY_2_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES: Pressure, bilateral use, shape matching.
ACTIVITY_2_ADAPTATIONS: Use soft dough and easy-grip rollers.
ACTIVITY_2_SAFETY_NOTES: Supervise to prevent mouthing.

ACTIVITY_3_OBJECTIVE: Use open-ended materials to create pretend baked goods.

ACTIVITY_3_DIRECTIONS: 1. Choose what to make. 2. Shape the dough. 3. Add safe large decorations. 4. Place on a tray. 5. Pretend to serve it.
