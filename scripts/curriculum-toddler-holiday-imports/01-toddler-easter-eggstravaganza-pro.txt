TITLE:
Easter Eggstravaganza

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Easter

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will explore Easter through colorful eggs, bunnies, chicks, springtime discoveries, sensory play, movement, art, and dramatic play. Activities encourage curiosity, creativity, early problem-solving, and social interaction while celebrating the arrival of spring.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore Easter and spring themes through play
• Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
• Build Easter-related vocabulary
• Encourage creativity and imagination
• Support social interaction and cooperative play

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic eggs, stuffed bunnies, chick toys, pastel paint, baskets, sensory bin materials, books, music player, ribbons, pom-poms

VOCABULARY:
Easter, Bunny, Egg, Chick, Basket, Spring, Hop, Nest, Flower, Pastel

BOOKS:
We're Going on an Egg Hunt
Llama Llama Easter Egg
The Easter Egg
Happy Easter Mouse
Peek-a-Boo Bunny

SONGS:
Here Comes Peter Cottontail
Bunny Hop
Five Little Easter Eggs
Spring Is Here
The Easter Bunny Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about Easter traditions, springtime changes, and signs of new growth. Invite children to share favorite Easter experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe language development, fine motor skills, problem-solving abilities, social interaction, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for younger toddlers, visual supports, adaptive art tools, sensory alternatives, and additional teacher assistance as needed.

Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Easter Sensory Bin) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to easter: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Easter Sensory Bin
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support easter learning through easter sensory bin.
DESCRIPTION:
Children explore Easter-themed materials in a sensory bin.
MATERIALS:
Plastic eggs, pastel rice, scoops, pom-poms
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Easter Sensory Bin before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and sensory exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Fill sensory bin with materials.
2. Encourage scooping and sorting.
3. Open and close eggs.
4. Explore textures together.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and fine motor development.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Easter Sensory Bin
Use of easter vocabulary or interest
Peer interaction and independence
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
