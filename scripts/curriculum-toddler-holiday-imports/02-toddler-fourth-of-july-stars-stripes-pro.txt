TITLE:
Fourth of July Stars & Stripes

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Fourth of July

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will celebrate the Fourth of July through patriotic colors, stars, flags, music, movement, sensory exploration, and creative activities. Children will explore red, white, and blue themes while developing social, cognitive, and motor skills through hands-on learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore patriotic symbols and colors
• Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
• Build vocabulary related to celebrations
• Encourage creativity and self-expression
• Support social interaction and teamwork

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Red, white, and blue paint, stars, flags, ribbons, sensory bin materials, books, music player, pom-poms, paper plates, bubbles

VOCABULARY:
Flag, Star, Red, White, Blue, Celebrate, Parade, Fireworks, Summer, America

BOOKS:
Red White and Boom!
My First Fourth of July
Baby Loves America
F Is for Flag
Stars and Stripes

SONGS:
You're a Grand Old Flag
Yankee Doodle
This Land Is Your Land
Red White and Blue
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss summer traditions, patriotic celebrations, and community events. Invite children to share their favorite summer activities.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color recognition, language development, social interaction, fine motor skills, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide adaptive art tools, larger materials, visual supports, sensory alternatives, and additional teacher assistance as needed.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Patriotic Sensory Bin
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support fourth of july learning through patriotic sensory bin.
DESCRIPTION:
Children explore red, white, and blue materials in a sensory bin.
MATERIALS:
Colored rice, stars, pom-poms, scoops
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Patriotic Sensory Bin before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and sensory exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Fill sensory bin with materials.
2. Encourage scooping and sorting.
3. Discuss colors and textures.
4. Explore together.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Color recognition and sensory development.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Patriotic Sensory Bin
Use of fourth of july vocabulary or interest
Peer interaction and independence
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
