TITLE:
New Year's Little Celebrations

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
New Year's

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will celebrate the New Year through music, movement, sensory play, counting activities, art, and social experiences. Children will explore concepts of celebrations, new beginnings, friendship, and making happy memories while developing important early learning skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore celebrations and traditions
• Practice counting and number recognition
• Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
• Encourage social interaction and cooperation
• Support creativity and self-expression

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Party hats, streamers, pom-poms, balloons, counting cards, music player, sensory bin materials, mirrors, stickers, craft supplies

VOCABULARY:
New Year, Celebrate, Party, Count, Happy, Friend, Music, Dance, Cheer, Together

BOOKS:
Squirrel's New Year's Resolution
Happy New Year Everywhere
The Night Before New Year's
New Year's Eve Countdown
Let's Celebrate

SONGS:
If You're Happy and You Know It
The Countdown Song
Celebrate Together
This Little Light of Mine
The Party Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about celebrations, family traditions, and fun memories from the past year. Invite children to share stories about special family events.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting skills, social interaction, communication, participation during group activities, and fine motor development.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive art tools, sensory alternatives, larger materials, and additional teacher assistance as needed.

CIRCLE_TIME:
Welcome song and name greeting connected to new year's.
Sing "If You're Happy and You Know It" with simple movements or props.
Show one prop or picture from today's activity (Party Sensory Bin) and invite toddlers to name what they see.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Outdoor gross-motor play tied to new year's: animal walks, pushing/pulling, or simple theme movement games for 5–15 minutes with close supervision.

SAFETY_NOTES:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Party Sensory Bin
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support new year's learning through party sensory bin.
DESCRIPTION:
Children explore a New Year's themed sensory bin filled with party materials.
MATERIALS:
Pom-poms, streamers, scoops, cups
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Party Sensory Bin before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and sensory exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Fill sensory bin with materials.
2. Encourage scooping and sorting.
3. Explore colors and textures.
4. Discuss party items.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and language development.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Party Sensory Bin
Use of new year's vocabulary or interest
Peer interaction and independence
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, hand-over-hand support, and a shorter turn. Allow parallel play instead of forced sharing.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely, keep materials too large to choke on, and clear walkways for active movement. Check outdoor surfaces before play.
