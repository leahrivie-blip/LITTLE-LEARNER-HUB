TITLE:
New Year's Little Celebrations

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
New Year's

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers will celebrate the New Year through music, movement, sensory play, counting activities, art, and social experiences. Children will explore concepts of celebrations, new beginnings, friendship, and making happy memories while developing important early learning skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore celebrations and traditions
• Practice counting and number recognition
• Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
• Encourage social interaction and cooperation
• Support creativity and self-expression

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Party hats, streamers, pom-poms, balloons, counting cards, music player, sensory bin materials, mirrors, stickers, craft supplies

VOCABULARY:
New Year, Celebrate, Party, Count, Happy, Friend, Music, Dance, Cheer, Together

BOOKS:
Squirrel's New Year's Resolution
Happy New Year Everywhere
The Night Before New Year's
New Year's Eve Countdown
Let's Celebrate

SONGS:
If You're Happy and You Know It
The Countdown Song
Celebrate Together
This Little Light of Mine
The Party Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about celebrations, family traditions, and fun memories from the past year. Invite children to share stories about special family events.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting skills, social interaction, communication, participation during group activities, and fine motor development.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive art tools, sensory alternatives, larger materials, and additional teacher assistance as needed.
