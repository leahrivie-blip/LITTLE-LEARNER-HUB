TITLE:
Ice Cream Shop Entrepreneurs

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Ice Cream Shop Business

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become ice cream shop owners as they create menus, take customer orders, count money, build ice cream creations, and operate their own classroom ice cream business. Through dramatic play, math activities, literacy experiences, and teamwork, children will learn early business concepts while having summer fun.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore basic business and money concepts
• Practice counting, sorting, and number recognition
• Build communication and literacy skills
• Strengthen problem-solving abilities
• Encourage teamwork and customer service skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Play ice cream sets, pretend money, menus, order forms, clipboards, aprons, cash register, play dough, art supplies

VOCABULARY:
Business, Customer, Order, Menu, Money, Price, Scoop, Flavor, Cashier, Entrepreneur

BOOKS:
Should I Share My Ice Cream?
Ice Cream Summer
Pete the Cat Screams for Ice Cream
Ice Cream Face
The Ice Cream Shop

SONGS:
I Like Ice Cream
The Ice Cream Song
Five Little Ice Cream Cones
Summer Fun Song
If You're Happy and You Know It

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss businesses they visit, practice counting money, and talk about customer service experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting skills, communication abilities, problem-solving, social interactions, and participation during dramatic play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual menus, larger manipulatives, simplified order forms, peer partners, and additional support as needed.
