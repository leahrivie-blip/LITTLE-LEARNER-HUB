TITLE:

Water Engineering & Summer STEM

PLAN:
Pro

published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become water park engineers as they design water slides, build water systems, test floating objects, solve engineering challenges, and explore how water moves. Through hands-on STEM investigations, children will learn about gravity, flow, construction, and teamwork while having summer-themed fun.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore engineering and design concepts
• Investigate how water moves and flows
• Practice problem-solving and critical thinking
• Develop measurement and observation skills
• Encourage teamwork and creativity

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Funnels, tubing, water tables, cups, buckets, toy boats, measuring cups, blocks, cardboard tubes, clipboards, journals

VOCABULARY:
Engineer, Design, Build, Flow, Gravity, Water, Measure, Experiment, Structure, Solution

BOOKS:
Rosie Revere, Engineer
Dreaming Up
What Do Engineers Do?
Water Can Be...
STEM Builds

SONGS:
The Water Song
Row Row Row Your Boat
The Engineer Song
Move and Build
Splish Splash

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to explore water play, building activities, and problem-solving challenges together at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe problem-solving, cooperation, engineering thinking, measuring skills, and scientific inquiry.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive tools, simplified challenges, peer partnerships, and additional guidance as needed.
