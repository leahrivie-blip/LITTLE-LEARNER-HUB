TITLE:
Zoo Veterinarians

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Zoo Veterinarians & Animal Care

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become zoo veterinarians as they learn how animals are cared for, explore animal needs, conduct pretend health checks, investigate habitats, and practice empathy through dramatic play and hands-on science activities. Children will discover how veterinarians help keep animals healthy and safe.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Learn about animal care and veterinary careers
• Develop empathy and responsibility
• Explore animal habitats and needs
• Strengthen communication and problem-solving skills
• Practice observation and recording skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Stuffed animals, toy medical kits, clipboards, animal charts, scales, measuring tape, bandages, animal books, habitat pictures

VOCABULARY:
Veterinarian, Healthy, Habitat, Animal, Care, Examine, Medicine, Rescue, Patient, Treatment

BOOKS:
I Want to Be a Veterinarian
Vet in Training
National Geographic Kids Animal Doctors
If I Were a Veterinarian
Zoo Animals

SONGS:
Old MacDonald Had a Farm
The Animal Care Song
Five Little Monkeys
Bingo
Animal Action Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about pets, animal care, and the importance of being kind and gentle with animals.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe empathy, communication skills, problem-solving, scientific thinking, and participation during dramatic play activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, adaptive tools, peer partners, simplified recording sheets, and additional guidance as needed.
