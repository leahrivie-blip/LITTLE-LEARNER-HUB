WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will participate in playful peek-a-boo experiences that encourage social interaction, visual tracking, language development, and early object permanence skills. Through songs, scarves, mirrors, movement activities, and playful interactions with caregivers, babies will build relationships, confidence, and curiosity while having fun.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop social connections with trusted caregivers
- Strengthen visual tracking skills
- Encourage early object permanence development
- Support language development through repetition
- Promote smiling, laughing, and social engagement
- Encourage reaching and movement during play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to play peek-a-boo during daily routines, diaper changes, and playtime. Repeating simple games helps infants build trust, anticipation, and early learning skills.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, social smiles, laughter, anticipation, visual tracking, reaching attempts, and responses to familiar people and games.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional visual cues for younger infants. Allow infants to participate at their own pace and adjust activity length based on attention span and comfort.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Scarf Peek-A-Boo Fun

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual tracking and social engagement.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants watch as teachers hide and reveal their faces using colorful scarves while playing peek-a-boo.

MATERIALS:
Soft scarves

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably on the floor or in supportive seating.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model peek-a-boo, use expressive facial expressions, and encourage interaction.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold scarf in front of your face.
2. Say "Peek-a-boo!"
3. Remove scarf and smile.
4. Repeat several times.
5. Encourage infants to watch and respond.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Social interaction
- Attention skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches scarf movement
- Smiles during game
- Anticipates reveal

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Classic Scarf Peek-a-Boo

CATEGORY:
Circle Time

OBJECTIVE:
Play classic peek-a-boo with a soft scarf.

DESCRIPTION:
Hide your face briefly behind a scarf and reveal with a gentle peek-a-boo.

MATERIALS:
Soft scarf

SETUP:
Sit face-to-face at infant level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep hides short for younger infants.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show your face.
2. Hide behind scarf briefly.
3. Say peek-a-boo as you reveal.
4. Pause for infant reaction.
5. Repeat a few times.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Object permanence
- Social joy
- Anticipation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches hide
- Reacts to reveal
- Shows anticipation

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.
