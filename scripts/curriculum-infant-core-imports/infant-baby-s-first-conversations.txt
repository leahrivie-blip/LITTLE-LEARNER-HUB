WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Communication begins long before babies say their first words. Throughout this week, infants will engage in meaningful back-and-forth interactions through facial expressions, coos, babbles, songs, mirrors, gestures, and responsive caregiving. These experiences help build the foundation for language development, social connection, listening skills, and emotional security.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage early communication skills
- Support cooing, babbling, and vocal play
- Strengthen caregiver-child interactions
- Develop listening and turn-taking skills
- Promote facial recognition and social engagement
- Build early language foundations

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk with babies throughout the day. Pause after speaking to allow babies opportunities to respond with facial expressions, sounds, or movements. These simple conversations help build strong language foundations.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, cooing, babbling, vocalizations, facial expressions, turn-taking behaviors, social smiles, and responses to voices.

ADAPTATIONS:
Allow infants additional processing time before responding. Reduce distractions during communication activities and position infants where they can clearly see faces and expressions.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Face-to-Face Chat Time

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage social interaction and early communication.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants participate in one-on-one face-to-face conversations with caregivers who respond to every sound, smile, and expression.

MATERIALS:
Floor mat

SETUP:
Position infant comfortably facing the teacher.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Maintain eye contact, respond to vocalizations, and model conversational turn-taking.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit face-to-face with infant.
2. Smile and greet the infant.
3. Talk using a calm voice.
4. Pause and wait for a response.
5. Respond to all sounds and expressions.
6. Continue the conversation naturally.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Social engagement
- Communication development
- Listening skills

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Makes eye contact
- Coos or babbles
- Responds to facial expressions

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Coo Back Conversation

CATEGORY:
Literacy

OBJECTIVE:
Respond to infant coos with turn-taking talk.

DESCRIPTION:
When infant coos or vocalizes, pause, then imitate and add a short word response.

MATERIALS:
Quiet space
Responsive caregiver voice

SETUP:
Choose an alert social window.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Leave long pauses; avoid flooding with talk.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Make gentle eye contact.
2. Wait for a coo.
3. Imitate the sound.
4. Add a short word like hi or yes.
5. Pause again for infant's turn.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Turn-taking
- Vocal play
- Attachment

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Vocalizes
- Watches mouth
- Takes another turn

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.
