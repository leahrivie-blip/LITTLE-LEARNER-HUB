WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants are naturally fascinated by faces, expressions, movement, and reflections. Throughout this week, babies will explore mirrors through sensory play, movement, songs, facial expressions, and social interactions. These experiences support self-awareness, visual tracking, communication development, emotional connections, and early cognitive skills while encouraging joyful exploration and discovery.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Promote self-awareness and identity development
- Encourage visual tracking and attention skills
- Support language development through face-to-face interactions
- Strengthen social-emotional connections
- Encourage reaching, grasping, and movement toward reflections
- Develop curiosity and exploration skills

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to spend time looking in mirrors with their infants. Talk about facial features, emotions, and expressions. Use mirrors during daily routines such as diaper changes and getting dressed to encourage communication and self-awareness.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe eye contact, social smiles, facial expression imitation, visual tracking, vocalizations, reaching attempts, curiosity, and engagement with reflections.

ADAPTATIONS:
Position mirrors at different angles and heights. Provide extra support for younger infants. Reduce stimulation if infants become overwhelmed and allow participation at each child's developmental level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Face Discovery

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage visual exploration and self-awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore their reflections while teachers identify facial features and encourage observation.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Floor mat

SETUP:
Place mirrors securely in the play area at infant eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Model language, point to facial features, and encourage interaction with reflections.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant near the mirror.
2. Point to the reflection.
3. Name facial features.
4. Smile and make expressions.
5. Encourage observation and interaction.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Self-awareness
- Visual attention
- Language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Watches facial movements
- Shows curiosity

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Big Mirror Face Study

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Study faces in a baby-safe mirror.

DESCRIPTION:
Sit together before a baby-safe mirror making slow facial expressions.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Mat

SETUP:
Secure mirror so it cannot tip.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep expressions gentle.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit in front of the mirror.
2. Point near baby's reflection.
3. Make a happy face.
4. Make a surprised face.
5. Wave at the mirror.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual attention
- Social imitation beginnings
- Self-awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Looks at caregiver face
- Reacts to expressions

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Window Mirror Light Play

CATEGORY:
Outdoor Play

OBJECTIVE:
Notice reflections in a safe window outdoors/indoors.

DESCRIPTION:
Stand by a safe window reflection or outdoor shaded glass door and play peek with reflections.

MATERIALS:
Safe window area
Shade

SETUP:
Ensure no hot surfaces or exit risks.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Hold securely near glass.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Approach window together.
2. Point to reflections.
3. Play a peek.
4. Narrate light and dark.
5. Step away to rest eyes.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Outdoor/indoor light awareness
- Reflection concepts
- Social play

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Looks at reflection
- Engages in peek
- Stays calm

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Secure hold near glass; supervise constantly; avoid direct harsh sun.
