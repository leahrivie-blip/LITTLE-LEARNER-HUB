WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore the world through their senses of sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste-safe exploration. Through play-based experiences, babies will engage with different textures, sounds, colors, movements, and caregiver interactions that support brain development, sensory processing, and early learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Encourage sensory exploration through play
- Strengthen visual tracking skills
- Develop listening and sound awareness
- Support reaching and grasping abilities
- Build positive caregiver-child interactions
- Promote curiosity and exploration

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about what babies are seeing, hearing, and touching throughout daily routines. Describe textures, sounds, and objects during play and caregiving moments.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, reactions to sounds, exploration of textures, engagement with sensory materials, and communication attempts through facial expressions, sounds, and movements.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide additional support positioning as needed. Reduce sensory input for infants who become overstimulated. Offer larger materials for easier grasping and exploration.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Texture Touch Exploration

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce infants to a variety of safe textures.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore soft, fuzzy, smooth, and bumpy materials through supervised sensory play.

MATERIALS:
Soft fabric squares
Textured fabric pieces

SETUP:
Arrange texture materials on a clean floor mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Describe each texture and encourage gentle exploration.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant comfortably on the mat.
2. Introduce one texture at a time.
3. Allow touching and exploration.
4. Name the texture being explored.
5. Observe infant reactions.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Sensory exploration
- Touch awareness
- Early language development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Reaches toward textures
- Shows preferences
- Explores with hands

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sound and Listen Rattles

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Develop awareness of sounds.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants listen to and watch rattles as they move and make sounds.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe rattles

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably or place on floor mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Shake rattles gently while describing sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show rattle to infant.
2. Shake slowly.
3. Pause and repeat.
4. Move rattle to different sides.
5. Encourage listening and tracking.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Auditory awareness
- Visual tracking
- Sensory engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Turns toward sounds
- Watches movement
- Smiles or vocalizes

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Silky Soft Stroke Path

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Explore gentle touch along arms and hands.

DESCRIPTION:
Caregiver strokes infant's hands and arms with silky and fleece fabrics.

MATERIALS:
Silky fabric
Fleece square
Mat

SETUP:
Wash hands and offer fabrics one at a time.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Always watch for overstimulation.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the silky fabric.
2. Stroke hand gently.
3. Pause for reaction.
4. Offer fleece contrast.
5. Finish with still hands and smile.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Tactile awareness
- Trust
- Body awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Notices touch
- Relaxes or alerts
- Looks at caregiver

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sound Bottle Watch

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Watch and listen to a sealed sound bottle.

DESCRIPTION:
Slowly tip a sealed sensory bottle while infant watches glitter or beads move and hears soft sound.

MATERIALS:
Sealed sensory bottle (infant-safe)
Mat

SETUP:
Inspect seal before every use.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Never leave bottle unattended.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Hold bottle in view.
2. Tip slowly.
3. Name look and listen.
4. Let infant bat the bottle.
5. Put bottle away securely.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual attention
- Auditory interest
- Cause and effect

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Tracks movement
- Listens
- Bats bottle

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Bottle must be permanently sealed; remain within arm's reach; inspect before use.
