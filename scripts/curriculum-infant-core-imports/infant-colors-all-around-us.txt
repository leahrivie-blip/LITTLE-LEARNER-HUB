TITLE:
Colors All Around Us

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–6 Months

THEME:
Colors

PLAN:
Free

published

Physical Development

Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore bright colors through sensory play, movement, music, and caregiver interactions. Throughout the week, babies will observe, reach for, and engage with colorful materials while building visual tracking, early communication, and sensory awareness skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop visual tracking skills
- Encourage reaching and grasping
- Support sensory exploration
- Build early language through color words
- Promote positive caregiver interactions

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Color scarves
Colored fabric squares
Baby-safe mirrors
Soft colored balls
Colorful rattles
Floor mats
Board books

VOCABULARY:
Red
Blue
Yellow
Green
Color
Bright
Rainbow

BOOKS:
Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See? | Bill Martin Jr.
Baby Colors | Roger Priddy
Color Zoo | Lois Ehlert

SONGS:
I Can Sing a Rainbow
The Color Song
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colors during everyday routines. Talk about colorful clothing, toys, foods, and household objects to build language connections.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, reaching attempts, engagement during songs, reactions to colorful materials, and early communication behaviors.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger graspable materials when needed. Adjust activity positioning to support each infant's developmental level and comfort.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rainbow Scarf Visual Tracking

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop visual tracking skills using colorful moving objects.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe brightly colored scarves moving through their visual field while teachers describe the colors being seen.

MATERIALS:
Rainbow scarves
Soft floor mat

SETUP:
Place infants comfortably on a floor mat in a quiet area.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Move scarves slowly and name colors while encouraging visual attention.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant comfortably.
2. Hold scarf within visual range.
3. Slowly move scarf left and right.
4. Pause frequently.
5. Repeat with different colors.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Attention skills
- Language exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Follows scarf with eyes
- Maintains attention
- Shows excitement through smiles or sounds

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Red Scarf Slow Track

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Track a bright red scarf with eyes.

DESCRIPTION:
Caregiver slowly moves a red scarf side to side while the infant watches from a comfortable position.

MATERIALS:
Red chiffon scarf
Floor mat
Support pillow

SETUP:
Place infant safely on mat within arm's reach.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Watch for cues to pause or rest.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Settle infant comfortably on back or side.
2. Hold the red scarf about 12 inches away.
3. Move slowly left to right.
4. Pause when eyes lock on.
5. Smile and name red softly.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual tracking
- Attention
- Caregiver bonding

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Tracks scarf briefly
- Focuses on red
- Shows calm interest

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.
