WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
This week focuses on looking, tracking, reaching, and warm caregiver talk with bright colors — not naming drills or crafts. Younger infants mostly look and listen; older infants may reach and grasp when ready.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Support visual attention and slow tracking with bright, simple materials
Offer supervised tummy-time looking with colorful anchors
Encourage reaching/grasping only when developmentally ready
Build connection through face-to-face talk and short songs
Share brief, visually clear board-book moments

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Large bright scarves (chiffon or soft fabric)
Soft colored cloth squares (too large to choke)
Baby-safe mirror
Large soft colored rattles/balls
Tummy-time mat
Simple color board books
Support pillow/roll for positioning

SONGS:

Twinkle Twinkle Little Star
Color Hello Song (LLH original)

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to name one bright color during a diaper change or walk — keep it playful, not a quiz.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Tracks a slow-moving color briefly
Looks between caregiver face and colorful object
Lifts head or reaches during tummy time when ready
Settles to voice during color songs

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger infants: closer, slower, shorter. Older infants: offer graspable large toys. Reduce stimulation if baby looks away.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bright Scarf Slow Track

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Support slow visual tracking with a bright moving scarf.

DESCRIPTION:
Baby watches a large bright scarf move slowly across their view while you name the color in a calm voice.

MATERIALS:
1–2 large bright scarves
Floor mat

SETUP:
Place baby comfortably on back or supported side on a mat.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Slower than you think — pause when eyes lock on.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
Look… see the bright red? Watching… watching.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Settle baby and gain eye contact first
2. Hold scarf about 8–12 inches away
3. Move slowly left, pause, then right
4. Name the color once or twice
5. Stop when baby looks away

LEARNING_GOALS:
Visual tracking
Attention
Language exposure

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Tracks scarf briefly; stays calm while looking.

VOCABULARY:
look
see
bright
red

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
Keep scarf away from face/neck; never leave draped on baby.

AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Infant 0–6 months (younger: look only; older: may bat scarf)

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Colorful Tummy Time Look

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage head lifting during awake tummy time with a colorful visual anchor.

DESCRIPTION:
During supervised tummy time, a bright toy or cloth sits in view to invite looking and brief head lifts.

MATERIALS:
Tummy-time mat
1 large bright toy or cloth
Optional support roll

SETUP:
Awake baby on tummy on firm mat; colorful item in front.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Chest-to-chest counts as tummy time for newborns.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
You are looking! Bright yellow is right here.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place baby on tummy when alert
2. Set bright item in easy view
3. Get face-to-face beside baby and talk
4. Celebrate any head lift or looking
5. Return to back for rest as needed

LEARNING_GOALS:
Neck strength
Visual engagement

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Lifts head briefly; looks toward colorful item.

VOCABULARY:
look
up
yellow

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
Tummy time only when awake and supervised. Back to sleep.

AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Infant 0–6 months (use chest-to-chest for very young infants)

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Face-to-Face Color Talk

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Connect caregiver face (best early visual target) with a simple color prop.

DESCRIPTION:
You hold baby close, smile, and slowly show one bright cloth while talking. Faces stay the main focus.

MATERIALS:
One bright cloth
Comfortable seating

SETUP:
Hold baby 8–12 inches from your face with cloth nearby.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Face first, prop second — never replace interaction with objects.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
Hi! I see you. Here is soft blue.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Gain eye contact and smile
2. Show cloth beside your face briefly
3. Name the color in a soft voice
4. Hide cloth and return to face play
5. End with a cuddle

LEARNING_GOALS:
Social engagement
Visual attention
Language

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Looks at face; glances toward cloth.

VOCABULARY:
hi
see
blue

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
Support head/neck; stop if baby turns away.

AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Infant 0–6 months
