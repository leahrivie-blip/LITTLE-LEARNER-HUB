WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will build strength, coordination, and confidence through developmentally appropriate tummy time experiences. Throughout the week, babies will explore colorful materials, mirrors, sounds, and sensory items that encourage head lifting, visual tracking, reaching, and early exploration while developing important gross motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Strengthen neck and shoulder muscles
- Encourage head lifting and visual tracking
- Support reaching and grasping skills
- Promote sensory exploration during tummy time
- Increase tolerance and enjoyment of tummy time experiences
- Build caregiver-child interactions through play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to provide several short tummy time opportunities throughout the day. Celebrate small successes such as head lifting, reaching, and longer participation times.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe head control, visual tracking, reaching attempts, tolerance for tummy time, engagement with materials, and interactions with caregivers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide rolled blankets or tummy time pillows for additional support. Reduce activity length when needed and gradually increase participation based on individual developmental readiness.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Tummy Time Discovery

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Encourage head lifting and visual attention during tummy time.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore their reflections while practicing head control and visual focus.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe mirror
Tummy time mat

SETUP:
Place mirror securely in front of infant during tummy time.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage infants to look toward the mirror and celebrate attempts to lift their heads.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant on tummy.
2. Position mirror in front of infant.
3. Encourage looking toward reflection.
4. Talk about what baby sees.
5. Praise all attempts to lift the head.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Head control
- Visual attention
- Self-awareness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Lifts head
- Looks toward mirror
- Maintains attention

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rolling Rattle Tummy Track

CATEGORY:
Gross Motor

OBJECTIVE:
Lift head to track a rattle during tummy time.

DESCRIPTION:
While infant is on tummy, slowly roll a soft rattle in an arc to encourage head lifting and turning.

MATERIALS:
Soft rolling rattle
Floor mat
Small support roll

SETUP:
Get on the floor at infant's eye level.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep bursts to 1–3 minutes.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant on tummy with chest support if needed.
2. Show the rattle.
3. Roll it slowly left.
4. Roll it slowly right.
5. Rest on back after a short burst.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Head control
- Visual tracking
- Strength

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Lifts head
- Turns toward rattle
- Tolerates tummy time

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
