WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore music, rhythm, movement, and sound through developmentally appropriate play experiences. Throughout the week, babies will listen to songs, experience gentle movements, explore musical instruments, and participate in caregiver-led activities that support sensory development, language exposure, and social connections.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Develop listening and sound awareness
- Encourage movement and body awareness
- Support language development through songs and repetition
- Promote sensory exploration through music
- Strengthen caregiver-child interactions
- Encourage participation in musical experiences

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to sing familiar songs during daily routines. Babies learn through repetition, rhythm, and hearing caregiver voices throughout the day.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe reactions to music, body movement, listening skills, engagement during songs, visual tracking, and social interactions during musical activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust volume levels for individual needs. Provide additional support during movement activities and allow infants to participate at their own developmental level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rattle Rhythm Fun

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Introduce infants to rhythm and sound awareness.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants listen to and watch rattles while exploring cause and effect through sound.

MATERIALS:
Baby-safe rattles

SETUP:
Seat infants comfortably or place on floor mats.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Shake rattles gently, model rhythm, and describe sounds.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Show the rattle to the infant.
2. Shake slowly near the infant.
3. Pause and repeat.
4. Move the rattle side to side.
5. Encourage visual tracking and listening.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Auditory awareness
- Visual tracking
- Sensory exploration

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Turns toward sounds
- Watches movement
- Smiles or vocalizes

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Heartbeat Drum Pat

CATEGORY:
Music & Movement

OBJECTIVE:
Feel gentle rhythm through light pats.

DESCRIPTION:
Pat a soft steady beat on a cushion while infant rests on your chest hearing your voice.

MATERIALS:
Soft cushion
Humming voice

SETUP:
Sit securely with infant on chest.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Keep pats feather-light.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Settle infant on your chest.
2. Pat a slow heartbeat rhythm.
3. Hum along.
4. Pause to feel stillness.
5. Kiss the top of the head.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Rhythm awareness
- Bonding
- Regulation

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Settles to rhythm
- Listens
- Shows calm

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.
