WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Young infants are naturally drawn to high-contrast black and white images. This week babies will explore bold patterns, shapes, and visual experiences designed to strengthen visual focus, tracking, attention, and early brain development. Through play-based activities, infants will observe, reach, listen, and interact with caregivers while building important developmental foundations.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
- Strengthen visual development through high-contrast experiences
- Encourage visual tracking and focus
- Promote attention and curiosity
- Support reaching and movement skills
- Build language through caregiver interactions
- Encourage social engagement during play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to show babies high-contrast images during playtime and diaper changes. Talk about what babies are seeing and provide opportunities to explore simple black and white patterns at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual focus, tracking abilities, attention span, reactions to patterns, reaching attempts, and engagement during activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Position materials closer for younger infants. Offer tummy time support rolls as needed. Reduce visual stimulation if infants become overstimulated.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
High-Contrast Card Exploration

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Develop visual focus and tracking skills.

DESCRIPTION:
Infants observe bold black and white pattern cards while practicing visual attention.

MATERIALS:
Black and white pattern cards
Soft floor mat

SETUP:
Place infants comfortably on a mat with cards nearby.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Hold and slowly move cards while describing what babies see.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Position infant comfortably.
2. Hold a card 8–12 inches away.
3. Allow infant to focus.
4. Slowly move card side to side.
5. Repeat with different patterns.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual focus
- Tracking skills
- Attention development

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Watches card closely
- Tracks movement
- Maintains visual attention

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger materials, shorter turns, and hand-over-hand support when needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise closely and keep materials developmentally safe for the age group.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Bold Card Gaze Garden

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Focus on black-and-white contrast cards.

DESCRIPTION:
Display one high-contrast card at a time about 8–12 inches from infant's eyes.

MATERIALS:
Black-and-white contrast cards
Card stand or hand hold
Mat

SETUP:
Use simple bold patterns only.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Watch for gaze aversion as a break cue.

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place infant comfortably.
2. Show first card.
3. Hold steady while infant gazes.
4. Slowly switch cards.
5. Smile and rest eyes.

LEARNING_GOALS:
- Visual focus
- Attention span
- Calm alertness

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
- Fixates on card
- Tracks slight movement
- Stays alert calmly

ADAPTATIONS:
Adjust positioning for each infant's comfort. Offer larger graspable materials and shorter bursts of engagement.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach. Use only infant-safe materials, avoid small parts, and never leave infants unattended on elevated surfaces.
