TITLE:
Fourth of July Celebration

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–6 Months

THEME:
Fourth of July

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore the Fourth of July through red, white, and blue sensory experiences, music, movement, visual exploration, and hands-on discovery. Activities focus on patriotic colors, stars, flags, summer fun, and social interaction while supporting early developmental skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore patriotic colors and textures
• Strengthen visual tracking skills
• Encourage sensory exploration
• Support language development through songs and stories
• Develop fine and gross motor skills through age-appropriate activities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Red, white, and blue scarves, soft stars, ribbons, sensory bags, mirrors, musical instruments, patriotic books, stuffed animals, textured fabrics

VOCABULARY:
Red, White, Blue, Star, Flag, Summer, Celebrate, Sparkle, Parade, Fireworks

BOOKS:
Red, White, and Boom!
Baby Loves America
You're My Little Firecracker
Stars and Stripes
My First Fourth of July

SONGS:
Yankee Doodle
You're a Grand Old Flag
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star
This Land Is Your Land
Red White and Blue

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about summer celebrations, patriotic colors, and family traditions. Share photos of classroom Fourth of July activities throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, social engagement, language development, and participation during music and movement activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual needs and comfort levels.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Fourth of July: Red White & Blue Discovery and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support fourth of july learning through red white & blue discovery.
Support fourth of july learning through patriotic scarf dance.
Support fourth of july learning through patriotic story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Red, White, Blue, Star, Flag, Summer

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Red, white, and blue scarves
Scarves, music player
Board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "Yankee Doodle" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to fourth of july.
End with a brief look at Red, White, and Boom! or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate fourth of july sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Red White & Blue Discovery
Engagement during Patriotic Scarf Dance
Engagement during Patriotic Story Time
Participation and interest in Fourth of July play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual needs and comfort levels.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Red White & Blue Discovery
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support fourth of july learning through red white & blue discovery.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore patriotic colors through touch and visual experiences.
MATERIALS:
Red, white, and blue scarves
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Red White & Blue Discovery before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model language and sensory exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place scarves within reach.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Describe colors.
4. Allow free exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Color recognition and sensory awareness.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Red White & Blue Discovery
Use of fourth of july vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–5 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
