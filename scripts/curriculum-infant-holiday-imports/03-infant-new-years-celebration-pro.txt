TITLE:
New Year's Celebration

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–6 Months

THEME:
New Year's

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will celebrate the New Year through sensory exploration, music, movement, colorful experiences, and social interaction. Activities focus on sparkling colors, counting down, festive sounds, and welcoming a new year while supporting developmental growth.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore new colors, sounds, and textures
• Strengthen sensory exploration skills
• Encourage social interaction and engagement
• Support language development through songs and stories
• Develop fine and gross motor skills through age-appropriate activities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Metallic scarves, ribbons, mirrors, sensory bags, musical instruments, soft stars, shiny pom-poms, books, bubbles

VOCABULARY:
New Year, Celebrate, Sparkle, Star, Count, Party, Happy, Friends, Music, Shine

BOOKS:
Happy New Year, Spot!
Squirrel's New Year's Resolution
Baby's First New Year
Happy New Year Everywhere
The Night Before New Year's

SONGS:
If You're Happy and You Know It
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star
The Countdown Song
Celebrate Together
Happy New Year Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about celebrations, special traditions, and new experiences. Share photos of classroom New Year's activities throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, language development, social engagement, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual developmental needs.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
New Year's: Sparkle Scarf Exploration and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support new year's learning through sparkle scarf exploration.
Support new year's learning through shiny scarf dance.
Support new year's learning through new year story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
New Year, Celebrate, Sparkle, Star, Count, Party

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Metallic scarves
Scarves, music player
Board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "If You're Happy and You Know It" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to new year's.
End with a brief look at Happy New Year, Spot! or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate new year's sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Sparkle Scarf Exploration
Engagement during Shiny Scarf Dance
Engagement during New Year Story Time
Participation and interest in New Year's play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual developmental needs.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Sparkle Scarf Exploration
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support new year's learning through sparkle scarf exploration.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore shiny scarves and colorful materials.
MATERIALS:
Metallic scarves
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Sparkle Scarf Exploration before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model descriptive language and encourage exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place scarves within reach.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Describe colors and textures.
4. Allow free exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and fine motor development.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Sparkle Scarf Exploration
Use of new year's vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–5 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
