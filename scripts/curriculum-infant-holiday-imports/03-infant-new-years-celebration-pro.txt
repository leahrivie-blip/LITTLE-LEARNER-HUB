TITLE:
New Year's Celebration

AGE_GROUP:
Infant

THEME:
New Year's

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will celebrate the New Year through sensory exploration, music, movement, colorful experiences, and social interaction. Activities focus on sparkling colors, counting down, festive sounds, and welcoming a new year while supporting developmental growth.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore new colors, sounds, and textures
• Strengthen sensory exploration skills
• Encourage social interaction and engagement
• Support language development through songs and stories
• Develop fine and gross motor skills through age-appropriate activities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Metallic scarves, ribbons, mirrors, sensory bags, musical instruments, soft stars, shiny pom-poms, books, bubbles

VOCABULARY:
New Year, Celebrate, Sparkle, Star, Count, Party, Happy, Friends, Music, Shine

BOOKS:
Happy New Year, Spot!
Squirrel's New Year's Resolution
Baby's First New Year
Happy New Year Everywhere
The Night Before New Year's

SONGS:
If You're Happy and You Know It
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star
The Countdown Song
Celebrate Together
Happy New Year Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about celebrations, special traditions, and new experiences. Share photos of classroom New Year's activities throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, language development, social engagement, and participation during group activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual developmental needs.
