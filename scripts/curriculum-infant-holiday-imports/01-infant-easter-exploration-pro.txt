TITLE:
Easter Exploration

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–6 Months

THEME:
Easter

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore Easter through colorful sensory experiences, spring-themed activities, music, movement, and hands-on discovery. Activities focus on baby chicks, bunnies, eggs, spring colors, and sensory exploration while supporting developmental growth.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore spring colors and textures
• Strengthen sensory exploration skills
• Encourage visual tracking and engagement
• Support language development through songs and stories
• Develop fine and gross motor skills through age-appropriate activities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic eggs, soft bunnies, chick toys, pastel scarves, sensory bags, mirrors, musical instruments, ribbons, Easter books

VOCABULARY:
Easter, Bunny, Egg, Chick, Basket, Spring, Hop, Yellow, Pink, Blue

BOOKS:
Baby's First Easter
Llama Llama Easter Egg
Happy Easter Mouse
You're My Little Bunny
Peek-a-Boo Bunny

SONGS:
Here Comes Peter Cottontail
Bunny Hop Song
Five Little Easter Eggs
The Easter Bunny Song
Spring Is Here

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about Easter traditions, springtime changes, and colorful decorations. Share photos of classroom Easter activities throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, social engagement, language development, and participation during music and movement experiences.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual needs and preferences.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Easter: Pastel Color Exploration and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support easter learning through pastel color exploration.
Support easter learning through pastel scarf dance.
Support easter learning through easter story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Easter, Bunny, Egg, Chick, Basket, Spring

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Pastel scarves, fabric squares
Pastel scarves, music player
Easter board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "Here Comes Peter Cottontail" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to easter.
End with a brief look at Baby's First Easter or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate easter sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Pastel Color Exploration
Engagement during Pastel Scarf Dance
Engagement during Easter Story Time
Participation and interest in Easter play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual needs and preferences.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Pastel Color Exploration
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support easter learning through pastel color exploration.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore soft pastel-colored materials through touch and sight.
MATERIALS:
Pastel scarves, fabric squares
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Pastel Color Exploration before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model language and sensory exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place materials within reach.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Describe colors and textures.
4. Allow free exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and color recognition.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Pastel Color Exploration
Use of easter vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–5 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
