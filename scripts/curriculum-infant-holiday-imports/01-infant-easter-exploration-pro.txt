TITLE:
Easter Exploration

AGE_GROUP:
Infant

THEME:
Easter

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore Easter through colorful sensory experiences, spring-themed activities, music, movement, and hands-on discovery. Activities focus on baby chicks, bunnies, eggs, spring colors, and sensory exploration while supporting developmental growth.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore spring colors and textures
• Strengthen sensory exploration skills
• Encourage visual tracking and engagement
• Support language development through songs and stories
• Develop fine and gross motor skills through age-appropriate activities

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic eggs, soft bunnies, chick toys, pastel scarves, sensory bags, mirrors, musical instruments, ribbons, Easter books

VOCABULARY:
Easter, Bunny, Egg, Chick, Basket, Spring, Hop, Yellow, Pink, Blue

BOOKS:
Baby's First Easter
Llama Llama Easter Egg
Happy Easter Mouse
You're My Little Bunny
Peek-a-Boo Bunny

SONGS:
Here Comes Peter Cottontail
Bunny Hop Song
Five Little Easter Eggs
The Easter Bunny Song
Spring Is Here

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about Easter traditions, springtime changes, and colorful decorations. Share photos of classroom Easter activities throughout the week.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, social engagement, language development, and participation during music and movement experiences.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger sensory materials for younger infants. Offer seated support during movement activities. Modify sensory experiences based on individual needs and preferences.

MONDAY

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Pastel Color Exploration
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore soft pastel-colored materials through touch and sight.
MATERIALS:
Pastel scarves, fabric squares
DIRECTIONS:
Place materials within reach.
Encourage touching and grasping.
Describe colors and textures.
Allow free exploration.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model language and sensory exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and color recognition.
