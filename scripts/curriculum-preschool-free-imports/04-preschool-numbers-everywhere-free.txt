WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore numbers and counting through hands-on play, songs, books, games, and everyday routines. Children will count, compare quantities, recognize numerals, and use numbers in pretend play while building mathematical thinking.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Count objects with one-to-one correspondence
Recognize and name numerals 1-10
Compare more, less, and same quantities
Use numbers in everyday contexts and pretend play
Practice fine motor skills through number activities
Build confidence with mathematical language

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Counting bears or counters
Number play dough mats
Dice and spinners
Number cards 1-10
Blocks and cubes
Clipboards
Play money
Measuring cups
Dot markers
Number line on floor

VOCABULARY:

number
more

BOOKS:
Ten Black Dots | Donald Crews
Chicka Chicka 1, 2, 3 | Bill Martin Jr.
How Do Dinosaurs Count to Ten? | Jane Yolen

SONGS:
Five Little Ducks
One, Two, Buckle My Shoe
The Ants Go Marching

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to count everyday items at home—stairs, snacks, toys. Ask children to find numbers on clocks, phones, and signs.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe counting accuracy, numeral recognition, quantity comparison, use of math language, and engagement in number play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide concrete objects for all counting tasks. Accept counting in child's home language. Offer number lines and visual supports.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Number Hunt in the Room
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Find numerals hidden in the classroom.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers search for number cards placed around the room and name each numeral found.
MATERIALS:
Number cards 1-10, clipboards, pencils
SETUP:
Hide number cards at child level before activity.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Support numeral naming and celebrate finds.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Explain that numbers are hiding around the room.
2. Search together and name each number found.
3. Mark found numbers on a clipboard.
4. Order found numbers from 1 to 10.
5. Place numbers on a class number line.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Numeral recognition
Active searching
Number sequence
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Names numerals
Participates in hunt
Orders numbers with support
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual steps, partner support, or a simplified materials set. Extend with an open-ended challenge for children ready for more.

Supervise tools and materials, review allergy-safe consumables, and keep pathways clear during active or outdoor play.
