WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will discover shapes in the environment through hunts, art, building, sorting, and pretend play. Children will name shapes, describe attributes, combine shapes to create designs, and find shapes in everyday objects.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify and name basic shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle)
Describe shape attributes such as sides and corners
Sort and classify objects by shape
Combine shapes in art and building
Find shapes in the environment
Use shape vocabulary during play

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Shape blocks and puzzles
Play dough and shape cutters
Shape sorting trays
Pattern blocks
Shape stencils
Geoboards (optional)
Shape picture cards
Building materials
Glue and paper shapes
Shape hunt clipboards

SONGS:
The Shape Song
Do You Know What Shape This Is?
Hop Like a Bunny (shape movement version)

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families to find shapes at home—in windows, plates, and signs. Take photos to share with the class.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe shape naming, sorting accuracy, attribute language, spatial reasoning in building, and shape finding in environment.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide shape word cards with pictures. Accept approximate shape names. Offer pre-cut shapes for gluing activities.
