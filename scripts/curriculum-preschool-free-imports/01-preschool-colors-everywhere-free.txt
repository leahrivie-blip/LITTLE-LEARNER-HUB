WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore colors through hands-on play, movement, art, songs, books, sensory experiences, and everyday discoveries. Throughout the week, children will identify, sort, match, mix, create, and talk about colors while developing language, problem-solving, fine motor skills, and creativity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify and explore a variety of colors in the environment
Build color vocabulary through play and conversation
Practice sorting, matching, and pattern skills with colors
Strengthen fine motor and gross motor development through color activities
Encourage creativity through open-ended art experiences
Promote social interaction and cooperative color play

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:

Washable paint and watercolor sets
Color sorting cups and trays
Pom-poms in assorted colors
Building blocks in primary colors
Color scarves and ribbons
Crayons, dot markers, and colored tape
Colored balls and beanbags
Sensory bin fillers and color picture cards
Paint store props (brushes, sample cards, smocks)

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colors during daily routines. Ask children to find colors at home, in the grocery store, outdoors, and during playtime.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe color recognition, matching and pattern skills, language use, participation in activities, fine motor development, and social interactions during color play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger materials for easier grasping. Offer hand-over-hand support when needed. Focus on exploration and exposure rather than requiring color mastery.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Color Hunt Adventure
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Introduce and explore colors found in the classroom.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers search for colorful objects around the room and place them into matching color baskets.
MATERIALS:
Color baskets, classroom objects, color picture cards
SETUP:
Place colored baskets around the room with a few sample objects in each.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide children as they search, identify, and sort colors.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce color baskets and name each color.
2. Invite children to find objects that match each basket color.
3. Place objects into baskets and discuss discoveries together.
4. Count how many items are in each basket.
5. Celebrate successful color finds.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Color recognition
Sorting skills
Language development
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Identifies colors
Matches objects to baskets
Participates in group activity
