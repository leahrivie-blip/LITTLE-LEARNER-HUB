WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore letters and sounds through playful literacy activities, sensory materials, songs, books, and hands-on building. Children will recognize letters, connect sounds to symbols, explore names, and build phonological awareness in meaningful contexts.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Recognize and name several letters
Connect beginning sounds to letters and objects
Explore letters in names and environmental print
Strengthen fine motor skills through letter formation activities
Build phonological awareness through songs and games
Develop confidence as emerging readers and writers

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Letter magnets and tiles
Play dough and letter mats
Sand or salt trays
Beginning sound picture cards
Alphabet blocks
Name cards
Letter stamps
Books with repetitive text
Clipboards and pencils

The Alphabet Tree | Leo Lionni

SONGS:
The Alphabet Song
Willoughby Wallaby Woo
Down by the Bay

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out letters on signs, packages, and books at home. Practice singing the alphabet song and finding letters in children's names.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe letter recognition, sound matching, name letter identification, fine motor letter formation, and engagement in literacy play.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer uppercase letters first if lowercase are challenging. Provide multi-sensory letter options and accept approximations in writing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Alphabet Circle Time
CATEGORY:
Literacy
OBJECTIVE:
Introduce the alphabet through song and visual cards.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers sing the alphabet song and explore letter cards together.
MATERIALS:
Alphabet chart, letter cards, pointer
SETUP:
Post alphabet chart at circle and distribute letter cards.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Lead song, track letters on chart, and invite participation.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Sing the alphabet song while pointing to letters.
2. Give select children a letter card to hold up.
3. Call children to stand when their letter is sung.
4. Review a few focus letters for the week.
5. Place letter cards in the literacy center.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Letter exposure
Alphabet sequence awareness
Group participation
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Sings along
Recognizes some letters
Attends during circle
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual steps, partner support, or a simplified materials set. Extend with an open-ended challenge for children ready for more.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise tools and materials, review allergy-safe consumables, and keep pathways clear during active or outdoor play.
