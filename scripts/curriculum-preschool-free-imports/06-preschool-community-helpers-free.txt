WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers explore many people who help a community — healthcare, fire/rescue, mail, grocery, construction, library, sanitation, and teachers — through dramatic play, process art, movement, and conversation. Anyone can do these jobs.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name a variety of community helpers and what they do
Use helper vocabulary in dramatic play
Practice cooperation and gratitude
Explore tools/places helpers use through play (not worksheets)
Create process-based art inspired by helpers (child-directed results)

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Helper hats/vests (assorted roles)
Doctor kit (play)
Mailbags, envelopes, stickers
Play food / grocery props
Safe construction tools (plastic) + blocks
Library props (books, stamp, cards)
Recycle/sort bins
Collage materials (red/yellow/black/blue paper, tape, glue sticks)
Helper picture cards (printable draft)
Community map / place signs (printable draft)

BOOKS:

SONGS:
Helpers in Our Neighborhood (LLH original)
The Wheels on the Bus
Thank You Helpers Chant (LLH original)

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to name one helper they noticed this week (no need to visit). Optional photo of a helper place from the neighborhood.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Uses helper role vocabulary in play
Cooperates in dramatic play
Connects tools/places to jobs
Shows gratitude language

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer picture cards for vocabulary. Dictate thank-you messages. Provide quieter helper stations. Present diverse photos of helpers of many genders/backgrounds.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Community Helper Discovery Basket

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration

OBJECTIVE:
Explore helper props and photos to build job vocabulary.

DESCRIPTION:
Children examine hats, tools, and helper cards from a basket and talk about who uses them.

MATERIALS:
Basket
Assorted helper props
Helper picture cards (printable)

SETUP:
Circle or small-group basket at child height.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Include library, mail, sanitation, grocery — not only emergency jobs.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
Who might use this tool? What job helps our community?

DIRECTIONS:
1. Pull one item and wonder aloud who might use it
2. Invite children to try hats/props
3. Match a prop to a helper card if they choose
4. Share which helper they want to play today
5. Return props to basket

LEARNING_GOALS:
Vocabulary
Curiosity

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Names helpers; matches tool to role; handles props safely.

VOCABULARY:
helper
tool
job
community

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
No sharp tools; supervise small props.

Preschool 3–5

ACTIVITY_NAME:

Create open-ended collage inspired by rescue/safety helpers using color choices children control.

DESCRIPTION:

MATERIALS:
Construction paper bits
Glue sticks/tape
Crayons/markers
Smocks
Optional helper photos for inspiration only

SETUP:
Art table with collage tray and blank paper.

TEACHER_ROLE:

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
What colors help tell your helper story?

1. Look briefly at helper photos (inspiration only)

3. Tear, stick, and draw your own ideas

5. Place on drying rack — every piece looks different

Process art
Fine motor
