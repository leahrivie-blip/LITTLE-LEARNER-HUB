WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will learn about community helpers who keep neighborhoods safe, healthy, and running smoothly. Through dramatic play, books, movement, and cooperative activities, children explore helper roles, tools, and ways to thank people who help.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name several community helpers and describe their jobs
Use helper vocabulary during pretend play
Practice cooperation and problem-solving
Build gratitude language for helpers
Explore tools helpers use safely
Connect helpers to places in the community

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Helper hats and vests
Doctor kit and bandages
Play food and kitchen tools
Mailbags and envelopes
Clipboards and pencils
Tool props (safe plastic)
Community map poster
Toy phones
Helper picture cards
Thank-you card materials

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Invite families to share names of helpers in their family or neighborhood for a class helper chart.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe role vocabulary, cooperative play, gratitude language, and ability to connect helpers to community places.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer picture cards for helper names. Provide dictated thank-you options for emerging writers. Create quieter helper stations for children who need less noise.

MONDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Community Helper Discovery Basket
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Explore helper tools and uniforms in a discovery basket.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers examine helper hats, tools, and photos from a sensory basket.
MATERIALS:
Helper basket with hats, tools, photos
SETUP:
Fill basket with safe helper props.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Name each item and its helper.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Sit in circle and explore basket items one at a time.
2. Try on hats and name helper jobs.
3. Match tools to helper photos.
4. Share which helper they want to be.
5. Return items to basket.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Helper vocabulary
Tool recognition
Curiosity
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Names helpers
Matches tools to roles
Handles props safely
