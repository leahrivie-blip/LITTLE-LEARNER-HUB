WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will observe and explore weather patterns through charts, experiments, movement, art, and dramatic play. Children will describe weather conditions, notice changes, and connect weather to clothing and activities.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Observe and describe daily weather conditions
Use weather vocabulary such as sunny, rainy, cloudy, and windy
Record weather on a class chart
Explore weather through sensory and science activities
Connect weather to appropriate clothing and safety
Develop curiosity about seasonal changes

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Weather chart and symbols
Thermometer (safe demo)
Rain sticks and water bins
Cotton balls for clouds
Blue paper and paint for rain art
Dress-up rain gear
Pinwheels and fans
Weather books
Clipboards
Season picture cards

SONGS:

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Families can watch the weather together each morning and report observations at drop-off for the class chart.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe weather vocabulary, chart participation, descriptive language, engagement in weather experiments, and connections to clothing choices.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real weather photos for support. Offer alternate indoor activities during extreme weather days. Accept one-word weather descriptions.
