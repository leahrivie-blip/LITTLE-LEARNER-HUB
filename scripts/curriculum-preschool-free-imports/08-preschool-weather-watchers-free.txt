WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers become weather watchers who observe, record, and investigate sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, and stormy weather. Clothing choices, safety, and process art support real outdoor noticing — not elementary meteorology worksheets.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Observe and describe daily weather using child-friendly vocabulary
Record weather on a simple class chart
Investigate wind, water, and sound through play experiments
Connect weather to clothing and safety choices
Express weather ideas through process art and movement

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Weather chart + symbol cards (printable draft)
Clipboards
Pinwheels / scarves / small fan (teacher-controlled)
Water/rain sensory bin tools
Dress-up weather gear
Paint, paper, cotton/cloud materials for process art
Drums/metal pans for thunder sound play
Season/clothing cards (printable draft)
Books about weather

FAMILY_CONNECTION:

Participates in investigations

ADAPTATIONS:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Weather Watchers Circle

CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery

OBJECTIVE:
Observe outdoors/window weather and record it on the class chart.

DESCRIPTION:
Children look outside, describe sky/air, and place a weather symbol on today’s chart square.

MATERIALS:
Weather chart
Symbol cards (printable)
Window/door access

SETUP:
Gather near window with chart.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Do this daily — repetition builds the science habit.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
What do you observe about today’s weather?

DIRECTIONS:
1. Look outside together
2. Share what you notice (sky, wet, wind)
3. Choose a weather symbol
4. Mark warm/cool with a simple choice
5. Predict tomorrow in one word

LEARNING_GOALS:
Observation
Charting
Vocabulary

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Describes weather; places symbol; uses weather words.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Rain Drop Sensory Investigate

CATEGORY:
Sensory Play

OBJECTIVE:
Investigate water ‘rain’ with tools and describe how it moves.

DESCRIPTION:
Children drip, pour, and splash in a shallow rain bin using cups, eyedroppers, and waterproof figures.

MATERIALS:
Shallow water bin
Cups
Eyedroppers/basters
Towels
Smocks
Optional rain stick

SETUP:
Sensory table rain station.

TEACHER_ROLE:
Add ice on hot days for temperature talk.

TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
What do you notice when the rain falls from higher up?

DIRECTIONS:
1. Listen to rain stick or rain sounds talk
2. Drip water like rain
3. Pour from high/low — what changes?
4. Use words wet/splash/drip
5. Dry hands and reset tools

LEARNING_GOALS:
Science inquiry
Sensory
Language

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Pours with control; uses descriptive words; collaborates.

VOCABULARY:
rain
drip
pour
wet

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:

SAFETY_NOTES:
Shallow water only; mop spills; no drinking bin water.

AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Preschool 3–5

ACTIVITY_NAME:

DESCRIPTION:
Children compare drum/pan sounds as ‘thunder’ play, then practice calm-down and indoor-safety talk.
