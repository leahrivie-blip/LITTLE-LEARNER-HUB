WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore farm life through animal sounds, care routines, sensory bins, books, songs, and dramatic play. Children will name farm animals, describe what they provide, and practice caring for living things.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name common farm animals and their sounds
Describe products animals provide such as milk and eggs
Practice caring routines for animals
Use farm vocabulary in songs and play
Sort and classify farm animals
Build empathy for animal care

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic farm animals
Barn playset or cardboard barn
Hay or straw sensory filler
Mud play dough
Farm picture books
Egg cartons (empty)
Buckets and brushes
Farm animal puzzles
Market baskets
Farm sound cards

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Visit a farmers market or watch farm animals online with family. Discuss foods that come from farms.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal naming, sound imitation, caring play, sorting skills, and use of farm vocabulary.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real photos alongside toy animals. Supervise sensory bins closely. Offer single-animal focus for overwhelmed children.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Farm Animal Discovery Basket
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Explore farm animals and textures in a basket.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers touch animals, hay, and eggs while naming farm creatures.
MATERIALS:
Farm animals, hay, fabric eggs, basket
SETUP:
Fill basket with safe farm items.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Name animals and describe textures.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Reach into basket and choose item.
2. Name animal or object.
3. Describe how it feels.
4. Match animal to sound card.
5. Return items gently.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Farm vocabulary
Sensory exploration
Animal-sound link
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Names animals
Imitates sounds
Handles materials gently
