WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore who they are through activities focused on their names, families, bodies, feelings, preferences, and unique characteristics. Through books, songs, art, mirrors, movement, and discussions, children will build self-awareness, confidence, communication skills, and positive relationships with others.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop self-awareness and confidence
Learn and recognize their names and letters in names
Explore body parts and facial features
Build emotional awareness and vocabulary
Strengthen communication skills
Celebrate similarities and differences among peers

Provide visual supports, family photos, and individualized assistance as needed. Encourage participation at each child's comfort level.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mirror Me
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Develop self-awareness through observation.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers explore their reflections and identify facial features using mirrors.
MATERIALS:
Child-safe mirrors, emotion cards
SETUP:
Place mirrors at child eye level with emotion cards nearby.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and encourage observation.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Invite children to look into mirrors.
2. Point out eyes, nose, mouth, and ears.
3. Encourage children to identify features on themselves.
4. Make faces to match emotion cards.
5. Discuss what makes each face unique.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-awareness
Language development
Observation skills
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Identifies facial features
Engages with reflection
Uses vocabulary
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual steps, partner support, or a simplified materials set. Extend with an open-ended challenge for children ready for more.
