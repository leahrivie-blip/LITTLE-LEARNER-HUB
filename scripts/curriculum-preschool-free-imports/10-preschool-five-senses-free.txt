WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste through safe sensory stations, experiments, games, and discussions. Children will name the five senses and describe how each helps them learn about the world.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name the five senses and body parts used
Describe objects using sensory language
Compare and sort by sensory attributes
Participate safely in taste-safe explorations
Conduct simple sensory experiments
Connect senses to daily experiences

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Smell jars (lemon, cinnamon, vanilla)
Texture boards
Sound shakers
Blindfolds (optional)
Magnifying glasses
Taste-safe samples
Feely bags
Color paddles
Listening walk clipboards
Sense picture cards

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Families can play I Spy and listening games at home. Talk about which sense helps with each activity.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory vocabulary, willingness to try explorations, descriptive language, sorting by attribute, and safe participation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Avoid known allergens in smell and taste activities. Offer opt-out for smell jars. Use taste-safe items only.

MONDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Five Senses Circle Intro
CATEGORY:
Literacy
OBJECTIVE:
Introduce five senses and body parts used.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers sing head song and match sense cards to eyes, ears, skin, nose, tongue.
MATERIALS:
Sense cards, body chart
SETUP:
Post body chart at circle.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model matching sense to body part.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Sing Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes.
2. Hold up see card to eyes.
3. Match hear, touch, smell, taste cards.
4. Children repeat matches.
5. Place cards on science shelf.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sense naming
Body part association
Group participation
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Names five senses
Points to body parts
Matches cards

Open-Ended Exploration

Play I Spy using colored paddles to focus sight.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers look through color paddles and spy matching color items.

Visual discrimination

Turn-taking game
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:

Gives clear clue
Guesses peer clues

TUESDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Mystery Sound Match
CATEGORY:
Fine Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Match shakers to picture cards by sound.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers shake sealed containers and match to pictures of rice, bells, or beans.
MATERIALS:
Sound shakers, matching picture cards
SETUP:
Prepare identical-looking shakers with different fills.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Encourage quiet listening.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Shake one shaker.
2. Listen carefully.
3. Match to picture card.
4. Explain what might be inside.
5. Trade shakers with peer.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Auditory discrimination
Matching by sound
Hypothesis language
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Discriminates sounds
Makes logical match
Describes sound

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Listening Walk
CATEGORY:
Gross Motor
OBJECTIVE:
Walk quietly and note sounds heard indoors or out.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers walk with clipboards and mark pictures when they hear matching sounds.
MATERIALS:
Listening walk clipboard, pencils
SETUP:
Prepare picture checklist of common sounds.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Demonstrate quiet walking.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Walk quietly together.
2. Mark picture when sound heard.
3. Whisper name of sound.
4. Compare checklists after walk.
5. Discuss loudest and softest sounds.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Auditory awareness
Quiet body control
Recording observations
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Walks quietly
Marks heard sounds
Compares with peers

Sound Story with Instruments
