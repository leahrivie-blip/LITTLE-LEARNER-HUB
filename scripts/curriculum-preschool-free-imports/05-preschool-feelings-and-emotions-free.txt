WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore feelings and emotions through stories, art, movement, discussions, and pretend play. Children will name emotions, recognize feelings in themselves and others, and practice helpful strategies for managing strong feelings.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify and name common emotions
Recognize emotional expressions on faces
Connect feelings to experiences and stories
Practice calming and coping strategies
Build empathy through discussion and role play
Express feelings through art and movement

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Emotion cards and posters
Mirrors
Feeling puppets
Calm-down bottles
Paper and crayons
Books about feelings
Yarn for faces
Play dough
Comfort corner materials
Scenario picture cards

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about feelings during daily routines. Share strategies that help your child calm down at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe emotion vocabulary, recognition of facial expressions, use of coping strategies, empathy in peer interactions, and participation in discussions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer calm-down tools and accept all feeling words. Provide picture supports and allow children to pass during sharing.

MONDAY:

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Circle Check-In
CATEGORY:
Circle Time
OBJECTIVE:
Begin identifying feelings during morning circle.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers choose a feeling face card that matches how they feel today.
MATERIALS:
Feeling face cards, circle chart
SETUP:
Place feeling cards in center of circle.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model choosing a feeling and naming it.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Greet children and show feeling cards.
2. Choose your own feeling and explain why.
3. Invite each child to select a card.
4. Group similar feelings together.
5. Place cards on check-in chart.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Feeling identification
Expressive language
Group belonging
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Selects a feeling card
Names feeling
Listens to peers

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Emotion Face Art

Art

Draw and paint faces showing different emotions.

Preschoolers create emotion faces using paper plates and art materials.
MATERIALS:
Paper plates, crayons, yarn, glue, mirrors

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Movement Mirrors
CATEGORY:
Music & Movement
OBJECTIVE:
Use body to show emotions during music.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers move their bodies to express happy, sad, angry, and calm feelings.
MATERIALS:
Music player, emotion cards
SETUP:
Open space with emotion cards visible.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Call an emotion and model movement.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Call happy and invite bouncy movement.
2. Call sad with slow movement.
3. Call angry with strong stomps then calm.
4. Call calm with deep breaths.
5. End with gentle hugs or self-hugs.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Body-emotion connection
Movement expression
Self-regulation
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Matches movement to emotion
Transitions to calm
Participates safely

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Calm Down Bottle Exploration
CATEGORY:
STEM/Discovery
OBJECTIVE:
Observe glitter settling as a calming strategy.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers shake calm-down bottles and watch glitter settle while breathing slowly.
MATERIALS:
Calm-down bottles, comfortable seating
SETUP:
Place bottles in calm corner with seating.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Guide slow breathing as glitter settles.
DIRECTIONS:
1. Shake bottle and watch glitter swirl.
2. Breathe slowly while glitter settles.
3. Notice how body feels calmer.
4. Try again with a partner.
5. Return bottles to calm corner.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Self-regulation strategy
Observation
Mindful breathing
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Uses slow breathing
Waits for glitter to settle
Names feeling before and after

Preschoolers take turns acting out emotions while others guess.
