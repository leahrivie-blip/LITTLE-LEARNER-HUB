WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Preschoolers will explore feelings and emotions through stories, art, movement, discussions, and pretend play. Children will name emotions, recognize feelings in themselves and others, and practice helpful strategies for managing strong feelings.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify and name common emotions
Recognize emotional expressions on faces
Connect feelings to experiences and stories
Practice calming and coping strategies
Build empathy through discussion and role play
Express feelings through art and movement

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Emotion cards and posters
Mirrors
Feeling puppets
Calm-down bottles
Paper and crayons
Books about feelings
Yarn for faces
Play dough
Comfort corner materials
Scenario picture cards

SONGS:

Skidamarink

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about feelings during daily routines. Share strategies that help your child calm down at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe emotion vocabulary, recognition of facial expressions, use of coping strategies, empathy in peer interactions, and participation in discussions.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer calm-down tools and accept all feeling words. Provide picture supports and allow children to pass during sharing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Feelings Circle Check-In
CATEGORY:
Circle Time
OBJECTIVE:
Begin identifying feelings during morning circle.
DESCRIPTION:
Preschoolers choose a feeling face card that matches how they feel today.
MATERIALS:
Feeling face cards, circle chart
SETUP:
Place feeling cards in center of circle.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model choosing a feeling and naming it.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Greet children and show feeling cards.
2. Choose your own feeling and explain why.
3. Invite each child to select a card.
4. Group similar feelings together.
5. Place cards on check-in chart.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Feeling identification
Expressive language
Group belonging
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Selects a feeling card
Names feeling
Listens to peers
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual steps, partner support, or a simplified materials set. Extend with an open-ended challenge for children ready for more.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Supervise tools and materials, review allergy-safe consumables, and keep pathways clear during active or outdoor play.
