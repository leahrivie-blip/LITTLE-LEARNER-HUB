TITLE:
Caring Hearts

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Family Connections — Caring Hearts

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers explore kindness, empathy, and caring through helping, sharing, comforting, art, music, books, and daily routines.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Recognize and express kind behaviors.
Build empathy for others.
Strengthen communication and social-emotional skills.
Develop fine and gross motor skills.
Create process art with expression.
Practice helping during classroom routines.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Baby dolls
Stuffed animals
Doctor kit
Bandages
Blankets
Washcloths
Toy kitchen
Play food
Blocks
Playdough
Washable paint
Cardstock
Dot markers
Stickers
Glue sticks
Sensory bin
Pom-poms
Scoops
Instruments
Scarves
Bubble machine

VOCABULARY:
Kind
Help
Share
Friend
Care

BOOKS:
Kindness Makes Us Strong | Pat Zietlow Miller
Hands Are Not for Hitting | Martine Agassi
Llama Llama Time to Share | Anna Dewdney
Bear Says Thanks | Karma Wilson
Have You Filled a Bucket Today? | Carol McCloud

SONGS:
Skidamarink
The More We Get Together
If You're Happy and You Know It
You Are My Sunshine
This Little Light of Mine

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Do one act of kindness together each day and share a favorite kindness moment with the class.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe kindness behaviors, sharing, kind words, dramatic play, fine and gross motor skills, communication, and emotional regulation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): simple helping tasks, teacher-modeled sharing, larger manipulatives, and brief groups. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): role-play conversations, cooperative games, independent helping, and expanded feelings talk.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Care for Baby Dolls
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Feed, rock, and comfort baby dolls with blankets.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers feed, rock, and cover baby dolls with blankets.
MATERIALS:
Baby dolls
Blankets
Bottles
Toy food
SETUP:
Prepare materials at child height and clear enough space for movement before toddlers arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay nearby, model language, and support toddlers as they explore, share, and try new skills.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. family. You are trying. I see you working. We can do it together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Set out materials at child height and invite toddlers to explore.
2. Model the first step with simple words and gestures.
3. Support each child as they try, naming actions and vocabulary.
4. Encourage turn-taking, effort, and peer awareness when appropriate.
5. Clean up together and transition with a short song or routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Dramatic Play skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, motor skills, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Care, Baby, Kind, Help
EXTENSIONS:
Invite children to revisit the activity later during choice time or with a peer partner.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer more one-on-one support and simpler steps for younger toddlers; extend conversations and independence for older toddlers.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable, toddler-safe materials. Supervise closely during movement, sensory, and art activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): shorter groups, larger manipulatives, more teacher modeling, and simpler choices. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): longer conversations, sorting/matching, cooperative play, and more independent exploration.

OBJECTIVE:
Dance to kindness and friendship songs.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers dance to kindness and friendship songs.
MATERIALS:
Music player
Scarves
SETUP:
Prepare materials at child height and clear enough space for movement before toddlers arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay nearby, model language, and support toddlers as they explore, share, and try new skills.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. family. You are trying. I see you working. We can do it together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Set out materials at child height and invite toddlers to explore.
2. Model the first step with simple words and gestures.
3. Support each child as they try, naming actions and vocabulary.
4. Encourage turn-taking, effort, and peer awareness when appropriate.
5. Clean up together and transition with a short song or routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Music & Movement skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, motor skills, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Dance, Kind, Happy, Friend
EXTENSIONS:
Invite children to revisit the activity later during choice time or with a peer partner.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer more one-on-one support and simpler steps for younger toddlers; extend conversations and independence for older toddlers.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable, toddler-safe materials. Supervise closely during movement, sensory, and art activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): shorter groups, larger manipulatives, more teacher modeling, and simpler choices. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): longer conversations, sorting/matching, cooperative play, and more independent exploration.
