TITLE:
We Belong Together

AGE_GROUP:
Toddler

THEME:
Family Connections — We Belong Together

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Physical Development, Science, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Toddlers celebrate friendships and relationships at school through cooperative play, sharing, and kindness that creates a caring classroom community where everyone belongs.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build friendships with classmates.
Practice sharing and taking turns.
Strengthen communication and cooperation.
Develop fine and gross motor skills.
Create open-ended art together.
Build confidence and a sense of belonging.

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Large butcher paper
Washable paint
Dot markers
Crayons
Glue sticks
Stickers
Blocks
Baby dolls
Instruments
Parachute or sheet
Bubbles
Bean bags
Balls
Playdough
Cardboard boxes
Scarves
Family and classroom photos
Friendship books
Sensory bin materials

VOCABULARY:
Friend
Together
Share
Help
Belong

BOOKS:
The Invisible String | Patrice Karst
Best Friends Busy Friends | Susan Rollings
Should I Share My Ice Cream? | Mo Willems
The More We Get Together | Maryann Cocca-Leffler
Friends Stick Together | Hannah E. Harrison

SONGS:
The More We Get Together
If You're Happy and You Know It
Skidamarink
Make New Friends
Hokey Pokey

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Ask families about a new friend this month and continue kindness, helping, and friendship talk at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe cooperative play, sharing, kind words, group participation, friendship building, fine and gross motor skills, and communication.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): shorter groups, simple partner play, larger materials, and extra support. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): cooperative problem-solving, peer conversations, independent choices, and extended dramatic play.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Build a Block Town Together
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play
OBJECTIVE:
Build a town together with blocks.
DESCRIPTION:
Toddlers build a block town together with teacher support.
MATERIALS:
Wooden blocks
Toy people
Cars (optional)
SETUP:
Prepare materials at child height and clear enough space for movement before toddlers arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay nearby, model language, and support toddlers as they explore, share, and try new skills.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. family. You are trying. I see you working. We can do it together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Set out materials at child height and invite toddlers to explore.
2. Model the first step with simple words and gestures.
3. Support each child as they try, naming actions and vocabulary.
4. Encourage turn-taking, effort, and peer awareness when appropriate.
5. Clean up together and transition with a short song or routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Dramatic Play skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, motor skills, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Build, Town, Friend, Together
EXTENSIONS:
Invite children to revisit the activity later during choice time or with a peer partner.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer more one-on-one support and simpler steps for younger toddlers; extend conversations and independence for older toddlers.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable, toddler-safe materials. Supervise closely during movement, sensory, and art activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): shorter groups, larger manipulatives, more teacher modeling, and simpler choices. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): longer conversations, sorting/matching, cooperative play, and more independent exploration.

SETUP:
Prepare materials at child height and clear enough space for movement before toddlers arrive.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Stay nearby, model language, and support toddlers as they explore, share, and try new skills.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"Look. family. You are trying. I see you working. We can do it together."
DIRECTIONS:
1. Set out materials at child height and invite toddlers to explore.
2. Model the first step with simple words and gestures.
3. Support each child as they try, naming actions and vocabulary.
4. Encourage turn-taking, effort, and peer awareness when appropriate.
5. Clean up together and transition with a short song or routine.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Music & Movement skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, motor skills, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Sing, Dance, Celebrate, Together
EXTENSIONS:
Invite children to revisit the activity later during choice time or with a peer partner.
ADAPTATIONS:
Offer more one-on-one support and simpler steps for younger toddlers; extend conversations and independence for older toddlers.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use washable, toddler-safe materials. Supervise closely during movement, sensory, and art activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Toddlers (12–24 Months): shorter groups, larger manipulatives, more teacher modeling, and simpler choices. Older Toddlers (24–36 Months): longer conversations, sorting/matching, cooperative play, and more independent exploration.
