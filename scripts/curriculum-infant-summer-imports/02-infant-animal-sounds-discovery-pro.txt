TITLE:
Animal Sounds Discovery

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–12 Months

THEME:
Animal Sounds

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore animal sounds through music, stories, sensory play, visual discovery, movement, and communication activities. Children will listen to familiar animal sounds, observe animal pictures, and engage in playful experiences that support early language development and auditory awareness.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore animal sounds through listening experiences
• Strengthen auditory awareness and attention
• Support language development through sound imitation
• Encourage sensory exploration and visual tracking
• Foster curiosity about animals and communication

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Animal picture cards, stuffed animals, mirrors, music player, animal sound recordings, sensory bags, scarves, puppets, board books

VOCABULARY:
Dog, Cat, Cow, Duck, Bird, Animal, Sound, Bark, Moo, Quack

BOOKS:
Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See?
Baby Animal Sounds
Moo Baa La La La
Dear Zoo
Farm Animals

SONGS:
Old MacDonald Had a Farm
Bingo
Five Little Ducks
The Animal Sounds Song
Baby Beluga

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out animals and imitate animal sounds during everyday routines and story time.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe listening skills, sound imitation attempts, visual tracking, communication development, social engagement, and participation during activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, additional sensory materials, adaptive positioning, and individualized support based on developmental needs.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Animal Sounds: Animal Discovery Basket and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support animal sounds learning through animal discovery basket.
Support animal sounds learning through animal song circle.
Support animal sounds learning through animal story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Dog, Cat, Cow, Duck, Bird, Animal

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Stuffed animals, animal cards
Music player
Board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "Old MacDonald Had a Farm" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to animal sounds.
End with a brief look at Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See? or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate animal sounds sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Animal Discovery Basket
Engagement during Animal Song Circle
Engagement during Animal Story Time
Participation and interest in Animal Sounds play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, additional sensory materials, adaptive positioning, and individualized support based on developmental needs.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Animal Discovery Basket
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support animal sounds learning through animal discovery basket.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore stuffed animals and animal pictures through touch and sight.
MATERIALS:
Stuffed animals, animal cards
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Animal Discovery Basket before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and animal sounds.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Present animal materials.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Name each animal.
4. Allow exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Language development and sensory exploration.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Animal Discovery Basket
Use of animal sounds vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–8 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
