TITLE:
Summer Colors

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–12 Months

THEME:
Summer Colors

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore bright summer colors through sensory experiences, movement, music, visual discovery, and hands-on play. Children will investigate colorful summer objects, nature items, and playful materials while developing curiosity and early learning skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore colors through sensory experiences
• Strengthen visual tracking and observation skills
• Support language development through color vocabulary
• Encourage fine and gross motor development
• Foster curiosity through colorful exploration

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Colorful scarves, textured balls, sensory bags, mirrors, flowers, beach balls, ribbons, music player, color cards, bubbles

VOCABULARY:
Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple, Bright, Color, Summer, Rainbow

BOOKS:
Baby Colors
Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See?
Bright Baby Colors
Hello Colors
Colorful Summer

SONGS:
The Rainbow Song
Red Yellow Green Blue
You Are My Sunshine
If You're Happy and You Know It
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colorful objects, flowers, toys, and nature items during everyday routines and outdoor adventures.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, color preferences, communication attempts, sensory exploration, social engagement, and participation during activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide high-contrast visuals, alternative sensory materials, adaptive positioning, and individualized support as needed.
