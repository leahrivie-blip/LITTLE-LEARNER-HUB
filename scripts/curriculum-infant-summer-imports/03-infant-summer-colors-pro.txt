TITLE:
Summer Colors

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–12 Months

THEME:
Summer Colors

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore bright summer colors through sensory experiences, movement, music, visual discovery, and hands-on play. Children will investigate colorful summer objects, nature items, and playful materials while developing curiosity and early learning skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore colors through sensory experiences
• Strengthen visual tracking and observation skills
• Support language development through color vocabulary
• Encourage fine and gross motor development
• Foster curiosity through colorful exploration

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Colorful scarves, textured balls, sensory bags, mirrors, flowers, beach balls, ribbons, music player, color cards, bubbles

VOCABULARY:
Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple, Bright, Color, Summer, Rainbow

BOOKS:
Baby Colors
Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See?
Bright Baby Colors
Hello Colors
Colorful Summer

SONGS:
The Rainbow Song
Red Yellow Green Blue
You Are My Sunshine
If You're Happy and You Know It
Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to point out colorful objects, flowers, toys, and nature items during everyday routines and outdoor adventures.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe visual tracking, color preferences, communication attempts, sensory exploration, social engagement, and participation during activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide high-contrast visuals, alternative sensory materials, adaptive positioning, and individualized support as needed.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Summer Colors: Red Discovery Basket and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support summer colors learning through red discovery basket.
Support summer colors learning through red song time.
Support summer colors learning through color story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Red toys, red scarves, red textured items
Music player, red scarves
Color-themed board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "The Rainbow Song" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to summer colors.
End with a brief look at Baby Colors or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate summer colors sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Red Discovery Basket
Engagement during Red Song Time
Engagement during Color Story Time
Participation and interest in Summer Colors play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide high-contrast visuals, alternative sensory materials, adaptive positioning, and individualized support as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Red Discovery Basket
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support summer colors learning through red discovery basket.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore red-colored objects through touch and sight.
MATERIALS:
Red toys, red scarves, red textured items
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Red Discovery Basket before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model color vocabulary and encourage exploration.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place materials in basket.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Name the color red.
4. Allow exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Color awareness and sensory exploration.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Red Discovery Basket
Use of summer colors vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–8 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
