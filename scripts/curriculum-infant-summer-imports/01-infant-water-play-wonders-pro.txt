TITLE:
Water Play Wonders

AGE_GROUP:
Infant 0–12 Months

THEME:
Water Exploration

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Infants will explore water through safe sensory experiences, movement, visual discovery, music, and hands-on play. Children will investigate splashing, floating, bubbles, water movement, and summer fun while developing curiosity and foundational learning skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
• Explore water through sensory experiences
• Strengthen visual tracking and observation skills
• Support language development through water-related vocabulary
• Encourage fine and gross motor development
• Foster curiosity and confidence through exploration

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Water mats, sensory bags, bubbles, blue scarves, floating toys, mirrors, cups, toy boats, music player, soft washcloths

VOCABULARY:
Water, Splash, Float, Bubble, Wet, Wave, Boat, Pour, Blue, Summer

BOOKS:
Splish Splash Baby
Baby Loves Water
Bath Time Fun
Ocean Babies
Water Wonders

SONGS:
Row Row Row Your Boat
Splish Splash
Baby Beluga
The Waves in the Ocean
You Are My Sunshine

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to provide safe water play opportunities such as bath play, water tables, and outdoor water exploration.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe sensory preferences, visual tracking, communication attempts, social engagement, curiosity, and participation during water activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide adaptive positioning, alternative sensory materials, individualized support, and modified experiences as needed.

MONDAY

DAILY_THEME:
Water Exploration: Water Discovery Basket and related play (introduce calm bonding, faces, and soft sensory play)

DAILY_OBJECTIVES:
Support water exploration learning through water discovery basket.
Support water exploration learning through water song circle.
Support water exploration learning through water story time.

DAILY_VOCABULARY:
Water, Splash, Float, Bubble, Wet, Wave

DAILY_MATERIALS:
Toy boats, cups, floating toys
Music player
Board books

DAILY_LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Science, Physical Development

CIRCLE_TIME:
Soft hello with each infant's name and a calm face-to-face greeting.
Hum or sing "Row Row Row Your Boat" at a low volume while offering gentle movement or visual interest tied to water exploration.
End with a brief look at Splish Splash Baby or a theme picture card, watching for engagement cues.

OUTDOOR_PLAY:
Take infants outdoors for fresh air on a shaded blanket or supervised stroller time. Narrate water exploration sounds and sights for 1–5 minutes, staying within arm's reach and watching for overstimulation.

DAILY_OBSERVATIONS:
Engagement during Water Discovery Basket
Engagement during Water Song Circle
Engagement during Water Story Time
Participation and interest in Water Exploration play

DAILY_ADAPTATIONS:
Provide adaptive positioning, alternative sensory materials, individualized support, and modified experiences as needed.

SAFETY_NOTES:
Arm's-reach supervision, choke-safe materials only, supervised tummy time, and no unattended water or elevated surfaces.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Water Discovery Basket
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Support water exploration learning through water discovery basket.
DESCRIPTION:
Infants explore water-themed objects through touch and sight.
MATERIALS:
Toy boats, cups, floating toys
SETUP:
Prepare materials for Water Discovery Basket before children arrive and arrange a clear, supervised space.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Model vocabulary and encourage curiosity.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

DIRECTIONS:
1. Place materials in basket.
2. Encourage touching and grasping.
3. Name objects.
4. Allow exploration.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory awareness and language development.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Engagement during Water Discovery Basket
Use of water exploration vocabulary or interest
Cue reading and calm participation
VOCABULARY:

EXTENSIONS:

ADAPTATIONS:
Shorten to 1–8 minutes. Offer side-lying or supported positions. Stop immediately if the infant shows overstimulation cues.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Remain within arm's reach at all times. Use only large, choke-safe materials. Never leave an infant unattended on a mat, changing surface, or near water.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
