TITLE:
Camping Adventure

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Camping

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy
Social Emotional
Physical Development
Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children pack for a pretend camping trip as they explore nature, camp routines, outdoor safety, and nighttime sounds. Sensory play, trail games, campfire dramatic play, and nature art help children build vocabulary and confidence in outdoor-themed learning.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn camping and nature vocabulary
Practice outdoor safety routines
Explore nature materials with senses
Retell camping stories and songs
Cooperate during trail and camp games
Strengthen gross and fine motor skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Flashlights
Sleeping bags or blankets
Pretend campfire
Binoculars
Compass prop
Nature items
Trail mix ingredients
Animal track cards
Tents or fort materials
Maps
Art paper
Camping books

SONGS:

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Plan a backyard or living room campout. Pack a small bag together and talk about what campers need.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe nature vocabulary, safety awareness, cooperative fort building, storytelling, and sensory exploration.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use visual camp routine cards. Offer quiet nature exploration for children who prefer calm activities.
