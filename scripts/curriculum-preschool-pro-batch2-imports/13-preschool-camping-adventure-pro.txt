LEARNING_DOMAINS:

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become outdoor adventurers as they explore the exciting world of camping through hands-on investigations, dramatic play, nature exploration, storytelling, STEM challenges, and creative activities. Throughout the week, children will learn about camping equipment, wildlife, outdoor safety, nature observation, and teamwork while building confidence and curiosity about the natural world.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore camping and outdoor adventure concepts
Learn basic outdoor safety skills
Develop observation and investigation abilities
Strengthen problem-solving and teamwork skills
Practice counting, sorting, and measuring
Build vocabulary related to nature and camping
Strengthen fine and gross motor development
Encourage imaginative and dramatic play

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to spend time outdoors together. Take a nature walk, observe wildlife, collect leaves, or talk about outdoor adventures and camping experiences.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's cooperation during dramatic play, problem-solving skills, nature observations, vocabulary development, and ability to follow safety guidelines.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and nature photographs. Allow children multiple ways to participate through movement, art, sensory experiences, dramatic play, or discussion.
