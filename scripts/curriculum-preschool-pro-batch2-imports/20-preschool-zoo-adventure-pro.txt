TITLE:
Zoo Adventure

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Zoo Animals

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy
Social Emotional
Physical Development
Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children tour a pretend zoo as they meet mammals, reptiles, and birds, compare animal features, and learn about zookeepers and conservation. Binocular play, habitat sorting, and animal movement games build vocabulary and wonder.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name zoo animals and their features
Sort animals by type or habitat
Use zoo and animal vocabulary
Practice observation with tools
Cooperate during zoo dramatic play
Discuss caring for wild animals

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Zoo animal figures
Animal picture cards
Binoculars prop
Footprint stamps
Sand sensory bin
Habitat sorting cards
Blocks
Paint
Zoo books
Tickets

SONGS:

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Look at zoo animals in books or videos. Talk about how zoos help protect animals.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe animal naming, habitat sorting, keeper role-play, movement imitation, and vocabulary.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real animal photos with figures. Offer one exhibit focus at a time.
