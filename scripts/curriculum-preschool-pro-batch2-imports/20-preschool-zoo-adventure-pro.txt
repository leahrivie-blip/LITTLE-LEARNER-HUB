WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become zoo explorers as they investigate animals from around the world. Through hands-on discovery, dramatic play, movement activities, sensory experiences, art projects, and STEM challenges, children will learn about animal habitats, diets, behaviors, and the important role zoos play in conservation and animal care.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify a variety of zoo animals
Explore animal habitats and needs
Learn about animal care and conservation
Develop observation and classification skills
Practice counting, sorting, and comparing
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build vocabulary related to animals and habitats
Foster curiosity and respect for wildlife

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to visit a zoo, nature center, or wildlife park if possible. Discuss favorite animals and how people can help protect wildlife and habitats.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to identify animals, classify animals by habitat or type, use new vocabulary, participate in dramatic play, and demonstrate curiosity during investigations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide animal picture cards and visual supports. Offer larger manipulatives when needed. Allow children to participate through movement, art, dramatic play, sensory exploration, or discussion.
