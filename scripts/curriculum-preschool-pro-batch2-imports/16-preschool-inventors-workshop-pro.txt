TITLE:

Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children enter an inventors workshop to design, build, test, and improve creations. Through tinkering, dramatic play, and STEM challenges, they practice creative problem-solving and learn that mistakes help inventors improve ideas.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Design simple inventions with available materials
Test and improve built creations
Use inventor vocabulary
Explain how an invention works
Cooperate during design challenges
Strengthen fine motor and planning skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Craft sticks
Cardboard
Tape and glue
Pipe cleaners
Recycled caps
String
Scissors
Markers
Blocks
Gears or wheels
Tool props
Invention books

SONGS:

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Build something useful at home from recyclables. Ask your child to explain how it works.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe planning, testing, redesign attempts, explanation language, and persistence.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer pre-cut materials and visual design templates. Celebrate attempts, not just finished products.
