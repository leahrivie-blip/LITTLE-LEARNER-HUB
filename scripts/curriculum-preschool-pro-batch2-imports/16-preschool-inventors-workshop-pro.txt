WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become inventors, engineers, and creators as they design, build, test, and improve their own inventions. Throughout the week, children will solve problems, explore simple machines, experiment with materials, create prototypes, and develop critical thinking skills while learning that mistakes help inventors learn and grow.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop creative problem-solving skills
Learn about inventors and inventions
Practice planning, testing, and improving designs
Strengthen engineering and building skills
Build confidence in sharing ideas
Develop teamwork and cooperation
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Encourage innovation and creativity

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to look around their homes and identify inventions that help people every day. Discuss how inventors solve problems and improve lives.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's creativity, persistence, communication skills, problem-solving abilities, teamwork, and willingness to test and improve ideas.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual examples and simple design ideas. Offer larger building materials when needed. Allow children to explain ideas through drawing, building, discussion, or dramatic play.
