TITLE:
Archaeology Adventure

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Archaeology

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy
Social Emotional
Physical Development
Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children become junior archaeologists as they dig for pretend artifacts, brush away sand, map discoveries, and learn how people study the past. Sensory excavation, mapping, and storytelling build patience, observation, and history curiosity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn archaeology and fossil vocabulary
Practice careful digging and brushing
Sort artifacts by type or size
Record discoveries with drawings
Retell stories about the past
Cooperate during excavation play

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Kinetic sand or rice
Small brushes
Plastic bones and artifacts
Magnifying glasses
Mapping paper
Clipboard
Paint
Blocks
Measuring tools
Artifact picture cards
Hats or vests
Archaeology books

SONGS:

Brush Away the Sand
Discovery Song

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Look at old photos or objects at home and wonder what they tell us about the past.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe careful tool use, sorting, mapping attempts, vocabulary use, and cooperative digging.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use larger artifacts for easier finding. Offer picture maps for recording discoveries.
