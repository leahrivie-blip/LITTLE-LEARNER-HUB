WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become archaeologists as they dig for artifacts, uncover fossils, investigate ancient discoveries, and explore how people learn about the past. Through hands-on excavation activities, dramatic play, sensory exploration, art projects, and STEM challenges, children will develop curiosity, observation skills, and a love for discovery.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Learn what archaeologists do
Explore fossils and artifacts
Develop observation and investigation skills
Practice sorting, classifying, and measuring
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build vocabulary related to archaeology
Encourage problem-solving and critical thinking
Foster curiosity about history and discovery

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to look for interesting rocks, shells, and natural objects outdoors. Discuss how people learn about the past by studying clues and discoveries.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's investigation skills, curiosity, ability to classify artifacts, use of vocabulary, participation in dramatic play, and problem-solving during excavation activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger excavation tools when needed. Use visual supports and photographs. Allow children to participate through digging, sorting, art, dramatic play, or discussion.
