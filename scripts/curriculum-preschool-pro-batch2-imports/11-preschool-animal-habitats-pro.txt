WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will explore where animals live as they investigate forests, oceans, deserts, and grasslands through sensory play, habitat building, dramatic play, and hands-on science. Throughout the week, children will sort animals by habitat, compare needs for food and shelter, and use new vocabulary while caring for pretend wildlife.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common animal habitats
Sort animals by where they live
Describe basic animal needs
Use habitat vocabulary in play and discussion
Build cooperative habitat models
Strengthen observation and comparison skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic animal figures
Habitat sorting mats
Sensory bin fillers
Blocks and cardboard
Blue and green felt
Sand and rocks
Tree and plant props
Magnifying glasses
Animal picture cards
Paint and paper
Binoculars prop
Books about habitats

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Talk about animals you see near home and where they live. Visit a library to explore habitat books together.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe habitat sorting, use of vocabulary, cooperative building, curiosity during investigations, and dramatic play roles.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use real photos alongside toy animals. Offer one habitat focus at a time for children who need simpler choices.
