WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become wildlife explorers as they investigate where animals live and why habitats help animals survive. Throughout the week, children will explore forests, oceans, deserts, arctic regions, and jungles through hands-on sensory experiences, dramatic play, STEM building challenges, movement activities, and creative projects. Children will learn how animals adapt to their environments while developing observation, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify different animal habitats
Match animals to their habitats
Explore how habitats meet animal needs
Develop scientific observation skills
Strengthen classification and sorting abilities
Build vocabulary related to animals and habitats
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster curiosity and respect for wildlife

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to discuss where different animals live. Take a nature walk and observe habitats near home. Talk about how people and animals both need food, water, and shelter.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to match animals to habitats, use habitat vocabulary, classify animals, participate in discussions, solve building challenges, and demonstrate curiosity about wildlife.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual habitat cards and animal photographs. Offer larger manipulatives when needed. Allow children to demonstrate understanding through movement, art, dramatic play, or verbal discussion.
