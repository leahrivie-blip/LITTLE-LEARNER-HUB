WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become insect explorers as they investigate the fascinating world of bugs. Throughout the week, children will discover insect characteristics, life cycles, habitats, pollination, camouflage, and the important roles insects play in nature. Through hands-on investigations, dramatic play, sensory experiences, art, and movement activities, children will develop curiosity and appreciation for these tiny creatures.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common insects and their characteristics
Explore insect habitats and life cycles
Learn about pollination and nature
Develop observation and classification skills
Strengthen counting and comparing abilities
Build scientific vocabulary
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster curiosity and respect for living things

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to look for insects outdoors. Observe butterflies, ants, bees, or ladybugs and discuss how insects help plants and nature.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to identify insects, classify bugs, describe insect features, participate in investigations, and demonstrate curiosity about living things.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide insect photographs and visual supports. Offer larger manipulatives when needed. Allow children to participate through observation, movement, art, or dramatic play.
