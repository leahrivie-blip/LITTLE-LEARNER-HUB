TITLE:
Amazing Insects

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Bugs & Insects

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

Language & Literacy
Social Emotional

Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children investigate the amazing world of insects through bug hunts, life cycle models, sensory play, and art. They learn insect body parts, habitats, and roles in nature while building observation skills and respectful curiosity.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name common insects and body parts
Describe insect habitats and behaviors
Sort insects by attributes
Use insect vocabulary in play
Create insect-themed art
Practice gentle observation skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Plastic bug figures
Bug viewer containers
Magnifying glasses
Leaf rubbings
Pipe cleaners
Black and yellow paint
Bug picture cards
Life cycle cards
Sensory bin filler
Tweezers
Art paper
Insect books

VOCABULARY:
Insect
Antennae
Wings
Metamorphosis
Larva
Pollinate
Colony
Camouflage
Hive
Crawl

BOOKS:
The Very Hungry Caterpillar | Eric Carle
Bugs! Bugs! Bugs! | Bob Barner
Waiting for Wings | Lois Ehlert

SONGS:
The Insect Song
Fly Like a Butterfly
Busy Bee Buzz

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Look for insects outside together. Observe quietly and talk about where insects live.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe insect naming, sorting choices, gentle handling, life cycle talk, and art details.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use photo cards for children uncomfortable with toy bugs. Offer larger tweezers for fine motor support.
