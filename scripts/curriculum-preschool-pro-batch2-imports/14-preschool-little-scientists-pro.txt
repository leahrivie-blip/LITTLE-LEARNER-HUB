TITLE:
Little Scientists

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Science

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Language & Literacy
Social Emotional
Physical Development
Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children become little scientists as they ask questions, make predictions, test ideas, and record discoveries. Hands-on experiments, observation trays, and science talk build curiosity, vocabulary, and critical thinking across the week.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Use science words such as observe, predict, and test
Conduct simple investigations
Record discoveries with drawings
Compare and describe results
Work cooperatively during experiments
Develop curiosity about how things work

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Magnifying glasses
Eyedroppers
Trays and cups
Water and vinegar
Baking soda
Objects to sink and float
Magnets
Rulers
Clipboards
Crayons
Science books
Safety goggles

VOCABULARY:
Observe
Predict
Experiment
Hypothesis
Compare
Record
Investigate
Discovery
Measure
Test

BOOKS:
What Do You Do with an Idea? | Kobi Yamada
Ada Twist, Scientist | Andrea Beaty
What Is Science? | Rebecca Kai Dotlich

SONGS:
The Scientist Song
Question Time Chant
Discovery Dance

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Try a simple home experiment such as sink-or-float in the bathtub. Ask what your child predicts will happen.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe questioning, prediction language, persistence during tests, recording attempts, and peer discussion.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use picture prediction cards. Offer one-step experiments with adult support as needed.
