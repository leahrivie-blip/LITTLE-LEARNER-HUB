WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become scientists as they ask questions, make predictions, conduct experiments, observe results, and explore the world around them. Throughout the week, children will investigate colors, magnets, water, plants, and simple chemical reactions while building critical thinking, observation, and problem-solving skills through hands-on discovery.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Develop scientific observation skills
Learn how to make predictions
Explore cause and effect relationships
Practice comparing and classifying objects
Strengthen vocabulary related to science
Build problem-solving and critical thinking skills
Encourage curiosity and questioning
Develop fine and gross motor skills

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to ask questions about the world around them. Explore simple science concepts at home by observing weather, plants, animals, and everyday changes.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's curiosity, ability to make predictions, problem-solving skills, scientific vocabulary usage, participation in experiments, and ability to discuss observations.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and picture cards. Offer hands-on materials for all learners. Allow children to participate through observation, discussion, drawing, movement, or experimentation.
