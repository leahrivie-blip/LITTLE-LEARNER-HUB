WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become gardeners and plant scientists as they explore seeds, flowers, vegetables, insects, plant life cycles, and the needs of living things. Through hands-on planting experiences, sensory exploration, dramatic play, art, STEM investigations, and nature observations, children will discover how plants grow and why gardens are important.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify the parts of a plant
Explore plant life cycles
Learn what plants need to grow
Develop observation and investigation skills
Practice counting, sorting, and measuring
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build vocabulary related to gardening
Foster responsibility through plant care

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to plant flowers, herbs, or vegetables together. Observe plants growing around your home and discuss what helps them stay healthy.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's ability to identify plant parts, describe plant growth, care for living things, use scientific vocabulary, and participate in gardening activities.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger gardening tools when needed. Use visual plant life cycle cards. Allow children to participate through planting, observation, art, dramatic play, or discussion.
