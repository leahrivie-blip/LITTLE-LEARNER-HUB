TITLE:
Gardening & Plant Life

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool 3-5 Years

THEME:
Gardening

PLAN:

STATUS:
published

Language & Literacy
Social Emotional

Creative Arts
Science
Math

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children explore seeds, soil, sun, and water as they learn how plants grow. Planting activities, garden dramatic play, sensory exploration, and observation journals help children connect to nature and understand plant life cycles.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Describe what plants need to grow
Observe seeds and seedlings
Use gardening vocabulary
Sort seeds and plant parts
Care for plants responsibly
Record plant observations

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Potting soil
Seeds
Small pots
Watering cans
Gardening gloves
Trowels
Seed picture cards
Flower and vegetable pictures
Magnifying glasses
Art paper
Garden books
Spray bottles

VOCABULARY:
Seed
Soil

Root

Leaf

Water
Harvest
Garden

BOOKS:
Planting a Rainbow | Lois Ehlert
The Tiny Seed | Eric Carle
Lola Plants a Garden | Anna McQuinn

SONGS:
Plant a Seed Song
Growing Growing
Garden Helper Chant

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Plant a seed at home or visit a garden center. Water together and watch for changes.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe planting steps, care routines, plant part naming, sorting, and journal drawings.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use fast-sprouting seeds. Provide visual planting sequence cards.
