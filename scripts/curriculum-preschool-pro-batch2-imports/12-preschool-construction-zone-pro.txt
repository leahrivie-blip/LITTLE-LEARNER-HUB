WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will become builders, architects, engineers, and construction workers as they explore how things are designed and built. Through hands-on building challenges, dramatic play, measuring activities, construction-themed art, and problem-solving experiences, children will develop teamwork, creativity, and critical thinking skills while learning about the exciting world of construction.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore how buildings and structures are created
Develop engineering and problem-solving skills
Practice measuring, counting, and comparing
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills
Build teamwork and communication skills
Learn about construction tools and jobs
Develop creativity through design challenges
Strengthen observation and planning abilities

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage children to notice buildings, roads, bridges, and construction projects in their community. Talk about how construction workers help build and improve places where people live and work.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's problem-solving skills, ability to work cooperatively, measuring skills, planning abilities, creativity during building activities, and use of construction vocabulary.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide larger building materials when needed. Offer visual examples of structures and blueprints. Allow children to participate through building, drawing, dramatic play, or verbal discussion.
