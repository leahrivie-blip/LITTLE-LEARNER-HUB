WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children become builders and engineers as they explore tools, blueprints, structures, and teamwork in a busy construction zone. Through block building, measuring, dramatic play, and design challenges, children strengthen problem-solving, math, and motor skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Explore building materials and tools safely
Practice measuring and comparing sizes
Design and test simple structures
Use construction vocabulary
Cooperate on group building projects
Strengthen fine and gross motor skills

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:

Cardboard tubes

Toy tools

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Look for buildings and construction near home. Count windows, compare heights, and talk about what builders might need.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe planning before building, tool use, measurement attempts, persistence with structures, and peer cooperation.

ADAPTATIONS:
Offer larger blocks and pre-cut materials. Use picture steps for multi-part builds.
