WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children learn about caring for pets through vet dramatic play, grooming routines, feeding schedules, and friendship activities. They build empathy, responsibility vocabulary, and social skills while exploring dogs, cats, and other beloved animals.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Name common pets and their needs
Practice gentle care routines
Use pet care vocabulary
Sort pet items by animal
Demonstrate empathy in play
Retell stories about animal friends

WEEKLY_MATERIALS:
Stuffed animals
Pet care props
Bowls and brushes
Leashes
Pet picture cards
Stethoscope prop
Carrier box
Blankets

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Talk about how your family cares for pets or animals nearby. Practice gentle touch together.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe gentle handling, care role-play, vocabulary use, sorting, and empathy language.

ADAPTATIONS:
Use photo cards for children unsure around stuffed animals. Model gentle care steps clearly.
