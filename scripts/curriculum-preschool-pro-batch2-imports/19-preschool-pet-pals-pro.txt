WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children will explore the wonderful world of pets while learning about responsibility, kindness, animal care, and pet habitats. Through hands-on investigations, dramatic play, sensory experiences, art projects, movement activities, and early science concepts, children will discover how pets depend on people for food, shelter, love, and care.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Identify common household pets
Learn how to care for animals responsibly
Develop empathy and kindness toward living things
Explore pet needs and habitats
Strengthen observation and classification skills
Build vocabulary related to animals and pet care
Develop fine and gross motor skills
Foster responsibility and cooperation

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Encourage families to talk about pets at home or pets they know. Discuss how animals need food, water, exercise, love, and proper care to stay healthy and happy.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Observe children's empathy toward animals, understanding of pet care, participation in dramatic play, vocabulary development, and ability to classify and compare pets.

ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports and pet photographs. Offer larger materials when needed. Allow children to participate through art, discussion, dramatic play, sensory experiences, or movement activities.
