WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children build a caring classroom community where everyone belongs through friendship, teamwork, inclusion, respect, and collaborative literacy, STEM, dramatic play, and creative projects that culminate the Family Connections unit.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Build friendships through cooperative play.
Practice teamwork and problem-solving.
Respect similarities and differences.
Communicate clearly and show leadership.
Connect literacy, math, and STEM through collaboration.
Celebrate classroom community.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Write a short note about what your child learned during Family Connections; display the Growing Together board; send home a memory page.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice inclusion of peers, group project cooperation, conflict resolution, clear communication, leadership, collaborative STEM, community pride, and reflection on growth and friendships.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): buddy pairs, visual teamwork supports, shorter collaborative tasks. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): lead discussions and games, complete sentence writing, complex engineering, help plan the celebration.

ACTIVITY_NAME:

CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Build a large classroom with blocks and loose parts.

Loose parts
Toy people
Signs
SETUP:
Prepare interest areas and place materials within reach before group time begins.

Facilitate discovery, model rich language, and guide preschoolers through cooperative play and problem-solving.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"I notice you are exploring belonging. Tell me about your ideas. How can we work together?"
DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the activity with a brief demonstration and theme vocabulary.
2. Invite children to explore materials and share their ideas.
3. Circulate to ask open-ended questions and support problem-solving.
4. Encourage peer collaboration and respectful communication.
5. Document observations and close with a brief reflection.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Open-Ended Exploration skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, problem-solving, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:

Use preschool-safe materials. Supervise scissors, sensory, outdoor, and building activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): picture routine cards, larger materials, teacher-supported writing, 3-step sequencing. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): independent writing, complex engineering, simple maps, expand measuring/graphing.

3. Circulate to ask open-ended questions and support problem-solving.
4. Encourage peer collaboration and respectful communication.
5. Document observations and close with a brief reflection.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory Play skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, problem-solving, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Favorite, Vote, Sensory, Celebrate
EXTENSIONS:
Offer extended challenges for older preschoolers or revisit during choice time.
ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, sentence starters, and peer partners as needed.
SAFETY_NOTES:
Use preschool-safe materials. Supervise scissors, sensory, outdoor, and building activities.
AGE_MODIFICATIONS:
Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): picture routine cards, larger materials, teacher-supported writing, 3-step sequencing. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): independent writing, complex engineering, simple maps, expand measuring/graphing.
