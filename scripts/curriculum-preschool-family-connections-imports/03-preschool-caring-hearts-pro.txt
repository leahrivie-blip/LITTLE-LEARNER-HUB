TITLE:
Caring Hearts

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Family Connections — Caring Hearts

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children explore kindness, empathy, compassion, and helping through conversations, cooperative play, literacy, STEM, and arts that show how words and actions make others feel valued.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Recognize and name emotions.
Practice empathy and kindness.
Demonstrate helpful behaviors.
Use communication for conflict resolution.
Connect literacy, math, and STEM to kindness themes.
Build a positive classroom community.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Complete the Kindness Challenge with home activities and share one act of kindness from home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Notice empathy, emotion naming, conflict resolution, cooperation, feelings expression, writing and storytelling, STEM perseverance, and including peers.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): picture emotion cards, sentence starters, modeled conflict language, brief groups. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): independent journals, complex role-play, expanded graphing and patterns, lead kindness discussions and helper roles.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Kindness Puppet Theater
CATEGORY:
Dramatic Play

Kindness scenario cards

Facilitate discovery, model rich language, and guide preschoolers through cooperative play and problem-solving.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:

2. Invite children to explore materials and share their ideas.
3. Circulate to ask open-ended questions and support problem-solving.
4. Encourage peer collaboration and respectful communication.
5. Document observations and close with a brief reflection.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, problem-solving, peer interactions, and independence.
