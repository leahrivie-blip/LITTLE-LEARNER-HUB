TITLE:
My Home & My Family

AGE_GROUP:
Preschool

THEME:
Family Connections — My Home & My Family

PLAN:
Pro

STATUS:
published

LEARNING_DOMAINS:
Social Emotional, Language & Literacy, Math, Science, Physical Development, Creative Arts

WEEKLY_OVERVIEW:
Children explore home, family routines, responsibilities, and ways families spend time together through literacy, dramatic play, STEM, math, and creative experiences that build appreciation for home life and early academic and social-emotional skills.

LEARNING_OBJECTIVES:
Describe home and family routines.
Compare different types of homes.
Practice sequencing and storytelling.
Use early math skills for sorting, measuring, and counting.
Engineer and build homes through design challenges.
Cooperate through dramatic play and group projects.

FAMILY_CONNECTION:
Complete the Our Favorite Family Tradition page; bring a drawing or short story; talk about ways everyone helps at home.

OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Describe home, sequence routines, cooperate in dramatic play, build stable structures, use measurement vocabulary, write or dictate, count and compare, take responsibility for classroom jobs.

ADAPTATIONS:
Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): picture routine cards, larger materials, teacher-supported writing, 3-step sequencing. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): independent writing, complex engineering, simple maps, expand measuring and graphing.

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Build Homes with Wooden Blocks
CATEGORY:
Open-Ended Exploration
OBJECTIVE:
Design and build homes using wooden blocks.
DESCRIPTION:

MATERIALS:
Wooden blocks
Foam blocks
Toy people
Road tape

Prepare interest areas and place materials within reach before group time begins.

Facilitate discovery, model rich language, and guide preschoolers through cooperative play and problem-solving.

"I notice you are exploring home. Tell me about your ideas. How can we work together?"

ACTIVITY_NAME:
Favorite Sensory from the Week
CATEGORY:
Sensory Play
OBJECTIVE:
Revisit a favorite sensory experience from the week.
DESCRIPTION:
Children vote on and explore a favorite sensory station from the week.
MATERIALS:
Materials from prior days
SETUP:
Prepare interest areas and place materials within reach before group time begins.
TEACHER_ROLE:
Facilitate discovery, model rich language, and guide preschoolers through cooperative play and problem-solving.
TEACHER_LANGUAGE:
"I notice you are exploring home. Tell me about your ideas. How can we work together?"
DIRECTIONS:
1. Introduce the activity with a brief demonstration and theme vocabulary.
2. Invite children to explore materials and share their ideas.
3. Circulate to ask open-ended questions and support problem-solving.
4. Encourage peer collaboration and respectful communication.
5. Document observations and close with a brief reflection.
LEARNING_GOALS:
Sensory Play skills, language development, social-emotional growth, and active participation.
OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITIES:
Note engagement, vocabulary use, problem-solving, peer interactions, and independence.
VOCABULARY:
Favorite, Explore, Sensory, Choose
EXTENSIONS:
Offer extended challenges for older preschoolers or revisit during choice time.
ADAPTATIONS:
Provide visual supports, sentence starters, and peer partners as needed.

Use preschool-safe materials. Supervise scissors, sensory, outdoor, and building activities.

Younger Preschoolers (3 Years): picture routine cards, larger materials, teacher-supported writing, 3-step sequencing. Older Preschoolers (4–5 Years): independent writing, complex engineering, simple maps, expand measuring/graphing.
