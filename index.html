Privacy, terms, refunds, and child data.

Clear policies help providers understand how Little Learner Hub handles accounts, billing, support, and child-related information.

Privacy Policy

How information is handled

Little Learner Hub may collect account details, support messages, billing status, saved resources, child profile details, observations, AI prompts, AI-generated content, and generated documents. We use these details to provide the platform, send support responses, manage billing, and improve the product. Payment details are processed through Stripe and follow PCI compliance.

Information should be used only for providing childcare resource tools, account access, support, billing, and product improvement. Payment details should be processed through Stripe with full encryption.

Terms of Use

Provider responsibility

Resources, AI outputs, forms, and policy drafts are templates for childcare providers to review and adapt. Providers are responsible for checking state licensing rules, program policies, and child safety standards before using any generated content.

AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not specific to a provider's state or program. Providers must review, edit, and approve all AI-generated content before sharing with families or using for official documentation.

Users should not upload sensitive information unless it is necessary for their own childcare recordkeeping and they have permission to manage that information.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Cancel anytime

Users may cancel a subscription from the billing area. Cancellation returns the account to Free limits after the active subscription period or immediately in the local demo flow.

Refund requests can be submitted through support at little.learners.hub.customer@gmail.com and should include the account email and billing concern.

Child Data and Privacy Note

Use minimum necessary information

Child profiles, observations, support plans, attendance, meals, and portfolios may include sensitive child information. Providers should enter only what is needed, follow family and program privacy policies, and keep data secure.

For production launch, child data should be stored with secure backend protections, access controls, and clear retention practices.

Help Center

Little Learner Hub FAQ

Quick answers for home daycare and childcare providers using the platform.

What is Little Learner Hub?

Little Learner Hub is an all-in-one platform designed for home daycare and childcare providers. It includes lesson plans, observations, forms, activities, printables, AI tools, child management, and weekly planning.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Little Learner Hub offers a Free Plan that lets you start immediately with no credit card required. Use it as long as you like.

What is included in the Free Plan?

  • 3 Child Profiles
  • 10 Observations
  • 5 Lesson Plans
  • 10 Forms & Printables
  • 10 Activity Ideas
  • 10 AI Generations per month
  • Access to Free Resources
  • No credit card required

Do I need a credit card for the Free Plan?

No. The Free Plan is completely free and does not require a credit card. You can use it for as long as you want.

What happens when I hit my free limits?

When you reach a free limit (e.g., 3 child profiles, 10 observations, or 10 AI generations per month), you'll see an upgrade prompt. You can choose to upgrade to Pro for unlimited access, or continue using your free plan within the limits.

What is included in the Pro Plan?

The Pro Plan includes unlimited child profiles, observations, lesson plans, forms, printables, activities, and AI generations. You also get access to Child Portfolios, Advanced Reporting, and priority support.

Can I try Pro free?

Yes! You can try Pro free for 7 days. A credit card is required to start the trial, but you won't be charged during the trial period. After 7 days, your subscription will automatically renew at the regular Pro price unless you cancel.

Can I cancel before being charged?

Yes. You can cancel your trial or subscription anytime from your account settings. If you cancel during the 7-day trial, you won't be charged. If you cancel after the trial ends, your Pro access stops immediately and you return to your Free plan.

What is the Pro pricing?

  • Pro Monthly: $19.99/month
  • Pro Annual: $199/year (about $16.58/month)
  • Founding Member: $9.99/month for life (limited to first 50 members)

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade to Pro at any time from your account or by clicking the "Upgrade to Pro" button when you hit a free limit.

Are the lesson plans editable?

Many resources are editable and viewable inside the platform. Resource details will indicate whether a file is editable.

What ages are supported?

  • Infants (0-12 months)
  • Toddlers (1-3 years)
  • Preschoolers (3-5 years)

Can I track observations for children?

Yes. The Observation Hub and Weekly Observation Tracker help providers document and organize observations. On the Free Plan, you can log 10 observations. Pro users get unlimited observations.

Can I create portfolios for children?

Portfolios are a Pro feature. Free users can track observations and goals, but portfolios (for compiling progress, samples, and reports) are available to Pro members only.

What AI tools are included?

  • Observation Generator
  • Lesson Plan Generator
  • Daily Report Generator
  • Parent Newsletter Generator
  • Parent Handbook Generator
  • Contract Generator
  • And more...

Can I view resources in the app?

Yes. Resources open inside Little Learner Hub based on your membership plan. You can view, download, and print most resources.

Can I reset my password?

Yes. Little Learner Hub supports password reset, email verification, and password changes through your account settings. These features work with Firebase Authentication.

Do you use phone verification?

No. Phone number is optional and used only as account contact information. SMS verification is not enabled because it adds extra cost.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We take privacy and security seriously and use secure systems to protect account information. Payment details are processed through Stripe with full PCI compliance. For production, child data is stored with secure backend protections and access controls.

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page or email little.learners.hub.customer@gmail.com for support, bug reports, or feature requests.
