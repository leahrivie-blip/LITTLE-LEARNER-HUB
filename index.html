Legal Center

Privacy, terms, refunds, and child data.

Clear policies help providers understand how Little Learner Hub handles accounts, billing, support, and child-related information.

Privacy Policy

How information is handled

Little Learner Hub may collect account details, support messages, billing status, saved resources, child profile details, observations, AI prompts, AI-generated content, and generated documents needed to operate the platform.

Information should be used only for providing childcare resource tools, account access, support, billing, and product improvement. Payment details should be processed through Stripe and not stored directly inside Little Learner Hub.

Terms of Use

Provider responsibility

Resources, AI outputs, forms, and policy drafts are templates for childcare providers to review and adapt. Providers are responsible for checking state licensing rules, program policies, family agreements, and professional requirements before use.

AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not specific to a provider's state or program. Providers must review, edit, and approve all AI-generated content before sharing it with families or using it for business records.

Users should not upload sensitive information unless it is necessary for their own childcare recordkeeping and they have permission to manage that information.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Cancel anytime

Users may cancel a subscription from the billing area. Cancellation returns the account to Free limits after the active subscription period or immediately in the local demo flow.

Refund requests can be submitted through support at little.learners.hub.customer@gmail.com and should include the account email and billing concern.

Child Data and Privacy Note

Use minimum necessary information

Child profiles, observations, support plans, attendance, meals, and portfolios may include sensitive child information. Providers should enter only what is needed, follow family consent requirements, and protect exported records.

For production launch, child data should be stored with secure backend protections, access controls, and clear retention practices.

Help Center

Little Learner Hub FAQ

Quick answers for home daycare and childcare providers using the platform.

What is Little Learner Hub?

Little Learner Hub is an all-in-one platform designed for home daycare and childcare providers. It includes lesson plans, observations, forms, activities, printables, AI tools, child management features, and more.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Little Learner Hub offers a Free Plan that allows providers to explore the platform and access a limited amount of content before upgrading.

What is included in the Free Plan?

  • 3 Lesson Plans
  • 15 Observations
  • 3 Forms
  • 5 Activities
  • 5 Printables
  • 10 AI Generations Per Month
  • Up to 3 Child Profiles
  • Weekly Observation Tracker

What is included in the Pro Plan?

The Pro Plan includes full in-app access to resources, child management features, future premium features, and 250 AI generations per month.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings.

Do I need to enter payment information for the Free Plan?

No. The Free Plan is available without a paid subscription.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade to Pro at any time.

Are the lesson plans editable?

Many resources are editable and viewable inside the platform. Resource details will indicate whether a file is editable.

What ages are supported?

  • Infants
  • Toddlers
  • Preschoolers

Can I track observations for children?

Yes. The Observation Hub and Weekly Observation Tracker help providers document and organize observations.

Can I create portfolios for children?

Yes. Pro users can create and manage child portfolios.

What AI tools are included?

  • Observation Generator
  • Lesson Plan Generator
  • Daily Report Generator
  • Parent Newsletter Generator
  • Parent Handbook Generator
  • Contract Generator

Can I view resources in the app?

Yes. Resources open inside Little Learner Hub based on your membership plan.

Can I reset my password?

Yes. Little Learner Hub is prepared for Firebase Authentication password reset emails, email verification, resend verification, and password changes. These built-in email/password features are expected to stay $0 at launch on Firebase's free tier.

Do you use phone verification?

No. Phone number is optional account contact information only. SMS verification is not enabled because it can add extra cost.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We take privacy and security seriously and use secure systems to protect account information. Real production account recovery requires Firebase Authentication to be connected before launch.

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page or submit a support request through the Help Center.
