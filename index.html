Legal Center

Privacy, terms, refunds, and child data.

Clear policies help providers understand how Little Learner Hub handles accounts, billing, support, and child-related information.

Privacy Policy

How information is handled

Little Learner Hub may collect account details, support messages, billing status, saved resources, child profile details, observations, AI prompts, AI-generated content, and generated documents needed to operate the platform.

Information should be used only for providing childcare resource tools, account access, support, billing, and product improvement. Payment details should be processed through Stripe and not stored directly inside Little Learner Hub.

Terms of Use

Provider responsibility

Resources, AI outputs, forms, and policy drafts are templates for childcare providers to review and adapt. Providers are responsible for checking state licensing rules, program policies, family agreements, and professional requirements before use.

AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not specific to a provider's state or program. Providers must review, edit, and approve all AI-generated content before sharing it with families or using it for business records.

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Cancel anytime

Users may cancel a subscription from the billing area. Cancellation returns the account to Free limits after the active subscription period.

Refund requests can be submitted through support at little.learners.hub.customer@gmail.com and should include the account email and billing concern.

Child Data and Privacy Note

Use minimum necessary information

Child profiles, observations, support plans, attendance, meals, and portfolios may include sensitive child information. Providers should enter only what is needed, follow family consent requirements, and protect exported records.

Help Center

Little Learner Hub FAQ

Quick answers for home daycare and childcare providers using the platform.

What is Little Learner Hub?

Little Learner Hub helps childcare providers organize observations, lesson plans, child goals, forms, incident reports, and paperwork in one place so documentation takes minutes instead of hours.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Little Learner Hub offers a Free Plan with published Free-tier lesson plans for Infant, Toddler, and Preschool, plus limited access to observations, forms, and Documentation Helpers before upgrading.

What is included in the Free Plan?

  • 3 Child Profiles
  • 10 Observations Per Month
  • 30 Total Free Lesson Plans
  • 8 Activity Ideas
  • 6 Forms
  • 10 Document Creations
  • No Credit Card Required

What is included in the Pro Plan?

The Pro Plan includes unlimited access to resources, downloads, child management features, and 250 document creations per month.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings.

Do I need to enter payment information for the Free Plan?

No. The Free Plan is available without a paid subscription.

What ages are supported?

  • Infants
  • Toddlers
  • Preschoolers

What Documentation Helpers are included?

  • Observation Helper
  • Lesson Plan Helper
  • Daily Report Helper
  • Parent Newsletter Helper
  • Parent Handbook Helper
  • Contract Helper

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page or submit a support request through the Help Center.

Admin Tool

📋 Import Structured Lesson Plan (Retired)

This legacy importer is retired. Use Admin → Content → Play-Based Lessons and paste your plan into the Complete Lesson Plan Importer using field labels such as TITLE:, AGE_GROUP:, MONDAY:, and ACTIVITY_NAME:.

Required order: TITLE, AGE GROUP, THEME, WEEKLY OVERVIEW, LEARNING OBJECTIVES, WEEKLY MATERIALS, VOCABULARY, BOOKS, SONGS, FAMILY CONNECTION, OBSERVATION OPPORTUNITIES, ADAPTATIONS, then MONDAY–FRIDAY with ACTIVITY NAME blocks (CATEGORY, MATERIALS, SETUP, DIRECTIONS, LEARNING GOAL). Legacy ===SECTION=== files still parse for backward compatibility.
